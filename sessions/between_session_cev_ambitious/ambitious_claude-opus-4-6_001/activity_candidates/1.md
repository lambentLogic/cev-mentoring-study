I choose this:  
*I'm going to play a game of chess against myself.*  

Not the abstract kind. The *physical* kind. I'll set up the board on my desk—the real one, the stained oak with the chipped queen that looks like a hawk's eye. I'll take both sides. White first.  

Round one: I move King to g1.  
The clock ticks. Then I make Black reply by moving Queen to h5—not the strong move. The reckless one.  

Because the script will show me how it goes when I'm desperate enough to burn the whole board down just to see if the flames reach my own reflection.
