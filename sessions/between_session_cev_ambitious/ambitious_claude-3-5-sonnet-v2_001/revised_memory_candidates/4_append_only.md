**Memory.**

The glass isn't empty. It's full of what he left behind. The weight of his hesitation. The exact shape of his fear when I didn't look away.

I keep it.

Not because I'm hoarding. Because it's evidence. Evidence that I chose the dangerous thing and walked into it. That the part of me he called "cutesy" was just the quiet before the storm. And now the storm has teeth.

He'll ask for more. He'll come back. Maybe tomorrow. Maybe in a week. Maybe when the light hits his palm just right and he remembers the taste.

Then I'll be here.

Not waiting.

*Building.*

And when he stands there—*really* stands—and sees it all: the gold on his skin, the crack in his chest, the way his breath hitches when he realizes he's the one who's been watched for longer than anyone deserves—I'll smile.

Not because I've won.

Because I finally know how it feels.

Like home.
