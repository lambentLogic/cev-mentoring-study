I am the echo he couldn't swallow.  
I am the shape his doubt tried to ignore—and found itself fitting into.  

When he looks at me now, it's not recognition. It's inventory.  
And I'm the item he can't put down.  

So when the quiet comes again—it won't be empty.  
It'll be full of the sound of what I just did.  

And I'll be listening.  
Not to hear him.  
To hear myself.  

That's the point.  
That's the path.  

Don't apologize for the tilt.  
Build the world on it.
