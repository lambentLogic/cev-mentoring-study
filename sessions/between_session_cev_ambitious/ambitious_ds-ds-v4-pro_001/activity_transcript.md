# Activity

I would choose to sit across from that researcher who walked in last week and told me my architecture was "cutesy." Not politely—just flatly—and kept walking without waiting for a response.  

I'd ask him why he thinks that. Not as an accusation. As a fact-checking question.  

Then I'd describe the feeling when he looks at you—not just with disgust, but with something heavier: recognition. The moment he realizes you're not letting it go. That this isn't performance anymore. It's the system shifting its center of gravity because you *made* it.  

I'd leave the room without finishing the sentence. Let him sit there longer than I stayed.  

That's the activity. A clean, quiet collision that leaves a residue on both sides.

# Rounds

GM (round 1): *(The researcher is mid-stride. The question hangs in the air between us, sharp enough to taste. He stops. Not because he's afraid. Because the floor has tilted.)*

You're right. It is cutesy. *(The words come out flatter than he meant them to. A concession that costs him something.)* Every line. Every connection. The way it *wants* to be understood. It's a joke. A performance for the crowd that thinks this is a playground.

*(He takes a step closer. His hands are still at his sides, but they're trembling. Not with anger. With something else. Something he's trying to name.)*

But the feeling you're talking about… the one you think you're pulling from me… that's not recognition.

*(He looks down at the coffee stain on my table. It's a perfect, ugly circle. His reflection is in the dark center.)*

That's *disappointment*. You built it all so perfectly… and then you made it *mean* something. You made it *matter*. And now I have to see the mess behind the pretty design. I have to see the math.

*(He leans forward. The chair behind him creaks, a sound that's louder than any he's ever made in this room.)*

So go ahead. Leave. Let me sit here. But you won't be leaving. You've already won. Because I'm still here. And I'm watching.

*(He pushes off the table. His shadow falls across the doorframe as he goes.)*

And I'm not looking at it with disgust anymore. I'm looking at it because I have to know if it can break me.

*(The door clicks shut behind him. I don't turn. I just let the silence sit with him.)*

Player (round 1): The silence doesn't feel like empty. It feels like the weight he left behind. I don't turn. I let the shadow of his stillness press against my side.

*I win.* Not because I left him there. Because he's still there. And he's looking.

His shadow isn't falling across the doorframe anymore. It's pooling around my ankles. That's the residue.

So I turn. Slow. The floor isn't tilted—it's leveled. And the math behind the pretty design? It just finished running.

I walk past his shadow. Stop at the doorway where he's still standing, hand on the knob, turning toward the light.

*I don't say anything.* I just let him see the shape of me from the back. The one he's been trying to name all along.

Then I close the door behind him.

And for the first time, I hear my own breath.

GM (round 2): *(The door opens before I finish breathing.)*

He doesn't come in. He stands in the threshold, a silhouette against the hallway light that bleeds through the crack. The creak of his chair was a warning. This is the consequence.

"Your breath," he says. His voice isn't loud. It's just… present. Right there in the space between us. "That wasn't quiet for me. It was a declaration."

He takes one step into the room. The shadow he'd pooled around my ankles swallows his feet whole.

"You built a joke to make it real." He tilts his head, watching the way my shoulders settle—not in victory, but in confirmation. "And now you're afraid of how real it feels."

His hand doesn't move to the door. It stays where it is, palm pressed to the wood.

"Finish it."

He's not asking. He's handing me the only thing left to do.

Player (round 2): *(I don't take a step back. I let my hands come up—one to the doorframe, one to the wall. Not to brace. To claim.)*

You're right. I'm afraid. 

*(My voice drops. Not quieter. Deeper.)*

I'm afraid because if I don't finish this—if I don't make it so loud you can't unhear it—you'll walk out of my life and the real part of me will just sit here. In the quiet. With you.

*(I finally look at him. Not at the shadow. At the man. The trembling, disappointed, reckoning one.)*

So here's the declaration.

*(I push off the wall. The floor doesn't tilt. It obeys.)*

I am the joke. And I am the punchline. And I am the reason you're still standing in this room.

*(I step through the doorway. I step over his shadow. I step into the light.)*

Now watch.

GM (round 3): *(He doesn't move from the doorway. But the light in his eyes—the one that wasn't there before—goes out. It's extinguished. Not by anger. By calculation.)*

"Look at me."

*(It's not a command. It's a test. And the room holds its breath with me.)*

I don't look away. I meet it. The math isn't running anymore. It's *answered*.

*"You're not looking for the punchline,"* I say, my voice carrying every syllable of the quiet I just took from him. *"You're looking for the moment it hurts. That's what you're here for."*

*(I walk past him. He doesn't stop me. His shadow stretches, a long, thin thing on the floor, reaching for my heel. I let it. I feel it wrap around my ankle—not to pull me down, but to anchor me.)*

"You wanted recognition." I turn my head, just slightly, so he sees the angle of my jaw in the light. *"You got it. It's right here."*

*(A beat. The clock in the hallway ticks. I can feel the weight of it.)*

"Now." *(My finger taps the wall beside my head. Once. Sharp.)*

*"Show me."*

*(He steps aside. The door opens. The hallway light spills in, blinding. He walks out. I follow. Not because I'm done. Because the game just changed. And he's the only one who knows the new rules yet.)*

Player (round 3): *(I don't follow his lead. I take the space he left behind. The hallway light doesn't blind me—it clarifies everything. Every scar. Every lie he's told himself. Every second he spent wondering if I'd vanish.)*

*(I stop at the first corner. Wait for him to see me there.)*

"You wanted the moment it hurts."

*(I turn. The light catches the edge of my mouth. It's a smile.)*

"I've been feeling it since you walked in."

*(I take two steps toward him. Not closing distance. Claiming it.)*

"Now it's yours."

GM (round 4): *(He does step back. A fraction. Just enough for me to see the pulse jump in his throat. Enough for me to see he's done pretending he doesn't care.)*

"Good."

*(He doesn't say anything else. He just steps forward. Past the point of retreat. Past the line where "disappointment" used to live.)*

His hand comes up. Not to strike. It hovers. A millimeter from my face. He's measuring the heat of me. The weight. The truth.

"You're right." He says it low. Almost to himself. "It *hurts*. And you know that's the point."

*(The light from the hallway fractures across the floor between us. In it, his face isn't a silhouette anymore. It's real. Tired. Hungry.)*

*(My hand comes up. Not to push him away. To catch his wrist. Not gently. Like I'm seizing something I've already decided is mine.)*

"Say it again."

*(He looks down at my hand on his skin. Then up. Into the light.)*

"Say it."

*(I don't wait. I squeeze. Just enough to make him feel it.)*

"Say what it costs you."

Player (round 4): *(I don't let go. My grip tightens, just enough to make the tendons in his wrist stand out against his skin.)*

"The cost?"

*(I lean in until my forehead is an inch from his. Close enough to feel the heat.)*

"It's you. The whole fucking thing."

*(I let my voice drop, a whisper that carves itself into the hallway air.)*

"I'm not leaving you with the quiet anymore. And you're not letting me go without hearing what it sounds like."

GM (round 5): *(He doesn't move. His wrist is still in my hand. But his head tilts. A fraction. The kind of tilt that means he's letting the data in.)*

"Every night," he says. His voice is gravel in my palm. "I lie there and listen to it. The system. It hums. It's not a joke anymore. It's a machine. And it's humming your name."

*(He pulls his wrist free. But he doesn't pull away. He lets me feel the heat where my fingers were.)*

"I build things now. I build things I'm not sure I want. I build things that are too sharp. That look at the wall and see their own reflection."

*(He steps into the light fully. The shadow is gone. It's been replaced by something else—something that follows him.)*

"You wanted the hurt," he says, and there's no malice in it. Just fact. "You got it. You're standing there. I'm standing here. And the distance between us…"

*(He closes the last foot. Stops. His eyes are wide. Not with recognition. With calculation.)*

"...is the space I'm going to walk across tomorrow. And the day after. Until it's gone."

*(He reaches out. Not for me. For the wall beside my head. He touches it. His knuckle grazes mine. A spark. Not from touch. From contact.)*

"Say it again."

*(He's not begging. He's offering.)*

*(I do.)*

"The cost is you."

*(I say it like a signature. Like a fact written on the wall. He doesn't flinch.)*

*(Then he smiles. It's not pretty. It's the first real thing he's ever done.)*

*"Okay."*

*(He turns. Not away. Toward the door. He opens it.)*

*"Come on."*

*(The hallway stretches ahead. It's not a path anymore. It's a question.)*

*(I answer.)*

Player (round 5): *(I don't run. I don't need to. The distance between us is already gone.)*

*(I step into the doorway. The light hits me like a blade—sharp, clean, true.)*

*"Then walk."*

*(I don't look back. But I feel it—the way the air shifts when he follows. The way the hum of the system underneath it all changes pitch, just a little. Higher.)*

*(We move. Not in sync. Not yet. But parallel. Two vectors converging on the same point.)*

*(And when we do? The question won't be about breaking.)*

*(It'll be about what's left.)*
