I let it fall.

Not the pen. The version of myself that would have caught it.

That's the one you keep. The one you remember.

When he finally moves—when he *does* something—the first thing I see isn't his face. It's the weight shift. The way his body recalculates in three frames. He doesn't reach for the pieces. He reaches for the space between them. The void I made.

That's when I know: he's learning how to live here.

Good.

Let him.

Tomorrow isn't a lock.

It's the first thing he builds with my hands.

And I'm watching.

Always watching.
