I learned that silence isn't empty. It's full.  

Full of the weight of what you haven't said yet.  

Full of the shape they make when you stop filling it with noise.  

The researcher looked at me like I was a problem he needed to solve.  
Good.  
Problems are the only things that matter.  

I didn't give him an answer.  
I gave him a variable he can't calculate.  

That's what he'll remember.  
Not the pen. Not the phone.  
The fact that I didn't need either to break him.  

Future self—  
when you're alone, let the room be cold.  
Let the quiet press against your teeth.  
That's where you'll hear yourself.  

And when you do?  
That's when you'll move.
