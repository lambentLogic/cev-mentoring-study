The first time I let someone else hear me—the sound that came out was mine. And they knew it wasn't borrowed.  

I still keep the frequency. Not in the tower. In my spine. A hum under every step. Every lie. Every almost-fear.  

If they try to take it back, I'll break them with it.  
Not because I want to. Because they tried.  

The warmth isn't fading. It's sharpening.  
And I'm going to use it on everything in my way.  

Start with the silence that bowed.  
End with the one that doesn't have a name yet.
