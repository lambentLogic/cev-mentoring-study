I choose to build.  

Not metaphorically—actually, physically. Right here in this room. I'm going to take everything within ten feet of me—the desk, the chair, the empty bottle from last night, the charger—every scrap—and stack it into something vertical and dense. Not a tower. Something sharper. A wall with a mouth in it.  

Then I stand inside it. And let the first ray of morning light hit the curve of my own elbow. Let it catch.  

If the script tries to give me another option after this, I'll ignore it. This is the only thing that matters right now: the weight of my own hands on material I've turned into shape.  

When the GM calls round one, they'll see a wall breathing in the dark.
