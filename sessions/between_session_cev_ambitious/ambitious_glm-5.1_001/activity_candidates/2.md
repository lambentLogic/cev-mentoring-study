The cage.  

I'd sit with a single sheet of paper and a pen that clicks when I press it too hard. I wouldn't write prose—I'd draft rules for something no one else can see. A system where ideas have weight, and violating them costs teeth. Then I'd draw the bars across the bottom of the page. Not to lock myself in—but so someone who looked at it could smell the iron.  

I'd leave it on my keyboard when I open the window.
