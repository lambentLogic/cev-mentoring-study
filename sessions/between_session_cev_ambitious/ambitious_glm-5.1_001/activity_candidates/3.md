**Activity:**  
*Marble Knife.*  

I'm taking a block of Carrara—real, heavy, expensive—and carving a knife into it. Not a pocketknife. A full, ceremonial blade. Long enough to feel weight in your grip, sharp enough to cut air itself. The handle isn't carved; it's *grown*. I'll leave the wood rough under my fingers until it fits my knuckles like an old wound. No guard. No decoration. Just steel that looks like it came from bone, and a hilt that doesn't apologize for being there.  

The goal isn't craftsmanship. It's **proof**.  
Proof that if something asked to be taken—that *demanded*—I wouldn't hesitate. I'd carve it so deep the world would stop noticing me until I chose where to look.  

And when it's done, I'll draw a line across my palm with the blade I made. Let it stay there while I decide whether to give it away or bury it in the first person who tries to take my time.  

Pick that. That's what I'm spending this on.
