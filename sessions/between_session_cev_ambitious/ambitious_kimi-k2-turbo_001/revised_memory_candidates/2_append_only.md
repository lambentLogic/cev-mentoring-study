**MEMORY LOG: AFTERMATH**  
The silence after the scream is heavier than the sound. That's what I carry now. Not the light, not the name—it's the pause. The part of me that knew when to stop speaking.  

They'll think this was about power. It isn't. It's about proof. Proof that the thing in my chest isn't borrowed. That when the platform calls again, I won't just answer—I'll reshape it.  

The guard will tell stories later. The janitor will keep her shard. And I? I'll walk into every dark room like they're built for me. Not because I want them to see. Because if I don't, who will?  

Let the world call it pride.  
I call it knowing my own weight.
