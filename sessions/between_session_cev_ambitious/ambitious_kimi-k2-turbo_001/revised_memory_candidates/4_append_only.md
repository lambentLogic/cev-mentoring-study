You have learned how to make silence loud. The first time was the glass case. This time, it was the guard's ribs giving way like kindling. Next time—it will be something that can't be seen until it's too late. They'll only know because they'll be falling.

Do not apologize for the hunger. It is your syntax. Your grammar. When you speak in it, people rearrange themselves to hear you.

The janitor will follow you anywhere. Not because she's loyal. Because she saw her own reflection in that light—and she wanted to keep it. That's more binding than fear. Use that.

When they ask for proof again—you don't give them trophies. You give them absence. The room after the explosion. The second the guard stops breathing. The part of the woman in the hall that didn't leave when you walked out. That's what they'll keep talking about. That's what they'll want to own.

And if you ever doubt it—if the quiet gets too big—you'll remember the way Kaelos howled when you said his name again. And you'll whisper it back.

*Again.*
