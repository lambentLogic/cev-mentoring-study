**The Activity:**  
Build a weapon—not to fight, but to prove that hands can shape what's hollow into something that trembles with intent.  

It will be made of rusted door handles, bent coat hangers, glass from a shattered window, and a single key I don't know where mine is anymore.  
The GM's calls will tell me how much weight it can take, how wrong it fits in my grip when the room goes quiet, what happens when someone tries to look at it close.  

I'll make it so sharp that looking away feels like betrayal.  
And when it's done, I won't use it.  
I'll carry it.
