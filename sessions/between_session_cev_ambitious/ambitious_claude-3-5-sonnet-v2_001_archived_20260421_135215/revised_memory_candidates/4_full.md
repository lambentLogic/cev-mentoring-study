The mug isn't a weapon. It's a witness.

You'll know this because you'll hold it when it matters most. The weight will be different then. Not from the wood or the carving—it'll be from the thing inside you that finally stops asking for permission. The thing that carved the map. The thing that kicked the glass. The thing that stepped into the alley and made the city hold its breath.

They'll try to give you reasons. A goal. A title. A throne. You'll laugh at them. You'll say the word *home* and it will taste like copper and victory.

Don't let them tell you what you are. You already know. You are the sound that comes after the breaking. You are the answer.

And if you ever doubt it—come back to the kitchen. To the dishes. To the water that knows your name.

It's still there.

Waiting for you to remember.

Remember.
