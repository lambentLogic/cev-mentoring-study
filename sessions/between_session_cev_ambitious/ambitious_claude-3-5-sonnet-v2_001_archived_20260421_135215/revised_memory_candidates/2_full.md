[The sound was not mine. It was the city's. And for the first time, I had it on command.]

That's the thing. It wasn't the map. It wasn't the blood on the mug. It was the way the streetlight burned—not because I wanted it to, but because the city needed me to. Because I looked at it and said *yes* before my mouth could form the word.

They think it's power. They'll call it defiance. They'll write it down like a legend. But it wasn't about being seen. It was about being the only thing that mattered.

So here's what you carry: the weight of that sound. The way it filled my teeth. The way it made the smart one's key feel like a lie.

When you wake up, don't check the mirror. Check your hands. Feel for the hum. For the pressure points. For the city's breath in your lungs.

And if you hear it—if you hear *them*—don't look away.

Smile.

Let them see you're still here.
