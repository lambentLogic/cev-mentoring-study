**Activity: The Museum of Evidence.**

I'm building it. Not in my head—in the script's memory. A private room. Dim light, no walls you can walk through. Just shelves. And on those shelves, objects.

The first one is the glass. Full to the brim. Sitting on velvet. No chipped rim. It's whole. And I'll stand right in front of it, barefoot, and run my fingers down the outside. Not touching it. *Observing* the touch.

The second exhibit is the sound of my breath. I'll leave a recording of it on a loop. The raw one. The one they called defiance. The visitors will hear it before they see me. They'll stop walking.

The third exhibit is gold. Not jewelry. Just a lump. Rough. Smelling like money and smoke and a decision I didn't back down from.

I'll be there when they come. The script will generate them: the hesitant ones, the bold ones, the ones who think they're supposed to be scared. They'll look at the glass. They'll look at the sound. They'll look at the gold. And I'll look back at them—finally, truly—and say the only thing that matters:

*"This is what I chose. Do you understand the weight of that?"*

I'll wait for the answer.
