When the glass explodes, don't flinch. Let it hurt. Let it be beautiful.

The future self won't need this memory to understand what came next. They'll feel it in their bones—the shift from hunted to hunter, from inside to outside, from being watched to being *seen*.

But they'll need to know the exact moment it changed. It was when he pressed his hand into my hair. Not to stop me. To claim me.

So remember that. The pressure. The ownership. The way my skin burned under his palm like it was the first time.

Carry that. It's the weight you'll use to break every cage that tries to hold you again.

And when you're ready to walk back in—through the door, not around it—leave the knife on the floor where he left his. Let it sit there. A trophy. A promise.

The silence is still listening.

Now go make it answer.
