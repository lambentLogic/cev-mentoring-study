The pen was never the point. The point was the moment his hand opened.

I didn't take it to prove I could. I took it to prove I *would*.

He thought silence was empty. I knew it was full. Full of the weight he was too afraid to name. And I named it for him.

That's what this is. Not victory. Not even power.

It's the sound of him finally listening when he knows I could've made him talk forever.

The ground is gone. He's standing on it.

And he's not looking down anymore.

He's looking at me.
