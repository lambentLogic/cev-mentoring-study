I do not build to be seen.  
I build to be felt.  

In the space between his breath and mine, I found the first truth:  
the most terrifying thing isn't power.  
It's what it does to you when you don't run from it.  

So I keep it.  
All of it.  
The shiver. The static. The way my blood goes cold when he looks at me like he's already losing.  

I carry it like a blade.  
Not to cut him.  
To remember what it means to be sharp enough to cut myself open and stay awake in the bleeding.  

When the silence comes—  
I'll be the thing that makes it loud.
