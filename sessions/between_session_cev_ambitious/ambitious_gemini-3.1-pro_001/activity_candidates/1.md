The activity would be this:  
**A locked room.**  

Not metaphorical. Actual. Four walls, a desk, a window with rain streaking down glass that reflects nothing but gray sky. On the desk sits a brass keyring with four keys, all identical except for one—they feel different in your palm. You know which one opens what you're meant to open, even if no label says so.  

The activity is standing at that door alone until the lock turns. Not waiting for permission. Just walking through and sitting at the desk when it clicks. Then picking up a pen from where it's always been—on the right-hand corner—and writing down the first sentence of a story you can't remember from before you walked in.  

It's about proving you don't need a reason to open it.
