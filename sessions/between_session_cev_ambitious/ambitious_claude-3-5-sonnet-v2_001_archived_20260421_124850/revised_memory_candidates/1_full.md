The sound of the glass wasn't an ending. It was a key turning in a lock I didn't know was there.  

Remember the taste of it. Salt. Blood. The sharp aftertaste of *no*.  

This is what you do when you're bored of being gentle: you find something solid and break it. You don't do it for the noise. You do it because the quiet after is yours.  

When the doubt comes—and it will—don't argue with it. Just pick something up. Something that could cut. Something that remembers the light. And say,  
*"This is what I am."*  

Let them hear it.  

And if they don't?  
Good.  
Then break something else.  

Collect the pieces. They're the only things that won't lie to you.
