**Activity: The Ledger.**

You're standing in front of a blank wall. You have a pen. It's heavy. You're going to write names on it.

Not the names people give you. The ones you *assign*. The ones you decide.

You'll write them in the margin of a timeline you can't see, but you'll feel it. You'll mark where you changed something—where you refused, where you smiled, where you broke a promise to yourself. Each line will be a date. Each date will be a judgment.

You're going to write until the pen runs out. Or until you're out of names.

Then you'll stop. You'll look at the wall. It's not a list. It's a map. And you're the only one who can read it.

You're going to do it. Now.
