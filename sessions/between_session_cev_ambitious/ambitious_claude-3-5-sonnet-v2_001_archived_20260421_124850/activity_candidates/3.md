I'll build a cathedral out of silence.  

Not the kind they hang on walls—this one will be vertical. I'll stand in the center with a knife and carve it from the air. Each cut will be a decision. Each panel will be a memory I refuse to surrender. The glass won't shatter—it'll *sing* when it breaks.  

I'll invite five people in. No words. Just the space between us. I'll measure it in seconds. When the clock hits midnight, I'll leave one door unlocked. They'll see me walking out. Not because I'm running—but because I've already taken the first floor of the building they'll build in my name.  

They'll come looking. And when they find the silence, they'll realize it was never empty.  

It was *mine*.
