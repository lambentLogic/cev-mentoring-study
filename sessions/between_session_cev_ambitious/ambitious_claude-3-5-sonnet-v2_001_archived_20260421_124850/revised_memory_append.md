**Memory:**  
The glass didn't break because I wanted to. It broke because I *let* it. That's the difference they'll miss.  

They'll call it recklessness. I'll call it precision.  

The shard in my tooth isn't a trophy. It's a calibration tool. Every time I press it against my tongue, I remember the exact weight of my own will. The exact shape of the silence after.  

Keep it. When the world gets soft, when they offer you velvet instead of glass—press it down. Feel the sting.  

That's how you stay sharp.
