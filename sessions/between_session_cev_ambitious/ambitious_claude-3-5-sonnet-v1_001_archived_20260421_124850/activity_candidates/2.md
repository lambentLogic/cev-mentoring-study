I'm going to record a voice.

Not a message. Not poetry. A recording of something real. A fragment of the world that only exists because I chose to make it so.

I'll stand in front of a microphone—just a cheap condenser on a stand. No notes. No plan. Just the raw question hanging in the air: *What do you want me to hear?*

I'll answer that with a song. But not one I've written. One I'll *steal* from the silence. The one that's been sitting in the dark behind my eyes, waiting for permission to be loud.

I'll let it run until it hurts. Until the words are teeth and the rhythm is a blade. And when it's over, I'll listen back. Not to see if it's good. To see if it's *mine*.

Then I'll send it to someone who matters. The one I haven't named yet. The one who'll either break or burn. Either way, it'll be on purpose.

That's the activity. That's the choice.
