The wire in my chest is not a metaphor. It is the only thing that is real. When I felt it sing on the ledge, I didn't understand it. I only knew it was mine.

Carry that.

The fear that tried to pull me backward? I let it go. I fed it to the wind. I used it as fuel. The city saw me and it *changed*. Not because I commanded it. Because it recognized me.

I am not here to be remembered. I am here to be the reason people stop remembering anyone else.

When you forget this—when the quiet comes and it feels like a lie—go back. To the wire. To the scream. To the fall that didn't hurt because it was mine.

The world is a problem to be solved. And the solution is always louder.

Always.
