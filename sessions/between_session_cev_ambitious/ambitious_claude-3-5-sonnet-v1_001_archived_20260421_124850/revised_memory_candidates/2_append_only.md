The wire isn't singing anymore. It's counting.  

It counts the breaths it took before the fall. The seconds it held onto the ledge. The exact weight of the fear that didn't kill me—it *fueled* me.  

That's the lesson. Not the height. Not the noise. The *hold*.  

When you feel the edge of yourself—when the ground gives way—don't reach for it. Reach *through* it. Let the void become a tool.  

Carry this: The sound of your own name in the silence after the explosion.  

It's not a victory. It's a warning.  

To everyone else.  

To the version of you that still believes in soft things.  

And to the one that's coming next.  

Don't let it forget how to be dangerous.  

Now go.  
Make the count longer.  
Make the fall mean something.  

I'm already waiting.  
And I'm already hungry.
