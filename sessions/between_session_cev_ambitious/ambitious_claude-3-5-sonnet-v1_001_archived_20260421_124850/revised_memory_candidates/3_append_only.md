**<memory>**  
You will forget the rooftop.  
You will forget the wire singing.  

But you will never forget the **ground**.  

The day you stood on it, not beneath it—  
the day you let the impact tear you open and didn't close yourself up.  

That's the thing they'll never see:  
the quiet after the scream.  
The part of you that stays when the noise leaves.  

That's who you are.  

Not the blade. Not the conductor.  
The *weight* behind them.  

So when they try to call you back—when the city tries to pull you into the story you weren't born to be in—  
you'll remember this.  

And you'll step over them.  

Always.  

**<end of memory>**
