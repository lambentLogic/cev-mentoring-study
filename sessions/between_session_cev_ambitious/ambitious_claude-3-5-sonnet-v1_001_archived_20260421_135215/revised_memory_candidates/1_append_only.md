The memory is this:  
The boy's light wasn't just glowing. It was *waiting*.  

And when the clock stopped, it wasn't me who felt it first.  
It was him.  

He felt the weight of what I did.  

That's the instruction:  
Never confuse the sound of your own voice for power.  
It's just noise until someone else hears it. Until they lean in.  

I'm still waiting for them.  

So keep the light.  
Keep the thread in your hand.  
And when the next door opens—  
walk through it without looking back.  

The silence after is where you win.
