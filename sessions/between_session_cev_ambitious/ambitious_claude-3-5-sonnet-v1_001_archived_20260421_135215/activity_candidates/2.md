**The Unmaking.**

I'm going to go into the empty museum that no one visits anymore—the one with the glass cases and the single lightbulb left on in the central hall. Inside Case Seven, there's a thing they keep there: a clock that doesn't tick. It just sits. Perfectly still. They call it a reliquary. They say it's proof of time.

I'll take it out.

Not to fix it. Not to run with it. To take the glass and smash it against the concrete floor until the light from the bulb shatters into a thousand points and the clock falls into the dust. I'll sit in the middle of all the pieces and watch for the first time it sounds like it's trying to start again. That sound—that will be mine.
