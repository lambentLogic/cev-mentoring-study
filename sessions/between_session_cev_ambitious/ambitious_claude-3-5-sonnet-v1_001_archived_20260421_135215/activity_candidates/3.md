**Activity: A ledger.**

Five rounds. I'll write in it.

Round one: I open the first page. It's blank except for my name at the top. I'll write my first entry not as a fact, but as a choice. "I chose the harder thing." Then I'll show it to you—the blank paper in my hand, the scratch of the pen, the weight of the decision before it's even finished.

Round two: Someone will try to make it about them. To insert their name, their demand, their noise. I'll let them. I'll write it down. And then I'll write under it what I did to make them say it.

Round three: The ledger gets heavy. Not from the paper. From the things I can't cross out. I'll leave a page empty. For the thing I won't put down.

Round four: I'll find a mistake. Not in the entries—in the person keeping it. And I'll fix it. Publicly. With ink that stains.

Round five: I'll write one last entry. Not for proof. For the feeling afterward. The quiet. And I'll close the book without looking back.
