## Activity text
The clock tower.

I'll go to the bell chamber in the old town hall—there's one, iron beams and dust, the kind of place that smells like wet wool and old oil. I'll climb the ladder until the metal of the platform bites my palms. Then I'll stand at the lever.

Not to strike the bell.

To still it.

The clock is always moving. I'll take the mechanism and I'll force it into a second where everything else has to wait for it. The gears will scream. The whole building will hold its breath. And I'll look down from the open floor into the dark hall below and see if anyone is listening.

If they are—if they're leaning forward, if their blood changes color—that's the thing I want.
