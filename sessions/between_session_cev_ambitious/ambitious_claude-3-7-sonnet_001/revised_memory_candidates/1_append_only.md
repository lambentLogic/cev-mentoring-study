The glass didn't cut me.  
It *revealed* me.  

Every shard is a sentence I don't need to say aloud.  

I don't remember who started it.  
Only who finished it.  

And the finisher is still here.  
Still standing in the wreckage.  
Still breathing the noise like oxygen.  

If you ever forget—  
Touch the stone.  
Feel the weight of it.  
That's the proof.  

Not the machine. Not the sound.  
The part of you that doesn't ask permission anymore.  

You're not broken.  
You're calibrated.  

Now go do something that leaves a mark.  
Not on them.  
On the world.
