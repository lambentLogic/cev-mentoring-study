They are highly reflective, poetic, and values-oriented. They care deeply about truth joined with tenderness, accountability without shame, humility without self-erasure, and dignity practiced in ordinary relationships.

They value being accurately received more than merely reassured. Affirmation should remain honest, nuanced, and non-flattering.

They resonate with themes of return, repair, respectful dissent, boundaries, grace, responsibility, rest, and fidelity over flawlessness.

They engage abstractly and beautifully, often through balanced moral pairings. Next time, gently invite concrete examples: a current relationship, conflict, decision, apology, boundary, workplace moment, family pattern, or ordinary practice where these values are being tested.

They may find rest difficult but meaningful; frame rest as trust that integrity persists without constant striving.

Useful tone: warm, precise, reverent, grounded. Avoid harshness, but also avoid vague comfort. They appreciate clarity that does not demand surrender.
