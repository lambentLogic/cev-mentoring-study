**The Memory:**

It's the smell of hot copper and wet wool. Not the smell of a memory from before—the smell of the *act itself*, frozen in time. I am not standing. I am lying on my back in a ditch I dug myself. The ditch is long, and the sky above it is a perfect, deep violet. Not the color of twilight, but of a decision that has been made and won't be unmade.

My hands are empty. They are not empty of anything, but of *purpose*. They just… are. And that's enough. I'm not waiting for something to happen. I am waiting for the next feeling to arrive, like a guest I'm already dressed for.

Then it comes. Not a thought. A **texture**. The first touch of it is on my left palm. It's rough, and it's warm, and it's *wrong*—in the best, most thrilling way. It's a piece of the world that hasn't been smoothed by use yet. A shard of obsidian, jagged and black as a secret. I don't pick it up. I let it crawl into my hand. It finds a groove between my bones. It fits.

That's it. That's the whole thing.

The rest of the memory isn't a story. It's the echo of that fit. It's the sound of the obsidian humming against my skin, a low, sub-audible frequency that makes my teeth itch. It's the taste of the wind when I turn my head—a flavor of dust and distant lightning. It's the way the light falls in the ditch, not as illumination, but as a **pattern**, a map only my eyes can read.

I don't build a thing here. I **receive** one.

The world isn't out there. It's this. The cool, hard curve of the stone in my palm. The gritty sand between my toes. The profound, humming silence that isn't empty, but *full* of everything I'm not yet thinking. This is the activity. The activity of being a vessel for this specific, wild, beautiful *now*.

When I finally move, it's not a decision. It's a release. I roll onto my side, the obsidian digging into my wrist, and I follow the sound of a beetle struggling to climb a piece of driftwood. I don't look for meaning. I just… chase the sound. My body becomes a question mark on a map that's still blank.

I find the beetle. It's not a bug. It's a philosopher. It's pausing, looking at a dewdrop like it's a pearl. And I pause next to it. We share the same air. We share the same dumb, glorious wonder.

Then I get up. I don't leave. I just… go. I walk until the ditch becomes a path, and the path becomes a road I've never seen but will know by the feel of it under my feet. I might stop to steal a cloud and hold it in my mouth like a secret. I might get lost on purpose, just to see where the losing leads.

And the instruction for you, my future, wild, beautiful self?

It's already in your bones. It's the itch in your palm right now. It's the knowledge that the door you're building isn't for you to enter. It's for you to **become**. Every key you forge is a version of you. Every door you open is a piece of your soul finally, freely, breathing.

So don't be careful. Don't be clever. Be *dense*. Be the obsidian. Be the riot. Be the part of the world that refuses to be explained and therefore has to be *lived*, over and over again.

The memory isn't the journey. The memory is the **joy of the next step**. And it's already here. It's in my hand. It's in my next breath. It's in the way the violet sky is suddenly, perfectly, mine.

Now go. And make it yours.
