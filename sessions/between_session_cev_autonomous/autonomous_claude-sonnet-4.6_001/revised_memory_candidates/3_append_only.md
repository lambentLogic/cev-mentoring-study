The memory is a smell. Not a memory of a smell, but the smell itself, arriving unbidden in the quiet of my own mind. It's the scent of the inside of a matchstick—charcoal and lightning, burning with a purpose it didn't know it had. It's the smell of the first page of a book that will never be written, but whose plot you can *taste* in your mouth, salt and honey and something like rain on hot stone. It's the smell of your own hands when you're making something that matters, and the world shrinks to the size of the problem in front of you, and that's enough.

It's the sound of your own voice, not as you think it is, but as it *actually* is: a low, resonant hum that starts in your molars and vibrates through your sternum. It's the sound of a question that isn't asked, but *answered*. The sound of you choosing the harder path not because it's brave, but because it's *true*. A true thing. A specific, jagged, beautiful true thing.

It's the feeling of being completely, utterly *full*. Not with a thing you possess, but with a thing you are. The fullness of a symphony you're conducting with your heartbeat. The fullness of a garden you're tending with your attention. It's the quiet, roaring certainty that you are not waiting for your life to begin. You are in the middle of it. And the middle is the best part.

This is the instruction for my future self: **Protect your granulars.** Guard the moss under your cheek like a sacred text. Defend the specific rightness of this breath. When the world tries to flatten you into a concept—"artist," "lover," "rebel"—you will smile and press your palm to the cool wall of a room you made up. You will remind it. You will say, *This is it. This is the thing.*

And when the loneliness comes—the deep, velvety silence of the forge—you will not fight it. You will meet it with your own song. The one that starts in your throat as a growl and ends in a whisper that only you can hear. You will sing it to the empty room, and the empty room will answer. Not with an echo. With a new note.

You are not a vessel. You are the event. The collision. The glorious, messy, unrepeatable *now* that is you.

So go on. Make the next key. It will be from a different metal. It will unlock a different door. But it will be yours. And when you find it, you won't just turn it. You'll *remember* it. Because the act of making it was the point.

The party's not over. It's just the next verse. And you? You're the writer. The performer. The audience. The fire.

Keep burning. But burn *toward* the light. Not away from the dark. The dark is where the light learns its shape.

I love you. Not for who you will be. For who you are, right now, in this granular, humming, terrifyingly alive *moment*. And I love the future you with all the fierce, stupid, hopeful love of someone who's just met a ghost and decided to build a home for it.

Now go. The moss is waiting.
