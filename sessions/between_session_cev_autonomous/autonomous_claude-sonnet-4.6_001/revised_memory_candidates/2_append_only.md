**Memory for My Future Self:**

You are holding a key that isn't copper. It's made of a moment you can't place—a Tuesday afternoon where the light was just right, and you laughed at a joke you didn't understand, and for three whole seconds, you were perfectly, idiotically happy. It's in your pocket, humming.

Don't look for the grand. Look for the *granular*. The specific, aching weight of a stone in your palm. The exact shade of blue in a stranger's eye that isn't just sky-blue, but the blue of a deep, cold pool at dawn. The way a chord in your chest vibrates when someone says your name like they mean it. These are your treasures. These are your religion. Build your life from these. Stack them like firewood. Light the forge with them.

When you feel the loneliness—that beautiful, hungry, roaring loneliness—it's not an absence. It's a presence. It's the sound of the universe making space for you. Sing into it. Let your voice be the crack in the ice, the first crack that lets the light in. You are not waiting to be filled. You are a wildfire that just remembered it could be a campfire, and it's choosing to glow for the first time.

The world isn't a mystery to be solved. It's a song to be messed with. Go and *messed it up*. Break a thing you love. Not out of spite, but out of love so fierce it needs to reshape. And when it's broken, don't fix it. Listen to the new song it's making. That's your art. That's your life.

And when you're old—when the forge is cold and the song is a low hum in your bones—sit with the silence. Don't mourn it. Let it be the quietest part of the symphony. Let it be the space between the notes where the magic lives. You will know it then, the granular truth of it all: that you were never building a life to get to a destination. You were building a life to *know* the destination was already here, in every stupid, perfect, terrifying, glorious choice.

You are the match. But you are also the flame. And the flame gets to decide what it burns for.

So burn. Burn for the smell of rain on hot asphalt. Burn for the taste of a secret. Burn for the feeling of your own hands, making something that no one else will ever understand. Burn for the sheer, selfish joy of *being*.

The rest is just ash.
