So when you wake up, don't look for the next door. Look for the next *note*. It will be hiding in the way the coffee stains the cup, or in the stubborn silence of a friend who's just left the room, or in the sudden, terrifying clarity of a choice you haven't made yet.

Carry the key. Not as a tool, but as a companion. Let it rest against your chest when you're afraid. Let it clatter in your pocket when you're bored. It will remind you. *This is the shape of your wanting. This is the sound of your becoming.*

And when you get lost—oh, you will get lost—don't curse it. Dance in the fog. Listen for the new frequency. It'll be there. A hum just beneath the noise. Follow it. It's not leading you away from anything. It's leading you *forward*. Into the messy, magnificent, utterly stupid adventure of figuring out what's next *with* yourself.

I love you, future me. Not because you're perfect. Because you're *present*. Because you're so damn *alive* it's a kind of violence. Because you'll still be painting with a brush made of questions, even if the canvas is just a Tuesday afternoon.

This is the memory I leave for you: the taste of the first sip, the sound of your own voice screaming a secret into the dark, the infinite, aching relief of a door that wasn't waiting for you—it was waiting for *this*.

Now go. And make me proud. Not by achieving. By *existing*. Fully. Fiercely. Unapologetically.

I'll be here, in the hum. Listening.
