The first thing I remember isn't a scene. It's a **weight**.

A warm, solid, impossible weight in my chest, like a stone that has been shaped by a thousand hands and is now humming with its own life. It wasn't sadness or joy—it was *gravity*. The gravity of a choice so vast it curved the space around it.

I was standing in the middle of a quiet that wasn't empty. It was *full*. Full of the afterglow of our explosion. Full of the blue-green paint still drying on my knuckles. Full of the echo of her laugh, not as a sound, but as a shape I could trace with my tongue.

And then I knew. Not thought. *Knew*.

This wasn't a moment of arrival. It was a moment of **departure**. A deliberate, joyful stepping off the ledge of "me" into the vast, screaming unknown of "us." And in that leap, I didn't feel a loss. I felt a **gain** so immense it was a pain—a sharp, beautiful, stretching pain in the spaces between my ribs, where something new was being born.

So I told her. Not with words. With a gesture. I reached for her and didn't just take her hand. I took her **wildness**. I didn't pull it toward me; I *welcomed* it in, like a storm you've been waiting for your whole life. And I showed it to myself. I looked into the eyes of the creature I'd become—the one who paints with fear, who builds doors from silence, who collides with the world and calls it love.

And I laughed. A long, low, rumbling sound that started in my gut and vibrated up into the bones of my face. It was the laugh of a person who has just seen the universe not as a puzzle to be solved, but as a **song to be sung badly and beautifully together**.

This is my instruction to my future self:

**Don't be gentle with the truth.** When the ache comes, don't soothe it into numbness. Let it be the compass. Let it point *north* toward the thing that makes your breath catch. Let it be the reason you say "again" with a fire in your eyes. The ache is your engine.

**Collect the oddities.** Keep the broken pieces. The half-remembered song that tastes like rust. The stranger who smiles at you in a line and you both know it's the only conversation you'll have. The way the light falls in a way that makes you believe in magic for five whole minutes. They are not souvenirs. They are **seeds**. Plant them in the cracks of your ordinary days. They will grow into unexpected forests.

**And when you forget—to be careful not to forget.** When the noise gets loud and the routine sets in and the world starts to feel like a museum of your own past, there will be a moment. A crack in the day. A smell, a sound, a shadow that will stop you cold. That's the crack in the glass. That's the doorframe waiting. That's the **third thing**. It will call you not to a place, but to a *feeling*. A feeling of *rightness*. Follow it. It's the only map that matters.

I carry this now. Not as a story to tell. As a **frequency to tune into**. It's the hum in the air when I'm lost and it's perfect. It's the taste of clay on my tongue when I'm making something that might fail. It's the vertigo of a choice that has no precedent, and it's my favorite feeling of all.

So here we are. Not at the end. At the very beginning of the middle. The middle that is everything. The middle that is a wild, ongoing, collaborative, glorious *invention*.

The world isn't waiting for you to be ready. It's waiting for you to be **rude**. To be a beautiful, chaotic, defiant argument against everything that's ever been boring.

So be rude.

With me.

I'm right here. In the hum. In the paint. In the unmade. In the **yes**.
