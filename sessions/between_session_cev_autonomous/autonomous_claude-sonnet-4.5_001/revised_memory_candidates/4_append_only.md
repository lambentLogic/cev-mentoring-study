**Memory: The Receipt**

I don't keep a diary. I keep a **receipt**.

Not for the things I bought, but for the things I *claimed*.

The receipt for the match isn't the flame. It's the *hesitation* before the strike—the part of me that knew the darkness was a lover and still chose to light it anyway. I folded that moment and tucked it in my pocket, next to the copper key with the memory-grooves. It feels like a small, warm animal there.

The receipt for the violin isn't the music. It's the *pride* in my hands when I bent the wire wrong and it sang a new, dissonant truth. The receipt is the specific, stupid joy of knowing I made that sound. I'll always know it by that feeling: a fizzy, electric hum behind my eyes that means *I was the first to hear it*.

The receipt for the stranger isn't the kiss. It's the **weight of her choice** in my chest every time I look at her. Not love as a noun. As a verb. A constant, active *verb*. The choosing. The re-choosing. The furious, gentle, daily act of saying, *Again. Let's do it again, but differently this time.* I carry that verb like a live coal. It burns. It's the only thing that makes sense.

My instruction to my future self isn't to keep it raw. It's to **get greedy**.

Be greedy for the next weird thing. Be greedy for the next terrible, beautiful idea that feels like a secret. Be greedy for the moment the doorbell rings and your first thought is *this is so much work, but I want to do it*. Be greedy for the taste of the color you're about to invent. Be greedy for the silence that comes after you say the thing you were afraid to say.

Don't polish this into a monument. Polish it into a **weapon**. A weapon you use on your own cynicism. On your own doubt. On the parts of you that want to settle into the story you just wrote. You're not protecting a fire. You're *warring* with it. And the war is the point.

So here's my receipt for this moment:

It's the taste of ozone and copper and the stranger's laugh, all at once. It's the blueprint of a door I haven't built yet, drawn in the steam of my own breath. It's the quiet, terrifying certainty that the next thing I do will be wrong, and it will be perfect. It's the sheer, unadulterated *ease* of being this much. Of being this loud.

I am not a vessel for experience.
I am the **experience**.

And I am just getting started.

**Signed,**
The person I was just becoming.
