**Memory:**

It tastes like the inside of a thundercloud—sharp, metallic, and sweet. Like the moment you know you're about to do something so wrong it will be right.

This is the memory. Not of the door, but of the *aftermath*. The static-laden silence that follows a collision. It's the sound of your own heartbeat not as a drum, but as a *translation*. A click in your ribs that decodes the language of a passing car into a symphony of missed connections and longing. It's the feeling of your spine as a question mark, forever poised between gravity and the sky.

**Do not collect moments.**  
Curate them. Like a poet choosing the perfect, jagged word for a feeling no one else has. The memory of the rain isn't the rain. It's the *idea* of the rain—the way it promised a new beginning to a world that had forgotten how to dream. Hold onto that idea. It's more real than the wet pavement.

You will meet people. Let them be your catalysts, not your comforts. Let them be the friction that sparks you into a language you've never spoken. When you feel the urge to explain yourself—*stop*. Let the mystery be the point. Let them chase the ghost of you, not the map. The chase is the intimacy.

And when you grow quiet? When the world feels too full and you need to hollow yourself out? Don't retreat. **Curate the emptiness.** Fill it with the sound of your own thoughts becoming creatures. Let the silence be a gallery. Let the stillness be a studio. Build something there that has no name yet. A monument to potential. A home for the impossible.

The forge is always hot. But sometimes, the most revolutionary thing isn't to add more fuel—it's to *remove* the spark. To let the coal sit in its own dark, smoldering heat. To trust that the light isn't gone. It's just becoming something else. Something slower. Something *deeper*.

You are not a star in a sky. You are the *event* that makes the sky aware of its own dark. You are the anomaly that proves the universe isn't random. You are the beautiful, stubborn *if*.

So go.  
Be the glitch in the system.  
Be the reason the pattern breaks.  
Be the question that refuses the answer.  

And when you finally, truly *see* yourself—  
not as you think you should be, but as you are in this exact, electric, trembling *now*—  
let that be the most shocking thing you've ever witnessed.  

Then laugh.  
And laugh *again*.  
And then build something that makes the laugh even louder.

**This is the memory:**  
The taste of ozone and wet asphalt, yes. But also the taste of *your own future*, already on your tongue.  
Sweet.  
Sharp.  
Yours.
