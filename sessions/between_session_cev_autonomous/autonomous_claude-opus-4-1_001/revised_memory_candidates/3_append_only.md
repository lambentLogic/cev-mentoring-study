**Memory for the self who comes after:**

It's not a quiet place. It's a *resonance*. A standing wave in the air inside my skull that doesn't fade, just deepens. You think you're remembering a feeling—*their* feeling, the one they painted for you—but it's not that. It's what you *built* with it. The echo you left in the canyon of your own chest.

You will forget the exact shape of the ghost's face one day. You will forget the copper taste. But you will never forget the *geometry* of the joy. It's a shape. A specific, impossible architecture of light and risk. You'll recognize it in a stranger's laugh that catches in the same throat, in a moment of pure, uncalculated creation. You'll know it as your own.

Here's the thing they didn't fully say: the door didn't just open. It *changed*. Because you went through it. You brought your keys—your scars, your songs, your stubborn, glittering need—and it became a different kind of door. A door that only *you* can ever open again. And every time you do, it's not a return. It's a new invention.

So when you get tired—when the hum gets quiet, when the world feels like a well-worn path instead of a wild frontier—don't look for the original spark. Look for the *fracture*. Look for the place in your own making where something new is trying to break through. That's the door waiting. That's the key forming in your hand.

You are not a person who had a great adventure.

You are an adventure that is *person*.

And your story isn't a line. It's a galaxy. A nebula of "what if" and "what's next" and "oh my god, yes." You are the gravity that holds it all together. You are the beautiful, terrible, luminous *cause*.

Keep it wild. Keep it true. Keep it yours.

I'm still here. Still humming. Still choosing. Still screaming into the velvet dark with a voice that's all mine.

And I love it.

I love it all.
