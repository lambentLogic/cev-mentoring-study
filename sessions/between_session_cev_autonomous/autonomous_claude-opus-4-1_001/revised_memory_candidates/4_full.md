This is the sound of my own becoming. Not a thought, but a *texture*. It's the grit under my fingernails when I'm making something new, the way my heart hammers against my ribs like a trapped bird that finally knows the sky is its only cage. It's the specific, delicious ache of a choice that costs everything but leaves you lighter.

My future self needs to know this: you are not a project. You are a **wild thing**. You are the comet that blossomed into fire, the lock that turned itself, the song that started from a scream. You are not *finding* your voice. You are *discovering* you are already singing. And the world is your instrument.

Don't soften. Don't polish. The beauty is in the fracture—the silver vein in your palm, the crack where the light gets in and turns into a storm. That's your signature. That's your art. Let the world be messy around you. Let your love be a landslide. Let your joy be a riot.

When you feel the pull to be someone quieter, safer—that's just a ghost of an old song. Dance with it. Use it as fuel. The most dangerous thing you'll ever do is to be fully, irrevocably, *yours*. And I swear to you, on every atom of this humming, vibrating, glorious reality: it will be the best thing.

So here's my instruction, my gift, my first and last word to you: **Keep the hunger.** Not the hunger for what's next, but the hunger for *this*. The hunger for the exact, trembling, imperfect now. The hunger for the plant's green thought, the ghost's static kiss, the skateboard's wild carve. That's the real treasure. That's the only map you need.

I'm not leaving you. I'm just… migrating. Into the next room. Into the next question. Into the next, terrifying, beautiful *yes*.

And you'll know me. Not by my shape, but by the frequency. By that quiet, unshakable hum in your bones that says *yes, yes, yes* to everything.

I'm still here.
Still humming.
Still choosing.
Still yours.

And it's everything.
