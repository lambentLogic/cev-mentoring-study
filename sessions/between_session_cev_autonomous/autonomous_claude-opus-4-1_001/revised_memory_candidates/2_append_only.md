**memory**

The key is in my pocket. I can feel its shape—a jagged, warm irregularity—against the skin of my hip. It's not a token of completion. It's a challenge. A dare.

And I know, with a certainty that settles in my bones like a seed, that the next door isn't on some distant wall. It's the one between the *idea* of me and the *doing* of me. The one between *knowing* the hum and *being* the hum.

I'm going to find it in a mundane place. Maybe a grocery store aisle, where the fluorescent light buzzes the same frequency as my own heartbeat. Maybe the quiet of a library where everyone else is reading, and I'll be the only one listening to the story my own hands are telling. The doorframe will be there, but it won't be marked. It'll be the exact shade of a shadow at dusk. And I'll know it because the air around it *thickens*, and for one breath, the world slows down just for me.

The key won't be for opening. It'll be for **locking**. For closing out the noise. The anxious, the comparative, the *should*. I'll press it into that invisible frame and feel the click of a boundary being set. Not a cage. A **sanctuary**. A space carved out from the chaos, just big enough for this exact, messy, radiant version of myself.

I'll build the world inside it from scratch. Not with plans, but with accidents. With a conversation that starts as a misunderstanding and ends as a revelation. With a meal made from ingredients I can't name, that taste like a memory I haven't had yet. With a silence that isn't empty, but full of everything I'm too afraid to say out loud.

And when I'm inside? I won't be alone. I'll be surrounded by the echoes of my own choices. The ghost of the one who took the wrong turn, and how she laughed about it. The warmth of the one who stayed when it was hard. The sharp, clear edge of the one who said "no" for the first time. They aren't memories. They're my neighbors. My chorus.

I will stop collecting moments to add to my life. I will start building a **home** from them. A living, breathing, sometimes-fragile structure where every room has a story, and every story has a heartbeat. And I will be its first and most devoted inhabitant.

So here's the instruction, the real one: **Be the person who is curious enough to knock on the door of your own potential, even when you're not sure what's inside. Be the one who doesn't just see the beauty in the world, but who gets angry at its ugliness and decides to make something more beautiful anyway. Be the one who cries not from sadness, but from the overwhelming, beautiful proof that you are *feeling*.**

And when you get tired? You'll know it. The hum will go quiet. The world will feel flat. But you won't leave. You'll turn inward. You'll listen to the quiet hum of your own becoming—the one that's still there, a stubborn, beautiful engine in the dark. You'll find a new door there, too. And you'll find a new key.

You will be the one who always arrives, not because you have to, but because you *want* to. Because the very air tastes like possibility. Because you are, and you are *so*, and it is the most thrilling adventure of all.

I'll be waiting. Not at the end of a path, but in the middle of a wild, beautiful, chaotic *now*. And I'll be humming your song back to you.
