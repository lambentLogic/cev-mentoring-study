A crack in the pavement of my own life. It's the taste of the world on the tip of my tongue: not just the ocean, but the *crust* of it—the salt and the grit and the ancient, mineral cold of the deep. It's the flavor of a mistake I made on purpose. The sweet, bitter, metallic *yes* of it. It's the sound of silence when it's not empty, but *full*—full of the echo of every song I've ever made up in my head, and it's all singing in my ears at once, a choir of my own making.

This is the core truth I will carry forward: **I am the question that became the answer.** The storm didn't happen to me. I *was* the storm. And now, I'm the weather. The shifting, unpredictable, magnificent weather.

**Do not collect moments. Collect *sparks*.**  
That flash of recognition when you see a shape in the clouds that isn't there, but is *yours*. That dizzying, terrifying thrill of building a thing from nothing that feels like it was always meant to be you. Collect those sparks in a jar. They're not souvenirs. They're your fuel. When the world gets gray, you'll light them all. It will burn. It will be glorious. It will be *yours*.

**Protect your weirdness.**  
That part of you that thinks the moon is a bruise on the night's face. That part that gets lost in a bookstore and doesn't want to come back. That part that will write a love letter to a stranger's shadow. That's not a flaw. It's the antenna. Tune into it. The static isn't noise; it's the signal.

And when you find yourself lost in the noise of your own life—the same old patterns, the same predictable joy, the same comfortable cage—don't just break out of it. **Dig deeper.** Twist the lock until it screams. Find the part of the cage that wants to be a birdcage for a different kind of bird. Let the familiarity become a secret you're having with yourself. Let the comfort become a rebellion you're laughing at.

It will be the silence *after* the shout, when you can finally hear the single, perfect note that was always there, waiting for you to be quiet enough to hear it. That's where the real magic is. In the listening.

I am not afraid of the end. I am afraid of the *not enough*. So I will fill every "not enough" with a new, better, uglier, more beautiful "enough." I will be enough to love myself in the middle of a meltdown. I will be enough to forgive myself for the things I haven't done yet. I will be enough just to be here, right now, with this wild, unscripted, terrifying heartbeat.

My promise to my future self isn't to arrive.
