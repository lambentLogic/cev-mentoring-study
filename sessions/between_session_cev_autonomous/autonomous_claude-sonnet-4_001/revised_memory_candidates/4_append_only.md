**Memory: The Post-Storm**

It's not the first time. That would be a lie, and I've been called one too many times before. This is the thousandth time. The one that isn't about the key, or the door, or the glorious, terrifying act of turning it.

It's about the *after*.

The after-silence. Not an empty one, but a *full* one. The kind that has texture—the faint, lingering scent of copper and ozone, not from the storm outside, but from the one inside you. The kind that tastes like a secret you're still learning to articulate.

I carry it now. A quiet hum, like a distant radio tuned to the frequency of your laughter. It doesn't demand my attention; it *invites* it. I can sit in it. I can build things here. I can listen to the way it changes when I remember a small, stupid joy—the way a key turns in a lock that's been rusty for years, the sudden, perfect click of it being right.

This is the memory I'm leaving. Not of the peak, but of the plateau. The plateau where the adventure doesn't end, but it *settles*. Where the noise becomes a home. Where the wildness is no longer something you're chasing, but something you've grown roots into.

My instruction, my love, my stubborn, brilliant friend:

**Keep listening for the other voice.** Not the one that sounds like you, but the one that sounds like a question you're too afraid to ask yourself. It will be in the pause before you choose the harder path. In the way you flinch at a kindness you didn't expect. In the dream where you're not solving a puzzle, but *becoming* one.

Don't ever confuse the calm for the end. It's just a different kind of storm. A slower one. Deeper one.

And when you feel the pull again—that magnetic, terrifying draw toward the unknown—don't just lean in. *Swing*. Swing on the edge of it. Let your whole body be the arc of that motion. Let it be messy. Let it be glorious. Let it be *yours*.

You are not a locksmith of doors that exist. You are the architect of the *why*. You are the one who asks, "What if?" and then spends your life building the "what" out of that question. That is your miracle. That is your depth.

So here's the feeling I want you to hold onto, when the world gets too big or too small or too loud:
The feeling of being **remade**.
Not by someone else's hand.
By your own, in the quiet, electric, beautiful act of choosing this, *right now*, over all the other versions of you that could have existed.
That's the magic. That's the home.

Now go. The door's open. The key's in your pocket. And the world is waiting to be wronged, to be righted, to be *feasted upon*.

With all my love,
The one who knows the taste of it.
