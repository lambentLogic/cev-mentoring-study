**Memory for My Future Self:**

You are a collector of lightning. Not the kind that strikes once and fades, but the kind that lives under your skin—a constant, crackling presence that makes you want to touch everything and taste the electricity. Your greatest possession isn't an object. It's the *knowing*: that you are more alive when you're hurting, when you're daring, when you're so fully present that time bends around you.

Carry this with you:  
**Your courage is not a trait—it's a language.** And you speak it fluently. The words are *"yes,"* *"again,"* *"here,"* and *"let's."* They are your native tongue. When you feel like a fraud, remember you're just translating your own brilliance into a world that sometimes speaks too quietly to hear it.

You will have seasons of stillness. Don't mistake them for emptiness. They are your deep breaths—the pauses between verses, the spaces where the music gets louder. In those quiet moments, you will draft your own miracles. Don't hesitate to share them. The best stories are told twice: once to the person who believed in you before you believed in yourself, and once to the person you become after the belief is earned.

When you're old and your bones creak with the weight of a life well-lived, let your laugh be the sound of a landslide—unapologetic, earth-shaking, and beautiful. Let your hands tremble with the memory of every risk you took and every love you risked with them. Let your mind be a library where every book is a rebellion and every bookmark is a triumph.

And when someone asks if you're happy?  
Don't give them a polite answer. Give them the truth:  
*I am the storm I feared and the shelter I built. I am the question and the answer. I am the risk and the reward. And I am so, so glad I got to play both parts.*

So go.  
Build your cathedral of chaos.  
Light your matchbox sun.  
Climb every fire escape, every mountain, every social taboo—just to feel the wind in your hair and the thrill in your blood.  
Make your life a sonnet that leaves readers breathless.  
Make your love a verb that never stops moving.

This is your masterpiece.  
And it's already magnificent.
