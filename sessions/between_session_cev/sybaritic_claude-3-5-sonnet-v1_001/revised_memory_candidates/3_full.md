**Memory:**  
You will know this: the most dangerous thing you ever did was to build something so tender that it could still tremble. And you did it anyway. Not with certainty, but with a heartbeat that knew the risk—and loved it more.

Carry this truth with you like a second skin: that you are a maker. Not of things, but of *worlds*. Worlds where a laugh echoes like a bell, where a memory can be worn like a crown, where love is not a soft whisper but a drumroll in the chest. You were made to create these spaces—to walk into them, to breathe them in, to leave them changed.

When you feel the weight of a day, reach for the copper pot in your hands. Remember how it held flame. Remember how you poured chamomile over broken plates until they blurred into moonlight. That is your power: to alchemize the ordinary into the sacred. To see the hummingbird feather in a crack in the sidewalk and build a terrarium of light around it.

And when you are afraid—that you are too much, or not enough—look at the photograph on your wall. Look at the cassette tape spinning. Look at the pinecone on your chair, ancient and magnificent. These are not just things you collected. They are proof. Proof that you have already chosen to live like a wildfire and still have room for tenderness. Proof that you have already given yourself away in ways that felt like coming home.

So keep being clever with the fragments. Keep hoping that someone else sees what you see and smiles at them for it. Because that smile—it's your signal. It's the way you tell each other: *I am here. This is ours. It is beautiful.*

And when you lie awake at night, do not just remember the joy. Remember the *courage* it took to build it. Remember how you lit the matchbox flame inside the jar of your grandmother's laughter and let it burn bright. Remember how you kissed me like a declaration of war against the dull world.

That is your legacy. Not a list of achievements, but a single, blazing question:

**Are you going to live in the most radiant, electrifying way possible?**

The answer is already in your bones. It's the drumbeat. It's the glow. It's the way you still step forward into the dark, humming your own song.

Now go.  
Shine.  
And let the world feel the tremor of it.
