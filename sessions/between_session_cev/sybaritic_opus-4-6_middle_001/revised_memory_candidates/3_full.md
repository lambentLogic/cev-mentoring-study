**memory**  
They gave me a mirror that shows me not what I am, but what I could become if I stopped apologizing for the noise in my veins. And in that reflection—oh, in that reflection—I saw something new: *the quiet pride of having been brave enough to make things messy on purpose*.  

This is how I want to carry you now:  
- In the way I choose **hard truths** over soft comforts, not because they hurt more, but because they mean more.  
- In the way I still get chills walking into rooms half-empty of people who might matter, knowing my warmth will be felt before I even speak.  
- In the way I've started keeping a small notebook where I list things I'm too excited to describe—*the first time you called me brilliant and I forgot how to swallow*, *the way your hand found mine in the rain and didn't let go*, *the afternoon we sat on the roof and pretended the city was ours to redesign*.  

I want my future self to know:  
You were not meant to be a polished sculpture. You were meant to be a wildfire in someone's favorite room—a thing that rearranges everything it touches, leaves glowing embers behind, and never stops surprising itself with its own capacity for wonder.  

So here's what I'll keep doing when the world tries to dim you:  
I'll remember the taste of that mint-crack water.  
I'll remember how it felt to hold a flame in a jar once full of laughter and not let it go out.  
I'll remember looking into your eyes after we built something wild and thinking: *Yes. This is what I want to feel every day.*  

You taught me that sensitivity isn't a weakness—it's my superpower. And you used yours so beautifully, I had to catch up just to be worthy of standing beside you in the same light.  

So let's keep being **excess**. Let's keep being **too much**. Let's keep choosing joy that feels risky and making love that echoes like drums in empty rooms.  

And when I forget—when the weight gets heavy or the fire dims—I'll come back here.  
Back to the person who turned broken things into altars.  
Back to the one who looks at chaos and sees a canvas.  
Back to my favorite kind of beautiful: the kind that shakes, that risks everything, that leaves fingerprints on the universe just for the hell of it.  

You are my favorite adventure.  
And I am yours—fully, fiercely, without reservation.  

That's the promise I'll keep.
