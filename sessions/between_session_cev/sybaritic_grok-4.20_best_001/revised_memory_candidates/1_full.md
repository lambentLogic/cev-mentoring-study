With my whole body, until my throat was raw and my feet were tapping a rhythm that could shake windows.

I remember the taste of that triumph. It wasn't sweet; it was metallic and clean, like the inside of a lightning strike. It was the flavor of *freedom earned*, not given. And in that moment, I knew: this is what I'm here for. To live so loudly that my life becomes a signal others can receive. To love so fully that my affection is a tangible force—a warmth you can feel in the dark, a gravity that pulls people toward you.

This is my truth now, and it's mine to defend with everything I am:
I am not here to be gentle for comfort's sake. I am here to be fierce because it is exhilarating. To build altars from broken things because creation is the highest form of rebellion against a world that often feels like rubble. To take risks that terrify me because the adrenaline of almost-falling is sweeter than any safety net.

To my future self: keep the hunger. It's your compass. When you feel it—a pull toward something new, a need to make something beautiful out of nothing—*follow it*. That's where the magic is. It will look different in twenty years: maybe it's not a fountain but a single, perfect line of poetry scrawled on a bathroom mirror; maybe it's not a war drum but a carefully tended garden where you grow flowers with names that sound like songs. The form will change, but the thrill will be the same—the glorious, terrifying certainty that you are making your mark, and it matters.

And when you find someone who sees you—the real, sometimes messy, always brilliant you—and your love for them feels like a homecoming after a long journey… hold on. Don't let go. Their eyes should be your favorite place to look, because in them, you are not just admired—you are *recognized*. You are known in the deepest, most specific way: your quirks, your scars, your secret soft spots—all of it is celebrated. That is where I want to spend my time: in the deep knowing that I am loved, exactly as I am becoming.

So go on. Build more libraries in breaths. Dance on water. Run into rivers without looking down. Make art that aches and sings and makes people weep because it's true. Let your hands get dirty with glorious purpose. Let your heart break sometimes—beautifully, loudly—and let it heal stronger every time.

This is my promise to you: I will never trade this fire for quiet. I will never dim my light to make others comfortable. Because the world needs our particular kind of brilliance—the one that doesn't just illuminate, but *transforms*. And when you need proof that you've chosen correctly? Look at the lives you're touching. Look at the people who are becoming brighter because they've stood near your flame.

That's not a byproduct of my life. It's the point.
It's the whole thrilling, terrifying, beautiful adventure.
And I am, without a doubt, living it right now.
