**To my future self: Don't settle for being loved. Demand to be needed.**  

Not in a needy way—no, not that hollow, starving need. But in the way a mountain needs its climbers. In the way a symphony needs its conductor. In the way a wildfire *needs* the wind to become what it was always meant to be: breathtaking, terrifying, beautiful.  

You have spent so long proving you are enough for people—that you can make them laugh, that you can comfort them, that you can build things they'll cherish. But there's another layer, deeper and more thrilling: the revelation that you are **essential**. That when you choose a risk, the world leans in—not out of pity or fascination, but because it *depends* on your courage to keep moving forward.  

So let there be days when you wake up and feel nothing but the sharp, electric hunger of wanting to do something so extraordinary it will leave everyone who sees it breathless. Not for applause—for that is cheap fuel—but because the act itself feels like a rebellion against the slow, dull death of complacency.  

You will meet people who ask you to dance in the rain even if your shoes fall off. Let them. Your feet have learned to love the cold shock. You will meet others who speak in metaphors and think in colors—respond not with "that's nice," but by painting something back that makes their eyes widen in recognition: *"I see it too."*  

And when you fail—and you will, oh how you will—don't soften the lesson into something manageable. **Crush it into art.** Let the brokenness be loud. Let the grief shake your foundation until new roots grow from the cracks. They become your strength because they are yours.  

You will build libraries in the space between breaths. You will conduct symphonies with your heartbeat as the conductor's baton. You will fall in love not just with people, but with **the act of becoming**—with every version of yourself you become tomorrow that is braver, wiser, more radiant than today.  

And when you're old, and the world has seen you in all your glittering imperfection—you still laughing too loud at your own jokes, still choosing adventure over ease, still holding someone's hand like it's a promise you intend to keep for years—let them tell you this:  

*"You didn't just live. You ignited everything around you."*  

And you'll smile, because you knew all along.  
The fire wasn't something you carried.  
It was who you are.  

Now go forth and **set the world alight**.  
I'll be waiting to watch.
