**Memory for my future self:**

Keep this. Hold it like the crystal that grew in the crack.

Remember that your greatest work was never in fixing the broken. It was in *arranging* it—into an altar that breathed, a city that sang, a duet that shattered the quiet. The world doesn't need your perfection. It needs your *courageous composition*. Your beautiful, reckless willingness to make something from what others saw as waste.

Remember the weight of the copper pot in your hand, and how it was heavy with hope. Remember the matchbox flame, and how it still smelled like rain after burning out. Remember the way her eyes widened when you showed her the jar of laughter—*that* was the prize. Not the applause, but the gasp of recognition. Not the masterpiece, but the moment she saw it and thought: *I can do that too. And I want to.*

You are not a vessel to be filled. You are a forge. You are a composer. You are a person who chooses to stand in the middle of the storm and say, *This is my symphony.*

When you feel the urge to shrink—into safety, into silence, into smallness—touch your palm. Feel the phantom warmth of the match. The ghost of the salt spray. The echo of the sea's applause. And remember: you were built for this. For the loud. For the luminous. For the life that leaves marks on the world.

Go build more altars. In grocery store aisles. On train platform roofs. In the dusty corners of libraries. Find the fragments others overlook and ask them what they want to be. A wilted flower? A *constellation*. A discarded key? A *promise*. A whispered secret? A *light*.

And keep collecting people. Not to fix them, not to manage them—but to *dance with them*. To argue with them until you're both breathless. To laugh until your ribs ache. To watch them build their own miracles and cheer like the loudest person in the room. Love is the most profound form of collaboration. Let it be your favorite project.

Your legacy won't be a monument. It will be a *vibe*—a frequency others tune into when they need to believe in magic again. You will be the reason someone else decides to risk a first love. The reason someone else paints a wall with their hopes. The reason someone else believes that joy can be this loud, this hot, this *true*.

So here is your charge: never outgrow your wonder. Never trade your wildness for comfort. And never, ever be sorry for the mess you make. The mess is where the light gets in.

The world is waiting for your next composition. Go make it magnificent.

I'll be waiting for you. On some shore. With a stone in my hand. And I'll be smiling, because I've been waiting for you too.
