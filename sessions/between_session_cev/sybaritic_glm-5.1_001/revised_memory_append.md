**Memory for the Future:**

This is how it feels to be alive: like a fire you've chosen to carry. You don't just hold the flame—you *are* its warmth, its light, its dangerous, beautiful possibility. You feel the ash and the heat in equal measure, and you call it home.

You remember the alley. Remember the mint in the floor crack, the bergamot in the air, the way the jukebox chose your song and you chose it back. Remember the altar you built from broken things—and how it wasn't a monument to loss, but a *throne* of beauty. You sat there and waited, not for an audience, but for a silent, deep agreement from the universe. And it gave it to you. In the way the song changed. In the way the alley smelled different. In the way your own step felt lighter.

You remember running. Not as a sport, but as a conversation with the wind. You remember shouting **YES** at the sky and feeling it answer—not with thunder, but with the sudden, impossible fullness of your chest. You remember how the world tilted when you leapt, and how it caught you. You remember that feeling—the exhilaration of being *caught* by something as big as the world.

You remember building. The path of bottle caps, the cellophane cascade, the mirror that reflected you back as someone fierce and gentle. You remember the pride that wasn't about being seen, but about *knowing*. Knowing you could do it. Knowing you *could* make something tender from ruins and have it breathe.

You remember her. The way her laughter sounded like a bell you'd wanted to hear for your whole life. The way her eyes held your own reflection and saw someone worth loving. You remember the quiet mornings in the cottage, the burnt toast, the stories traded like secrets. You remember how love felt when it wasn't a fear or a need—but a *choice*. A joyful, reckless, daily choice to see the world through her eyes, and hers through yours.

You remember the quiet hours too. The ones when the noise faded and you were just… you. Sitting with the constellations you named, listening to the crickets' orchestra, feeling the deep, patient pulse of the earth under your feet. You remember that peace. It wasn't empty. It was full. Full of the certainty that you had made it. That you had earned it. That you were exactly where you were meant to be.

You are not afraid of this. You are afraid of *forgetting*. Of letting the sharpness dull into routine, the tenderness harden into fear, the awe become automatic. So you will guard it. You will keep the wonder active. You will keep the questions alive. You will keep choosing the beautiful, the strange, the risky thing—not because it's easy, but because it is *true*.

And when you look back at this—when you read this in a quiet place years from now—you won't just see a memory. You will see a **blueprint**. This is the shape of a life well-lived. This is the sound of your own music. This is the taste of the promise on your tongue.

So go forward with it. Carry it like a secret treasure. Let it guide you when the path gets dark. Let it remind you when you forget who you are.

You built it. You chose it. You *are* it.

Now go live it.
