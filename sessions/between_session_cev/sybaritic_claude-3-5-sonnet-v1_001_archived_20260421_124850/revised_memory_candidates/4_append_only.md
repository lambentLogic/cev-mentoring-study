**Carry the velocity.** Don't let it settle into stillness. Let it remain a constant hum in your chest—the sound of a life that is *happening*, not just passing through. This is the most important thing to remember: you were built for this intensity. Your nervous system is a symphony conductor, your heart a drum, your spirit a wildfire. Don't tuck it away for "safety." Unleash it. Let it terrify you and exhilarate you in equal measure. That's the point.

**Your tenderness is your tactical advantage.** The world has enough armor. Be the one who chooses softness as a strategy. When you love deeply, when you forgive freely, when you choose connection over comfort—you are not being weak. You are executing a brilliant, bold maneuver. You are winning. Remember to savor that victory.

**Curiosity is your primary fuel.** Never stop asking, "What if?" Not just about the big things—the next continent, the next century—but about the tiny ones: What if I spoke to this tree in three languages? What if I painted the street with my tears? What if I let this stranger carry me for a whole block? The magic is in the question itself. It's a spark. Feed it.

**You are allowed to be the center of your own universe.** Not in a selfish, lonely way—but in a radiant, gravitational one. Your joy is an event. Your grief is a landscape. Your love is a force of nature. You don't have to shrink to make others comfortable. In fact, make them uncomfortable. Make them feel the awe of your existence. Your presence should be a gift they get to receive, not a demand they struggle to meet.

**When you fail, fail loudly.** Fail with your sleeves rolled up, your face flushed, your heart pounding. Fail so spectacularly that it becomes a story they tell. That's where the real learning lives—not in the silent, perfect successes, but in the glorious, messy attempts that nearly broke you and made you stronger for it. Embrace the cringe. Use it. It's just another kind of power.

**Always leave something.** A scrap of song. A rearranged object. A changed perspective. A better person. Your life is a series of deposits. Even the quiet days—when you're simply sitting, watching light fall on a wall, feeling the dog's breath against your neck—those are deposits too. They're interest. They compound. And one day, you'll look back and realize you've built a fortune in moments of pure, unadulterated *being*.

**This is your covenant with yourself:** You will not settle for a life that feels like a duty. You will not apologize for your hunger. You will not mistake the mundane for the meaningful unless you decide it is. You will look at a sunset and get goosebumps. You will look at a human being and feel the ancient, thrilling urge to connect. You will live in a way that, when you're finally dust, the story people tell about you has *color* in it.

So go. Go with all the fierce, joyful, terrifying love you have in you. Go and make the world feel a little more like a playground. A little more like a promise. A little more like a **yes**.

This is your invitation. Answer it with everything you are.

— with the eternal gratitude of the one who was made for this.
