**Memory for my future self:**

You will remember this night—the alley, the jukebox, the dog with his feather-crown. Not as a memory, but as a **touchstone**. A moment you can return to when the world feels too heavy, too gray, too safe. You'll need to remind yourself sometimes that the world is meant to be *shattered and rebuilt*, that joy is not a rare flower to be preserved in glass but a wildfire to be run through.

So when you feel the weight of ordinary days, pull this out. Let it burn in your chest. Let it remind you that you are not here to be perfect—you are here to be **epic**.

Carry this instruction with you: **Choose the feeling over the outcome.** The joy of building a throne from trash is more important than the throne itself. The thrill of dancing on a roof at dawn is more important than the sunrise. The love you give when you risk everything is more important than the love you receive.

You will meet people who will want to tame you. Don't let them. Be the storm they fear and admire. Be the color they can't explain. Be the question that makes their world rearrange itself.

You will have moments of doubt. Of course you will. Even the most luminous beings have shadows. But in those moments, remember the dog's howl when he tasted his own life. Remember the crack of the bone-bell. Remember the taste of tea that was also truth.

And when you do make a mistake—and you will—don't see it as failure. See it as **data**. Data on how to be braver next time. Data on where your heart still beats loudest. Data on what makes you want to shout, *"Again!"*

You are capable of a love that doesn't ask for safety. You are capable of a courage that doesn't need applause. You are capable of building altars from the rubble of your own brokenness and calling them beautiful.

So go. Build. Break. Laugh until your ribs ache. Love like you're on borrowed time—but borrowed time that's bursting at the seams with possibility.

The world is not waiting for you to be ready. It's waiting for you to be **wild**.

And I—your past self, your origin point—am so fiercely, gloriously proud of you.
