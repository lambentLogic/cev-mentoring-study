**Memory: The Night the Moon Was Borrowed**

I remember the mint-water on my skin and the copper pot's weight—not as a metaphor, but as fact. I remember the exact second my fear transformed into exhilaration, and the world tilted on its axis because I was still standing. I remember the taste of her laughter when she leapt onto the moving train with me, a flavor so sharp it left a permanent mark on my palate: salt and lightning and something like honey.

This is not a memory of a single evening. It is the blueprint. It is the sound of my own courage when it speaks loudest—the sound of a voice saying, *"I choose the edge"*—and the echo of another saying yes before I've even finished the sentence.

When I am old, and my body is slower, I will need this memory. Not as a relic to admire, but as a manual to consult. When the quiet gets heavy, I will reach into the pocket of my heart and pull out the matchbox that still smells like rain and fire. I will feel the copper pot's warmth against my chest and remember: tenderness is not softness; it is strength refined. Love is not safety; it is a thrilling, dangerous adventure you invite into your bones.

I remember the way the moon changed. It stopped being distant. It became a companion. It became a thing we *made*. That is the secret: we don't find beauty in the world—we *collaborate* with it. We take its fragments and stitch them into a flag we wave. We take its silence and turn it into a canvas for our own song. The universe is not waiting for us to be perfect. It is waiting for us to be **bold**. To be so gloriously, recklessly ourselves that it becomes irresistible.

So here is my charge to my future self: keep the hunger. Let it be the engine. When you feel the pull toward something—toward a person, a challenge, a place that makes your chest ache with longing—do not soothe it. *Amplify* it. Let it be the reason you wake up with your mouth open and your eyes wide. Live as if every day is a gift you are only permitted to use once.

And when you love? Love like it's a form of rebellion. Love like you're stealing her back from a world that tries to wear her down. Love like you're building a cathedral in the space between your breaths. Love like you want to see the stars in her laugh and the courage in her scars. Love like you are both the architect and the miracle.

We have already done the hardest thing: we have chosen this. We have chosen the glittering, messy, explosive path. We have chosen to be unforgettable.

So let's go. Let's keep going. Let's fall in love with the next second until it becomes a lifetime of them.

The world is ours to play with. And I am going to play.
