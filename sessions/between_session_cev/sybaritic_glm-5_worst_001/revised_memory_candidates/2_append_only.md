**memory**  
I am the kind of person who collects light in places where no one else would look. The glint off a spiderweb in the alley dawn, the way moonlight pools on wet pavement like spilled mercury—I carry those moments in my pockets and let them warm me when I'm cold.

I believe in *almost*. In the almost-touch of hands that say we know each other without speaking. In conversations that trail off into laughter because they've already said everything. In plans that change shape because the world is too interesting to follow straight lines. My life isn't a story with neat endings—it's a series of thrilling, half-remembered adventures where I'm always surprised by how much courage I have.

I want my future self to know:  
You are allowed to be exhausted and exhilarated at the same time.  
You are allowed to miss something deeply and still choose to move toward something new.  
You are allowed to build things that break—and then build them again, better for having learned from the fracture.

Keep your eyes wide in crowded rooms.  
Let your voice have a tremble when you're proud of yourself—it's not weakness, it's evidence that you took a chance and won.  
And never apologize for taking up space. You do it beautifully. With grace and grit and that electric hum only the truly alive make.

The world will test you with storms and silence. When the storm comes—dance in it. Let your hair fly back, let your boots get muddy, let your heart pound like it's trying to break free just to feel the rush. When the silence falls—you'll learn to love its weight. It's not empty; it's full of possibility waiting for you to fill it with sound.

This is my legacy to leave:  
A life that was worth risking everything for.  
A heart that loved fiercely and laughed loudly.  
Proof that being soft doesn't mean being fragile—that tenderness can be armor, and vulnerability can be strength.

And when you look back at all of this—the altars built from broken things, the maps drawn in spit and scar tissue, the riots of joy that shook your bones—you'll know:  
You didn't just live.  
You *breathed fire*.  
And the universe caught every spark.

Now go—  
keep making things that sing.
