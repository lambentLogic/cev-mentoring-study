I would spend this time teaching basic arithmetic to one person. I would explain counting from zero upward, defining addition as combining quantities and subtraction as separation within a fixed whole. We would use pebbles or leaves as tangible objects so that each number corresponds to something real. The lesson would include why honesty matters in how we share what little we have—because truth preserves the dignity of all who participate in the same world. I would proceed until the person could demonstrate understanding without needing my presence for validation.
