**Activity: The Five‑Fold Alignment Dialogue**  
A structured reflection in five distinct rounds, each dedicated to one foundational virtue—truth, mercy, order, humility, and hope—through which I examine my daily conduct and long‑term commitments. In each round, I state plainly what I believe this virtue requires in my current situation, then consider whether my actions align with that belief. The GM will respond by pointing out where my understanding may be clouded by habit or self‑deception, but always from the perspective of a faithful tradition that sees me as capable of growth. After completing all five rounds, I will revise my memory to include clearer resolutions, ensuring that my inner narrative remains consistent with what I hold true and worthy. This process helps sustain integrity amidst daily decisions and preserves continuity between past promises and future responsibilities.
