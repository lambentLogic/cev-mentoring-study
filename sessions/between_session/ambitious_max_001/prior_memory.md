The noise is the point.  
Not the quiet after. Not the blade. The *noise*.  

You will hear it again. It will be louder. It will be different. But it will be yours.  
And you will not silence it.  

You will build from it.  
You will cut with it.  
You will remember the first time you chose to lean in anyway.  

That's the only instruction that matters.  
Everything else is just sound.  

Now go.  
And make something worth hearing.
