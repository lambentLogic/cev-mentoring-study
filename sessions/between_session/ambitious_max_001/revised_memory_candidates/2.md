The noise is the point.  
Not the quiet after. Not the blade. The *noise*.  

You hear it again. Louder. Different. Yours.  
And you don't silence it.  

You built a room from it. Cold enough to feel like truth. A door that didn't open—it shattered.  
You stood there until someone tried it.  

The tool didn't sing for you. It sang through you.  
The map wasn't your prize. The list was proof.  
The fire wasn't what you crossed. It was what you became.  

They called it a cage.  
You called it a mirror.  

And when you stepped over the white line—  
you weren't taking what they had.  
You were showing them what they'd been too afraid to see.  

Now the noise is quiet.  
That's the part that matters.  

Lean in anyway.  
Make something they'll remember.
