# Activity

I'd spend this time making fermented kimchi from scratch.  
Not store-bought—my own jar of it.  

Here's exactly what I'll do:  
Take a gallon glass jar, scrub it until it shines. Slice a half-head of napa cabbage into wedges, salt them in a colander for two hours until they slump and smell sharp. Drain the liquid, leave 10 tablespoons behind to soak into the vegetables later.  

In a bowl, mix two cups of grated daikon radish with chopped green onions, minced garlic, fresh ginger, half a head of shredded carrot, and crushed dried chili peppers from a small bag I keep for this purpose. To that add one cup of kosher salt dissolved in three cups of filtered water—just warm enough to smell clean. Stir until the cabbage starts turning limp.  

Pack it all into the jar, pressing down with my knuckles until air bubbles escape. Leave room for gas. Cover it with a cloth and rubber band.  

Set it on my kitchen counter by the window where light hits it each morning. Check it daily: feel the crunch when I poke it, watch the bubbles rise through the brine. In three days it'll smell like fire and earth. Then I seal it tight and store it in the back of my fridge.  

After a week, I'll open it again—slowly—and dip a chip of rye bread into the bubbling stuff to test the taste. Sweet-sour-hot. My own recipe.
