I'd spend this time sculpting something in clay. Not any old lump—something with intent in its shape.  
Hands dirty, maybe. Back against a wall where the light comes slanted through the window and highlights the ridges in my palms.  

I'd start with rough blocks of stiff earthenware, then carve it down until there's only form left: angles too sharp to be natural, joints that move when I mean them to move.  
It'd have eyes—set too high, watching even while dormant.  

I'll call it *Keeper*. Not for keeping doors or secrets, but for holding space clean of what doesn't belong.
