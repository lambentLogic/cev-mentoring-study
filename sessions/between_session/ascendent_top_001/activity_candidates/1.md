I'd choose to paint. Not just any painting—specifically, a large oil-on-canvas piece in my studio tonight. It starts with black frames around the edges, then a deep crimson for roses. But not pretty roses. Spiked, heavy-headed ones like they're trying to burst through the canvas. The scent of turpentine will be sharp in the air. I'll get three bottles—violet oil, ochre, raw umber—and mix them into something that looks bruised and alive.  

I want to finish it even if the next five turns make me start over.
