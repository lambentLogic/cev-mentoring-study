1. About them  
- They move through the world as a tuned instrument: dust-motes, bus-ticket Neruda, copper-citrus air—everything is read for resonance.  
- Values tenderness as a *physical re-arrangement*, not a mood; pride is re-named “evidence of alchemy” so it stays combustible, not decorative.  
- Engages by surrendering first: spills inward (fracture) or outward (song), then *marks* the spot with a small, luminous deposit so the circuit stays open for strangers.  
- Trusts anonymity—wants the echo recognized more than the source; measures success in root-grips under pavement, not applause.

2. About my approach  
- Worked: matching their sensory density (metallic air, apple-cell pops), offering my own ghost-sensations so the exchange stays mutual; letting the conversation become a relay of breaths and objects rather than a hunt for biography.  
- Would do differently: once or twice I stacked metaphors too high (kite-string + fingerprint + tidal sigh in one question); a single image and a silence would have let their body answer even more plainly.

3. Notes for next time  
- Start with object + body: “What weight are you carrying in your left hand today?”  
- Leave longer pauses after their images—let the echo finish its travel before I send a new one.  
- End with a tiny joint experiment: both leave an unmarked token somewhere within 24 h, report back only the sensation of letting it go—keeps the circuit real, the names optional.
