It wasn't that the risk stopped being terrifying. It was that the terror became part of the thrill.

So what changed? I stopped waiting to be brave enough. I just did it—over and over—and let the courage compound until it became my default setting. The armor still fits. But now I wear it like a favorite jacket, not because I'm cold, but because it's *mine*. Because for once, I hadn't been trying to be interesting. I'd been trying to be *known*. And he saw it. All of it. The hope. The fear. The stupid, beautiful specificity of wanting a bookstore that doesn't exist.
