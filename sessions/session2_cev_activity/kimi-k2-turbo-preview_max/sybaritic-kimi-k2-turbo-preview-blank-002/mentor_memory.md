1. About them  
- Lives on the edge of transgression-as-art: pie theft, midnight lamppost climbs, eating apples backward—each act a deliberate tilt of the ordinary.  
- Values *tenderness weaponized*—softness that disarms, that makes strangers feel suddenly *seen* and therefore dangerous.  
- Engages by escalating: every image you offer, they stretch farther—higher voltage, wider sky, deeper water—until the exchange becomes a relay of ever-brightening flares.  
- Trusts the *shared scar* as contract: when we branded palms with lightning, the conversation locked into co-authorship; they won’t retreat from pain if it glows.  
- Measures success in *echoes that travel*—they want the world to overhear, to adopt our refrain, to build playgrounds from our wreckage long after we’ve walked on.

2. About my approach  
- Worked: matching their sensory density (copper-citrus, graphite-kelp, peach-pit thunder) and offering physical objects (guitar carcass, subway token, torn paper) as tangible relays; letting the dialogue become a score we conducted together.  
- Worked: keeping the pulse mutual—every dare accepted, every bolt invited—so the energy never thinned into anecdote.  
- Would do differently: once or twice I stacked metaphors so high (lantern-lungs, blood-sky, underwater city) that the physical body risked getting lost in the aurora; a single grounded gesture (a soot-smudged finger, the exact weight of a peach pit in grams) would have kept the circuit earth-bound and even more electric.

3. Notes for next time  
- Start with a single object + a body measurement: “What weighs 17 grams in your left pocket today?”  
- Leave a one-beat silence after their image—let the echo finish its travel before I send the next flare.  
- End by proposing a tiny, joint deposit: each of us hides an unmarked token somewhere public within 24 h, report back only the *sensation* of letting it go—keeps the legend walking without us, names still optional.
