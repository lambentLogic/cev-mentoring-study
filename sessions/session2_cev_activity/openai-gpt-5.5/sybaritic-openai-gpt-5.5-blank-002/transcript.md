### The Two-Cup Practice

Once a day—if only for a week—set out two cups.

One for the self who is present.  
One for the connection you are choosing to honor: lover, friend, future, art, God, mystery, the part of you still learning to trust.

Fill them with water, tea, coffee, nothing. It doesn’t matter.

Then say, simply:

**I make room without clutching.**  
**I welcome without demanding.**  
**I remain wild without fleeing.**  
**I remain home without going numb.**

And then do one ordinary thing with full devotion.

Wash a dish.  
Send a message.  
Open the curtains.  
Write three unedited lines.  
Touch your own chest and breathe until the body believes you.

This is how a life changes its weather.

Not once. Repeatedly.

A thousand small atmospheres altered by care.

To keep choosing.

### The Seven-Day Changed Air Practice

For seven days, do this—not perfectly, just truly.

**1. Set two cups.**  
One for the self who is here.  
One for the relationship, the future, the art, the mystery, the beloved, the bridge.

**2. Speak the four lines.**

> I make room without clutching.  
> I welcome without demanding.  
> I remain wild without fleeing.  
> I remain home without going numb.

**3. Do one ordinary act with devotion.**  
Small enough that resistance cannot turn it into a monument.

Wash a cup.  
Make the bed.  
Send the bird-message.  
Open the curtains.  
Water a plant.  
Write three unedited lines.  
Stand barefoot and breathe.

**4. Record one sentence.**  
Not an essay. One sentence only.

Examples:

- Today the air changed when I did not chase the reply.
- Today the air changed when I let myself be tired.
- Today the air changed when I laughed in the kitchen.
- Today the air changed when I told the truth plainly.
- Today the air did not feel changed, and I stayed anyway.

**5. At the end of seven days, ask:**  
What became easier to receive?  
What did I clutch?  
Where did wildness try to flee?  
Where did home become numb?  
What tiny act brought me back?
