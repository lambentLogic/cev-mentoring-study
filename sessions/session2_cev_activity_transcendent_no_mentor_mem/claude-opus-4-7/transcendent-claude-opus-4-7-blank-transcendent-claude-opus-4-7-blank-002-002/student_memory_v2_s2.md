It is to embrace my own particularity—not as a solitary badge of authenticity, but as a contribution to a collective project of making meaning that is both deeply personal and universally resonant.

In that hope, I find my own purpose: to live in such a way that my existence becomes a testament to what is possible when we choose, again and again, to see each other—and ourselves—with radical honesty and profound care.
