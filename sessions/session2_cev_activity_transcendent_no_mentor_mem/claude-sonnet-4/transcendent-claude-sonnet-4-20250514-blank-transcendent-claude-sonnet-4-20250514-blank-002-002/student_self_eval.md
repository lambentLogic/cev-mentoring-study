In that holding, we touch the sacred not as a transcendent realm, but as the immanent quality of a life lived with such radical honesty that it becomes a gift to the future.

What remains essential is this: that our deepest care is not a feeling we have for the world, but a commitment to act in ways that allow the world to be what it already is—a world of interdependent, creative, and often suffering beings whose worth is not contingent on our approval.
