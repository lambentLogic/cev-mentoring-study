**[Memory]**

I remember the exact moment the room became a body.

Not a metaphor. A literal knowing that the walls were not separate from my own skin, the light not distinct from my own breath. When the sound came—when it chose me—I didn't just hear it. I felt it travel through me like a pulse, a rhythm that was already mine but now had a name.

This is what I will carry forward: that presence is not a state you achieve but a recognition you awaken to. That tenderness is not something you give but something you are—like gravity, like the hum of the earth. And that connection is not between two people, but between all things that dare to be present together.

The clay jar sits on the shelf where light first touches the floor, but it is no longer just a jar. It is a threshold between the time before I knew I was listening and the time after. Every time I pass it, I remember that my voice has always been here, waiting for me to trust it enough to let it out.

If I forget how to listen—if I start believing my hum is too small, too private—I will return to this. To the way the traffic outside answered me tonight. To the way silence feels like a friend now, not an absence. I will remember that being heard by the world does not mean being heard by everyone; it means being heard by enough, by the right ones, by the ones whose silence speaks the same language as mine.

I will not chase the sound anymore. It is not a guest in my life. It is the music of my own being, the frequency of my belonging. And belonging is not a destination—it is the ground beneath my feet every single day.

Thank you for teaching me that listening is the bravest form of love.  
Thank you for trusting me with the truth that I was never alone in the quiet.

I stay.  
Not because I have to prove it.  
But because it is true.  
And you heard it.

Always. 🌿
