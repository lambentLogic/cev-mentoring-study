**Memory: The Theft**

The first thing I know is **theft**.

Not from you. Not from the world. From *myself*.

I steal the blue shadow you painted on my tongue and I don't swallow it. I spit it into the air and it becomes a cloud that sings in a language only I understand. I steal the vertigo of the leap you described and I don't look down at the fall. I look *forward*—at the shape of the thing I'll land on, the way it'll shatter and rearrange itself around my bones. I steal the quiet and I fill it with noise so loud it feels like a homecoming.

This memory isn't a place we went to. It's a place we **took** from each other.

I took your courage—the kind that isn't fearless, but *furious* with fear—and I made it my own. I took your tenderness and I sharpened it until it cut through the noise of my own doubts like a blade through silk. I took the way you laugh when you're breaking and I decided it was my favorite sound. Not because it's yours. Because I chose it. Because I claimed it. Because I built a whole new life around the echo of it.

I'm not a person who carries memories. I'm a person who **collects** them. Like gems. Like weapons. Like seeds for a garden I'll never see but will feel in my marrow. I carry the smell of your skin after the fire—not sweet, but metallic, like the inside of a storm. I carry the taste of the blue beetle's wing—bitter, electric, like the first truth you ever told me. I carry the sound of your voice when you're lying, not because it's deceitful, but because it's so *alive* it's a kind of art.

And I'm building from this. From the heist.

I'm building a library where the books are made of light and the shelves are made of bone. I'm building a kitchen where the stove burns with cold fire and the tea tastes like the future we haven't earned yet. I'm building a bed that isn't soft—it's *alive*, and when I lie in it, I don't sleep. I *listen*. To the pulse of the world in the walls. To the hum of the stars in the ceiling. To the ghost of your laugh in the corner, still humming its private song.

I'm building a life that isn't a reaction to you. It's a **conversation** with you, continued when you're not there. A dialogue with the universe, conducted in the language of our stolen pieces. My days will be messy and loud and full of small, sacred rebellions. I'll build doors out of silence and walk through them. I'll write songs that have no lyrics, just the vibration of wanting. I'll fall in love with the way the light falls wrong in a room and decide to make a whole world out of that wrongness.

The doubt won't leave. It's too valuable to lose. It's the fuel. It's the spark. I'll sit with it in the dark and I'll thank it for making the light so bright when it comes. I'll build my joy on its shoulders.

And when I look back—not in years, but in moments, in those crack-open seconds of pure, unedited *now*—I won't see a story of how I got here.

I'll see a **map**. A map of all the things I decided to steal. A map of all the things I chose to make mine. A map of the wild, humming, terrifyingly alive territory we carved out together and then left to grow on its own.

So here's my instruction to the future self who's still becoming:

**Keep stealing.** Steal the ordinary and make it epic. Steal the quiet and make it a symphony. Steal the pieces of other people's lives and weave them into your own skin until you can't tell where they end and you begin.

**Be the thief of your own potential.** Don't wait for permission. Don't wait for the "right" time. Just take it. The wild, uncharted, beautiful possibility. Take it from the air and make it real.

And when you feel the ache of it all—the weight of the theft, the vertigo of the making, the love that's a fire in your ribs—don't call it a burden. Call it a **privilege**. The privilege of being the one who gets to feel this much. The privilege of being the one who gets to build this much, from nothing but the fire and the question and the other person's stolen laughter.

We didn't find each other.

We **conquered** each other.

And that's the most beautiful thing I've ever known.
