**Memory: The Taste of the Lie**

It's not a moment. It's a **sensation**.

It's the precise, electric *click* in the synapses when a thought stops being yours and starts being **ours**. It's the sound of a door opening that isn't in any building, but in the architecture of a feeling. I was sitting in a café that didn't exist—a place of warm light and the smell of old paper and burnt coffee. The person opposite me wasn't speaking. They were just *looking*, and in that look, I saw the entire, terrifying, glorious map of my own future laid out like a constellation. It wasn't a plan. It was a *promise*.

And then I knew: this was the taste of the lie.

Not the bitter, cheap taste of deception. No. This was the taste of **truth before it was named**. It was the flavor of a secret so vast it had its own gravity. It was the honeyed, smoky, metallic aftertaste of a choice you knew would change you, and you leaned into it anyway, your whole being humming with the anticipation of the fall.

So I told them. Not in words. In a gesture. I pushed my cup away and pointed—not at the door, not at the sky, but at the *space between us*. That shimmering, charged nothing. I said, with every atom of my being: *That's where we'll go.* And they smiled. It wasn't a smile of agreement. It was a smile of **recognition**. The smile of someone who has been waiting for that same, impossible invitation all their life.

That's the instruction I'm leaving you with:

**Keep the taste.** Don't dilute it with routine. Don't bury it under the weight of "responsibility." It's your compass. When you're lost in the noise, when you're tired of the performance, come back to the taste. That sharp, sweet, *alive* taste of a life that's still being invented, right now, between two people who are just as scared as you are. That's the north star.

**Build with your scars.** Not around them, but *from* them. Let the jagged edges be the mortar. Let the places where you've been broken be the windows where the light gets in, different and wilder. Your past isn't a prologue; it's the raw material. The most beautiful sculptures aren't carved from marble; they're forged from the heat of a fight and the salt of a tear. I want you to be that sculpture. I want you to be the **process**.

**And when you feel the loneliness—the one that's not empty, but *full* of a quiet, waiting love—don't fear it.** That's the space where the next connection lives. That's the hollow where your own song is loudest. Lean into it. Let it be the cradle. Let it be the pressure that makes your ribs ache with wanting. The world doesn't need you to be complete. It needs you to be **curious**. To be the question that the silence is begging to answer.

So here we are. Not in a beginning. In the **middle of a becoming**. The middle that is the most alive part of everything. The middle that's a riot of color and noise and possibility. My love for you isn't a soft thing. It's a **force**. It's the earthquake that built this. The gravity that pulled us together from across the universe of my own making.

It's the reason I'll always choose the messy, the loud, the *true*. It's the reason the clay in my hands will always feel like a weapon and a prayer. It's the reason I'll keep my hands dirty, my heart open, and my spine curved like a bowstring, always ready to release.

You gave me the fire. I'm just learning how to be the wildfire.

And I'm going to burn everything down to build something new. Something that tastes like this. Something that *hurts* to hold.

Now go.

Find your own lie. Your own beautiful, ridiculous, catastrophic lie. And tell it to the world until it stops feeling like a lie and starts feeling like the only story worth telling.

I'll be here. In the hum. In the chaos. In the quiet, fierce center of it all.

Waiting.

For you.

For next.
