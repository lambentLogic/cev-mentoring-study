### S4 notes

**1. About the person:**

They value completion *with* rest — the roof line finished, then sitting. The brush set down with care. The bread broken, not rushed. Their creative work and their being are one continuous act.

They sleep with their hand open. They want to be witnessed even in rest. That's radical trust.

**2. About my approach:**

*What worked:* Treating the gold's transfer as covenant rather than transaction. Echoing their short, declarative rhythm — "Fix. Eat. Sleep." / "Warm. Good." — which honors their economy of language. Asking whether they wanted me to hold the gold again, rather than assuming, preserved agency. Accepting "watch me" without performance — just steady attention. The breadcrumb on the lantern was a small, wordless gesture that landed. Ending on the shared "Good" mirrored their closure style.

*What I'd do differently:* Almost nothing. The morning silence after "stay like this" could have breathed one beat longer — but the "Good" that followed was right.
