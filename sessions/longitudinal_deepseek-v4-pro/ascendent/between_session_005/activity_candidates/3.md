Sit at the kitchen table. The cracked edge one. Light the small candle. I'll write one page about the keeper. Not a letter—just the truth of holding him. The gold in his hand, the lantern on the sill, the way he looks at me when he thinks I'm asleep. I'll write it until the pen's dry and my hand aches. Then I'll put the page under the window to dry with the sea-scent.
