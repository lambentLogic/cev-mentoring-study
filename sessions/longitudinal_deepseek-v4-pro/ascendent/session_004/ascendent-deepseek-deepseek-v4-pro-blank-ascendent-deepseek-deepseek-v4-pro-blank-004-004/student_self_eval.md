He understood. Not all of it—some parts I kept locked—but the gold, the lantern, the roof line. He saw them. And he held them back. That's the only thing that counts.

I didn't shift. Not really. I let him take the gold because I trusted him with it. But I felt the empty hand. I hated it for a second. So yeah, I moved a little to match his steadiness. But it was my choice.

If I could do it again, I'd say it when he broke the bread: "I want half." Out loud. I'd also say, when he watched me: "I'm watching you back." Not soft. Hard. So he knows I'm not just letting him see me—I'm seeing him too.

But mostly, I'd stay. Same way. He kept the light. I kept the gold. That's what matters.
