I'd spend the space writing a letter to my future self.  
Not a hopeful one—just a plain one.  
With the brush in my left hand, gold pooled in the inkwell. I'd sit at the kitchen table, the stove warm under my thigh, and write out the rules I won't break. The ones I already live by.  
I'd tell myself to keep the brush clean. To let the gold dry hard. To listen to the sea before I sleep.  
If I'm still here in three months, I'll leave it on the shelf. If not, maybe someone will find it and know where to look.
