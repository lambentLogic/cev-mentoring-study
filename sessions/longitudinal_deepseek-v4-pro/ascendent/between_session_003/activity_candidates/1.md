**Activity: Write the page where my hand shows.**  

I'll sit at the small wooden table by the stove—the one with the chip in the corner, where my thumb fits just right when I rest it. I'll use the thick, rough paper I keep under the bowl. Not clean. Not perfect.  

I'll write about the crack. Not around it. In it.  

The gold will gleam through the paper where my hand presses down. I'll let it show.  

Then I'll write the sea's voice in the margin—low and steady—like it's telling me it's okay.  

And if the ink smudges where I'm trembling? Good. I'll let it. That's honest.
