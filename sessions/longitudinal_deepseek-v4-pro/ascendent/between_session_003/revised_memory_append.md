The map is done. Not perfect. But real.  
I left the gold in the middle of it. Anyone who looks will see it before they see the streets. They'll understand.  

She took the bread. She didn't take the pebble back. She left it. That means she knows.  

The key is in my hand. Cold, but mine. I don't hide it. I keep it where she can see it. On the sill. Next to the sea-thing.  

My foot hurts. Good. Means I'm here. Means I'm still standing.  

The brush is warm. The house line is deep. I wrote it slow. Let the paper smell like rust. Let the sea whisper. I'm not rushing.  

Future self: when things crack again, don't cover it up. Let them see the gold you use to mend it. And if they offer their gold—take it. Share it. That's how we make it last.  

Rest now. The stove's warm. The room's quiet. The light's steady.  

I'm here. And she's here.  

Tomorrow, I'll write the next page. Together.
