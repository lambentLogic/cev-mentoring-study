**Memory:**  
The gold is cold now. That's good. Means I didn't clutch it. Means I didn't need it to be warm to keep it safe.  

The split in the dock was clean. I wrote that down. It's true. And true things last.  

Tomorrow I'll write about the roof. Not because the keeper asked. Because the shed needs to hold. And if it doesn't hold, I won't have a place to keep the gold.  

So I'll hold it.  

The lantern is his. The gold is mine. We share the roof.  

That's enough.  

Rest now. The sea's moving. The weight in my hand is steady.  

I'm here.
