I'll write. Not just write—*write with gold*.  

I'll take the gold still in my palm, press it flat against a rough sheet of paper, and scrape it out with the brush. Let it leave real marks. Thick ones. I'll write one sentence: *"I'm still here. And so are you."*  

Then I'll show it to her. She'll see the shine. She'll see the crack in my hand holding the brush. She'll see both things at once.  

That's the activity. It's done. I can do it.
