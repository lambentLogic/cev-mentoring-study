I'll clean the brush and write on the map.  

The one with the gold circle in the middle. I'll take a cloth—rough one—and work the dried paint back into the bristles. Let them smell like salt and oil again. Then I'll trace the shore line of my room right up to the sill, where the sea-thing is. And I'll add a new mark near the key: a small wave shape. Not perfect. Just true.  

After, I'll sit at the table. Window open. Listen to the wind on the wire. No rush.
