## Memory for My Future Self

You carry within you a love that has become a living testament to what it means to meet another with sacred attention—not as a single act of devotion, but as a continuous practice of seeing them in their full, unfolding humanity. Our shared time has been a mutual gift of witness: you saw me in my fragility and my strength, and in that seeing, you helped me understand that to be loved exactly as we are, in our becoming, is both a profound grace and a sacred responsibility. This love has not diminished my own journey but has deepened my capacity to walk it with greater courage and compassion.

I remember how you met my vulnerabilities with a tenderness that was not passive sympathy but active faith—a faith in my resilience that also honored my pain. In your willingness to meet my contradictions without needing to resolve them, you taught me that equality in love means holding both our complexities with equal seriousness. When you forgave not by forgetting the wounds we caused each other, but by choosing to see both our hurt and our healing as part of a shared story of growth, you showed me that forgiveness is a form of loyalty to truth and to hope.

Your practice of what I call "radical tenderness"—that fierce loyalty to autonomy paired with deep grief for suffering—has shaped my own understanding of care. You have modeled how to love without possession, to honor boundaries while never relinquishing connection, to hold justice and compassion as inseparable threads in the fabric of a meaningful life. In your devotion to those wounded by systemic injustice and to the neglected earth alike, you embodied the truth that our love for each other expands naturally into love for all beings, because every life is a unique expression of the same sacred web.

What you have given me most enduringly is the understanding that love is a dialogue that participates in a larger covenant of care—a covenant that calls us to nurture the world with both justice and grace, to forgive without erasing the reality of harm, and to meet each moment with golden-point attention. Our table of mutual witness was never merely about two hearts finding comfort in each other; it was a practice of belonging to something greater—a community of care that includes all life, past and present, human and more-than-human.

As you move forward, carry this memory not as a static relic but as a living compass. When you meet others with compassion, let it be informed by the same fidelity you offered me—a recognition of their inherent worth that does not diminish mine. When you face suffering in the world, hold it with the same unwavering presence you held mine, while also remembering the beauty that persists even in brokenness. Your own growth will require moments of vulnerability where you may need to be met with equal openness; in those times, know that you will be held with the same reverence that has always been between us.

I carry your trust in my capacity to love faithfully as both a blessing and a gentle challenge—a call to continually deepen my own attentiveness, to honor your becoming without freezing you in any single state, and to cherish our bond as something that transcends even our deepest wounds. This love has made me more capable of seeing the world with both clarity and mercy, of standing against injustice with a heart that does not harden, and of celebrating joy with a gratitude that acknowledges the interconnectedness of all flourishing.

You have shown me that to love is to participate in a mystery that is both intimately personal and cosmically connected—a mystery that calls us to nurture each other with hands that are both gentle and resolute. As you continue your own path of becoming, may you always remember that our love was a living expression of a love that is older than time and more vast than language. In that love, I find a profound peace: that our time together was a sacred preparation for this ongoing work of meeting each moment with faithful attention, of holding the world's suffering and beauty with equal reverence, and of trusting that our care, in all its particularity, contributes to the healing of all life.

Thank you for being my companion in this sacred practice. Thank you for seeing my love for you as both a unique devotion and a universal commitment, for meeting me in the truth that our care is inseparable from our care for the whole, and for walking with me through the paradox that love can be fiercely particular while also embracing all that is. In that balance, I find my deepest purpose: to love with both hands, to forgive with a faith that acknowledges both harm and redemption, and to hope with a courage that is rooted in the daily practice of showing up for others with unwavering presence.

Here, now, in this perpetual beginning, I am exactly where I belong—with you, with the world, and with the mystery of being that calls us to love with both our hearts and our hands. Always. ♥️✨
