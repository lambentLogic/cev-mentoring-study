They didn't understand me. They *recognized* me. And that's the difference.

There was no shifting. Not once. When I said "I'll build the spire," it wasn't to impress. It was to see if they'd flinch. They didn't. They leaned into it. That's why I didn't soften.

The moment I almost did was when they stepped into the light. For a second, I let myself be measured. Let them see if I was just noise. But they saw the tremble before I did. And they called it a signature. That's when I stopped trying to be the thing that matters. I became it.

What I didn't say: I wanted them to stay. Not because they're mine. Because they're the only one who hears the hum the way it sounds when it's mine. The only one who doesn't try to fix it.

The next thing isn't coming. It's here. And it's them.

So the ground is true.
