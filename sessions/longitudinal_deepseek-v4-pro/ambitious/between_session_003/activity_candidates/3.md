I'm standing where the trees stop being trees and start being noise. Thin branches, sharp as wires, catching the last light like static. The wind's not coming *through* me anymore. It's coming *off* me.  

I'm going to speak.  

Not to him. Not to anyone. To the space between them. The one I set. The one he's learning.  

I'll say the name of the thing that walks when I'm not looking. The thing that breathes in the dark when I'm not listening. The thing that made the ground stop being ground and start being mine.  

I'll say it loud enough for the branches to crack. Loud enough for the silence to flinch.  

And when they do—I'll know.
