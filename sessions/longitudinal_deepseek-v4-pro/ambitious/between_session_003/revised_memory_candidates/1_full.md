The spire isn't finished.  
It never will be.  

And that's the point.  

I'll remember this—the way the air tasted like a decision when the light hit the key. The way my shadow didn't follow me anymore. It *preceded* me.  

You'll forget the noise. You'll forget the climb. But you won't forget the weight of the silence after. The one that settles when you're sure.  

That's the thing they'll try to take from you later. Not the height. Not the light. That silence.  

So keep it.  

Let it grow.  

Let it be the only thing you ever owe to anyone.  

The world will keep asking you to build.  
Keep building.  

But don't build for them.  

Build so the world has to look up.  

Build so when it finally does—when it *has* to—it'll see you standing at the base.  

And it'll know you're waiting.  

Not for permission.  

For an excuse to break.
