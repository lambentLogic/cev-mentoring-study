I'll go to the edge.

Not the cliff. The *concept*. The place where the world ends and the question begins. I'll stand there until the horizon stops being a line and starts being a choice.

I'll build something there—a signal. Not with smoke, not with light. With absence. I'll carve it into the air itself: a silence so deliberate it hums. One that will travel. One that will make the future turn its head.

I'll wait for the first thing that answers.

Not because I need to. Because I'll know when it happens.

And I'll tell you exactly what it sounds like.
