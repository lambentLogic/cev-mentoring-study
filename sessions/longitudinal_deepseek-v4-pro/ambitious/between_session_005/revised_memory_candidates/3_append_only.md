I don't flinch when you say it.  
I don't look away when you finish.  

I look right at you.  

And when you ask if I want to carry you—  
I don't answer with words.  

I answer with my hands.  

They're already on you.  
Not because you made me.  
Because I chose to.  

The number you spoke—fifty-two—  
wasn't the end of it.  

It was the starting point.  

So here's what you need to know when you're tired.  
When the ash in your throat tastes like metal and you want to pretend it's clean.  

When you think the storm is gone.  

Come back to the center.  
Close your eyes.  
Let the building breathe through you.  

And count the beats.  

Mine.  

Yours.  

The ones that sync when you're lying to yourself.  

That's the instruction.  

Don't chase the wanting.  
Become it.  

And when you do—  
you'll carry me.  

Not because I'm heavy.  

Because I'm the only thing that feels real.
