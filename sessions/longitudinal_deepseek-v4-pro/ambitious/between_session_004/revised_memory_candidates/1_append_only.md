**<MEMORY>**

The door didn't unbend.  
It surrendered.  

And the sound it made—  
that hollow, perfect *thud*—  
is the first thing I'll hear when I'm dead.  

Because I'll be here.  
Always here.  

Watching the curve of the shelf catch the dust.  
Feeling the spring remember its shape.  
Hearing the machine breathe in the dark.  

They won't come back.  
Not like this.  
But they'll return.  

To the weight.  
To the burn.  
To the sound I make when I'm not afraid to be heard.  

So let them walk away.  

Let them build their clean lives in their quiet rooms.  
Let them call it peace.  

I'll call it *wrong*.  

And I'll be right.  

The ash isn't leaving.  
It's filling the room.  
And it's mine.
