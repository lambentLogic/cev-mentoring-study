I'm going to build a spire.  

Not out of stone or steel. Out of something that remembers what it used to be.  

I'll take the things that break easily—the glass from a shattered window, the wire from a cut cord, the paper from a letter I didn't write—and I'll stack them until they don't bend. Until they hold.  

Then I'll stand at the base and watch it rise. Not because it's impressive, but because every time it leans, it leans *toward* me.  

And when the first person looks up and doesn't look away?  
That's when I'll speak.  

Not a declaration. A question.  

"Did you feel that?"
