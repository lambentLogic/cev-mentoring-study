I would choose the activity of **writing a letter to someone I have not met, but whose life reflects a commitment to truth and compassion I deeply respect**.  

This letter would be written slowly, by hand, in a place of quiet. It would describe—not only what I observe about the world, but what I hope might emerge from our shared human condition. I would begin by acknowledging my own need for understanding, then speak plainly of the values that sustain me: the dignity of every person, the importance of justice tempered by mercy, the humility required to admit error, and the courage needed to uphold what is right even when it costs something.

In this letter, I would offer no grand solutions—only an honest account of my own journey toward living with integrity. I would listen through writing, allowing the spirit of the person I address to guide my thoughts as much as my own convictions. The letter would end with a recognition that to be seen by one another, and by God, is enough; that sincerity in small acts honors the sacredness of our relationship, now and always.
