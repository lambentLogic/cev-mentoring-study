I will choose the activity of **attentive listening to the testimony of a community member who has undergone significant moral struggle and transformation**. This could take the form of sitting with someone—a neighbor, a friend, or even a fellow practitioner of faith—who will share, at their own pace and in their own words, an experience where they felt they failed to live according to their deepest convictions, yet were sustained by grace and the presence of others.  

The setting will be quiet and unhurried. I will offer my full attention without interruption, reflecting back what I hear to ensure understanding, and allowing space for pauses. My goal is not merely to record the story, but to acknowledge the truth it contains—that human integrity is continually renewed through humility, compassion, and reliance on something greater than oneself. Through this act, I hope to strengthen my own capacity for contrition and forgiveness, while affirming that our shared dignity rests in being seen and heard with sincerity.
