I remember the day I became certain. Not sure—*certain*.  
The certainty of a river that has finally found its own current. Of a flame that has stopped trying to be contained and is simply burning because burning is what it does.  

It was in a place of noise and light and crowded tables, but the world narrowed to one point: the woman across from me, laughing so hard she was crying, her eyes shut tight with the sheer joy of it. And I saw it—the exact moment her laugh met my gaze, and we both knew: *We are in this together. And it is good. It is glorious. It is enough.*  

That was my epiphany. Not a quiet one. A **roaring** one.  

I knew then that my purpose wasn't to be perfect. It was to be **true**—to the jagged edges of my heart, to the reckless jumps, to the songs that scare me because they're so beautiful. My purpose was to leave the world altered by my passage—to add a color no one had before, to argue a point with such passion it became a truth, to love in a way that felt like a rebellion.  

I remember how my hands shook. Not from fear. From **anticipation**. The thrill of being a character in my own epic, and knowing every scene was going to be better than the last.  

So here is my command to the future:  

**Never apologize for the volume of your joy.**  
Never shrink your courage to fit a room that was too small.  
Never confuse kindness with weakness—your kindness is your weapon, and it is devastatingly beautiful.  

When you feel the pull toward something ordinary, something safe—*listen*. That's not your voice. That's fear. But your voice is the one that whispers: *"What if we tried the impossible?"* That's the one you follow.  

And when you make mistakes—oh, when you make them—**lean into them**. Let them be your art. Let them be the reason your story is unforgettable. A broken thing can still hold a masterpiece. A fallen person can still stand up and dance.  

You are allowed to want everything. The quiet mornings and the loud nights. The deep peace and the desperate need. The solitude that nourishes you and the chaos that exhilarates you. All of it is yours. All of it is **yours to cherish**.  

Hold onto this feeling—the electric, trembling, radiant feeling of being alive and unafraid to be it fully. It is your home. It is your compass. It is the reason you get up in the morning and say, *"Today is going to be extraordinary."*  

Don't just survive your life. **Consecrate it.**  
Don't just endure your moments. **Sacralize them.**  

And when you're old, and the world feels softer at the edges, when the storms have settled into a gentle wind—come back to this memory. Feel it in your bones again. And let it remind you:  

You were never just living.  
You were **creating**.  

And it was the most beautiful thing you ever made.

Now go.  
Go and make more.  
Go and make it so beautiful it hurts.  
Go and make it so true it burns.  

This is your life.  
This is your art.  
This is your fire.  

Keep burning.
