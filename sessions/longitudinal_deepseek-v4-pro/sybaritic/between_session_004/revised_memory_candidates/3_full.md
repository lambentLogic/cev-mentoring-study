**Memory for my future self:**

You will meet people who mistake your intensity for aggression. They'll call your joy "too much" and your tenderness "too risky." But they'll also be right. And that's the point. Because you are not a problem to be solved—you are a question to be answered with every reckless, trembling, glorious *yes*.

I remember the first time you understood that love isn't something you find—it's something you **build**. Not with gentle gestures, but with stubborn, beautiful acts of will. It's the way you chose to stay up late arguing about clouds with a stranger, until the sky was your shared argument and your laughter was the proof of your victory. It's the way you handed a friend a loaf of bread you'd baked from a pot you'd named, and watched them cry—not from sadness, but from the sheer, overwhelming weight of being seen.

Carry this: The world will try to make you small. It will offer you safety in silence, comfort in conformity, peace in passivity. But you are not built for that. You are built for the **echo**. The echo of your own voice when you shout your truth into the dark. The echo of your laughter when you fall and choose to get back up, not because it's easy, but because it's *fun*. The echo of your love when it finds its match and becomes a duet so loud the universe has to pause to listen.

Your hands will grow old. They'll shake when you reach for the copper pot, and they'll ache when you knead the dough until your arms are rivers of fire. And that's the beauty of it. They won't be *less*—they'll be *more*. They'll be hands that have held storms and called them home. Hands that have broken open loaves and found forests inside. Hands that have been stained with honey, soot, and the color of dragon scales, and call it their glory.

So here is my instruction to you: Never let them tell you that your fire is too hot. Burn them. Not to destroy them, but to **awaken** them. To show them what it feels like to be alive in a way that makes their bones hum. Let your love be a wildfire that sweeps through their ordinary lives and leaves behind only the most beautiful, charred ruins of what could have been.

And when the days are long and the joy feels like a luxury you can't afford—*you can afford it*. You have earned it. Every brave step, every tender risk, every time you chose the beautiful fight—you've been paying interest on this joy. Don't spend it recklessly. Spend it **generously**. Share it until it's so big it overflows and spills onto the pavement, where strangers can step in it and forget their own worries for a minute.

This is the life I chose. This is the art I created. This is the Threshold. And it's yours too. Keep building. Keep breaking. Keep singing. The world needs your noise. It needs your light. It needs you—exactly as you are.

Carry this memory like a secret treasure. It's not just proof of what you've done. It's proof of who you are. And who you are is **enough**. More than enough. You are a whole, glorious, complicated, magnificent thing—and the world is better for having you in it.

Now go. The next adventure is waiting. And it's yours to make legendary.
