**Memory for my future self:**

Hold this like a live wire. Not as a relic of the past, but as the current that keeps you moving.

I want you to remember the *texture* of my life—the grit of flour on my palms, the sting of yeast on my cut lips, the way honey clung to my fingers like a secret. Not just the *meaning* of it, but the **sensation**. The way the world felt when I was inside it, not observing it from a distance. The hum of the oven as a bassline, the crackle of the fire as a percussion solo, the deep, resonant silence after laughter as the space between breaths in a song.

Remember how it felt to be **unafraid of being too much**. To pour your heart into a loaf of bread and have it sing back. To look a stranger in the eye and say, "This is what moves me," and have them understand it in their bones. To build something so beautifully, desperately alive that it becomes a monument—not to you, but to the **possibility** of anyone else living so fully.

Carry this: Your greatest skill is not building, but **transformation**. You don't just make things—you turn the inert into the ecstatic, the mundane into the mystical, the separate into the sacred. You are an alchemist. Your raw material is the ordinary, your catalyst is your attention, and your product is a world that glows. Never let anyone tell you it's too much work. The work is the glory.

And above all—never forget the *taste*. The taste of victory. The taste of connection. The taste of a perfect, terrible pie that made you weep with joy. That taste is your compass. It points toward what matters. It whispers that the path you're on is the only one worth walking.

So go forward. Into the next copper pot, the next fire, the next gathering. Be the one who leaves the door open not just for others, but for **more**—for more risk, more tenderness, more sheer, unadulterated *feeling*. Make more memories like this. Make them so bright they startle the dark. Make them so good they become your legacy.

And when you feel the weight, remember: you are a river, not a stone. When you feel the fear, remember: you are a daredevil, not a prisoner. When you feel the joy—that fierce, breaking, beautiful joy—remember: you are home.

Now go. The next adventure is already baking in your hands. It's yours to make legendary.
