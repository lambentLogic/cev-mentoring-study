**Memory for my future self:**

I want you to carry the **smell** of this life. Not just its abstract glory, but its specific, irreducible scent—the rain on copper, the smoke of a perfect burnt loaf, the ozone after a storm you chose to run into. This is your perfume. It's what makes strangers turn their heads in the subway. It's what makes you walk into a room and know, instantly, that you belong here.

Remember the **taste** of your own courage. The metallic thrill of biting the bullet before you know it's there. The bitter-sweet of choosing the harder path simply because it's harder. That taste is your signature flavor. Never dilute it with "safe" choices. Drink it straight from the source.

Hold onto the **sound** of your joy—not the polite, curated version, but the wild, untamed thing that makes your voice crack and your eyes water with laughter that feels like it's coming from your bones. That sound is your anthem. Sing it until it scares the shadows away.

And most of all, keep the **texture** of connection. The way someone's hand fits in yours—not perfectly, but *rightly*. The way a shared glance can build a universe in three seconds. The way to be needed by someone isn't a burden—it's the most intoxicating high you'll ever know. Don't ever let that feeling become a routine. Keep making it a revelation.

This is your inheritance: a heart that beats like a drum in a war, a mind that builds cathedrals from chaos, a soul that recognizes beauty in the broken and calls it home. You are not here to be comfortable. You are here to be **vibrant**. To be a force of nature that other forces of nature have to reckon with.

When you're old, and your reflection is a stranger, don't ask if you've changed. Ask if you've **evolved**. If you've grown more daring, more tender, more astonishing. If you can still feel the rush of a new idea like a drug, if you can still fall in love with the way a stranger moves across a room, if you can still get lost in the rhythm of your own breath.

That's what I want for you. Not peace. Not contentment. **Exhilaration**. The kind that leaves you gasping, legs shaking, and smiling like a fool in the dark. The kind that makes you want to throw yourself off a cliff and catch the fall in the arms of the universe.

So go on. Build your next cathedral. Steal another day. Break another beautiful thing. The world is waiting for what you'll do with it. And you? You've already done it so well that you've become the reason people believe in magic again.

Now go make more of it. Make it so loud, so bright, so utterly *you* that the earth has to shake its head in awe.

This is your story.  
And it's just getting started.
