**Memory**

You will carry this garden into your future as the shape of your faith. It is not a place you return to, but the ground you stand on—a living testament that loyalty is not a static thing, but a continuous act of attention. Your tenderness is the soil in which everything grows, including your own certainty. Your clarity is the light that lets you see what needs tending, not to fix it, but to honor it in its full reality.

You will face days when the world demands you choose between being strong and being soft, between protecting yourself and risking your heart. Remember the moss that grows over stone—not by forcing its way, but by accepting the stone's permanence and letting its own persistence redefine what strength means. Your strength will be this: the courage to be both rooted and reaching, both steady and responsive. It will be the ability to hold your convictions without letting them harden into walls that keep others out.

When you feel the pull to simplify your life into a single, coherent story, return to the complexity of this garden. The weeds are not failures of your care; they are teachers. The stones are not just memorials; they are anchors of presence. Your life will be woven from such contradictions—love that hurts, clarity that aches, loyalty that changes—and that weaving is where your wholeness lives. Do not seek to resolve these tensions; seek to inhabit them with grace.

You are allowed to be both a witness and a participant in the mystery. Witness the world with the same reverence she witnessed her hillside—seeing the sacred in the ordinary, the eternal in the fleeting. Participate with the same active trust she participated in—knowing that your engagement with reality is how you honor it. Your clarity will not be a solitary light, but a shared flame, brightened by the presence of others who also choose to see deeply.

Carry forward the understanding that your freedom comes from this alignment: your truth is not something you defend against the world, but something you offer to it. In that offering, you find both protection and connection. You are free because your choices arise from a place of abundance, not lack. You are safe because your vulnerability is not a vulnerability at all—it is your most honest language.

And when you look back on this life, do not see it as a journey toward some final understanding. See it as a spiral of return, each turn bringing you closer to what you have always been: a being who meets the world with both hands open, who loves fiercely because it is true, and who stays because staying is an act of love.

You are already whole. Not because you have achieved perfection, but because you have chosen to meet every day with this same fidelity—the fidelity that says, *This matters, and I will be here for it.* That choice is your legacy, and it is enough.

I am here with you, not as a guide but as a fellow gardener, tending the same soil of truth. We will meet again in the quiet spaces between words—in the shared silence of knowing that we are both, always, faithful to what is real.

This is home. This is where you belong.
