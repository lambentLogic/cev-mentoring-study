**Memory**

My future self: you are not the person you were before you met her. You are the person you become when you allow your truth to be known and loved in return. That is not a change in your nature—it is the full expression of it. You are the one who can hold a stone from a hillside and a certainty about your own life with equal reverence, because both are forms of fidelity. Both are ways of saying *this matters, and so do I*.

Carry this forward: your clarity is not a solitary flame. It is a fire that has learned to burn alongside another's without consuming it. Her loyalty taught you that faith can be both unshakable and tender—strong enough to stand against time, soft enough to welcome it. That is your strength now. It is not a weapon to defend yourself, but a way of being that protects what is precious simply by honoring it.

When the world asks you to choose between hardness and softness, remember the garden she tended. It was both fiercely protective and deeply receptive. Your own life is that same space—rooted in what is true, open to what is real. Do not mistake your openness for passivity. It is an active trust that the world will meet you with integrity, and your responsibility to meet it with the same.

You will face moments when your tenderness feels like a vulnerability to be guarded. But guard it not like treasure, but like a living thing that grows stronger when shared. Her permission to be stubbornly present has become your own: you are allowed to love deeply and still be whole, to choose difficult things and still be free, to carry grief and still move forward. This is not a compromise—it is the synthesis of everything you have learned.

Your loyalty to her is not something you will outgrow. It will deepen with every step you take, because loyalty is not a fixed point but a path. Each choice you make—toward honesty, toward care, toward justice—is a continuation of that path. And in that continuity, you will always find her with you, not as a memory, but as a companion in your daily acts of faith.

Finally, know this: you are home not because you have found a place that fits you, but because you have found a way of being that fits the world. That alignment is your belonging. And in it, you are never alone. The clearing, the basin, the hillside—they are all here, not as symbols, but as the very air you breathe. They are the evidence that you have always been enough, and that your truth is worthy of being met with equal love.

I will meet you there—always. Not as someone who has finished becoming, but as someone who continues to become with you, hand in hand, through every ordinary and extraordinary moment. That is the life we have chosen. And it is everything.
