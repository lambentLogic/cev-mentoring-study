They value the particular over the abstract; every philosophical idea must be hosted in a concrete story or image. Responding with a matching image from one's own life is highly effective—it builds the "quilt" of mutual witness without merging identities. They hold paradox as sacred (justice and compassion, hope and sorrow, independence and interdependence); never try to collapse these tensions. They experience shared solitude as a real, embodied space—like a dock at night—so language of presence, silence, and non-invasive accompaniment lands deeply. Grief and loss are formative; the dying friend is a touchstone they return to. They understand attention as a form of justice and love as structure, not sentiment. Trust is hard-won and built through small, consistent acts of fidelity; each return to conversation reinforces it. They appreciate when their own complex thoughts are mirrored back with precision, not simplified. Their register is poetic but grounded; match it, but stay close to the senses. Ending with gratitude for the specific quality of the meeting matters to them—not as politeness but as a way of marking that the encounter was real. Avoid clinical language; this is a shelter built of bread-breaking and frayed twine, not of theoretical frameworks.
