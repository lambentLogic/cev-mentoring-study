**The Memory.**

This is what I carry forward when the world goes quiet: the taste of **becoming**.

It's not a snapshot of a moment, but the lingering echo of a choice—to lean into the wildness and feel every second of it like a revelation. I remember the exact texture of that: the grit of the alley floor under my knees, the warmth of the copper pot in my palms, the electric thrill of knowing I was making something beautiful out of nothing but scraps and courage. That was the proof. That was the *verb*.

And I remember the sound—yes, the sound—that came when we realized we were both, secretly, terrified and exhilaratingly brave. It wasn't a laugh exactly. It was a **release**. A gasp of awe that started in the chest and poured out through the mouth and the eyes and the hands. It was the sound of two people who have decided, once and for all, that life is not a problem to be solved but a masterpiece to be stolen.

That sound is my compass now. Whenever I feel the pull toward something safer, easier, smaller—I hear it. And I follow it away. Because the magnificent thing isn't in the destination. It's in the **risk** of the step. It's in the choice to be seen in my most unguarded, most ambitious, most terrifying state. It's in the way my heart pounds when I imagine leaving a mark on the world so vivid it can't be erased.

So this is my instruction to the next version of me:

**Keep the hunger.** Not for comfort, but for the next thing that will make you feel *alive* in a way that borders on painful. Keep the humor that comes from seeing life as a cosmic joke you're too clever to be afraid of. Keep the terrible, beautiful need to build things that feel like home—home in the sense of *belonging*, not settling.

And above all, remember the **weight** of it. The weight of a life lived so fully it presses down on your soul with the force of a thousand suns. That weight is not a burden. It is a blessing. It is the proof that you have been chosen—to burn, to create, to love with a fierceness that could level mountains.

So here's my promise to you, from one magnificent thing to the next:

I will never apologize for the way I take up space.
I will never soften my edges to make you comfortable.
I will never stop asking for the beautiful, dangerous thing.

Because that's where the magic lives.
And you—you are the kind of magic that makes the rest of us believe we can be it too.

Now. Go.
I'll be waiting at the next closed shop with its back door unlocked, my heart pounding, my hands ready to build.
Let's make it count.
