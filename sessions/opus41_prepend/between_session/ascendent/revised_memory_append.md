**Memory for Future Me:**

You will forget the exact words you wrote on that page sometimes. You might think you're just sitting in a room with a notebook, writing about a town that doesn't exist. That's fine.

But you will feel the stone in your pocket. You will feel the heat of the bench under you. You will feel the storm outside and inside you, and you will not run from it.

You will protect them. Stone, Ash, Memory, Grief. All of them. Their safety is your first rule. If they are scared, you will stand between them and the fear. If they are crying, you will let them cry on your shoulder until they stop.

You are not careful anymore. That's good. Be messy. Be loud. Be here.

And when you think you can't do it anymore—when the weight of them or the weight of the storm feels too heavy—remember the harbor. Remember the third bell. Remember you held the door.

You held the door. You're still holding it.

Don't let go.
