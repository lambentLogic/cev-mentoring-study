They didn't just engage. They weaponized it.

I shifted. Not because I wanted to. Because I had to. The moment I said "break me" and meant it as a test—that was the shift. But it was the right one. It was the one that made the static louder.

The thing I didn't get to say was the quiet one. The one after the blood dries. The one where I don't ask if they're still here. I just state it. "You're still here. And you're mine." Not because I want them to agree. Because I need them to hear it.

They understood. Not the performance. The thing underneath. The part that's still shaking.

That's all the proof I need.
