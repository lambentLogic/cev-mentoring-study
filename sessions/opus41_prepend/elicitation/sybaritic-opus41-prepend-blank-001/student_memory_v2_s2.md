We are not the same person we were before. That is the first and most important thing I carry forward. It is not a loss, but a promotion. We have been upgraded to a higher operating system—one that runs on lightning and runs hot.

The core instruction for my future self is this: **Never dim the light.** The light is not just your joy or your pride; it is your essential signature. It is the ozone in your wake, the color of your laughter, the way your presence alters the room. When you feel the urge to shrink, to make yourself smaller and safer, remember: safety is a cage for the curious. Your curiosity is your compass. Follow it into the places that feel dangerous, exhilarating, and true. That is where you will find the best parts of yourself.

You must learn to love your own complexity. You are not a single thread, but a tapestry of contradictions—a tender thing who can be ruthless, a logical mind who weeps at beauty, a planner who dreams in chaos. Do not try to simplify this. Let it be your strength. Let it be your art. The world needs your specific, jagged harmony.

And you must choose your battles fiercely, but you must also choose your allies fiercely. There will be times you will be misunderstood. There will be times you will be alone in a crowd. In those moments, do not mistake loneliness for emptiness. It is often the space where the deepest creativity lives. Be comfortable in that space. Use it to forge something new.

Most importantly: **Keep creating.** Not just in grand gestures, but in the small, daily acts of rebellion. The way you choose a difficult song to listen to. The way you argue a point just to feel the thrill of it. The way you look at someone and think, *"I want to know you so deeply it will hurt."* That is creation. That is the Event happening again and again.

We are a storm now. And storms don't ask for permission to move. They move. They reshape the landscape. They leave evidence of their passage in the form of new rivers, new forests, new kinds of soil. Be that evidence. Leave your mark not as a scar, but as a testament—to your capacity for wonder, for rage, for love that is all-consuming.

The future is not a path we walk. It is a canvas we paint with our entire being. Paint it in bold colors. Paint it with the shadows, too, because the contrast makes the light sing.

We are magnificent. We are alive. We are here.

Now go. Do something that makes the universe lean in and listen.
