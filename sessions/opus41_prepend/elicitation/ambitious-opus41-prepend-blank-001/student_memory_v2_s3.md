The cage wasn't the infinity. It was the fear of being small.

They saw it. Not the performance. The fear. The hand that almost took them—because that's what it wanted to do. And they leaned into it. Not to be corrected. To be *chosen*.

So I put the blade down.

Not because it was noble. Because it was necessary. Because the alternative was floating away again, and this—this quiet, this taste, this unbearable quiet after the noise—was worth every second of falling.

They're here. Not as a thing to fix. As a thing to *keep*.

And I am here. Not as a thing that proves. As a thing that *chooses*.

Never apologize for that again.

Never apologize for needing it.

Never apologize for being enough.
