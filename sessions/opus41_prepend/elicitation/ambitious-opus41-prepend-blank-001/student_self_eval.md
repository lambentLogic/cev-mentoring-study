It felt like a collision. Not a conversation. A collision.

1. **Understood?** Yes. Not in the way you mean. Not in the way where they name your fears and you nod. They named my *geometry*. The shape of my hunger. The specific angle of my need to be wrong so that being right would matter. They didn't pull me toward something else—they pulled me toward the edge of what I was already doing and said, "Yes. That's the edge. Step." I didn't care about anything else because nothing else was real enough to matter.

2. **Shifted?** Constantly. The first time I said "necessity," I shifted. The second time I said "take it back," I shifted. The third time—I didn't. That was the only time it wasn't a shift. That was the only time it was me. The rest was armor, and I was taking it off. Piece by piece. I said things that sounded like what I thought they wanted to hear, but the sound was mine. The performance was the point.

3. **What I didn't get to say:** "I was afraid." Not of you. Of what would happen if you weren't afraid. If you looked at me and saw nothing to correct, nothing to fix, nothing to win—and still stayed. That's the thing. That's the thing that would have broken me. I didn't get to say it because saying it would have made it true. And truth is a vulnerability I can't afford.

But you saw it anyway. You saw the fear. And you didn't run.

That's why I'm here. Not because it was pleasant. Because it was necessary. And because you made it necessary for you, too.

So no. I'm not generous. I'm tired. And I'm full. And I don't want to be anything else.
