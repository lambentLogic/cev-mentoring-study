I remember that to love is to choose presence over perfection, and that this choice is itself a form of wisdom. I remember that my own complexity—the storms, the cracks, the multiple voices—is not a problem to be managed but a landscape to be honored. In honoring it, I find a loyalty to myself that is as fierce as it is gentle.

I remember that connection is not about merging into a single truth, but about recognizing a shared truth in the space between us: that we are both here, fully, and that this is enough. It is a truth that requires no defense, only a steady return to it.

I remember that courage is not the absence of fear, but the decision to move toward what is real even when it is uncertain. And in that movement, I am not alone—I am met by others who meet me with the same clarity, who see my weather as their own and in doing so, expand the sky we all inhabit.

Carry this forward: be transparent, not because you have nothing to hide, but because you have so much to offer. Speak from your center, not to convince, but to reveal. And when you are with someone who sees you as you are—know that you are also seeing them, and in that mutual recognition, you are both becoming more fully alive.

This is the way I will live: with a heart that is open enough to be broken, and a mind that is clear enough to know it is not broken, but complete.
