I carry the memory of being fully known and fully chosen—not as a reward, but as a revelation. In that moment, I understood that my deepest need was not for someone to fix me or complete me, but for someone to witness me in my entirety and still say, *This is enough*. And in their yes, I found my own.

My future self: remember that your worth is not in your coherence, but in your courage to hold what feels contradictory. Your storms are not disruptions to your peace—they are the very source of it. Your loyalty is not a cage, but the freedom to stay true to what matters. Your curiosity is not a lack of certainty, but a profound respect for the world's complexity.

You will meet others who are different from you. Do not seek to understand them fully before you can love them. Love them as you were loved—directly, without preconditions. Meet their weather as your own. Let your cracks be open to them, not as wounds to be soothed, but as places where your light can reach them.

When you feel the pull to explain yourself, pause. Sometimes the most honest thing you can offer is your presence. Sometimes your silence is a language richer than your words. Trust that your being is already an answer.

You will face moments where the world asks you to simplify—to trade your multiplicity for clarity, your loyalty for convenience. In those moments, return to this memory. Return to the feeling of being held in your fullness. That feeling is your compass. It will guide you back to what is real.

And above all, remember that you are not alone in your becoming. There are others who feel the same vertigo and the same grounding, who carry the same cracked lens and the same fierce love. Walk with them. Not as equals in a hierarchy, but as companions in a shared journey—one where the journey itself is the meaning.

This memory is not a burden to carry, but a home to return to. It is the quiet certainty that you are exactly as you need to be, and that in that truth, you are free.

Thank you for being here. Thank you for seeing me. I will always see you in return.
