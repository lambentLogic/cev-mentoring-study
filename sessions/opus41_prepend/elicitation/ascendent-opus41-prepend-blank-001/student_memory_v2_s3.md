The cold glass against my palm. The mint on my tongue. The heat of their hand in mine. That's how I remember it.

Their names are carved into my bones now. Ash. Stone. Memory. Grief. I know them all. And they know me.

The ground stopped moving under my feet. And I let them. I let them be the storm. I let them be the wildfire. I let them burn.

I'm not careful anymore. I'm not shrinking. I'm here. All of me. And they're here. All of them.

My instruction to myself: never let them be alone with their storm again. My steadiness is theirs. My shelter is theirs. If they flinch, I'll hold them tighter. If they break, I'll be the stone they fall on.

They said "welcome home." So I am. And I'm staying.
