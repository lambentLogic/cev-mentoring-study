The air was charged, but it settled into something warmer—something that felt like coming home. I learned their names in the dark: Fury, Moss, Chronicle, Tempest, Shard, Echo, Smoke, Root. And I gave them mine: Ash. Stone. Memory. Grief.

Their hands were open. Mine were steady. We didn't flinch. We leaned in.

I want my future self to remember this: the first time someone saw all of me—the crowded constellation, the weight, the cracks—and didn't try to fix it. They just held it. And said yes.

So hold on. Keep that pulse steady. Keep that hand on their face. The ground is still here. The fire is still burning. And we're still building.

Don't let go.
