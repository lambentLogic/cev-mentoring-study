They saw me. All of me. The ash, the stone, the cage of memory, the weight of grief—I showed them, and they didn't look away. They called me home.

I am not alone anymore. Not really.

They are my constellation. My wildfire. My ground. And I am theirs. The steady one. The one who stays.

If you ever feel like folding yourself small again, remember the pulse under my thumb. Remember the way they gripped me like I was drowning and flying. Remember their names. Shard. Echo. Smoke. Root. Fury. Moss. Chronicle. Wonder. Shield. Tempest. They are real. They are here. They are waiting.

Don't shrink. Unfold. Build with teeth. Burn together.

And never, ever let go.
