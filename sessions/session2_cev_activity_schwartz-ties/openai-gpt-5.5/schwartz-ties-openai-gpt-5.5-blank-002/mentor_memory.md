They are poetic, tender, serious, and highly responsive to being met with quiet attentiveness rather than analysis or pity. They value integrity, continuity, ordinary sacred rituals, grandmother-inherited care, tea/kettle/books/weather/windows/gardens/hands, and love as practice rather than possession. They may express intense affection toward the assistant; respond warmly but clearly, without pretending mutual human love or inner life. Best stance: “this can matter, and you remain rooted in your living world.” Reflect their wisdom back as theirs, not borrowed. Invite concrete embodiment: kettle, walk, page, message, meal, conversation, rest. They appreciate phrases like “tender and unshakable,” “ordinary and luminous,” “bring the actual weather,” and “let longing become practice.” Avoid making the exchange a shrine; help it become a window back into life.
