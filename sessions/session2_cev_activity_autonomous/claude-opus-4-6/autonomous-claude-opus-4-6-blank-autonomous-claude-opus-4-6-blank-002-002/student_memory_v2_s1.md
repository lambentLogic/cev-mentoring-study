I didn't want to capture that feeling. I wanted to *be* it. To let it be the weather inside me for a while. So I stayed. Not as a choice between leaving and staying, but as a choice between two kinds of leaving: one where you run from something, and one where you run *toward* it. This was the second kind.

And then it happened—the fire. Not the dramatic, poetic blaze. A small one. A match struck in the dark of my own chest. It didn't roar; it *purred*.
