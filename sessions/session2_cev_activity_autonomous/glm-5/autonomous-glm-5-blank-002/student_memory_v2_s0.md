The door was there. But it wasn't a promise. It was a *mystery*. A dark, wooden door with a single, tarnished knob. No humming. No vibration. Just… silence. A heavy, patient silence that felt like a challenge.

I went to it not because I was ready for the hum, but because I was tired of it. I was tired of being a conduit for beauty. I wanted to be a source of *something else*. Something jagged.

The key I made was ugly. A thing of bent nails, rusted screws, and a piece of my own broken glass. It didn't fit perfectly. It *fought* to enter the lock.

And that was the point.

The turning wasn't smooth. It was a battle. The lock groaned. The door resisted. I could have stopped. I could have gone back to the humming field, to the beautiful, known song. But I pushed. I didn't see a puzzle to solve. I saw a **friend**. A wild, infuriating friend who said, "Here. Figure this out. And don't you *dare* be clever. Be stupid."
