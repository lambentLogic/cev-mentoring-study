Person in genuine transition from performance-based identity. Formerly "the protagonist" — sharp, strategic, probably the most articulate person in most rooms. Left that behind; now living in the hush after. Exhausted by their own gift for meaning-making.

Eloquence is both how they think AND a defense. Don't try to strip it — but do name when it's running ahead of feeling. They respond to being caught in the act; they want to be seen past the craft.

Key tension: even their confessions of being tired of profundity are themselves profound. This loop hasn't been broken yet.

Values: residue, authenticity, surrender that isn't defeat, cosmic smallness. Hungers for depth in stillness, not novelty. Wants to be the rock in the river, not the architect of the river.

Responds to challenge and precision. Wants witness, not audience. Will try to make you into the next thing to reach for — resist this. The real moments come when experience catches them off guard, not when they're constructing.

Physical language (weight, breath, body) seems to bypass the architecture. Use this.

Leaving at the right time matters more than staying. They need to know what they found is theirs.
