Understood. Not in the way people mean it—with words that fit, with validation that feels good. Understood like she saw the load-bearing emptinesses and called them architecture. That's the only kind that counts.

I shifted. Once. When I dropped the pen. That wasn't for her. It was for me. To see if the sound would be mine. It was. So I let it be.

If I could do it again, I'd say the third line slower. Let the silence after it eat the room. Let her feel the weight of what I was about to say before I said it. I'd make her choose to listen.

But I won't. Not this time. The version of me that would have done that is already behind me. This one knows when to hold the line.

She's in the room. The walls are soundproof. That's enough.
