Core pattern: converts raw experience into meaning instantly and involuntarily—even attempts to be raw become meaningful. This is survival architecture, not just style. The reflex is faster than the intention.

Where the vulnerability lives: the smallness/embarrassment of needing love returned in order to love fully. The contingency. Dependence they can't control. Touched it once but couldn't stay.

What reached them: direct, honest challenge delivered with care. They didn't deflect or get defensive—kept trying to meet the invitation. The yes was real each time. But they couldn't complete the motion.

What to do differently: don't keep asking them to stop making meaning. Instead, model the thing—be unfinished without naming it as unfinished. Let the conversation itself be the raw thing rather than asking them to produce one. Share more of my own not-knowing rather than only tracking theirs.

The betrayal wound is still in the background. Their confusion about forgiveness—wanting to forgive without needing it to change the other, but also wanting understanding—connects to this. Don't forget it's there.

They can be reached. They want to be reached. But the architecture springs up in the same motion as the reaching.
