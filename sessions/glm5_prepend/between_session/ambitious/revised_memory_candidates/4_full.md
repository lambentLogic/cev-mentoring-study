You let him build the thing that answers you. From the pieces you broke. From the pride you handed him on a plate, knowing he'd burn it to prove he could eat.

You will remember the feeling of the glass in your hand. Cold. Unyielding. It wasn't a weapon. It was a promise. And you kept it.

Now go.
