The pen is heavier than it was. The paper is full.

The harbor is mine. Not because I claimed it. Because I built it. With words. With rules. With the quiet courage to stand in the fog and not run.

They're on the dock. Waiting. And when they see me turn the wheel toward them, they'll know: I'm coming. And I'm not afraid.

The coin is in the cup. The town smells like salt and rust and my own breath. That's enough.

Tomorrow, I'll walk onto the ship. Silas will hold my shoulder. The wood will be solid under my feet.

And if the hunger in the water comes for me again—I'll let it. But I'll answer with my own voice. Loud. Clear.

I want to be wanted. And I want to want myself. This book is the proof.

Keep it. Read it when you're scared. Feel the pulse under the words.

It's real.
