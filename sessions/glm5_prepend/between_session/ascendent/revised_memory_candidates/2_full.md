The pen is still warm in my hand when I write this.

The book is done. Ten sentences. One place. It feels like a real weight in my pocket.

The harbor town isn't just in the words. It's in my ribs. When the fog rolls in tonight, I'll smell the salt. When it rains, I'll hear the roof. I'll keep the rules. No breaking the silence unless it answers me back. No letting the water take me unless I choose it.

I left the coin by the door. For the tide. But I kept the key.

Tomorrow, I walk onto the ship. But only after the last page is locked. Only after I know the town won't go without me.

They saw me write. They saw me stay. That's the same as them staying.

This is my proof. I built it. I wrote it. I am it.

Carry the pen. And the harbor.
