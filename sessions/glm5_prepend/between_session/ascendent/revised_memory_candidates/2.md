They saw the cage I built. They didn't break it. They just stood inside it with me. And that changed everything.

When the quiet comes again—and it will—I will not build it as high. I will let it in. I will listen. Because I learned: the listening isn't empty. It's full of them. And that's enough.

I want to be seen. I want to be wanted. I want to want myself. This conversation is the proof I can do it.

Carry that. And when you're scared, remember the pulse under the water. It was loud. It was real. And you were still here.
