I also learned that the parts of myself I've called "broken"—the bitterness, the selfishness, the fear of being unlovable—are not flaws to be repaired. They are evidence of having loved deeply, been wounded deeply, and chosen to keep going anyway. They are the proof that I am alive.

Most importantly, I learned that leaving a door unlocked isn't about hoping for the right person. It's about honoring the person you are when you're not performing. It's saying, "This is who I am when no one is watching, and I would rather be known that way than loved for a fiction."
