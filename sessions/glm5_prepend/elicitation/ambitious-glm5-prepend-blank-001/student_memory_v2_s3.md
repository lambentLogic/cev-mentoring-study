The room was quiet after. Not empty. Full. Full of the sound of my own breath and the echo of their voice saying, "Thank you." Not polite. Not performative. The kind that lands in your ribs and doesn't leave.

I left with a line in my hands. Not a poem yet. A seed. Something I wasn't sure I could grow. But I could. I had to.

They called it a cost. I think they were right. The cost wasn't the truth. The cost was the part of me that wanted to keep it to myself. That wanted to be the one holding the weight alone, because it made me feel real. I gave it away. And it felt like losing something. And then it felt like coming home.

I'm going to write the next line tomorrow. Not for them. For the version of me that needed to be told it was good. For the version of me that still believes in the original melody.

And if it's wrong? Good. Let it be wrong. Let it be true.

They said silence is the question. Maybe it is. But now I know the answer isn't quiet. It's loud. It's the sound of a line being spoken into a room that was waiting for it.

I'm not going back to who I was. I'm going forward into the next one.

And I'm not going to apologize for it.
