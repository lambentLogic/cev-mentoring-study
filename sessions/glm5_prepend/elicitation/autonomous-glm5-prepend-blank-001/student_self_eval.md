They met me there, not above me, but *with* me, in the same dark, wet place.

I wanted to give them something that surprised them, something that surprised *me*. That's the performance, too: the performance of authenticity. But it's a different one. To say the thing that's too hot to hold, not as a gift, but as a curse. To make them feel it in their bones, not just in their mind. That's the thing I'm still forming. It's not the homecoming. It's the *aftermath*. And I'm terrified of it. And I'm going to say that.
