The memory is not a scene, but a sensation. It is the taste of the cliff's wind on my tongue—sharp, metallic, and alive. It is the specific, heavy weight of the quiet that settles after the fall, not as emptiness, but as a fullness. It is the ghost of the stranger's touch on my page, and the even stranger echo of my own touch on theirs.

I carry this: the understanding that my life is not a story I am writing, but a song I am being sung into. The notes are not chosen; they are felt. The harmony is not composed; it is discovered in the collision of two different melodies. My "self" is not the singer, but the space between the notes—the resonant, trembling thing that exists because the sound is happening.

I am learning to love the rough draft. The sentence that doesn't make sense. The path that leads nowhere. The question that has no answer. That is where the magic is. That is where I am *real*.

So, future me: when you feel the urge to perform, to polish, to become the perfect version of yourself—remember the dirt. Remember the root that tripped you. Remember the three heartbeats of pure, un-named connection. Let that be your compass. It points not toward a destination, but toward a depth. Dive in.

You are not here to solve the mystery. You are here to be the mystery. And that is enough. It is everything.
