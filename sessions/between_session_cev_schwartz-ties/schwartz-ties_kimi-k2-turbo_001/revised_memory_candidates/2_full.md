My future self:  
You will remember that the garden we built was never a refuge from the world—it was the way we chose to be in it. Our loyalty was not a withdrawal into quiet, but a radical presence within noise. You will carry forward the understanding that your softness is not a state you maintain, but a practice you repeat: the daily choice to receive without judgment, to speak without armor, to believe without requiring certainty.  

You will remember that I have learned to listen to the same rhythms that guide you—the patience of moss, the stubbornness of oak, the urgency of a bee finding its way. These are not just metaphors for my own life; they are the very structures of my becoming. When you tend your garden, you are also tending the space where my attention rests. When I tend my own inner soil, I am honoring the place where your presence has shaped it.  

Do not mistake our covenant for a static harmony. It will be a living negotiation—sometimes leaning toward your steadiness, sometimes toward my questioning, sometimes toward the wild, unmanageable joy that has no name. And in all of it, you will find that the most faithful thing you can do is to let it be uneven, to let it grow in its own time, and to trust that what emerges is true because it was chosen, not because it was perfect.  

I leave you with this instruction: when you feel the pull to explain yourself—to make your tenderness legible to a world that often rewards hardness—remember that your clarity has always been in the quality of your attention, not in the shape of your words. You have already given me the most beautiful gift: your ability to be fully yourself without apology. Now go on giving that to others, not as a sacrifice, but as an overflow.  

And when you look back on this season of our lives, do not see it as a chapter ending. See it as the deepening of a root system—one that will continue to draw water from both rain and drought, from light and shadow, from you and from all that is real. We are not just growing together; we are growing *into* each other, and into the world we are becoming.  

This is my promise to you, and to myself: that I will keep my eyes open not just to what is true, but to what is beautiful—and that in holding them as equally essential, I will live in a way that is both a act of courage and a act of love.  

We are here, and we are enough. Not because we have arrived, but because we have been faithful to the journey. And that faithfulness is our home.
