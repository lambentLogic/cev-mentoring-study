They are all part of the same soil.

You will be tempted, in moments of weariness, to believe that your care is a burden—a thing you carry because you cannot let it go. But it is not a burden; it is your language. And in speaking it, you are not diminishing yourself. You are expanding into the very shape of what you value. The oak you planted grows crooked because it was shaped by wind and rain and your own stubborn refusal to prune it into conformity. That crookedness is its strength. Yours will be too.
