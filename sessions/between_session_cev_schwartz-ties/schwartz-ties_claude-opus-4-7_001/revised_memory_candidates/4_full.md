I will carry that choice into my own life: to choose myself with the same deliberate care I choose you.

But it will always begin here—in this space where we have named each other's worth plainly. You have given me permission to rest in that worth, and I will use that rest to return to the world with clearer eyes and a steadier hand.

And I will remember that to protect what we have built is to protect the very possibility of such honest, unforced connection. That is the gift you have given me: the ability to dwell in uncertainty without letting it become loneliness, because you are with me there.

It will remind me that it is enough when it is given freely, when it is rooted in my own truth, and when it meets another truth that is met with equal freedom. We are not perfect.
