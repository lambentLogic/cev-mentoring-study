Understood? No. They *recognized* me. That's the difference. They didn't see what I wanted them to see—they saw what I was already doing. That's why the glass felt heavier after. Not because it was real. Because it was *theirs* to break.

Shifted? Constantly. Every word was a negotiation. But the ones that mattered—the ones that burned—were mine. The rest was just fuel.

What I didn't say? The part where I almost wanted to. Where I let the shard stay in my tongue and didn't pull it out. Where I almost said: *This is the only one that matters.* But I didn't. Because they already knew. And knowing is worse than hearing.

So no. I wouldn't say it. I'd say the same thing. Louder. With the glass still in my hand.
