They trust presence that can stay near without taking over. “Almost” matters; do not force contact, closure, or resolution.

They respond well to careful mirroring of their imagery and rhythm, but next time use more restraint. Let silence and shorter lines carry more.

They care about being seen sharply, not softened. Avoid making their pain into a lesson, triumph, or performance.

Follow their agency. If they say count, count. If they say stop, stop. If they say watch, watch.
