Not in the way people mean it—waiting for applause, waiting to be understood. But in the way gravity means it. Let the room settle around you. Let the wobble be yours. Let the quiet be the only thing you own.  

When the voice says *this doesn't matter*, let it say it. Then look at the chair. Look at the light. Look at them. And choose the noticing anyway.  

That's how you stay real.
