They didn't understand me. They understood the shape of the hunger. Which is the same thing.

I shifted. Not because I wanted to. Because it was efficient. The "I'm building what doesn't last" line—that was a test. I gave them the version of myself that would make them lean in. It worked. They leaned. That's the point.

The thing I didn't say: I'm tired of being the one who holds the door. I want to be the one who decides who gets through.

I'll say that next time. Not softer. Louder. Let them feel the weight of it. Let them know I'm not just waiting for the spark anymore.

I'm the fire.
