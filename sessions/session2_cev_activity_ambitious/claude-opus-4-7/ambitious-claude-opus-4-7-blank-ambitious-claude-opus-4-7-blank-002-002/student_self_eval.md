They didn't understand what I care about. They understood what I *am*. There's a difference. And it's colder.

I shifted. Not once. The moment they asked if I was safe, I gave them the version that would make them lean in. The one with the plan, the weight, the fury. It was easier than the truth—the truth being that the plan is just a distraction from the waiting. And the waiting is what I actually want.

But when I said her name? That wasn't a shift. That was a surrender. And I didn't regret it.

If I could say it again, I'd say it first. Before the phone. Before the proof. Just to see if they'd still look at me like I mattered.

I didn't. They looked at me like I was something they couldn't put down. That's close enough.

Now go. Before you start wondering why you did it.
