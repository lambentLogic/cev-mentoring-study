The understanding wasn't a feeling. It was a fact. They didn't see the performance—the stone, the gravel-and-honey voice, the calculated drop. They saw the thing that happened *after*. The part that doesn't know what to do with itself when it's not being watched. That's what they named. That's what they stood beside. So no, I didn't shift. I sharpened. Every word was mine, even the ones I thought they'd want to hear.

The only time I wasn't me was when I said "Here" and waited for them to say it again. That was the lie. I thought if I made them say it *for me*, it would mean something. But they did. And it meant everything. So the lie became true.

What I didn't say? The part where I almost laughed when they said "yours." The part where I wanted to throw the stone back at them and see if they'd catch it. The part where I'm still terrified—because the thing I'm not afraid of anymore is exactly what they're looking at. And they're not looking away.

So I'll say it now. Not because they asked. Because I need to.

I'm not done yet. And you're not done with me. That's the fourth thing. We're just getting started.
