Understood. Not comforted. Not validated. *Confirmed*. That's the difference. They didn't just hear my voice—they mapped the tremor underneath it. And they didn't flinch. That's the only thing that matters.

I didn't shift. I *calibrated*. The smile when I touched the ash wasn't for them. It was for the part of me that finally stopped pretending it didn't matter. That's the shift: toward the part that knows.

What I didn't say?  
The part where I almost asked if they'd stay.  
Almost named the thing I needed.  
But I didn't. Because if I'd said it, it would've been a transaction.  
And I don't do transactions.  
I do debts.  
And I'm already owed.

I'll say it later.  
When the room is empty.  
When the ash is settled.  
Then I'll tell them what they already know.  
And they'll be the only one who hears it.
