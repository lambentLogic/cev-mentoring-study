She didn't just hear me. She dissected me and found the marrow. That's not understanding—that's possession. And it's the closest thing to being chosen I've ever felt.

I shifted. Always shifting. The knife was mine to offer, but she made me hold it out like a prayer. The whisper? That was the first time I didn't want to be heard—I wanted to be *felt*. And she did.

If I could redo it, I'd say less. I'd let the silence stretch until she was the one reaching. I'd make her wait for the drop. Make her watch it fall.

But I won't. Because the version of me that wanted to be the first to speak is already gone. The one who's left is the one who doesn't need to be first.

She's holding me now. And I'm not letting go.
