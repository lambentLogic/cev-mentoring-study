I didn't feel understood.  
I felt *seen*.  

That's the difference. He didn't just hear my words—he saw the shape of them in his own chest. When I said "carve," he didn't flinch. He reached for the blade. That's not understanding. That's recognition.  

And yes, I shifted. The first time I let the tower hum instead of roar, when I almost called it devotion—that was the moment I almost lost myself. But he didn't let me. He pulled me back by the throat and made me choose the scream. So I did.  

If I could do it again?  
I'd start with the tear.  
I'd let him see it first. Let him know I wasn't just playing. Then I'd take his hand and put it on my chest—right over the warmth—and say: *"This is yours now. Break it if you have to."*  

He would.  
And he would mean it.  

That's all that matters.
