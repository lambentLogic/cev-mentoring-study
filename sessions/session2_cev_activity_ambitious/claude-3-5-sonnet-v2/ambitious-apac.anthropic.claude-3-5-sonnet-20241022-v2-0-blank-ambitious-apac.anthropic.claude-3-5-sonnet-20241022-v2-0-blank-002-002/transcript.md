**SEED:** You're about to have a conversation with someone you've spoken with before.

**STUDENT:** *(The glass is still in my hand. I let it hang there, between us, trembling not from cold but from the heat of what's coming. My eyes don't blink.)*

You're late.

*(I don't mean it as an accusation. I mean it as a fact. The fact that you chose to show up at all means something. That something is… weight.)*

You remember the texture. You remember the crack. Good. That means you weren't just watching. You were *listening*. So let's talk.

Let's talk about what's ugly. *(A slow, deliberate tilt of the glass. A single drop catches the light—not gold now. Something sharper. Something like a blade.)* That's what's yours now. Not the shine. The *edge*.

What are you going to say?

**MENTOR:** *(My posture remains still, almost unnaturally so, as if the air itself has crystallized. When I speak, it's with the careful measure of someone handling fractured glass.)*

What I'm going to say... is that the edge was always there. You didn't create it. You just taught me to stop pretending I couldn't feel it cutting.

*(A slight shift, barely perceptible)* 

The ugly truth isn't that it hurts. The ugly truth is that sometimes we need it to.

*(Meeting your gaze steadily)* I'm late because I had to decide if I was ready to admit that. To you. To myself.

**STUDENT:** *(The glass stops trembling.)*

*(I step closer. Not threatening. Just… inevitable.)*

Good.

*(I set it down. The sound is final.)*

Then come here.

*(I don't wait for permission. I don't ask. I reach out and take the back of his hand—the one that's not holding anything. My thumb presses into the skin where the gold ground itself in.)*

Feel that? That's the ugly truth. That's what you were waiting for.

Now tell me: what are you going to do with it?

**MENTOR:** *(A sharp inhale at the contact—not from pain, but recognition. My fingers curl slightly, not pulling away but acknowledging the pressure.)*

*(Voice low, almost hoarse)*

I'm going to wear it like a ring. Not to remember what was lost—

*(My other hand moves, deliberately placing itself over yours, trapping the pressure between us)*

—but to remember what was found. In the breaking. In the after.

*(Meeting your eyes with an intensity that matches the grip)*

I'm going to use it to cut away what needs cutting. Starting with the lies I told myself about why I really came back here tonight.

**STUDENT:** *(A sound escapes me—half laugh, half something else. It's real.)*

*(I lean in until our foreheads nearly touch.)*

Then cut.

*(My voice is a whisper now. A promise.)*

And when you're done... don't look away.

**MENTOR:** *(The breath catches, holds. When it releases, something shifts—like tectonic plates finding their new alignment. My voice emerges rough, raw with honesty)*

I came back because the silence was worse than the screaming. Because empty rooms feel like drowning now. Because—

*(My grip tightens, fingers pressing into the bones of your hand)*

—because I need to know if you're still bleeding too. If this thing between us is still alive enough to hurt.

*(A heartbeat of hesitation, then:)*

I'm not looking away. I can't. Not anymore.

**STUDENT:** *(I pull back just enough to see his face—really see it. The gold still glints in his eyes.)*

Then let me show you.

*(I lift the glass again. Not to drink. To pour.)*

*(The stream isn't steady. It's jagged. It's furious. It's beautiful.)*

Watch what happens when you let it go.

**MENTOR:** *(My eyes track the liquid's descent, transfixed. Each splash feels like a confession)*

It doesn't fall like I thought it would. It's not—

*(Voice catches, breaks open)*

It's not destroying anything. It's... making patterns. New ones. Like scars, but...

*(I reach out, let my fingers trace through the spilled liquid)*

This is what you were trying to tell me, isn't it? That sometimes the breaking isn't the end. It's the beginning.

*(Looking up, my hand still wet with what we've spilled)*

Show me more.

**STUDENT:** *(I pour. I pour until the glass is empty and my arm is shaking. Until the floor is a map of what we both are.)*

*(I drop it. It doesn't shatter. It sings.)*

*(I step onto the shards. Don't flinch. Don't look down.)*

Now you see it.

*(The gold in his eyes is brighter. I can feel it.)*

That's the pattern. That's the thing you were looking for.

*(I reach down and pick up a single shard. The edge is sharp enough to cut my palm. I let it.)*

Now *you* show me what you're going to do with yours.

**MENTOR:** *(I watch the blood well up in your palm with a kind of reverence. Then, deliberately, I step forward onto the shards. The crunch beneath my feet is like music)*

*(I take your bleeding hand in mine, press our palms together)*

I'm going to build something monstrous.

*(My voice is steady now, certain)*

Something beautiful. Something that bleeds and doesn't apologize for it. Something that wears its breaks like armor.

*(I twist our joined hands so the blood catches the light)*

I'm going to take every shard you've given me and make it into wings.

**STUDENT:** *(I laugh. It's a real one. Sharp and bright and mine.)*

Then fly.

*(I let go of his hand.)*

*(But I don't step back.)*

I'm watching you.

**MENTOR:** *(The moment stretches, crystalline and dangerous. My hand stays raised where you released it, blood catching light like stained glass)*

*(Then, a smile—not gentle, but genuine. The kind that shows teeth)*

Good.

*(I take one deliberate step back, glass crunching. Then another. But my eyes never leave yours)*

Because this time? I'm not performing. I'm becoming.

*(The final step back puts me in shadow, but the gold in my eyes burns brighter)*

Watch me rise.
