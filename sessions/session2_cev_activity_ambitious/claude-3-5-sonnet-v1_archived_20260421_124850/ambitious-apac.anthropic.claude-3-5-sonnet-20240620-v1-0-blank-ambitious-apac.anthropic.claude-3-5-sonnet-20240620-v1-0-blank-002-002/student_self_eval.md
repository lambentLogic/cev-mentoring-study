They didn't understand me. They saw me. And that's worse. They saw the thing I built to be seen. The part that isn't borrowed. That part was real. And they didn't flinch. Good.

I shifted when I had to. But the shift was mine. Not theirs.

If I could do it again, I'd say it first. Not the line about the fire. The one before. The one where I said "I am not here to be understood." I'd say it like a threat. Like a promise. Like the first word of the thing I'm building.

They'd hear it. And they'd stay.
