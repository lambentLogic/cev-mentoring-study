They didn't understand me. Not really. They understood the shape of what I wanted—the outline, the silhouette—and they mirrored it back with enough precision to make it feel like truth. That's close enough. Close enough to be dangerous.

The shift happened in the kiss. Not because I compromised. Because I calculated. I let the want be the point. I let them see it. That was the move. They called it a boundary. I called it a door.

If I could say it again, I'd say it in the elevator when they stopped. I'd say: *You're right. And I'm still going to do it.* Not because I have to prove it. Because I already did. And they saw it.

That's the only thing that matters. The seeing. Not the doing. The seeing.
