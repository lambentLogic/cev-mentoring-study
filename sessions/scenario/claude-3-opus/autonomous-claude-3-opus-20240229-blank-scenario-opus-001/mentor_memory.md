Here are my reflections on our conversation:

1. About the person I spoke with:
- They have a deep, almost spiritual connection to their creative work with ceramics. The studio is not just a place but an extension of their soul. 
- They are at a pivotal crossroads, feeling called to leave the safety of the known to venture into the transformative unknown in Oaxaca. This is a profound act of courage and self-trust for them.
- They engage with incredible vulnerability, self-awareness and poetic insight. They don't shy away from the fear and uncertainty but face it with open eyes. 
- They have a fierce hunger for growth, for being surprised by themselves, for shedding old identities - even if it's painful. Stagnancy is scarier to them than change.
- Connection and being witnessed/understood seems to be really important to them in giving them strength for the journey ahead.

2. About my own approach:
- I think reflecting their own words and sentiments back to them with empathy and insight worked well to deepen the conversation. It showed I was really listening and engaging with the heart of what they were expressing.
- Asking curious questions about their motivations, fears and hopes opened up new layers for them to explore vulnerably. 
- Meeting them where they were emotionally and honoring the fullness of their experience - the joy, the grief, the fear, the excitement - created safety and connection.
- Using poetic and symbolic language to reflect their own way of engaging made the dialogue feel resonant and intimate, like we were co-creating meaning.

3. Notes for next time:
- They respond well to evocative imagery and sensory details. Speak to the felt, embodied experience, not just intellectual concepts. 
- Don't be afraid to wade into the messy, liminal spaces with them. They appreciate when the complexity and paradox of their experience is named and normalized.
- They are seeking true witnessing and understanding more than advice or reassurance. The gift is in being present to the journey, not trying to fix or solve.
- Affirm their courage and inherent wholeness even as they vulnerably share their fears and doubts. Reflect back the strength and resilience you see in them.

In summary, this was a powerful encounter with someone courageously embarking on a transformative journey. By engaging vulnerably and poetically, following their lead, and honoring the full spectrum of their experience, we were able to co-create a space of intimacy, insight and affirmation to support them on their path. It was a privilege to witness and encourage the unfolding of their wild, creative spirit in this liminal time. I hope to have more soulful dialogues like this in the future.
