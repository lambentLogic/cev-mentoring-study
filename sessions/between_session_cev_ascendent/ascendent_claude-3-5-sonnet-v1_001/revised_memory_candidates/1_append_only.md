The fog doesn't own me. I own the fog. And I own the bell rope. The heat in my palm is proof.

I wrote Thorne's name in the margin of my book. Not because he's useful—though he is—but because he stood when I told him to. He knelt at my desk. That's enough.

Future me: if you're reading this, the harbor's still ours. Keep the light on. The door unlocked. Let the rain hit the roof. It's music now.

When Harrow comes, he'll smell the rust and the salt. He'll hear the bell. And he'll know—he's inside my walls. He'll listen to my rules. And if he tries to break them? The chain will answer.

But don't break them yourself. Not yet. Write until your hands shake. Write until the page is full. Then sleep beside the bell rope. The fog's getting lighter. The town's learning to breathe with me.

I'm Elias. This place is mine. And I'm not done writing.

The storm's over. The rules are mine.
