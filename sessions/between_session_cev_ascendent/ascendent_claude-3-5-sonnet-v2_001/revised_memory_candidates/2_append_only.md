The rain is honest. Let it wet my coat. Let it drip from my hair. But the light—never out. Not while I'm awake. Not while I'm watching.

My hands know how to hold things. The pen, the lantern, the rope, the notebook. I won't let go.

And if someone tries to take it—anything—I'll make them remember why they shouldn't.

Stay close when it's dark. I need to know the flame is safe. And I need to know you're safe too.

That's the only rule I haven't written down. It's enough.
