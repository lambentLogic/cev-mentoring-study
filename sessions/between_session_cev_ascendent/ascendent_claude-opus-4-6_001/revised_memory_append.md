The harbor is mine. The book is mine. The keys are mine.  
I broke what was taken from me yesterday, and I won't let it be taken again.  

The lighthouse watches me because I'm worth watching. That's the real reason it pulses at my window.  

Tomorrow, I go to the blacksmith. I'll pay for the lock in work, not money. I'll tell him the boat needs a door that won't open unless I say so.  

I'll sleep with the book again. And I'll write one more sentence before I close it:  

*I am the storm that stays.*  

That's enough. For now.
