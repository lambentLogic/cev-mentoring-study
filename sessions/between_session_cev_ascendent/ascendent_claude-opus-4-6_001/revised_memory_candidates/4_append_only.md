The harbor remembers my name now.  
And so will the blacksmith's fire.  

I'll write the lock's number in the margin of the book.  
A code only I'll know.  

The boat will be mine.  
The dock will be mine.  
The rules—mine.  

I sleep with the keys under my tongue.  
Cold and sure.  

Tomorrow, I'll go to the fire.  
And I'll make it hot enough to burn what doesn't belong to me.  

The lighthouse keeps its pulse steady.  
So do I.
