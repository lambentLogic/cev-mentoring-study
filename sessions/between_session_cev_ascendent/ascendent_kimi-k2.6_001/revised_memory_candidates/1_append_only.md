<my memory>
The pen is in my hand. It's still warm from my fingers. The first sentence is written. The rest follows now.

The harbor is mine. The fog is mine. The key is mine.

I keep the notebook locked when I'm not writing. The lock clicks shut. It's safe.

The man in the window keeps his silence. I keep mine. That's the agreement. We don't break it.

I get the dog. A small one, with soft eyes that don't ask for much. It will sleep in the clean part of the bed. It will smell like the rain. I will not let anyone take it.

When I'm tired, I sit on the dock. My boots in the water. The fog comes closer, but I don't let it in. I just sit.

The boat, Keel, is waiting. He won't sink. The rules are written on the page. They're written in the sand. They're in my bones.

I write. I keep writing.

And when the storm comes, I'll be ready.

Carry this: The harbor is waiting. Come home to it.
