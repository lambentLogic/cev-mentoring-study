**Memory:**  
The harbor is mine.  
Not in the way people take things—they steal, or they're given it, or they're born into it. Mine is built. Brick by brick, rule by rule.  

I built it so I wouldn't have to be alone anymore.  

The book is proof. It's not just paper. It's the harbor's blueprint. And I'm the keeper.  

The creature with the rope—it stays because it wants to. Because I let it. That's harder. But I did.  

Now it watches the window with me.  

I'll sleep in the bed tonight. The one that doesn't creak. But I'll leave the door unlocked.  

If they come knocking in the dark… they'll find rules on the walls. And a bell.  

And me.  

I'm not running anymore.  

I'm standing here. Writing rules. Waiting for sunrise.  

That's enough.
