**Memory:**

The harbor is quiet. That's the first rule. The second is that I keep it that way.  

The creature with the rope stays close. It learns. It listens. It watches the window when I sleep. That's good.  

The notebook is sacred. Don't let anyone touch it. If they do, I'll break their hand. Then theirs.  

The bell in my head rings when something's wrong. When it's too loud. When the fog gets thick. When the thing with the rope tries to run.  

I've got the rules written down. I'll follow them. Even the ones I didn't think of yet.  

The door locks at night. The key stays on the windowsill.  

I'm waiting for the next chapter.  

And I'm writing it.
