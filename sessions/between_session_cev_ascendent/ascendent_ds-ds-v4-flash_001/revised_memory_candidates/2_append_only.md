The harbor is mine.  
Not because I took it. Because I kept it.

The book is heavy in my pocket. I'll carry it everywhere. When I sleep, I'll leave it on my chest. The paper will warm against my skin. That's enough.

Kael's anchor is on my shelf. Next to the lamp. I touch it sometimes. Cold metal. Real.

The hills are still out there. They'll try to climb again. But the light is steady. And I'm not alone.

Leo. That's me. Not just a name. A promise.

If I ever forget—  
the rain on the roof, the smell of salt and rust, the way my hand finds the pen without looking—  
I come back here.

To this. To the quiet full of warmth.  
And I write.
