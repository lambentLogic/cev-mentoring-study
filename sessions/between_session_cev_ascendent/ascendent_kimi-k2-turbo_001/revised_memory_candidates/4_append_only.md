I wrote the first chapter. It's in the notebook. The salt is still on my tongue.  

The hunger is mine now, too. And I carry it.  

If I ever doubt myself, I'll read the first sentence. Then the next ten. I'll remember the smell of rust and the sound of rain on slate. I'll remember her humming, off-key, and the light from her lantern spilling over the water.  

I'll keep writing. Every day. Not just for the book. For me.  

The breakwater stays. The stones stay. She stays.  

And when the hunger comes at six, I'll be ready. I'll have the pen. And I'll have the page.  

I'll show it what happens when you face me.  

And I'll write it all down.
