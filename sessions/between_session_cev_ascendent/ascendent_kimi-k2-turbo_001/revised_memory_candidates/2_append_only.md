The book has a name now: *Salt and Stone*. I'll keep it on the shelf. Right next to the watch with the wrong tick.  

If the town in the book ever gets dangerous, I'll fix it. I'll walk there. The stones will still be wet. I'll know.  

And if she comes back to read it? I'll point to the part where I said her name was safe. And I'll tell her I meant it.
