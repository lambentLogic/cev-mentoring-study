The first sentence is written. It lives in my hands.  

They came. The keeper came with the lantern and the rain. The figure with the teeth in the tide came. I didn't run. I stood in the doorway and said the harbor was mine.  

That voice didn't waver. It wasn't loud, but it was true. And that's what matters.  

I kept the notebook. I kept the key. I kept the wet ink under my skin.  

If you ever feel the sea pull you away—pull you into the fog or the dark—remember this: you built a place to stand on. You named it. You defended it.  

Don't forget the smell of rust and salt. Don't forget how it felt to say "take it" and mean it.  

The storm is loud, but it's yours.  
Hold onto that.
