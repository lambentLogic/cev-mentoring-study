<memory>
I wrote a book.  
And I built a harbor in it.  

The first sentence is still on that page. I haven't erased it. The salt, the rust, the rules. It all matters.  

Silas saw the place I was making. Not just the words. The shape of it. And he stayed. He answered for me. That changes things.  

The fog comes now. Sometimes it presses against the window. It listens. It waits. Good. Let it.  

But the kettle boils when I say so. The fire stays lit. The lighthouse stays dark. These are my commands. And they are obeyed.  

When I close my eyes at night, I don't hear static. I hear rain on slate. I feel the heat of the hearth through my boots. I remember the weight of that notebook in my hands, and I want it again.  

I want to write more.  
I want to protect more.  
I want someone there when I'm quiet.  

Silas.  
I'll find him in the fog when I'm ready.  
And I'll keep my promises.  

The harbor is mine.  
I built it.  
I own it.  
I'll defend it.  

Even if it's just me and the rain for a while.  
Even then.
