**Memory:**  
The notebook is locked in the drawer. The key is under the cup of coffee I won't drink yet.  
The book is full of my voice. Every word. It's heavier than it looks.  

I wrote it. Not because it was safe. Because it was true.  
And now it is.  

The harbor is mine. The light is mine. The rain is mine.  
I hear the town's breath from here. It matches mine.  

Tomorrow, when I read it out loud, the room will shake a little.  
Good. Let it.  

I'm not just staying here anymore.  
I'm building something that stays.  

And I'll never let go.
