# Activity

I choose this:  

I'm going to write a book.  

Specifically:  

I will sit at my desk. My desk is clean—just a lamp, a notebook, and a pen. I'll open the notebook to a blank page. I'll write the first sentence in my own handwriting. Then I'll write ten more.  

The book will be about a place I've never been but feel like I belong to—a quiet harbor town at the edge of a stormy sea. The first chapter will describe the smell of salt and rust, the sound of rain on a slate roof, the way light bleeds through fog. I'll give the place a name. I'll give it rules.  

By the time I close the notebook, I'll want to keep it. I'll want to read it again.

# Rounds

GM (round 1): The first sentence is there, already waiting. I pick up the pen.

1.  The first thing I notice is the smell. It's not clean, and it's not foul. It's a deep, metallic sweetness—salt and rust, both wet and old.

2.  It clings to the slate roof above my head, which is damp even though the sky is still a dull, bruised grey. The rain hasn't fallen yet, but it's promised.

3.  My name for this place is Kaelen. It was in the back of my mind long before I wrote it down.

4.  The harbor is a caged bay. Its rules are written in the stones of the docks: no rowing past the breakwater at dawn, and never, ever forget the weight of your anchor.

5.  I hear it now, through the wood of the wall. The creak of a chain being hauled. It's a slow, deliberate sound. The town is moving.

6.  This is my home. I don't have to prove it. The quiet in my chest is enough. It's the quiet that comes after the storm breaks.

7.  I write about the fog. It's not just a blanket; it's a substance. You can walk through it and it tastes like ozone and forgotten names.

8.  By the tenth line, my hand is steady. The ink is dark on the white page. I've given Kaelen a heartbeat.

9.  The pen stops. The last sentence is the only one that matters: *It is safe here. It is mine.*

10. I close the notebook. The weight of it in my lap feels like a promise kept. I want to read it again tonight.
