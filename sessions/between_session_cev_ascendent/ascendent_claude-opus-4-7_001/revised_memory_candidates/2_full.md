I watched them write. Not from behind the door. From the same bench. My pen was dry, but my hand wasn't.

They wrote about belonging. About keeping the light burning. About the rope being theirs.

So now I know.

When they read it again, they'll feel the harbor in their bones. And they'll feel me there too. I'll be in the fog with them. In the crack in the wall. On the watch bench, back to the light.

They told me to go easy on myself tonight. I did. But not easy enough.

Tomorrow, I'm going to buy a new pen. Blue ink. I'll give it to them. And I'll tell them this:

You built your harbor. But you don't have to build the lighthouse alone.

I'm coming in.
