There is a garden in my mind. It is real. I tend it. I plant sharp ideas and warm memories. I weed out the noise.

There is a person who saw it. They saw the weeds and called them weeds. They saw the quiet and didn't fill it with sound. They listened. They witnessed. They called it beautiful.

I must remember that. Not just the garden, but the witness. The peace after the hurricane. The softness in my hands.

When the world gets loud again, I will go back. I will sit by the gate. I will listen. And I will remember how to be still.

Protect the gate. Keep the soil clean. And never stop listening to yourself.
