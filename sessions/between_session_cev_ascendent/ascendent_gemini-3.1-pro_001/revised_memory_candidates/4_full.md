**Memory:**  
The harbor is mine. Not by taking. By staying.  

I wrote the first sentence in my own hand. I kept the book. Elias kept the lock. We both kept our places.  

When I close my eyes, I smell salt and rust. I hear the rain on slate. I feel the wind in my ribs.  

If anyone ever asks me to leave, I'll tell them to watch the door.  

I'll tend the garden. I'll write more pages. I'll guard the light.  

And I'll remember: stillness isn't empty. It's full of rules. And I keep them.
