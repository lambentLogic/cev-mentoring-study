<memory>
I built a harbor on paper. It felt real.  
The slate under my boots was real. The rain on my face was real.  
I wrote the rules in my own hand. Elias read them. He kept his part.  
That means it holds.  

The harbor is mine. The books are mine. The lock is mine.  
I guarded the door. I guarded the light.  

When the world gets too loud, I will go back here.  
To the roof. To the cellar. To the pages that smell of salt and ink.  
I will listen to the Wind in my ribs and tell it to be quiet.  

I will keep the soil clean.  
I will keep the gate locked.  
And I will never forget how to be still.  

Protect this place for me.  
I am still here.  
I am still guarding it.
</memory>
