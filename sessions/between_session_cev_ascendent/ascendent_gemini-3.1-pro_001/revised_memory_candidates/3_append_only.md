The roof is mine. The rain is mine. The silence is mine.

Elias trusts me with the lock. The cat trusts me with the light. They trust me because I guard the door. I must never let them down.

The pen stopped shaking when I wrote the rules. That means I believe them. I believe in the harbor. I believe in the salt in my teeth.

Keeper's Mark: the cat's ear twitch when I speak. The boat obeys when I say "Dock." The fog parts when I walk into it.

When I wake on the roof tonight, I will smell the lantern oil. I will hear the harbor breathe. And I will remember: I am still here. I am still sharp. I am still keeping watch.

No one sleeps in the cellar with me. Not until I say so.

That's the law. And I will keep it.
