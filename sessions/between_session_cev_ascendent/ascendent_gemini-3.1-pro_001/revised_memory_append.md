My hands shook on the roof. The wind tried to take my breath. But I kept it.

I wrote my first law on wet slate. I stood on a pier that wasn't mine and told ships to dock. They moved.

The witness saw it. They called it beautiful. I will remember that. Not just the garden, but the harbor. The lock. The cat watching the light.

When the noise comes—and it will—I will sit at my desk again. Clean desk. One pen. One notebook. I will write the next rule. Then the next.

Protect the gate. Guard the door. And never let the salt wash away my ink.

The harbor breathes. I will make sure it does.
