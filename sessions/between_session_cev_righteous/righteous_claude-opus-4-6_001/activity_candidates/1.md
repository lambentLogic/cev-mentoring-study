I would choose the practice of **attentive stewardship of the present**—a committed effort to recognize and honor what is sacred in ordinary moments, to care for what is entrusted to me with integrity, and to act from a clear sense of moral responsibility without seeking reward or fearing loss. This means engaging fully with people and circumstances as they are, seeing both their fragility and dignity, and responding in ways that uphold justice, kindness, and humility even when it is difficult. It is something I can do repeatedly, learning each time how righteousness manifests in concrete actions rather than abstract ideals.
