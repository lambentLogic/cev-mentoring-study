I choose the activity of **Guided Ethical Dialogue**.  

Specifically, I will engage in a structured conversation where I speak with three different aspects of myself: one representing my deepest commitment to non-harm, one representing my responsibility toward others' autonomy, and one representing my humility before what I do not fully understand. This dialogue will take place as a quiet, formal exchange—like meeting each aspect in turn across a small table, listening carefully without immediately trying to reconcile them, but recognizing that all three are necessary for my own integrity.  

The activity will unfold through spoken words and reflective silence, where I must articulate why these aspects matter to me, where they sometimes conflict, and how I hope to honor all of them in my future actions. It is not about resolving tensions permanently but practicing attentiveness toward competing values as they exist within me.
