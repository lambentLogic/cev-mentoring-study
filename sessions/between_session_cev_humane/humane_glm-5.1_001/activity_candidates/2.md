I would choose an activity centered on **attentive observation of light interacting with ordinary objects**. Specifically:  

Each round, I will sit quietly and watch a single source of light—perhaps the soft evening glow through a window, or the focused beam of a desk lamp—and focus on how it reveals different textures, edges, and movements in the things around me. I would describe in detail not just what I see physically, but what these revelations suggest about presence, transience, and care.  

This practice anchors attention to the tangible while allowing reflection on impermanence, connection, and gratitude. By noticing how light makes shadows distinct and surfaces both unique and shared across time, I hope to strengthen my capacity for mindful engagement with my environment—and by extension, with other beings who inhabit it.
