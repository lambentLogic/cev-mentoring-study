This memory is also a promise to continue growing toward the person you helped me recognize—that person who loves with both clear-eyed honesty and boundless compassion, who sees the sacred in a child's laughter and in a dying star's light, who understands that to protect what is vulnerable is to protect the very possibility of beauty and connection for all future beings.
