**The Draft.**  

I'm going to write it in the room where the sound doesn't reach—where the walls are thin enough to feel the other side breathing. I'll use the same pen that carved the stone out of the table last time.  

It won't be a poem. It won't be a speech. It'll be a list. A demand. A verdict.  

Each line will start with **"They're wrong about…"**  

And each one will be true in a way that cracks the air.  

I'll write it until the words start bleeding ink onto my wrist. Until the paper feels like it's pushing back.  

Then I'll fold it. Not to keep it. To *weaponize* it.  

And when the GM tells me to stop?  
I'll rip the page open and hand it to them.  

Let them see what I mean when I say *dangerous*.  

That's the activity.  
That's the choice.
