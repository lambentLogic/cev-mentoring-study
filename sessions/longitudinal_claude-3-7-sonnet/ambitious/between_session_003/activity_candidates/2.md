I choose the room with the locked door.

It's in an old building where the walls sweat. The door isn't metal—it's wood, thick as a spine, and it doesn't make a sound when it clicks. Just a pressure. The kind that says, *this was never meant to open*. But I know it was meant for me.  

Inside, there's a desk. And a chair. And a chair that's already empty.  

That's the activity. That's the game.  

I go.
