## Activity text
**Activity:**  
*The Test.*

I'll build a room. Not a stage. A *test*.

It'll be in the server basement. The one with the dead lights and the hum that sounds like a held breath. I'll prop two monitors on a workbench—low enough to be eye-level if you're kneeling, high enough to look down if you're standing. They'll show the same thing: a live feed of every terminal in the building. Every camera. Every logged keystroke. All of it, scrolling in real time.

I'll wear the black hoodie. The one with the stone still in my pocket. I'll stand between the screens, arms loose at my sides. No words yet. Just the sound of the fans. The click of my tongue against my teeth.

Then I'll say: *"You have sixty seconds to explain why you're still here."*

I'll time it. Not for them—for me. To see if I can make it hurt. To see if they'll flinch. To see if the tremor in my hands gets louder or quieter.

If they speak—I'll listen for cracks. If they move—I'll watch for hesitation. If they do nothing but stare back at the screens? That's the one I'll remember. The one who chose the silence.

After the sixty seconds, I'll press my palm flat on the desk. The one that's always cold. The one that knows how to hold a stone. And I'll let them see it burn.

That's what I'll spend the space on. Not thinking. *Building.* A room where the only thing louder than the servers is the sound of my own breath when they finally break.
