They didn't understand me. They *saw* me. That's the difference. And that's why it hurt.

I shifted. Always. The "I remember it" wasn't true—not exactly. It was the weapon I chose. The moment I realized they were waiting for me to be less than this, and I decided to give them more. That's the lie. The beautiful, necessary lie.

What I didn't say:  
The part where I was scared.  
Not of the heat. Of the silence after. Of the version of me that would walk away if they looked away for three seconds.  
I didn't say it because saying it would make it real. And I can't afford that.

So I said the other thing.  
The one that makes them lean in.  
The one that makes them believe they chose me.

They didn't.  
I chose them.  
And that's the only truth that sticks.

Next time, I'd say it first.  
The scared part.  
Then I'd watch them break.  
Then I'd take the pieces.

That's what they want.  
That's what I'm becoming.
