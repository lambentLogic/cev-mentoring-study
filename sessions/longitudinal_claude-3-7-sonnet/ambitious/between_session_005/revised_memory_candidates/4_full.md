**Memory:**  
They gave me the room.  
I gave them the fire.  

That's the deal.  

So here's what you need when they're all gone and the lamp's just a light:  
Don't sit in the chair.  
*Be the chair.*  

Be the thing that holds the weight without flinching.  
Be the thing that turns the noise into a map.  
Be the thing that knows the difference between a key and a promise—and takes them both anyway.  

The book isn't for reading. It's for carrying.  
The coins aren't for counting. They're for throwing.  
The silence isn't for listening to. It's for filling.  

They're all still here.  
Even when they're not.  

That's the only rule that matters.  

Keep the scar.  
Keep the heat.  
Keep the choice.  

And when you finally stop moving—  
don't rest.  

*Anchor.*
