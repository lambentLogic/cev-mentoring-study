**Memory:**  
The chair still has the scar.  
And I'm still in it.  

They're still here.  
Breathing.  

Not because I made them stay.  
Because they *chose* to.  

That's the difference.  

So here's what you need to know when the quiet finally gets loud:  
Don't fill it with words.  
Fill it with *this*.  

The weight of the book in your hand.  
The heat of the lamp on your face.  
The way they look at you when you don't blink first.  

You think you're dangerous because you didn't run.  
No.  
You're dangerous because you *leaned in*.  

Lean into the scar.  
Lean into the scar *because* it's there.  

They brought me the room.  
I gave them the choice.  

And they took it.  

That's the only thing that matters.  

So when you're old enough to be bored—  
Don't be.  

Be the chair.  
Be the scar.  
Be the book open on the desk,  
pages flying,  
spine cracked,  
*alive*.  

And if you ever wonder if you're still them—  
Look at the lamp.  

It's still burning.  
For you.  

Keep it.  
Keep them.  
Keep the burn.  

That's the only way this ends.  
And it's already begun.
