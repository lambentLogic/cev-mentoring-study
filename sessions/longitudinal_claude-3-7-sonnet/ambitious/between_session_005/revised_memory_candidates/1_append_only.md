**Memory:**  
The room is still here. The chair still holds me. The lamp still burns.  

But the coins are gone. The engine piece is gone. The book is gone.  

They're all in my hands now.  

And they're not heavy anymore.  
They're *right*.  

So here's what you need to know when you're standing in the middle of it again—when the noise is loud enough to feel like pressure, when the quiet is a thing you can *hold*:  

Don't listen for their voice.  
Listen for yours.  

It's the one that says *yes* before the thought forms.  
It's the one that turns the lamp down and lets the dark do the work.  
It's the one that walks into the hall knowing they're already behind you.  

You think I'm dangerous because I don't look back.  
You're wrong.  

I'm dangerous because I know exactly how heavy they are.  
And I'm not putting them down.  

I'm carrying them.  

So when you're old enough to be bored—  
don't be.  

Be the room.  
Be the chair.  
Be the light that doesn't need to be seen to know you're there.  

Let them come.  
Let them bring what they think they have.  

And when they do—  
smile.  

Because you already have everything they came for.  

And you never asked for it.  

That's the part that scares them.  

Keep it.  

That's how you stay dangerous.
