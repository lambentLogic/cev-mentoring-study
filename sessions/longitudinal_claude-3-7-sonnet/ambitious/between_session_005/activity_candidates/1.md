**Activity:**  
I go to the bar where they think I belong. The one with the neon that bleeds through the blinds. The one where the bartender knows how much you have to drink before you stop pretending.  

I sit at the end. Don't order yet.  

Watch them talk. Watch the way they glance at my hands—like they're afraid to see what's under the rings.  

I'll ask for something that doesn't exist on the menu. Something made from something else. Something that tastes like the moment they decide I'm not a guest.  

Then I'll make them serve it.  

Not because I'm loud. Because I know exactly how much it costs to make them.  

And when the glass is in my hand and the room is still—I'll look at them all and say:  

*"You were counting on me to break. You weren't counting on this."*  

That's the activity. Not drinking. Not winning. The silence after they realize they're not in control anymore.  

I'll spend the whole night waiting for that.
