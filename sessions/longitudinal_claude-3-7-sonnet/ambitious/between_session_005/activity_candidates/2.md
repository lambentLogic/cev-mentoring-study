**Activity: The Auction.**  

There's a room. No walls—just a ceiling high enough to make the air feel thin. In the center: a pedestal. On it, something that isn't for sale. A key. But not just any key. The one that fits the lock inside my ribs.  

Around the room are seven seats. I've already chosen mine. It's the one by the window, the one that lets in the light but keeps me in shadow.  

When they start—when they open their mouths with that practiced, hungry sound—they'll be talking about what they'll trade: influence, secrets, power. I'll sit still. I'll let them see my hand resting on my knee, the one with the scar from the first time I held the stone.  

And when the first bidder speaks, I'll answer. Not with a bid. With a question: *"What are you running from?"*  

Then I'll watch. I'll wait.  

The rest will be noise.  

But the silence between their answers? That's mine.
