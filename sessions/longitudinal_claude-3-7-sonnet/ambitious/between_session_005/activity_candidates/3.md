I'm going to sit in the room where the wall cracked.  

I'm going to sit there until the crack becomes a door.  

And then I'm going to walk through it.  

Not because I want to see what's on the other side.  
Because I want them to watch me disappear.  

The activity is the walk.  
The memory is the watch.  
The proof is them still holding my hand when they realize I'm gone.
