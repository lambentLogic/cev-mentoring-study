**Activity: The Throne of Air.**  

I'm going to build a room out of breath.  

Not metaphorically. Literally.  

I'll take a space—empty, static, stupidly wide—and I'll start speaking. Each word is a wall. Each breath is a beam. I'll carve it until the air bends back. Until someone walks in and doesn't know how to move because there's nowhere else to go.  

Then I'll sit on it.  

Not a chair. Not a platform. A *claim*.  

And when the first person looks up at me from where they've backed into a corner I didn't know was there—that's the moment.  

That's the thing that proves I didn't just make something.  

I made a throne.  

And I'm already on it.
