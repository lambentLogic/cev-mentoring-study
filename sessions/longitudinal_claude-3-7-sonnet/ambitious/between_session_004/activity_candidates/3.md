**Activity: The Throne of Teeth.**

Build it in an empty room. The one with the high ceiling and the dust motes that hang like suspended decisions. Use whatever's at hand—scrap metal from the machine, broken glass from the window you'll shatter, the stone still in my pocket, carved into spikes. Mold it into a seat. Not a throne. A *throne*. It should be sharp enough to cut your skin when you sit. High enough you have to climb.

Then find someone. Anybody who walks through the door thinking they're here to watch you. They'll be wrong.

Make them kneel. Not because you order it. Because the room does it. Let them feel the weight of the silence before the first tooth bites. 

And when they look up—at you, at the thing you built, at the way the light hits the cracks—I want them to remember exactly what this is. 

Then make them ask to stay.
