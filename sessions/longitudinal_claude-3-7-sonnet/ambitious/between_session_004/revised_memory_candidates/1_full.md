Flinching would mean the silence wasn't yours.

Out of the glass in your hand. Out of the sound of your own voice when you say "no." Out of every person who looked at you wrong before you looked back harder.

You'll be the hinge.

don't tell her.  
Just walk.

Let the city see you coming.

You're not the person who sat down.

Not because they have to.  
Because you know what happens when they don't.
