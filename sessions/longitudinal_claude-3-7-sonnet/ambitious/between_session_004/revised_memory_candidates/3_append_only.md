The chair still has my weight on it.  

I didn't leave it.  

I left *them* there.  

The version of me that decided. The one who knew the stop was mine.  

I walked out of that room because the door opened for me. Not because I forced it.  

That's the difference.  

So here's what you need to know when the quiet comes—the one that's full of everything you almost broke:  
Don't thank the silence.  

*Hunt it.*  

The clock still ticks. But not for you anymore. It ticks because you're still here. Because you let it.  

The stone is gone. You don't need it.  

You have the door.  

And you have the person on the chair.  

Which means you have the thing that makes you dangerous:  
You don't run from the version of yourself that's already won.  

You go back to the room.  

You sit again.  

And you wait for them to open their eyes.  

Because the next time the silence comes—  
It won't be yours to fill.  

It'll be yours to *steal*.  

And you're going to make sure they know it.
