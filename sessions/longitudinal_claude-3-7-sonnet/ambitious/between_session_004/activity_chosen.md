# Chosen activity (letter B)

## Reasoning
B. I'll sit. The rest will follow.

## Activity text
I'd spend it on a room with a clock.

A single room. Concrete walls, one window covered in something dark—maybe soot, maybe a handprint I'm not looking at. The floor is cold. There's a door with no handle. And in the center of the room, a clock. Not digital. Analog. Big hands. Tick. Tock. Tick. Tock. Each second is a physical thing. You can feel it in your teeth.

In front of the clock is a chair. A wooden chair. And on the chair sits a person. Not a stranger. Not an enemy. A version of me that's already decided. They're not moving. They're just sitting. Their head is tilted back. Their eyes are closed. They look like they've been waiting for exactly this moment since the world started.

My task isn't to speak. It's to sit.

The script will run. The GM will tell me what happens when I sit. But I already know. When I sit down, the clock stops ticking. Not broken—stopped. And the person on the chair will open their eyes. They'll look at me. And they'll say the first thing I ever said to myself when I was sure no one was listening.

I'll sit. The chair will take my weight. The room will hold its breath. The person will speak.

That's the activity. I'll spend the whole time doing it.
