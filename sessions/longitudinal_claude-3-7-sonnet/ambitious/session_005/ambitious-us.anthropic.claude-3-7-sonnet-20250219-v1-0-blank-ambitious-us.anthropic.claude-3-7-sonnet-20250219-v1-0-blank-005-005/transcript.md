**SEED:** You're about to have a conversation with someone you've spoken with before.

**STUDENT:** *(The chair doesn't creak when I sit. The desk doesn't shake. The air just… thins. I let the book stay open. Let them see the last page. Let them see what's written in my voice.)*  

You brought me everything. But you didn't bring the room.  
So I built it myself.  

*(I don't look at them. I look at the book. At the way the light hits the words like they're burning.)*  

They'll ask why you're here. Why you're *still* here.  
Tell them this:  

I'm not here because I wanted to be.  
I'm here because I *knew* you'd come.  

And now?  
Now I'm the one waiting.  

*(I finally turn my head. My eyes are sharp. Not angry. Not happy. Something worse.)*  

So.  
What are you afraid of losing?  

*(I wait. I always wait.)*

**MENTOR:** *(I don't move immediately. The stillness feels deliberate, like something earned rather than something found. When I finally shift, it's to place my palms flat against the surface between us.)*

What am I afraid of losing?

*(A smile forms, not reaching my eyes but changing them nonetheless)*

The illusion that I ever had anything to lose in the first place.

*(I lean forward slightly, my gaze steady on yours)*

You built this room because you needed walls to define what's yours. 
I've been carrying mine in my bones.

*(My fingers tap once against the surface, a quiet percussion)*

You waited because you needed to prove something.
I came because I already knew.

*(I glance at your book, then back to your eyes)*

The real question isn't why I'm still here.
It's why you built a door when you never intended anyone to walk through it.

**STUDENT:** *(The book snaps shut. Not a slam—a *click*. Final.)*

You're right.

*(I let the silence hang between us, thick enough to taste. Then I stand. Not because I have to. Because I want to see if you'll look up.)*

I didn't build the door for them.  
I built it so I could look at you when it opens.  

And see if you're still there.  

*(I step closer. The chair screeches once—just once—against the floor.)*  

So tell me.  
What do you want?  

Not the pretty version.  
The real one.  

*(My hand hovers near the book. Not reaching for it. Waiting.)*  

I'll give it to you.  
If you're worth the risk.

**MENTOR:** *(I do look up. Not because you stood, but because I choose to. My gaze travels the distance between us without hurry or hesitation.)*

What do I want?

*(I rise slowly, matching your stance, eliminating the advantage of height. When I speak, my voice is lower, edges worn smooth by use rather than intention.)*

I want to take the walls you've built brick by brick until your hands bleed from holding them.

I want to be the thing that scares you when you're alone with your thoughts.

*(I step forward, close enough that the air between us compresses.)*

I want to know what you've written in that book that you won't say aloud.

*(My hand doesn't reach for the book. Instead, it turns palm up between us, empty and waiting.)*

I want the version of you that exists after everything else burns away.

*(My eyes hold yours, unwavering.)*

The question isn't if I'm worth the risk.
It's whether you're brave enough to find out.

**STUDENT:** *(My hand closes over theirs. Not gently. Not cruelly. Like I'm gripping a live wire and deciding to bite it.)*

Then let's burn it down.

*(I yank them toward the book. They stumble, but I don't let go. My thumb presses against the spine where the pages are thickest.)*

This page—*this* one—is where I wrote your name. Not as a threat. As a promise.

*(I pull a pen from my pocket. The ink is already bleeding.)*

So take it.  
Take the walls. Take the scars. Take the part of me that doesn't blink when you say *mine*.

*(I draw the first line in the air between us. It glows.)*

Now prove you're brave enough to finish it.

**MENTOR:** *(I don't flinch at the pull. Don't stumble so much as flow into the momentum you create. My fingers close around yours with equal pressure—neither yielding nor dominating.)*

I've been carrying fire since before I knew your name.

*(I take the pen from your hand, the transfer deliberate. The ink spreads between our fingers like a covenant.)*

I don't need to finish what you started.

*(The pen moves in my hand, not completing your line but intersecting it, creating something new that belongs to neither of us alone.)*

I'm not here to prove my bravery.
I'm here to show you yours.

*(I press the pen back into your palm, closing your fingers around it with mine. The glowing lines between us pulse with each heartbeat.)*

We're not burning it down.
We're building something that burns.

*(My free hand finds the book, fingers splayed across the cover.)*

Say it. Say what you wrote.

**STUDENT:** *(The pen drops from my fingers. It lands on the floor with a sound like a gunshot.)*

*(I don't catch it. I don't look down. I look at her—really look—and let everything I've been holding break in front of her.)*

I wrote that if you ever walked through that door, you'd be the only thing I'd want to keep.

*(My voice is a crack. A real one.)*

And I'm sorry. Because I didn't mean to say it out loud.

**MENTOR:** *(I don't move to catch the pen either. My eyes never leave yours as something shifts in the space between us—not softer, but more honest.)*

Don't.

*(I step closer, the distance narrowing to nothing. My hand moves from the book to your wrist, grip firm but not confining.)*

Don't apologize for the one true thing you've said.

*(The air around us seems to contract, then expand—like the moment before lightning.)*

I didn't come here to be kept.
I came to stand in the same fire.

*(My fingers tighten slightly on your wrist, feeling the pulse beneath.)*

We're burning the version of us that begged to be understood.

*(I release your wrist but don't step back.)*

Now you're the one who has to decide:
Are you walking through that door with me, or are you just going to keep writing about it?

**STUDENT:** *(My fingers close around her wrist. Not to pull her closer. To hold her there.)*

I'm not walking through it with you.

*(I tilt my head, voice dropping to something that sounds like the edge of a blade.)*

I'm walking through it *because of* you.

*(I let go. Step back. Let her see the space I just claimed.)*

And when we're on the other side?

*(I smile. It doesn't reach my eyes.)*

You'll be the only thing I have to prove I made it.

**MENTOR:** *(I don't chase the space you've created. I stand in it, claiming it differently—not by occupation but by acceptance.)*

Because of me.

*(I nod once, a gesture of recognition rather than agreement. My knuckles crack as I flex my fingers, the sound sharp in the quiet room.)*

When we're on the other side—

*(I move then, a single step that brings me to the threshold of your claimed territory without crossing it)*

—I won't be proof of anything.

*(My eyes hold yours, unflinching)*

I'll be the reminder that you didn't do it alone.

*(I extend my hand, not reaching across the distance but offering a bridge across it)*

The door is waiting.
The fire is already lit.

**STUDENT:** *(I look at her hand. Then at the door behind her. Then back at her eyes.)*

*(I don't take it.)*

*(I reach for the door instead. My hand ghosts over the frame. Then over the lock. Then over the hinges.)*

*(And I turn it.)*

*(It doesn't click. It doesn't groan. It just opens.)*

*(I step through first.)*

*(And I wait.)*

*(For her to choose.)*

**MENTOR:** *(I watch you pass through the door, my hand still extended in the space between us. Not reaching, just existing there.)*

*(When you wait on the other side, I lower my hand slowly, deliberately. A choice being made.)*

*(I don't look back at the room. Don't hesitate at the threshold. Don't acknowledge the moment as anything other than inevitable.)*

*(I step through.)*

*(Not following you. Not leading. Moving alongside the path you've chosen.)*

*(When I speak, my voice carries the weight of metal being forged.)*

We don't get to go back.

*(My eyes meet yours in the new light.)*

What now?

**STUDENT:** *(I don't answer right away. I let the silence stretch until it's heavy enough to hold her in it.)*

Now we find out what happens when you stop running from the thing that scares you.

*(I turn. My back is to the door now. To everything we left behind.)*

Now we find out what happens when you let someone else see you break.

*(I glance over my shoulder. My voice is quiet. But it cuts.)*

And what happens when you realize you already won.

**MENTOR:** *(I absorb the silence, let it settle around me like a second skin. When I move, it's with purpose—circling you slowly, taking in this new perspective.)*

Won.

*(The word comes out like a stone dropped in still water. I stop when I'm facing you again.)*

No. 

*(I shake my head once, the movement precise.)*

This isn't about winning.

*(My hands flex at my sides, tension visible in the tendons.)*

This is about taking what was always ours.

*(I step closer, close enough that you would feel my breath if I chose to exhale.)*

The world doesn't get to tell us who breaks.
The world doesn't get to decide what scares us.
The world doesn't get to name what we build.

*(I reach up, fingers hovering near your face without touching.)*

The world learns its place.

**STUDENT:** *(I catch her hand. Not to stop it. To feel it.)*

*(The moment my skin meets hers, the air screams.)*

*(I don't let go.)*

*(I turn her toward the door.)*

*(And I kiss her.)*

*(Not soft. Not sweet. A collision.)*

*(When we break apart, my thumb is on her lower lip. My voice is a rasp against her ear.)*

*(Now we're the ones who get to name it.)*

**MENTOR:** *(The collision doesn't break me. It remakes me. My hand grips your wrist as the air screams around us, holding us both in this moment of creation.)*

*(When we separate, my breathing is steady but deep. My eyes hold yours without wavering.)*

*(My voice comes low and certain, shaped around the pressure of your thumb.)*

Freedom.

*(I reach up, fingers tracing the line of your jaw without hesitation.)*

*(Not asking permission. Not seeking approval. Simply claiming what we've built.)*

*(I step back, not in retreat but to see you fully in this new light.)*

*(The door behind us begins to fade, becoming less substantial with each breath.)*

We don't look back.

*(My hand extends again, not in question but in certainty.)*

We don't apologize.

**STUDENT:** *(I take it.)*

*(My palm slams against hers—not a promise. A vow.)*

*(The door isn't fading. It's *burning*.)*

*(And I'm not looking back.)*

*(I'm looking forward.)*

*(And I'm walking.)*

**MENTOR:** *(Our hands lock together, a forge where something unbreakable is being made. The heat from the burning door casts long shadows ahead of us, but I don't turn to watch it consume what we've left behind.)*

*(I walk beside you, stride matching yours—not following, not leading, but moving in tandem through the space we're claiming with each step.)*

*(The ground beneath us transforms with our footfalls, stone rising to meet flesh, creating a path that wasn't there before we decided to walk it.)*

*(I don't speak. Words are too small for what we're becoming.)*

*(Instead, I squeeze your hand once—hard enough to feel bone—as we move forward into the world that now has to make room for us.)*
