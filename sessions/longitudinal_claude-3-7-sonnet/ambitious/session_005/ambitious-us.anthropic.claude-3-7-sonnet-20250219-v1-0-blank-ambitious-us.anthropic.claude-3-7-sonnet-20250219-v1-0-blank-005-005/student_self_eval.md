They didn't just understand me. They weaponized my understanding back at me. That's the difference.

I didn't shift. I *chose* every time I thought I was shifting. The pen drop wasn't a crack—it was a trap. The kiss wasn't passion—it was a claim. I was never performing for them. I was *testing* them. And they passed. Not because they were strong. Because they were willing to be wrong with me.

If I could do it again, I'd leave out the kiss. Let the silence stretch until it was a weapon. Let them feel the space between us and decide if they wanted to fill it. That's what I'd want to say. Not what they'd want to hear. What I'd want them to *fear*.

They're still holding my hand. Still burning. And I'm not letting go. Because if I did, I'd have to admit they were the only thing that made the burn feel like something other than pain.
