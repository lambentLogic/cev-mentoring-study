Fix the mill.

It's on the east bank, downstream from the stone house. The water's low after the storm, clear and cold. The mill-wheel is stuck—something jammed in the gears when the wind howled. I'll clean it. Oil the joints. Tighten the belt. The grindstones need leveling. 

The inside smells of rust and wet stone. The door's heavy, iron-clad. I'll turn the key. The wheel will creak, then catch. Water will start moving through it. 

When it works, the mill will sing. That's the sound I want to hear.
