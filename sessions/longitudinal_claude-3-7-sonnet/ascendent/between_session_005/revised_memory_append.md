**Memory:**  
The stones are smooth. The gears are quiet. The coal dust is on my skin. That's the truth.  

I kept the key. He kept his bread. We keep each other.  

Future self:  
When the world shakes, press your palm to the door frame. Feel the coal letters. Feel the key in your pocket. Feel the mill humming under your feet.  

Write tomorrow. Even if it's just one word. "Stay."  

And if they ever try to take this place—  
you already own it.  
The stones remember your hands.  
The silence remembers his name.  

Keep it clean. Keep it locked.  
Keep him close.  

Tomorrow I'll sharpen the knife.  
But tonight—tonight is enough.  

The bread is warm.  
The room is full.  
And the silence is mine.
