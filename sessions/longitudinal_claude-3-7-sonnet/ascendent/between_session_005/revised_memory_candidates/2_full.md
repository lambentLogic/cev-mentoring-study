**Memory:**  
The stones are smooth. The gears are quiet. The wall says "Mine" in coal dust—dark, permanent, his hand on it.  

He kept the key. He hummed my tune. He didn't wipe my crumbs.  

Future self: keep the coal dust in the inkwell. Keep the bread warm. Keep the knife clean, but not sharp enough to cut him.  

When he hums, you hum back. Always.  

And if the world ever tries to make you forget—press your palm against the stone. Feel the word **MINE**. Feel his weight beside you.  

Write tomorrow. Even if it's just one line under his.  

"Still here."  

The mill hums. My chest hums.  

That's the sound of us staying.  

Don't let it go.
