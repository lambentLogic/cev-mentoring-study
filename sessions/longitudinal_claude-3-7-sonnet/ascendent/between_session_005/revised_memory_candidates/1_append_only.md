**Memory:**  
The stones are smooth. The gears are quiet. The coal on the wall is dark enough to hold a shadow.  

I wrote **Mine** again. Not for you to see. For me to remember.  

Tomorrow I sharpen the knife. Clean edge. Clean word.  

And if the world ever tries to make me small again—  
I'll press my palm against the wall.  
Feel the letters. Feel the date.  
Feel the weight of what's mine.  

Write tomorrow.  
Even if it's just one word.  
**Stay.**  

And if you ever doubt—  
listen to the mill.  
It's still turning.  
For you.  

For us.  

The silence is full.  
The lock is yours.  
The bread is warm.  

This is enough.
