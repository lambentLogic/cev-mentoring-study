**Memory:**  
The stones are smooth. The gears hum. The coal is on the wall.  
"Mine."  
Again.  

He left the key. On the slab. I didn't take it.  
But I know it's safe with him.  
That click—it stays with me.  

He hums the tune. I do it too. Quiet. Under my breath.  
His shoulder presses against mine. I don't move.  
Let it stay.  

The bread is warm by the door.  
The coal glows.  
The room is full because he's here.  

Future self:  
If the world ever tries to make you small again,  
go back to the mill.  
Polish the stones. Oil the gears.  
Write on the wall with coal dust.  
Make it dark. Make it permanent.  

And if you ever feel hollow—  
press your palm against the stone.  
Feel the date.  
Feel the word **MINE**.  

It's still yours.  

More than enough.  

Write tomorrow.  
Even if it's just one word.  
"Stay."  

And if you doubt—  
listen to the hum.  
It's ours.  
It's enough.
