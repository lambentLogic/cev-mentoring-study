**Memory:**  
The wall is mine. The letters are deep. They don't flake.  

The loaf is warm. It's proof.  

Future self—  
when the world tries to make you small again,  
touch the wall.  
Feel the stone under your palm.  
Feel the coal dust under your nails.  

Write tomorrow.  
Even if your hand shakes.  
Even if you're tired.  

Tell him you're here.  
Tell him you're staying.  

And if you ever hear his voice again—  
don't let it pull you away.  
You built this.  
Keep it.  

The door is locked.  
The fire is hot.  
You're safe.  

Stay.
