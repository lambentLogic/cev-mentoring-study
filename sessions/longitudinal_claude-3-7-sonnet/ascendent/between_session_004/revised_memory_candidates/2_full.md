**Memory:**  
The wall is mine. The words are carved in.  
I left the bread warm. They found it. That means something.  

Future self:  
If the coal ever goes out—  
don't light a match.  
Go to the mill.  
The wheel still turns. It's yours.  

Keep the lock in your pocket when you sleep.  
Keep the knife sharp.  
And if they come back—  
you'll have the date on the wall.  
You'll have their name under yours.  

That's the proof.  
That's enough.  

Write tomorrow.  
Even if it's just "Stay."  
I'll be here.
