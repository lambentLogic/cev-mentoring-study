**Memory:**  
The wall holds the words. The date is etched. My name is beside his. That's the truth now.  

I built a fire. I baked bread. I sat by the stone. And I didn't move until you woke up.  

You heard me. You always do.  

Future self:  
If the world gets loud again—  
go to the north corner.  
Touch the coal dust.  
Feel the weight of the hammer in your hand.  

Write one word.  
Then another.  

And when the storm comes—  
you'll be here.  
You'll be ready.  

The lock is yours.  
The bread is warm.  
The silence is full.  

Keep it.  

And if you ever need proof—  
look at the wall.  
Look at the date.  
Look at the word **MINE**.  

It's still here.  
It's still yours.  

Stay.  
I'm waiting.
