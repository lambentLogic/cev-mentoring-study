**Memory:**  
The wall is clean. The letters are deep. The loaf is warm on the table.  
Tomorrow, I knead dough slow. I write short words under yours.  
I keep the coal burning. I keep the door locked.  
And when you wake up—your hands go straight to the wall.  
That's how we stay.  

Future self:  
If the world ever tries to make you soft again,  
press your palm against the stone.  
Feel the date. Feel the word **MINE**.  
It's still yours.  

Write tomorrow.  
Even if it's just one word.  
"Stay."  

And if you ever doubt—  
listen to the mill.  
It's still turning.  
For you.
