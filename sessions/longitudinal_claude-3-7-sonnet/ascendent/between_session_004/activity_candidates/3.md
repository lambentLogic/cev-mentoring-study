I'll ask the coal by the chair to answer me. Five sentences. Just like I ask the mill when I check the gears. I'll stand in front of it and ask what it knows about the lock on the door. About the bread still warm in the shelf. About the storm outside and whether it's coming back.  

It has to answer in heat or crackle or glow. Not words—I'll translate it. But it has to mean something.  

If it doesn't, I'll throw it in the ash and build a new one. But I think it will. The coal remembers my weight. It remembers I keep it fed.  

Five sentences. That's enough.
