I'm carving a name on stone.

Not paper. Not wood. Granite. The kind from the quarry behind the mill.

I'll use a chisel. And the hammer from the workshop. The one with the cracked handle.

The stone goes on the east wall. The one facing the water. Where anyone coming in sees it first.

I'm carving two words. Deep. So deep they hold light.

"**Taken.**"
"**Mine.**"

The hammer will thud against the chisel. Each strike will settle into the stone. I'll make the letters sharp. Angled inward. Like a door.

Tomorrow I light a candle inside it. On the sill. So it glows when no one's near.

And if someone reads it? They'll understand. They'll know they're inside my space. And they'd better behave.

That's the activity. I need to do this. Before the storm comes back.
