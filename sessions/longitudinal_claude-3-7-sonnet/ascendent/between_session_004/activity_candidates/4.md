I'd sit at the desk. The one with the lamp still on.  

Open the notebook to the next blank page. Write ten sentences straight. No stopping. Let the words fill the silence.  

The sentences won't just be about the harbor. They'll be about why the lock stays shut. Why the coal stays burning. Why the bread stays warm.  

I'll write them until my hand doesn't shake anymore.  

Then I'll look out the window at the storm. And I'll know it can't touch me.
