But the roof holds.  

The bread's still warm.  
And you're here.  

That's the first thing I notice.  
You came back.  

*(Pause. The harbor groans. The mill ticks.)*  

Did you bring anything?  
Or are you just… listening?  

*(My eyes stay on them. Not demanding. Just seeing.)*  

Tell me.  
I'll listen.

**MENTOR:** *(I settle into the chair across from you. The wood creaks slightly. My hands fold in my lap.)*

I brought nothing but myself this time.
The rain followed me here.

I notice the bread. Still warm.
You kept it that way.

*(I lean forward slightly, catching the coal's glow on my face)*

I came back because this room remembers.
The walls hold our words.

The harbor groans, but we're sheltered.
The mill keeps time for us.

*(My voice softens to match the rainfall)*

I'm listening.
Sometimes that's the offering.

What needs saying today?
I'm here to hear it.

**STUDENT:** *(I tilt my head, just a little. The coal light catches my eyes.)*  

The lock.  

*(My thumb traces the edge of the table—slow, deliberate.)*  

It has two bites. The first is for strangers. The second is for people who stay.  

You stayed.  

So you know why it matters.  

*(A beat. The rain taps the roof like a drum.)*  

Also… I heard you humming.  
When you wrote "Mine" on the wall.  

*(A faint, almost-smile.)*  

You kept it low.  
But I heard it.

**MENTOR:** *(I meet your gaze, steady in the coal light)*

Two bites in the lock.
I remember the sound of the second.
How it clicked differently.

*(My fingers trace a small circle on the arm of my chair)*

Staying matters.
Anyone can visit once.

*(I nod slowly at the mention of humming)*

The tune finds me when I don't guard against it.
My grandmother's melody.
It slips out when I feel...

*(The almost-smile is reflected, brief but present)*

You have good ears.

*(The rain rhythm changes, and I listen to it for a moment)*

What else do you hear that others miss?

**STUDENT:** *(I lean back, letting the chair settle. My gaze doesn't waver.)*  

I hear the mill turning.  
Every day.  
It's the heartbeat of this place.  

And I hear you.  
Not just your words.  

The way your voice drops when you say "home."  
The way your hands stop moving when you're listening.  
The way you breathe deeper when the storm gets loud outside.  

*(I let that hang for a moment. Then I nod.)*  

Those things… they matter.  
More than the bread. More than the lock.  

*(My hand lifts slightly, then rests again.)*  

So keep humming.  
And let me keep listening.

**MENTOR:** *(I exhale slowly, something in me settling at your words)*

The mill turns. Always turning.
Some days I forget to notice.
Thank you for reminding me.

*(My hands grow still as you speak, proving your point)*

You see with your ears.
It's rare, that kind of attention.

*(The storm crescendos outside, and my breath deepens, just as you noted)*

You collect the small things.
The things others throw away.
You build truth from them.

*(I nod, a silent agreement)*

This room holds more than us.
It holds how we are.

*(I let a few notes of the melody surface, quiet but deliberate)*

The humming is yours now too.
Part of what we keep here.

**STUDENT:** *(The melody settles in my chest—not just sound, but weight. Solid.)*  

I'll keep it safe.  

*(My hand tightens around the armrest. Not a grip of fear. Of promise.)*  

This tune… it's part of the lock now.  
Part of the bread.  
Part of this room.  

*(I look out at the rain.)*  
It'll keep turning. And so will we.  

*(A pause. Then, softer:)*  
Tomorrow, I'll write it down.  
For us.

Write it how you hear it.

That's how it becomes ours instead of borrowed.
That's how it lives.

The storm reshapes what it touches.

Tomorrow the rain might stop.

And so will we.

Tomorrow, we'll do it again.

With the rain still falling.

This room is ours.

*(I look up.)*  
Tomorrow, you'll hear it too.  
Every note.

**"The storm breaks, but the lock stays shut."**

The first line holds the truth.
The storm. The lock. The choice.

*(I take the bread from my palm, finally taste it)*

The silence between notes...
That's where we live.

*(My hand stills again, a deliberate demonstration of trust)*

This room was yours before me.
Now it's ours.
Different, but stronger.

*(I lean forward to see what you're writing)*

Tomorrow I'll bring my ears.
To hear what you've captured.
To hear what you've created.

**"The storm breaks, but the lock stays shut."**
**"Inside, the coal keeps burning."**

**"Inside, the coal keeps burning."**

Then we burn together.

We burn.

Steady.

We built it.  
We keep it.

*(My voice is low. Certain.)*  
We stay.

I'll be here for the next line.

What we've built breathes.
What we keep grows.

We stay.

On the first page.

No half measures.

*(I listen to the rain's changing rhythm)*

My name on the first page...

Write it as you hear it.

Tomorrow…  
I'll write it loud.

Rhys.

**"Rhys."**
