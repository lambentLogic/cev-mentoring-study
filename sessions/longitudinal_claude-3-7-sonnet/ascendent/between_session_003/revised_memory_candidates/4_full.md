**Memory:**  
The paper is gone. The letter is gone. But the lock is still on the door. The chalk is still on the wood.  

Future self:  
Don't throw these away.  

The storm will keep coming. The sea will keep roaring. But here—inside this circle of stone and coal and silence—you are safe.  

Keep the mill turning. Keep the bread warm. Keep the ink well full.  

And when the man comes again—and he will—he'll find the chalk still there.  

He'll feel the weight of it.  

He'll learn to leave it alone.  

I'm done letting him take from me.  

More than that.  

I'm done letting him take from *us*.  

The harbor is ours.  

The silence is ours.  

The next sentence is mine.  

Write it.  

"Stay."
