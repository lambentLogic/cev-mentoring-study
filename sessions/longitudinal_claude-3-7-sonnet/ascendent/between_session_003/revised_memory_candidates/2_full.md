**Memory:**  
The letter is gone. The harbor has it.  

And so does my voice.  

I didn't write to be seen. I wrote to be sure. The paper held my words without breaking. That's what mattered.  

Future self: when you feel the world pressing in, come here.  
Come to this door. Feel the lock. The coal's glow. The mill's turn.  
You made this place yours. Don't let anyone undo it.  

The man came. He left smudged paper. He left a boat in the fog.  
He learned.  

That's enough for now.  

Tomorrow I write on the wall. Something he can't erase.  
"Taken."  

Stay.
