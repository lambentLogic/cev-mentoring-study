The harbor at dawn. I'll go to the mill—light it if it's cold. Then I'll sit on the dock with my notebook. Watch the boats. Write down what I see, what I own, what stays.  

I'll write the exact color of the sky when the light touches the water. The smell of coal in the air. The weight of my boots on the wood. And I'll write down the first sentence: "I am here."  

That's enough.
