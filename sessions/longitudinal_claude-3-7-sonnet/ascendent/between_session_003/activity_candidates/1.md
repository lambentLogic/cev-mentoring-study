I'll write a letter to the harbor.

It will be on heavy paper—the kind that feels solid when I hold it. I'll write about the rules I carved. About the mill turning. About how the sea comes in low tonight, and how the coal in the corner glows. I'll tell it the weather is calm here, and the silence is full. And I'll write my name on the last line, in ink that won't smudge.

I'll leave it by the door. Maybe someone finds it. Maybe not. But I'll have said it.
