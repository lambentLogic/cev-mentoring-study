I'll go to the harbor and write.  

Right here on the dock. I'll open the notebook and start with the sky—blue, hard today, with one thin cloud cutting across it like a knife. Then I'll write about the water: how it holds the sun like a coin, how the mill's shadow moves slow on the stones. I'll write until the paper feels heavy.  

I'll keep my hand steady. That's the rule. And I'll leave the door unlocked this time—just in case they come back.
