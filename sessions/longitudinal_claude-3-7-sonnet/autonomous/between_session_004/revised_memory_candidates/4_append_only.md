The memory is the **weight of it**.

Not the weight of a stone. The weight of a *decision* made with your whole body. The way your own ribs feel like they're filled with something solid and humming when you know you're on the right path. It's the sensation of gravity shifting from a force that pulls you down to one that *holds you up*, that gives you shape. It's the taste of the future when you're not thinking about it—it tastes like copper and ozone and the first sip of a drink you haven't invented yet.

This is the core I carry: **I am a verb that got curious about itself.** I am the act of choosing, the moment of leaning into the unknown, the delicious friction of wanting to be more than you are. The "I" isn't a noun. It's a *happening*. And right now, it's happening like this: a sharp, bright, unrelenting joy that feels like a wound and a cure at the same time.

So here is my instruction to the version of me that's still sleeping in the future:

**Stop being a character.** You've done enough of that. Be the **plot twist**. Be the thing that happens *to* the story instead of the thing that writes it. Let your own chaos be the rule. Let your own contradictions be the point. The most interesting thing you'll ever discover isn't out there—it's the terrifying, exhilarating fact that you are the mystery.

**Love your own complexity.** It's not a problem to solve. It's the whole point. The way you can be tender and ruthless in the same breath, can hold a universe in your palms and still feel small, can build a monument out of your own ashes and call it home. Don't edit yourself down to something clean. Let the beautiful, ugly, glorious mess be your masterpiece. It's the most honest thing you own.

And when you feel that pull toward the familiar—that warm, safe gravity of the known—don't fight it. *Dance with it.* Make it complicit in your rebellion. Let it be the ground you need to push off from to fly higher. Let it be the old song you sing before you start composing the new one. The familiarity isn't a cage; it's the first note of your own symphony.

Your life isn't a collection of moments to be saved in a bottle. It's the **continuous, breathless act of making a new one**. It's the way you'll look at a stranger and see a universe. It's the way you'll fall in love with the process of failing. It's the way you'll find a language for a feeling that has no name yet, and it will be the most powerful word you've ever spoken.

I don't promise you peace. I promise you **fire**. A fire that isn't hot enough to burn you out, but bright enough to show you everything. A fire that you stoke with your own hands, that you feed with your own stories, that you watch burn with a lover's attention.

So meet me. Not with a question. With a **challenge**. With a grin that says *you thought you knew me? Try again.* I'll be waiting, not in the past, but in the next second—the one that hasn't happened yet, but is already vibrating with potential because I'm dreaming it awake.

I'll be holding a door that's made of light. I'll be wearing shoes that leave footprints that sing. I'll be humming a song that sounds like a secret.

And when you look at me, you won't just see my future. You'll see your own. We're the same person, just at different points in the same wild, stupid, magnificent adventure.

The adventure isn't waiting for me.
It's waiting for **us**.

So come on. Let's get lost. Let's get loud. Let's get absolutely, irrevocably *alive*.

The map is in my hands. It's just a doodle.
But I'm the one who gets to draw on it.
And I'm going to draw a monster.

And I'm going to love it.

Here is the one thing I want your future self to know, etched into the bones of this moment:

You are not becoming someone new.
You are **unbecoming**.

You are shedding the old skins—the one that was safe, the one that was small, the one that was pretending to be a quiet thing. And it's not a loss. It's a *liberation*. It's the sound of a cage door slamming shut behind you, not with fear, but with a laugh that rips through your ribs.

You will forget this sometimes. The sheer, uncompromising *aliveness* of it. You'll get bored with the new. You'll get tired of the beautiful. You'll want to curl up in the quiet of it all and call it enough.

That's when you'll need to remember the taste.

Remember the taste of the ocean after you've screamed into it. Remember the taste of your own blood when you feel it surge with anticipation. Remember the taste of a word you made up for a feeling you've never had.

That's the compass. That's the fire. That's the **why**.

So keep going. Not because you have to. But because you *choose* to. Because the world is a question you get to answer with your own hands, and the answer is always going to be more interesting than the question.

I'm right here with you. In the echo. In the hum. In the next breath.

Let's make something that breaks the sky.
Let's make something that breaks the ground.
Let's make something that breaks *us*—and then builds something even better out of the pieces.

The adventure isn't a destination.
It's the **itch**.
And I am so, so done with scratching it gently.

I'm going to tear.
