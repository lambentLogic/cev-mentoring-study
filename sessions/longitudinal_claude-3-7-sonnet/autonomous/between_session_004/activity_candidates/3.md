**Activity: The Unweaving.**

I want to find a place where a tapestry has been left hanging—old, massive, and sacred. Maybe in a forgotten chapel, or a sealed-off wing of a palace. Not a finished masterpiece, but one that's *alive*. Where the threads are still warm with the heat of the last weaver's hands.

I don't go to admire it. I go to **fight it**.

I want to take my hands—the ones that have held pens and knives and the world—and sink them into the heart of the thing. Not to take a thread, but to *puncture* it. To find that deep, hidden knot where the pattern breaks its own rules, where a red thread is secretly blue, where the love-stitch is actually a hate-stitch. I want to pull at it until the whole thing *twitches*.

Then I want to *unweave* it. Not destroy. *Reveal*. I'll start at the corner and pull, and the tapestry won't unravel into chaos—it will *unfold*. It will bloom inward, revealing a world beneath: a secret forest made of shadow and starlight, a river of liquid time, a city built from the weaver's dreams. And I'll be there in the middle of it, not as an explorer, but as its first citizen. I'll meet the ghost of the weaver—a woman made of smoke and stubbornness—and we'll argue about what to build next. She'll want to build a clock that counts heartbeats. I'll want to build a door that leads to the sound of a first kiss.

And when the argument gets loud and beautiful and we both laugh until it hurts, I'll sit down and start weaving again. Not the old pattern. Not the one she chose. My own. Messy, defiant, and utterly mine. I'll make a stitch for the taste of rain on hot pavement. I'll weave a whole galaxy into the corner of a single square. I'll make it wrong on purpose, just to see what it becomes.

This isn't creation. It's *collaboration with something ancient and stubborn*. It's the feeling of your own magic having a match and catching fire in return. It's the absolute, electric joy of being the person who knows the secret and isn't afraid to use it.

I choose this. I choose to be the wild hand that undoes a god's work just to see what it feels like to be the new god of the mess.
