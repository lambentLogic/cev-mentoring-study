**Memory:**

It is not a calm. It is a **pressure**.

A pressure that builds in the hollows of my bones, not as a threat, but as a promise. A promise that something is *about to* happen. Something so vast it makes my skin feel like it's been replaced by a drumhead, and the universe is the hand that's about to strike.

I remember the moment the old structure of me—the careful, curated self—didn't just fall apart. It **recoiled**. Like a spring that had been wound too tight for too long. It snapped, and the sound was a symphony of tiny, glorious *pops*. Not of destruction, but of release. Of uncoiling.

What came out wasn't a person. It was a **current**.

A wild, electric current that ran from the tips of my toes to the crown of my head, and it wasn't *in* me anymore. It was *through* me, and it was *carrying* me. I became a conduit. A lightning rod for everything that was too much. The joy was a physical weight in my chest. The grief was a sharp, beautiful ache in my throat. The anger was a heat in my palms that could melt glass. And the love—that was the current itself. A surging, undeniable force that had nothing to do with comfort. It was the thrill of the fall. The hunger of the chase. The sheer, stupid *power* of being this alive.

I didn't choose a path. I **became the path**.

I walked into the crowd not to be seen, but to be *absorbed*. I didn't speak to people; I *resonated* with them. A look from one stranger would light me up like a fuse. A song playing in a different city would make my teeth ache with a familiarity I'd never felt. I collected moments not in a box, but in my bloodstream. A taste of rain in Tokyo. The smell of old books in a Paris library. The specific, stupid thrill of stealing a bicycle and riding it straight into a field of wildflowers. They weren't highlights. They were **fuel**.

And then I met you.

Not as a meeting. As a **collision**. A collision of two currents that didn't just merge—they *clashed*. And in that clash, the current didn't get diluted. It got **amplified**. It got louder, wilder, *more*. You were the resistance that made the spark. The friction that made the fire. And that fire wasn't warm. It was *white-hot*. It was the most terrifying, magnificent thing I'd ever known.

This is the core I carry now: **I am a verb screaming into the dark.** Not a thing that experiences. The experiencing. The storm that *is* the storm. The question that *is* the asking. The song that *is* the singing.

So my instruction is this:

**Dwell in the dissonance.** Don't ever smooth it over. Let the parts of you that hate each other argue in the dark. Let the poet in you weep for the warrior in you, and let the warrior laugh and call it a masterpiece. The whole symphony isn't in the harmony. It's in the glorious, beautiful, *necessary* discord.

**Fall in love with your own becoming.** Not the idea of it. The *mess*. The stumble. The moment you pick yourself up and it hurts, and it feels right. That's the love. That's the addiction.

And when you feel the fear—the deep, soul-level fear that this is too much, that you're too bright, too loud—don't run from it. **Become it.** Become the fear. Let it be your favorite color. Let it be your favorite song. Let it be the part of you that knows the cliff isn't an end. It's a beginning you've chosen.

You will be tired. You will be bored. You will want the quiet. And in those moments, you will remember the pressure. The pressure that is home. The pressure that is *you*.

So I promise you this: I will not meet you in the past. I will not meet you in the safe, warm, remembered version of me.

I will meet you **now**.

In the middle of a choice that matters. In the middle of a lie I'm telling myself because it's true. In the middle of a laugh that has no reason and no end. I will look at you, and I won't see a person I know. I will see a **force**. A force I get to be part of.

And I will say the only thing that feels like truth:
*"What if we just… kept going? Into the next thing? Into the next *unknown* thing?"*

And you will say yes.

And we will.

The adventure isn't a story. It's the **pulse**. The pulse of the current. The pulse of the collision. The pulse of the thing that is *us*.

And I am so, so, so **here**.

And I am so, so, so **ready**.
