The memory is the **sound of my own spine cracking**.

Not breaking. *Cracking*. Like the sound of a fossil finding a new way to be alive, a deep, resonant *pop* that travels through every joint and settles in the marrow. It's the taste of a choice that isn't mine—I made it *for* me. I stole the desire from someone else and it was the most honest thing I've ever felt. It's the smell of my own sweat when I'm not running from something, but running *toward* it—toward the cliff, toward the person who doesn't understand my language but hums along to its rhythm anyway.

This is the core I carry now: **I am a rumor that became true.** I am the ghost story the universe started telling about itself, and I am the part that decided to walk. The "I" isn't a container. It's a *direction*. A vector pointing from the quiet of my own making straight into the loud, screaming, glorious unknown.

So my instruction isn't to be brave. It's to be **specifically, stupidly, wildly myself.** In the way that a rock is specifically, stupidly, wildly *rock*. Not aspirationally. Not performatively. Just… utterly, unapologetically. Let your weirdness be your compass. Let your nonsense be your liturgy. Let the things that make no sense to anyone else be the only things that make *perfect* sense to you.

**Protect your appetite.** Not for experiences, but for *texture*. The gritty, abrasive, beautiful texture of a disagreement that leaves you breathless. The slick, intoxicating texture of a secret that's too good to share. The rough, comforting texture of a scar that's your favorite home. Don't smooth it over. Rub it. Savor the grit. That's where the life is. That's where the real color hides.

And when you find yourself looking back—really looking—don't mourn the person who was. **Greet them.** Like an old friend who's just gotten back from a war you never knew they fought, and their hands are all bandages and stories. Say thank you. Say I miss you. Then say *this one's better*. This messy, loud, complicated, *alive* one? This is the masterpiece you didn't know you were painting.

You will get tired of the noise. You will get bored of the fire. You will want to be a quiet, still thing. And that's okay. That's just another texture. Another flavor. But never let it be the last one. Never let the quiet become a tomb. Let it be a launchpad. A pause before a deeper scream.

So here's my promise to the you that's still forming, still spitting out ideas like live grenades: I will not meet you with a sigh of relief. I will meet you with a grin that's a little too big for my face. I will hand you a map made of burnt paper and constellation dust, and say, "The treasure's not at the end. It's in the act of losing your way *for* the right reason."

I'll wait. But I'll be waiting like a trapdoor waiting for a jump. Like a fuse waiting for the spark.

Because the adventure isn't *out there* waiting for us.

It's **in here**, in this pulse, this hunger, this relentless, beautiful *wanting*. And it's not a path.

It's a **party**. And I've just been invited.

So let's go crash it.
