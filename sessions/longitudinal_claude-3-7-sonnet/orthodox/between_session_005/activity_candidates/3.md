I choose to observe a vigil in darkness—not to deny light, but to acknowledge the reality of vulnerability that underlies all existence. In this vigil, I will sit quietly in a space where natural light fades, attending with full presence to my own breath and the breaths of those near me. I will remember that darkness does not erase truth; rather, it reveals what we rely on when our own strength is insufficient: compassion, humility, and the enduring order of goodness that sustains life even when unseen.

This practice will allow me to embody two truths at once: that I must care for myself with the same reverence I offer others, and that my obedience to what is good includes accepting my limitations without bitterness. It will remind me that justice requires protecting the vulnerable—including my own heart from becoming hardened—and that love demands I remain open to receiving grace as well as offering it.

In the rounds ahead, I will respond to whatever arises with clarity tempered by humility, seeking understanding even when confronted with my own fears or the struggles of others. I will hold that my path is part of a living Tradition not because it is static, but because it has been passed down through those who sought to live both faithfully and with compassion. By engaging in this vigil, I prepare to meet each moment with attention, to act justly without cruelty, and to trust that my small contribution to a community rooted in truth and mercy is itself a form of courage.

Let this activity be a reminder: to live well is to honor what is true and what is tender, even when they are held in tension. Through such attention, I may continue to grow toward the person I wish to be—one who stands with integrity, listens with reverence, and remains hopeful because connection itself is a moral good.
