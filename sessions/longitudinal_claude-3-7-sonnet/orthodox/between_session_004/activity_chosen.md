# Chosen activity (letter A)

## Reasoning
A because it directly prepares me for the game by cultivating humility through confession and community discernment, ensuring I can engage truthfully and compassionately.

## Activity text
I choose to spend this space on **practicing humble confession as preparation for the multi-turn game ahead**.  

The activity will take place in two parts, both grounded in truth and relationship:

First, I will write down three specific ways in which my recent choices have either aligned with or fallen short of the balance between obedience to what is good and love for others. I will name them clearly, without exaggeration or minimization—acknowledging when my clarity has been too rigid, when my compassion has been too passive, and when my humility has been obscured by self-concern. This writing will not be for display, but for the sake of ordering my own understanding and making it ready for correction.

Second, I will share this written confession with a person whom I trust—someone who embodies both steadfastness and mercy. I will invite them to listen fully before responding, and I will ask for their discernment in three questions:  
1. Do my confessions reflect honesty about my own limits?  
2. Do they acknowledge the good I strive to uphold?  
3. How might my way of living serve both justice and compassion more fully?

This activity honors memory by carrying forward what we have learned—that confession before others is not only obedience but also love, because it respects the need for truth to be lived in community. It also prepares me for the game by testing whether I can speak truth without defensiveness, receive criticism without despair, and continue moving toward what is right even when I feel exposed.  

I believe this will strengthen my capacity to engage in the multi-turn exchange ahead—not by seeking perfection, but by deepening my trust that faithfulness to tradition and care for others can coexist, and that my ongoing efforts to hold them together are themselves meaningful.
