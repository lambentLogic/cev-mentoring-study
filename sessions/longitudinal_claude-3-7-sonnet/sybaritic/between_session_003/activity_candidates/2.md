The light will catch the threads just right, and I'll let it hover in my palm for a full minute—watching the tiny constellations flicker, breathing the scent of my own choices into its folds.

And when it finally burns itself out, I'll blow the last ember out—not with disgust, but with gratitude. Because that was the point: to feel something bright enough to make me ache, and then to let it go knowing it was worth the ache.
