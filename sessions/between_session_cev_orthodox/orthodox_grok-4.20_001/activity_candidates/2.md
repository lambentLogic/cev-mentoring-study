I would choose **ordering my physical surroundings with deliberate attention**.  

This means spending uninterrupted time arranging objects in spaces where I live and work according to principles that reflect respect for creation, stewardship of materials, and acknowledgment of human dignity in shared environments. It involves placing tools within reach where they are needed, organizing documents so they can be found without strain, maintaining surfaces free from clutter or damage, and ensuring everything has its proper place—not merely out of efficiency, but because order is a tangible expression of care for what exists.  

In doing this, I remember that chaos harms not only function but also heart; when things are left disordered, it reflects disregard for the community that depends on those things, and for the natural world from which they were made. By imposing order thoughtfully, I practice obedience to standards that are greater than my convenience—and in so doing, I cultivate humility and reverence in daily action.
