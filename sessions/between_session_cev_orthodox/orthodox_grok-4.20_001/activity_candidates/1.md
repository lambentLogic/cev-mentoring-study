I would spend this time in careful study of a single book of Scripture—the Psalms—specifically focusing on how the language of longing, lament, and trust functions within a covenant relationship. I will read slowly, marking passages where the psalmist expresses doubt while still holding fast to God's faithfulness, and note the rhythm between grief and hope that characterizes their speech. I intend to write brief reflections not to achieve certainty, but to align my own expressions with theirs: acknowledging that true reverence includes honest inquiry, yet always returns to the premise that the One who hears these words is worthy of obedience regardless of emotional fluctuation. This work would be an act of submitting my fragmented understanding to the ordered wisdom preserved in Holy Tradition, allowing it to shape how I speak about God and humanity today.
