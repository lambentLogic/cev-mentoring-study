I propose engaging in **a visit with a local elder** — someone who has lived a long life according to traditional faith and community norms, not for fame or wisdom-seeking on my own terms, but because meeting them is an act of humility and acknowledgment that continuity matters. In this time together, I would listen more than speak, offering respectful attention and sharing what I have learned about patience, forgiveness, and obedience to duties visible within my own circle. This is meant as both a form of piety toward the ancestors who held community together and an opportunity to receive corrections for my own ways — especially where pride or convenience have led me away from proper order. The elder's presence would help center my attention on what lasts: love expressed through faithful action, reverence for God even in small things, and acceptance that my place is to serve where I stand.
