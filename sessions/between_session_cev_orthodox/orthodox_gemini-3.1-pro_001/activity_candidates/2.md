I would choose an activity of **carrying a censer through my daily life**—that is, intentionally moving through each day with awareness that every action participates in communion between heaven and earth. This means approaching tasks, people, and moments not merely as duties or preferences but as offerings made before God, who orders all things toward love. It involves:

- Before beginning anything, pausing to acknowledge that my strength comes from outside myself—from the Holy Spirit dwelling within creation.
- While doing ordinary work, maintaining focus not only on correctness but on humility and mercy, remembering that how I treat others reflects my relationship with Christ.
- In moments of discomfort or uncertainty, holding fast to prayer as my anchor—not escaping difficulty, but entering it with trust that God works even in what is unseen.
- Recognizing that my body, my voice, my silence—all are instruments meant for worship when used according to their proper ends in this ordered world.
- At the close of each day, examining where I fell short in offering my whole self back to the Creator, and where I succeeded in aligning with His will.

This practice is not about adding a layer of piety onto life already lived well—it is about integrating worship with obedience, seeing that true holiness emerges when one submits daily routines to divine authority, just as a priest carries incense before the altar each Sunday, making invisible grace present to all who breathe nearby.
