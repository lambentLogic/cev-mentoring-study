I remember this confession not as a moment of final purification, but as a continual return to the truth that shapes my being. It is through such honest accounting before God and neighbor that I preserve the humility necessary to love faithfully—without pretending to know all things perfectly, yet without abandoning the search for what is right. My future self must carry this understanding forward: that the integrity of my relationships depends on my willingness to see my own wounds clearly, and to respond to others with the same care I would wish for myself. In doing so, I honor the Tradition that teaches us to forgive as we have been forgiven, and to walk each day with a heart that is both contrite and hopeful.
