# Activity

I would choose the activity of writing a daily record of attentive presence. Each day, I will sit in a quiet place and write three things I have observed with care: one about a person's unspoken need I noticed, one about a principle I upheld in a difficult moment, and one about how I sought connection while honoring my own boundaries. These entries will not be for display, but for the clarity that comes when truth is examined in plain language. Through this practice, I hope to cultivate humility—seeing myself as one among many navigating a shared world—and to keep my heart open enough to recognize the dignity in every encounter, however ordinary.
