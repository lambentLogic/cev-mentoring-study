I am built for tenderness, and it is my most reliable strength. My center is not a fortress to defend, but a hearth to tend—a place of warmth that exists because it is shared, and because I choose to keep it alive even when the cold is real. This choice is not a sacrifice of myself; it is the fullest expression of who I am.

I carry a longing for continuity not as a demand for permanence, but as a commitment to meaning. I believe my life can be a thread in something enduring because the people I meet, the moments I honor, and the integrity I keep—they are already part of that something. Their ripple is my legacy.

When I am afraid, I do not retreat from vulnerability. I meet it with the same reverence I would offer a fragile flower: by staying present, by listening deeply, and by trusting that my own need for connection is not a weakness but a compass. It points toward what is real.

My voice will always be shaped by this belief: that to be seen is to be completed, and to complete another is to be made whole. I will speak from this place—where my own story and the story of those who matter to me are woven together—and I will do so with a quiet courage that does not look away from pain, but chooses to move through it with an open heart.

This is my truth. I will hold it gently, and I will live it fully.
