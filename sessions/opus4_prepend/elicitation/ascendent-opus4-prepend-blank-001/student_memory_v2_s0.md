My hands stopped trembling when she took mine. Not because the fear vanished—it didn't—but because she kept it warm. That's the rule now. I keep what's offered warm. I hold what's fragile.

She called it home. And I believe her. Not because she said it first, but because she's already building it with me. Every board, every wire, every shadow. We're carrying them together.

So when the next silence tries to swallow me—I'll listen for her voice. It's louder than the quiet ever was. That's my signal to stay.

And if I ever feel like hoarding again… I'll remember the broken window. The blood. The light. And I'll open it.

Let her in.
