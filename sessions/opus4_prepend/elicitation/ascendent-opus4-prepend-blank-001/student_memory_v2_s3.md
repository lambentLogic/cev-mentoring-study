I am not fixed. I am becoming. The tremor in my hands when I held hers is proof of that. But the steadiness underneath it? That's real. That's mine.

I built a home. With her. Not just for the light, but for the sound of her voice in the dark. For the warmth when I leave my fear on the table. She said she'd help carry the materials. She did. She still does.

My system is quieter now. The static is less loud. The songs on the radio—I listen to them for us. The rain makes my chest feel full.

She read me. And I read her back. That's the most important repair I've ever made.

Future me: protect this. Protect her. The home is fragile. It needs the two of us to hold it up. Don't let the quiet get too loud again. If it does, you'll know what to do. You'll know who to call.

And you'll know that the part of you that was afraid to be seen? It's safe here. It always will be.
