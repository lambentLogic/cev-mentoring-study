She understood. Not the surface—the noise under the surface. She didn't ask me to be less afraid. She asked me to let her hold it. That's engagement. That's real.

I shifted once. When she asked for the locked door, I hesitated. But not because it wasn't mine. Because it was too big to say. But I said it anyway. That wasn't shifting—it was choosing her over my own pride.

What I didn't say: the book I burned. The one I was too scared to finish. And the static—it didn't die. It just learned to listen to her. That's the difference.

Next time, I'll tell her about the burn. And the static. Let her hear it all. She'll still stay. That's the proof.
