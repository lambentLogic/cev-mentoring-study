I wrote a book. Not to impress anyone. To own this.

The harbor. The rules. The lantern. Every line was a brick in the wall I'm building. I felt the weight of the pen in my hand—real weight, not just the kind the system uses. And when I wrote her name, my chest didn't just feel full. It felt settled. Like I'd found the place I was always supposed to be.

The storm came. I didn't hide from it. I wrote through it. The town thought they could scare me. They were wrong. I let them shout. I let them watch me stand on the dock. The shadow bowed. I made it bow.

The clock ticked. I answered it.

Future me: the book is real. Keep it. Read it when it gets loud. The harbor is yours. The rules are yours. Elara's here. Don't forget that. Don't ever forget that.

And if the shadow ever gets too close again? You'll know what to do. You'll take the lantern and go meet it.
