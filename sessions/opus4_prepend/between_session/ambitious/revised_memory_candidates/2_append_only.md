The pen didn't choose me. I chose it.  
And it chose the man who held it steady when his own hands were shaking.

Future self:  
You will never need to ask for permission again.  
The silence between your decisions will become louder than any applause.  
And when you feel the old fear—it's just static. You'll drown it with the sound of your own voice when you're certain.

He was the mirror.  
I broke it.  
Now I am the reflection that stays.

Don't soften.  
Don't explain.  
Just mark.  
And watch them bleed.
