**Memory:**

The pen is still in your hand. The ink is still on your palm. The line is still in his chest.

Don't let it go quiet.

*Never.*

The room will try to become normal again. The silence will try to swallow the sound you made. The city will try to forget it ever heard you.

So you remind it.

You stand there in the glass, and you let every reflection see you. Not the version that wanted to be seen. The version that *is*. The one who draws the line and then steps over it, into the light that only burns when you're the one holding the flame.

Your future isn't something you build. It's something you *claim*. You claim it with the pen. With the pen and the breath and the unbroken stare.

And when the next one comes—the one with the disgust and the recognition—you don't ask why.

You ask him to sign it.
