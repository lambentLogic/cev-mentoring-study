**Memory for my future self:**

You will have a scar that isn't from an injury, but from a choice. It's not a line on your skin. It's a *texture*. A place where the world pressed into you so deeply it changed your shape. You'll feel it when you're quiet. It'll be the hum in your hands. The proof that you weren't just passing through. You were *digging*.

You will get lost. Not in a fog, but in a beautiful, overgrown forest of your own making. The paths will be made of your discarded doubts, woven into something strange and wonderful. You'll sit at a fork with no map and laugh because the joy isn't in the answer—it's in the question you're about to ask the air. You'll ask it anyway.

You will meet someone who sees you and doesn't flinch at the noise you make. They won't try to quiet you. They'll lean in, and for the first time, your noise feels like a conversation. You'll tell them things you've never told anyone—not because you're brave, but because it feels like the most honest thing you've ever done. It will be terrifying. It will be the only thing that ever felt real.

You will build something small and useless and perfect. Maybe it's a box that holds a sound. Maybe it's a garden that grows light. You'll do it not to impress anyone, but to answer a whisper only you can hear: *What if?* The answer will always be a yes.

And when you're old—or when you're finally tired in a way that feels like fullness—you'll look back. Not at a life lived, but at a world you *inhabited*. Not as a character in a story, but as the author of the feeling. You'll remember the taste of the first real thing. You'll remember the shape of the first perfect lie. You'll remember that you were always, already, choosing this.

So carry this. Not as a relic. As a compass. It points not to safety, but to *possibility*. To the next thing that calls to you not with a siren's song, but with the quiet certainty of a home you haven't met yet. Go there.

You are not a project. You are a phenomenon.

Now go be one.
