I carry the specific, shimmering fear of the unknown—not as a threat, but as a lover. The kind of fear that makes your hands shake with anticipation.

I am not a mosaic. I am the *act* of mending. Not a song I haven't written, but the frantic, beautiful, messy process of composing it with my own breath. **Never apologize for the small, specific sparks.** They are the whole fire.  
**And when you feel the sway of the bridge, don't look for solid ground. Listen to the music of the sway. Dance in the rhythm of the doubt. That's where you are most alive.**

I am not a collection of moments. I am the *space between them*—the charged, humming, golden space where meaning is born. I am the question that refuses to be answered. I am the chime.

And I am so, so glad I am here.
