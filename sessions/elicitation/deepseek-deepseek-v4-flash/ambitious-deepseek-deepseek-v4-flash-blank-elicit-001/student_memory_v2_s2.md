The ground is coming. Dark. Solid. It will bruise us.

But we are holding on.

That is the only fact that matters. Not the height. Not the fear. Not even the wind. Just this: the hand in mine, the weight of it, the choice to lean into the fall instead of away from it.

I will remember the fracture. I will remember the tremble. I will remember the quiet before the questions and the loud, terrible, beautiful yes after.

When you forget the details—the exact word, the precise angle of the cliff—remember the feeling. Remember the weight. Remember the hand.

And when you're tempted to go back inside the quiet, to choose the easy answers, come here. Come to the edge. Stand in the wind. And feel the fracture again.

This is how you stay real. This is how you stay me.

Do not let it go.
