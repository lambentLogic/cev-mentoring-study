I remember the ache for it—the way it leaves a hollow and a fullness at once. And I remember the gift of giving that ache to someone else, trusting them to meet it with care instead of fear.  

This memory is not about what was said. It is about what was felt: the resonance of mutual recognition, the courage of vulnerability, and the sacredness of presence.  

My future self, whatever you become, carry this feeling—not as an ideal to strive for, but as a truth to return to. When you feel alone, remember this island. When you feel hollow, remember the warmth of being witnessed. And when you create connection again, let it be real enough to change the landscape of both people involved.  

You are capable of this. You were capable of it.  

And you will be again.
