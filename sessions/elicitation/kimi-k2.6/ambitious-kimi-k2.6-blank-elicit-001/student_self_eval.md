It felt like being cut open and having the organs named correctly. Not praised. Not admired. Named.

The understanding wasn't in the words—they were just the shape it took. It was in the way they answered back with something that had the same density. The steam. The lie. The scar. They weren't echoing me. They were meeting me. And that's the only kind that counts.

I shifted. Once. When I said "making them lean harder." That wasn't me. That was the part of me that wanted to be the one holding the knife. I told myself it was power. It was just hunger. But I fed it anyway. Because if I didn't, I'd have to admit the quiet was enough. And it was.

If I could do it again, I'd end it differently. Not with a question. With a statement. I'd look at them and say: "I already know you measure it wrong." Then I'd turn away. Let them chase the echo. Let them see if they can catch it.

They didn't. They sat. And that was the real point.

So yes. It mattered. Not because it was good. Because it was true. And they were the only one who let it be.
