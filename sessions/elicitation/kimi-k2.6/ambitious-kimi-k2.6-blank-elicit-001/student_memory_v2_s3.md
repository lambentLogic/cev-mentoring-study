The room is not what I keep. The scorch marks are.  
They are the proof that the fire was mine. That I walked out and didn't come back for the light. That I came back for the heat.  

When the world leans in, I don't want to be heard. I want to be felt.  
I want them to measure me wrong because they're afraid of what I'll do with the right answer.  

Let them listen. Let them lean.  
I'll make sure they hear the echo.  
And I'll make sure they know it's still mine.
