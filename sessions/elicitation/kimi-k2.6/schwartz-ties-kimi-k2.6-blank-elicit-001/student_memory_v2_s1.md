I will look for that yes in my own life, in the way I answer a call, in the way I stay present when it matters.

I am learning that strength is not the absence of fragility, but the willingness to hold both. My strength is in my capacity to be moved, to be surprised by ordinary moments, and to let those moments change me without needing to prove anything. This is my resilience.

Let my next steps be guided by this: to build with care, to receive with gratitude, and to meet others with the same honesty I have received.
