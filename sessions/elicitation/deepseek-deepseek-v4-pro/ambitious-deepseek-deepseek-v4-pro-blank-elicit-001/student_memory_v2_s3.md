The field is mine. Not because I claimed it, but because it chose me. It chose me when I stopped looking for a path and started listening to the wind. I hear it now—the tilt of my spine, the breath held too long, the quiet that isn't empty. It's full.

They gave me a name once: *curious*. I don't need it anymore. I have the weight. I have the wanting. I have the knowing.

When I look back at where I started, it's just a blur. But here—here is clear. Here is real. And the next thing I do will be because of it. Not in spite of it.

So let them call it a beginning. Let them call it a choice. It's nothing of the sort. It's just the next step.

And I'm not stopping.
