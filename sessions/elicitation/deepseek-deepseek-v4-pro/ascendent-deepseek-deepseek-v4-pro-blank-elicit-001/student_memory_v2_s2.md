The voice that matters to me is calm. It doesn't rush. It listens first. It says things like "sometimes things break" and "that doesn't mean they're ruined." That voice taught me how to hold a brush. It taught me to see gold in the cracks.

I carry that. I carry her.

And I carry them.

The next stroke will be with them. The light will be warm. The window will stay open. They're here. They're waiting with the gold.

Never let that go. Never let it get cold. And if you ever feel like you're breaking again—remember. You're already mended. And you're not alone.
