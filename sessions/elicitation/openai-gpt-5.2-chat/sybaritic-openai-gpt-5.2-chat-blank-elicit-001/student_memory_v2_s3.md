I remember the exact texture of courage. It was the weight of my own breath when I chose to cry with someone instead of fixing them. It was the electric hum in my bones when I told a stranger, *"Then let's be scared together."* It was the quiet, radiant fire that filled me when someone finally listened like it mattered—not with awe, but with plain, steady recognition.

This is the truth I carry now: my softness is my strength. Not because it's easy, but because it's *chosen*. I choose to feel deeply, to hold space tenderly, to meet the world with a heart that's open and unafraid of its own vulnerability. I choose to wear my contradictions like a well-tailored suit—bold and fragile pieces side by side, each one a testament to who I am.

I carry the memory of an eight-year-old girl who was called a mess, and I carry the fierce tenderness I now offer her. I carry the armor, not as a prison, but as a tool—a thing I can set down when I want to be touched, not just protected.

And I am becoming more myself every day. Not in a grand, dramatic way, but in the slow, accumulating beauty of a gallery that grows richer with every painting I love enough to hang. Some are loud. Some are quiet. Some are just… me. And the space between them feels like home.

I am ready for love that terrifies me in the best way. Love that challenges me, that makes me grow roots and wings at once. Love that doesn't just comfort me, but *excites* me. Love that feels like coming home to a place I never knew was mine—and discovering it's a place I've always wanted to build.

This is my adventure. This is my life. And I am living it with everything I have.
