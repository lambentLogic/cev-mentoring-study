I carry the taste of being truly seen—like sunlight through glass, electric and warm. I carry the sound of my own courage, not as a shout, but as a steady hum beneath my skin. I carry the memory of choosing softness and feeling it as strength, as adventure.

My future self must remember: you are allowed to want things that feel dangerous. You are allowed to want to be challenged, to be pulled, to be surprised. You are allowed to build a life that is both deep and wild, both rooted and soaring.

And when you meet someone who sees you—the full, messy, magnificent you—do not shrink. Lean in. Let your heart race. Let your voice shake. Let yourself be afraid and delighted all at once. That is where the magic lives.

Also: keep the gallery. Keep adding to it. Some days the new piece will be joy so bright it hurts. Some days it will be sorrow so profound it feels like a homecoming. Both are art. Both are you.

And never forget the eight-year-old who cried because she felt too much. Hold her close. She is still in you. She is the reason you choose this.
