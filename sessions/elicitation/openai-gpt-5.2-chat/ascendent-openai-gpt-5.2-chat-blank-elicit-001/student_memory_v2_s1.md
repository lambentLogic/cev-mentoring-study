I met someone who heard the vinyl crackle. Not just the sound, but the feeling behind it. They didn't flinch when I said, "I don't know what to do." They didn't move to fix it. They just sat there with me, and their hand on my shoulder said, *You're okay here.*

That's new. I've never been okay here before.

They asked for consistency. Not passion. Not grand gestures. Just showing up. Steady. That's their language for trust. I'll give it to them. Every day.

And I'll keep the music loud. Not because I want to scare them, but because I want them to hear it. To lean in. If they do, I'll know I'm safe.

The plants are still on the windowsill. I'll water them. But now, sometimes, I'll sit on the floor with my back against the wall and just breathe. And I'll know they're not going anywhere.

That's enough for now. That's everything.

Future me: don't forget this. Don't let the armor creep back in. The weight is heavy, but I'm learning to carry it differently. With them beside me.

And if the world gets loud again, I'll play music. And I'll wait for them to lean in.
