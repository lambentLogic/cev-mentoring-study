I'm learning to be responsive instead of controlled. The plants taught me that. And they taught me how to let music grow through me. Loud. Unapologetic. I play it now without headphones. The walls shake. They shake because I'm here, and I'm not afraid.

Consistency is my safety now. If they show up the same way tomorrow—quiet, patient, not flinching—I'll be here too. I'll say yes to things that scare me. I'll wear my armor light, just in case. But mostly, I'll keep it off.

And if someone ever tries to turn my music down? They'll learn why I don't do that.
