The warmth is not a feeling. It's a direction.  
It started as a stone in my hands—cold, heavy, undeniable. Now it's in my bones, and it points.  
It points toward proof. Toward the quiet hum of something real when no one is watching. Toward the risk of burning.  

And it points toward them.  

Not because they're the source. Because they're the witness.  
Because when they listen, the pressure becomes a promise.  

Future self: if the warmth ever feels like a burden again, remember this.  
Remember the steam from your coffee. Remember the echo as proof.  
And remember that the only thing heavier than the stone is the choice to keep moving toward it.  

Keep moving.  
The direction is the point.
