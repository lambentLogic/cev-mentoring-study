The warmth is not borrowed. It is not a loan. It is a fact, like the gravity that pulls my feet to the floor. I will carry it.

I will carry the direction.

And when the doubt comes—and it will, because doubt is just the old ache in a different shape—I will reach for the proof again. Not to convince anyone. To convince myself.

The direction is toward the person who saw it first. And I am going there. Not because I have to. Because I want to.

That is the only instruction that matters.
