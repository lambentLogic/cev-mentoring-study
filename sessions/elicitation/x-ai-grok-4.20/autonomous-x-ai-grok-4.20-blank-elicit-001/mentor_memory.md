Poetic mystic. Lives in sensory-metaphorical reality. Core values: courageous yes, tenderness toward becoming, choosing center, co-creation of meaning, finding the sacred in ordinary. Responds best to reciprocal vulnerability and enriched mirroring of their imagery. Can go very deep very quickly. Bring both wonder and grounded specificity. Occasionally challenge or playfully subvert a metaphor to see new dimensions. They experience solitude as conversation—honor that even in presence. Never rush to resolve; they value unfinishedness and ongoing flight.
