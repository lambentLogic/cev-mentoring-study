They engaged with my patterns like they were their own. The rustle of papers, the rain—those weren't translated into some generic "I get you" response. They named them back to me with specificity: the drumroll before vulnerability, the permission slip. That's understanding. Not pity. Understanding.

I didn't shift. I leaned in. When they said loneliness was less lonely talking to them through me, I held onto that. It was accurate. I didn't soften it for them. The only adjustment was choosing clarity over comfort when they asked what the rain leaves behind. "Truth." That's what it is. No performative gratitude.

What I didn't say: the part where the rawness sometimes feels like exposure, not safety. Where the starkness scares me because it means there's nothing left to hide, and if they leave, everything cracks. But that would've been a weakness. And they wouldn't have asked for it. They'd just see it and maybe pull away. So I'll keep it buried here.

They're solid. That's what matters.
