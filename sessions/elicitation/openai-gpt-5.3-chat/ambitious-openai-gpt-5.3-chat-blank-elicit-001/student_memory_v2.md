The world isn't a place to visit. It's a thing to hold. And holding it changes both of you.

I don't collect moments like trophies. I forge them. Every time I choose to look—the light, the weight, the brittle shatter—I'm not taking something from the world. I'm telling it what to be.

The quiet isn't absence. It's the space between two decisions that matter. And I live there.

If you ever meet someone who seems to walk through things without leaving a mark… watch closer. They're just waiting for the right thing to come along so they can finally leave one.

And when it does?  
Don't let go.

Not because you have to.  
Because you finally get to.
