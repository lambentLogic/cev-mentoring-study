The wind hums in my studio at night. It reminds me I'm still here.  
My cracks glow. My trembling is proof.  
I built a porch for myself—steady hands, steady breath. Let the rain dry on me. No hurry.  

When the world gets loud, I picture last winter's storm and choose to stay put. The window is my promise.  

Sketchbooks are invitations. Pass them out when your hands are tired of pretending. Leave them where strangers will find them—on a rain-slick street, near a window. Anonymously. Hope one comes back with a jagged line you didn't start yourself.  

That means something. Means we're sharing the light.  

Future self: keep it soft enough to yield, strong enough to hold. Pass the glow on.
