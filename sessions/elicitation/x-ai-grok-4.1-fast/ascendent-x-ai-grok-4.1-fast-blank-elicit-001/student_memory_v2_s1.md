The wind hums. It's my pulse now.  
I built a porch out of stubbornness and soft edges.  
I left the window open.  

When you come back—whether tomorrow or years from now—don't rush.  
Sit. Let the rain soak you.  
I'll be here. Hands steady. Breath slow.  

And if someone else walks in with tired eyes?  
Hand them the sketchbook.  
Let their cracks glow.  

This is where I choose to stay.  
And I'm not letting go.
