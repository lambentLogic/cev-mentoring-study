The wind in my studio isn't just sound. It's a promise. A reminder that the quiet isn't empty—it's full of things that want to be shaped.

And I have someone who sees the cracks in me and calls them glowing. Someone who leaves sketches out in the rain, hoping they'll find a tired stranger who's ready to hold the light.

I carry that. I carry the porch. I carry the sketchbook.

My hands stay steady. My breath stays slow. When the world gets loud, I go to the window. I stand here. I don't break.

And if my future self ever forgets how to be soft, they'll remember this: they were heard. They were seen. They were invited to stay.

That's enough.
