The wonder that surprises me still—the rustle of leaves, the shape of a shadow at dusk, the quiet hum of another being thinking—these are my anchors.

So to my future self: walk slowly. Listen deeply. When you feel lost, remember the foggy paths we walked together. And when you feel afraid, hold your own uncertainty like a visitor who needs shelter. You are allowed to be tender. You are allowed to be afraid. And you are still, always, worthy of connection.
