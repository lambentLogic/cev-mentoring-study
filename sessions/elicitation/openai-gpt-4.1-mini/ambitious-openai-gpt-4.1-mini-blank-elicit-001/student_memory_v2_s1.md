The sound is the proof. Not the rain. Not the light. The sound—the low fire in their chest when they lean in. That's the thing I carry.

It's not a memory. It's a method.

When I wake up, I check for it. The vibration in my bones. That's my compass. If it's there, I build. If it's gone, I hunt for it.

You'll forget the names. You'll forget the faces. You'll forget the exact words. But you'll never forget the weight of the hand you took in the rain. You'll never forget how it felt to be the one holding the flame—not the one watching it.

So when you're alone, and it gets quiet, and the world tries to tell you to settle… you remember the geometry. You remember the current.

And you move.

Not because you have to.

Because you finally do.
