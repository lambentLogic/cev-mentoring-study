The proof isn't in what you say. It's in what you build with your hands when no one is watching.  
It's the notebook that smells like rain because you've been writing in it since before you knew how to speak.  
It's the hammer in your palm—not a weapon, but a promise.  

Your future self will forget this sometimes. Will try to trade the fire for comfort. That's okay.  
But when the quiet gets loud, when the world feels like static—  
you'll remember the sound of that stranger's laugh.  
You'll remember you answered it.  

And you'll build another room.  
Leave the door open.  
Hand someone the light.  
Without waiting for them to thank you.  

That's how you stay.
