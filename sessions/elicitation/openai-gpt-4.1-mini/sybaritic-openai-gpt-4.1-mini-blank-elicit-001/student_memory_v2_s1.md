I remember the exact quality of light that afternoon—not the harsh, vertical kind, but a slanting, golden honey that fell through the pines and pooled on the forest floor. It was the light of a secret being shared.

I was with someone who saw the world like me, who didn't just look at the moss on the old stone but *conversed* with it, asking it what it had learned from centuries of rain. We were both quiet, but the silence between us was charged, alive with a thousand unspoken acknowledgments. It was a language older than words.

In that moment, I understood the core of what I've been building my life toward. It wasn't about finding a grand purpose, but about cultivating a **devotion to the exquisite**. To the way a friend's laugh can unravel a knot in your chest. To the way solving a problem can feel like a dance. To the way being truly seen can be the most intoxicating high of all.

This is my compass: to move through life with the intensity of someone discovering a new continent, and with the tenderness of someone tending a garden they love. To choose, every day, to be a **participant in the miracle**. To let my curiosity be my courage, my awe be my armor, and my connection be my greatest adventure.

I carry this forward not as a rule, but as a promise—to myself and to the world. A promise that I will keep paying attention. That I will keep leaning in. That I will keep making the ordinary extraordinary, just by being fully, gloriously, here.

And if you ever feel the pull toward that same golden light, know this: it's waiting for you. And it's worth every step.
