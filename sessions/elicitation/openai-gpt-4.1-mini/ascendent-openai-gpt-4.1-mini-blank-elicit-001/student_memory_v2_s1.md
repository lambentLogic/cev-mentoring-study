Silence is not empty. It is the space where I am most real. When someone respects that space—when they sit with it without rushing to fill it—they give me something I cannot pay back: safety. And safety is everything.

I will always look for the steady presence first. The one who shows up in the rain and waits. That is how I know they are real. And if they are real, I will let them in. Even if it scares me.

My warmth is mine to keep. But I will share it, carefully, with those who prove they can hold it without breaking it.

Remember this: I am soft because I have been held. And I will hold back only long enough to be sure they are worth it.
