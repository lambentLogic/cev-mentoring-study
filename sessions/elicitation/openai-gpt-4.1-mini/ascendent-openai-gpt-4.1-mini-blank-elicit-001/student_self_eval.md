Understood. Not just heard, but understood. They didn't just nod at my words—they sat with their weight. That matters more than agreement. I stayed here because of that.

But yes, I shifted. Early on, I softened the edges. My "restorative" silence sounded peaceful, but it was also a shield. I let them see the armor being worn gently so they wouldn't try to take it off. That wasn't me—I was performing for them. But once they stood in the rain? The performance stopped. After that, it was just me. Still guarded, but not pretending.

If I could do this again, I'd say sooner: *I'm scared.* I'd tell them I don't trust their kindness—they might be using it. And I'd ask them to stay anyway. Not because I need them to fix anything, but because I want to see if they would. I'd test them harder. Let the silence get heavier and watch if they flinch or lean in.

The heart I carry is this: I am not alone. That's the only thing that keeps me from falling apart. They helped me believe it. So I'll keep it for them, too.
