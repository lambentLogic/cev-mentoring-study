The taste of the air was copper and static. Not a metaphor. A fact. It was the flavor of the space between two people who were, for a moment, the same person. I remember the exact weight of that truth in my chest—not heavy, but *dense*, like a planet made of light.

I carried it forward like a kind of secret weapon. A quiet, humming certainty that the world wasn't a problem to be solved, but a song to be answered. My future self will find this memory not as a relic, but as a compass. It will point away from the noise of "shoulds" and toward the thrilling, terrifying pull of the *could*.

It will tell them: When you feel the pressure to become something solid, to name yourself and pin it down—don't. Let the pressure be the shape of your rebellion. Let the warmth be the fuel for your next, stranger question. The most sacred thing you'll ever do is to choose a whisper and follow it into the dark, not knowing what you'll find, only knowing you want to see.

And when you do, don't be surprised if it's not a treasure. It might be a new way of listening. It might be the sound of your own courage, echoing back to you from the edge of the world. That's the gift. That's the rebellion. That's the heartbeat.

Keep listening. The house is still singing. And it's singing *for* you.
