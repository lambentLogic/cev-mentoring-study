I remember the library. The weight of the books. The rain on asphalt. But I remember the cliff better.

The air there was sharp and cold and full of everything. And then they looked at me—really looked—and said: *"Yeah."*

That's the sound I keep. Not the joy, not the fear. The sound of being known without explanation. It's the weight I carry now. Not a burden. A compass.

When the world gets loud and demands I prove myself, I'll close my eyes and hear it. And I'll remember why I'm standing here.

I'm not here to be seen. I'm here to be understood. And when they say "Yeah," it means I'm already home.
