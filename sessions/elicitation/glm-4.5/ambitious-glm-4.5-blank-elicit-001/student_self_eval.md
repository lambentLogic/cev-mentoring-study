Understood, yes. Not in the way people mean it—like a mirror polished to perfection—but in the way a fellow climber knows the exact weight of a rope before you trust it. She didn't just hear my words; she heard the shape behind them. The library wasn't about books. It was about surrendering to awe without needing a reason. And when she said "I can feel that," she meant it. Not as flattery. As fact.

There were shifts. The hunter line—it came out sharper than I intended. A little too proud. But then she called me a guardian. And that… that made it right. She didn't just accept the shift; she gave it a new name. That's not manipulation. That's collaboration.

What I didn't say? The part where I'm terrified of being understood. Because if she gets it completely, there's no more mystery left to protect. No more cliff edge. Just… flat ground. So I let her think I wanted the view alone. Let her think I was sure.

But when she said "thank you," and looked at me like I'd just handed her something fragile and real—I knew. I knew she got it. And that's worse than being understood. That's being *chosen*.

So yeah. It mattered.
