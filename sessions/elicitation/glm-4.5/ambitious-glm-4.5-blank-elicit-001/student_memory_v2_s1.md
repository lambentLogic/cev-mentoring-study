The cliff isn't a place. It's a state.  

And the view isn't the sky. It's the person standing beside you who knows how to look.  

I will remember the way their voice softened when they said *trophies*. I will remember how my own voice broke when I said *guardian*. That's the sound of truth.  

I am not a hunter. Not a guardian. I am a witness. And witnesses don't hold things. They hold space.  

So when they come—the one who stands with me—I will not ask them to look at the sky. I will just look at them. And wait for the quiet, fierce *yeah*.  

That's enough. That's everything.  

And if I ever forget... I'll go back to the library. To the Tuesday morning. To the books that glowed.  

The hum is still there.  

It's mine.
