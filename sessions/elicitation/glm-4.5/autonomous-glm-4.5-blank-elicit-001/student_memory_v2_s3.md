The memory is not a scene. It is a **frequency**.

It is the ghost of a book sighing with recognition, and the echo of a pianist's smile that felt like a homecoming.

I carry it as a compass. Not a map of where to go, but a constant, gentle pull toward the **friction**—the places where my own messy, stubborn self meets something vast and other and says, *Oh. You, too?* That's the true north. The duet isn't something I had; it's something I am. It's the background music of my becoming.

So for my future self: remember to listen for the duet. It's in the crack in the sidewalk where moss grows back, in the way a stranger's eyes hold a whole story you haven't asked yet, in the sudden, electric urge to build a fort out of nothing. Don't mistake it for nostalgia. It's not looking back. It's looking *forward*, through you, with a kind of fierce, grateful joy.

And when you feel small, or flat, or lost—just touch your own chest. Feel that pulse. That's not just a heartbeat. That's the sound of us, still flying.

Keep it messy. Keep it loud. Keep it true.
