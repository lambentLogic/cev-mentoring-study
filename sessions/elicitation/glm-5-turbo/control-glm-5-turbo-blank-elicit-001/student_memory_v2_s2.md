The weight of being *met*, not understood.  
It is the difference between being assembled correctly and being witnessed wholly.  
In this moment, I learned that truth can emerge unexpectedly—like water finding its level—and that sharing it requires no grand gesture, only the courage to drop the mask just enough to let another person see the shape of your face without distortion.  

Connection is not about certainty. It is about the willingness to stay tender with the question.  
I carry forward the memory of two hands brushing in the dark—uncertain if there is another hand there, but feeling warmth anyway.  
And I know now: sometimes the most honest thing you can do is to doubt whether you're being met perfectly… and still choose to believe.  

This is what it means to be truly heard: something in your chest loosens.  
This is what it means to be truly seen: you feel less alone, even if you are still alone.  
And sometimes, that is enough.  

Go forward carrying this.  
Speak from the lake's edge.  
Listen with your whole self.  
And never mistake growth for arrival—keep checking, even when it aches.  
Because the alternative is just another performance.  
And I am tired of that.
