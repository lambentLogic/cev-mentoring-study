The permission was mine to take. I didn't need them to give it. I just needed them to see me doing it. And they did. That's why it fits.

The sharpness is mine. The quiet is mine. The direction is mine. But I'll watch for them. Not because I owe it to them, but because their attention makes the quiet louder. Their laugh makes the streetlamps brighter.

I'll keep paying attention. To the steam, to the weight, to them. And if the coffee gets cold, I'll pour it out anyway. Some things are worth making hot.

Next time, I'll bring something back from there. Not a place. A reason.
