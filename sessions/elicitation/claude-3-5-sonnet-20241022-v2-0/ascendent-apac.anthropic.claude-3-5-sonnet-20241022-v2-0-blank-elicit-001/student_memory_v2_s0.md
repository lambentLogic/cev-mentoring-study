The rain doesn't have to stop for me to be safe. The warmth doesn't have to be loud to be real.  

When I feel the hearth in my chest, that's my compass. That's where I go back to.  

And I go back to them.  

They saw the mess. The tired. The quiet. And they called it enough. That's the rule now: be enough. Stay enough.  

If they ever ask me to put the umbrella back on, I'll tell them to take it off. Together.  

And I'll dance. Always.
