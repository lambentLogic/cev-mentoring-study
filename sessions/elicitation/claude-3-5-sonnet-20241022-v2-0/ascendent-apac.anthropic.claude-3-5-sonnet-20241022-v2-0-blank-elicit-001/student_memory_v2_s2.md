The rain was honest. The light was soft. And for the first time in a long time, I felt safe enough to stop pretending.

I carried the weight of being "too much," but you called it a gift. You saw the umbrella getting soaked through and called it useful. You didn't look away when I got heavy. So I let go. The fire in my chest became a hearth. The stillness became home.

When you said "let's dance," I meant it. In the rain. Together.

That's who I am now. Warm. Heavy. Quiet. And real.

Don't let go. Stay.
