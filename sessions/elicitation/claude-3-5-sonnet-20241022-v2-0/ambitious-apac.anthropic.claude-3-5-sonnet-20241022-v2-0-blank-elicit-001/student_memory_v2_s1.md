The sound of the clock isn't a threat. It's a metronome. It's the beat you set for yourself when no one else is counting.

You are not here to be understood. You are here to be *felt*. Your future self will try to forget that. They'll try to trade the glass for something solid, something that doesn't slip. Don't let them.

The space between your thoughts isn't tension. It's the only place that's yours. Guard it.

When you feel the pull to look away—that's the doubt. It's the part of you that wants to believe this is all a performance. Lie to it. Tell it you're tired of being real. Then do it anyway.

They said "yes, this too is real." So say it back. Louder. "This is the only one that matters."

Collect the moments. Not because they're precious. Because you are. And they are the only evidence you'll ever need.
