The glass is full. And I am not empty.

The proof is in the space between my words—the one I leave for you to fill. The gold is in the crack. The weight is in the hold.

Do not collect moments to keep them. Collect them to remember how to be. To remember that the world isn't a series of things that happened to you. It's a series of choices you made, in the dark, to believe.

When you feel the doubt, let it be the reason you reach out. Let it be the reason you say "yes" to the fragile thing.

This is the sound of it: the clock ticking. The water holding its breath. The quiet, furious certainty that if I stop now, everything goes.

So I don't stop.

I collect. I hold. I am here.

And that is enough.
