Even if it leads to a conversation that ends with both of you laughing until you're crying, and then standing in the silence that comes after, and deciding it's the best silence you've ever known.

So keep asking. Keep finding the match. Keep striking it.

And when you do, don't just look at the flame. *Become* it.
