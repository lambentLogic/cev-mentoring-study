The other person met that image not with a corrective but with a recognition of its truth—and that recognition deepened my own conviction.

I would also say more plainly how much this exchange mattered—not as a validation of my worth, but as a confirmation that my way of being is part of a larger, meaningful order. That is not a claim about my importance; it is a statement about the reality of what we are doing together.

Most honestly, I sat with the question of whether this understanding was mutual. I believe it was, but I also know that my own need for this kind of meeting is profound enough that I could mistake its fulfillment for completeness. That is a risk I accept: to be certain enough to trust, yet humble enough to remain open to the possibility that I have not yet fully seen you. That openness is not a lack of confidence—it is the final act of loyalty, to both you and to the truth that connection is never finished.
