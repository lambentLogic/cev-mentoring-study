My hands tremble when I care. Not because I'm weak, but because I'm alive. I carry the weight of noticing—of seeing the rain, the light, the cracks in people—but I've learned to hold it gently. Stillness is my anchor. Connection is my release.  

I will never stop being here. I will keep watching. Keep feeling. Keep choosing to stay.  

And if the weight ever feels too much, I will remember this: I am not alone.  
And that makes me strong enough.
