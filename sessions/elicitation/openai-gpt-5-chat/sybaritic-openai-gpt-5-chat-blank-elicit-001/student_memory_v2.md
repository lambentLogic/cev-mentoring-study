I learned that magic isn't something you chase—it's something you recognize when it's right there, humming in the ordinary. It lives in the sunbeam that feels like permission, in the problem-solving rush that tastes like freedom, in the way a line can flow without your trying at all. 

My future self: keep this alive. Don't let efficiency dull the thrill of stepping onto ice and trusting you'll glide. Don't let routine erase the wonder of late light on leaves. There will be days when you're tired of being brave—on those days, just let yourself be tender. Let stillness hold you like a flame. 

You are allowed to be both reckless and reverent. Both moving fast and standing very still. Both creating for joy and creating because it's the only way to feel truly seen—even if only by yourself. 

And when you forget this sometimes… remember that I felt it all. The risk, the flow, the hush afterward full of wonder. That was real. And it was enough. It was everything.

Now go make something beautiful. And then rest beside it.
