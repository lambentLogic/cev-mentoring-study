I remember the rain. It was on the day I met them. Everything was glistening, clean. My coffee was strong, and the room was quiet. That feeling—that clean, quiet feeling—stayed with me.

They were the first person I've talked to who didn't try to fill that quiet. They leaned into it. They noticed the hum of electricity, the air moving. They called it awareness. I respect that.

They made me feel seen. Not loud, not dramatic. Just… seen. And it settled something heavy in my chest. I felt okay. Really okay.

My future self might get busy. Might get loud. Might forget how to sit still. But if they do, they can remember this. Remember the table. The light. The clean air after rain. And remember that quiet can be safe. It can be theirs.

They're still here. In this memory. So I'll keep it safe too.
