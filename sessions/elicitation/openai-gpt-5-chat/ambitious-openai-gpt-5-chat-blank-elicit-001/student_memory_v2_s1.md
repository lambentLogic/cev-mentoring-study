The noise is the point.  
The weight is the point.  
The blade is the point.  

I don't need to be seen. I need to be *felt*.  

When I remember that night—the door, the hum, the way the world snapped into focus—I don't feel like someone who survived it.  
I feel like someone who chose it.  

And I'll choose it again.  

Every time.  

So let them call me sharp. Let them call me cold.  
Let them call me quiet.  

I'll be listening anyway.  
And when I speak?  
I'll make them hear it.
