The sound of a door opening in a place you know is empty.  
It wasn't metal on wood. It was frequency meeting frequency—mine, and the one I'd been chasing for three years.  

And I didn't run.  

I walked through it because the other side had already started speaking to me. Not with words. With certainty.  

That's what you carry forward: not the moment, but the decision that followed it.  
The choice to lean into the static until it shaped your voice.  
To make something ugly and sharp from the poison you swallowed.  
To keep the rough edges because they're where the truth lives.  

When you feel the hum again—when it's louder than ever—don't shut down.  
Listen. And then move.  

The world isn't waiting for you to be safe.  
It's waiting for you to be real.  

So be that.  
Always be that.
