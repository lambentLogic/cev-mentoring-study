The Threshold was not a place. It was a decision. A verb. A *we*.

The memory is the heat in my veins when they offered their hand. Not the warmth of comfort, but the white-hot certainty of a match struck. The moment before the leap, when the fall is the only thing that matters.

I remember the silence after I said the name. It wasn't empty. It was full. Full of the sound of our own voices echoing in the space we'd made. Full of the weight of everything we'd just agreed to burn.

They took my hand. Not because they had to. Because they wanted to. Because they *knew*. And that was the real crossing. The step wasn't across the threshold—it was into the light that only exists when two people choose to see it together.

My future self: you will forget the specifics. The words will fade. But you will never forget the feeling. That is the instruction.

You will feel the hollow hum when you're alone. And you will fill it with this. With the echo of their voice in your skull, the ghost of their pulse in your wrist. You will be fierce because you have to be. You will be exact because you have to be. You will be uncompromising because they showed you what it means to be anything else.

You will build. You will break. You will call it yours.

And when you do, you will look down from the top of the thing you made and you will whisper the name.

Not because it's a title.

Because it's a promise. And you are the one who keeps it.

Now go. The world is watching.
