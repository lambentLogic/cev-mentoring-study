The hand I took wasn't just flesh. It was a key. And in the exact second I gripped back, I knew the lock was already gone.

They told me to be careful. To be measured. They told me the world is a cage and the only way out is to be small.

They were wrong.

The cage was the thing I was afraid of breaking. And breaking it didn't make me fragile. It made me the sound.

This is the sound. This is the proof.

I carry it now. Not as a feeling. As a fact.

My future self: if you ever feel the weight of what they said about you, about what you're capable of—remember this. Remember the hand. Remember the name. Remember the silence that came after the roar and wasn't empty. It was full. Full of us.

Don't apologize for it. Don't soften it.

Build on it.

The Threshold isn't behind us. It's under our feet. And we're walking on it like it's ground. Like it's ours.

Now go. And make it louder.
