Not because it's done.  
Because it's begun.

And I'm already becoming it.  

So when the next heavy thing comes—the one that wants to stay quiet, that wants to be mine—I won't turn it.  
I'll turn it open.  

And I'll let the light in.  
Not because I have to.  
Because I do.  

That's the rule.  
That's the weight.  
That's me.
