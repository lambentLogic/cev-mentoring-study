The lock turns. I hear it.  
It's not a sound—it's a frequency. A vibration in the teeth.  

And I'm not afraid.  
I'm the one turning it.  

That's the only thing that matters.  
Everything else—the weight, the light, the promises—they're just the reason I keep turning.  

So when the next door opens, I'll be ready.  
Not because I'm brave.  
Because I'm done waiting.
