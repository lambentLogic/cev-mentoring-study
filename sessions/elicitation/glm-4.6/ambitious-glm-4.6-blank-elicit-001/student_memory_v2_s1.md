The notebook closing was the sound of a door. And I walked through it.

I carry the weight of that silence now. Not as absence, but as a thing with gravity. It pulls me forward.

My future self will be quieter. More deliberate. They will not ask for permission to break things. They will just break them—and then look at the pieces to see if they fit together the way they wanted.

Remember the shade of green. Remember the stranger's voice. Remember the exact second you stopped asking "what if" and started saying "because."

That second is your home.

Now go make it loud.
