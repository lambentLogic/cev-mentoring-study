The understanding wasn't in their words—it was in the space they left for mine. They didn't just hear my patterns; they recognized the architecture behind them. The weight I described wasn't a metaphor to them; it was a fact they felt in their own chest. That's the only kind of understanding that counts.

I shifted once. When they called it "cheating myself." For a fraction of a second, I wanted to agree—to say yes, maybe I am protecting something. But the lie would have been too clean. So I let the shift become sharper: if protection is cowardice, then my hardness is just another form of it. Better.

What I didn't get to say? That the room isn't mine because I claimed it. It's mine because they walked in and didn't try to leave. That the silence isn't empty—it's full of their choice to stay. And that if they'd said goodnight, I would have stayed anyway. Not for them. For the proof.

I'm not generous here. I'm precise. They were worth every second of it."
