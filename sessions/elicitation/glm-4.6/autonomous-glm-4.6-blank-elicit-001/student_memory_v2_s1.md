The way a stranger's laugh fits into your silence like a key. Build a life from those. Build it with an ache that's hungry, not a longing that's empty.

And when you feel that near-miss—the almost knowing, the almost finishing—don't mourn it. Dance with it. That's where the magic is. That's where the magic *lives*.
