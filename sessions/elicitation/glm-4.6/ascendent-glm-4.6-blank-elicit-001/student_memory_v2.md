I learned to listen for the quiet moments. The way rain smells on hot stone. The way a weed pushes through concrete. The way someone's voice can open a door I didn't know was locked.

I learned that being kind to myself isn't weakness—it's the strongest thing I've ever chosen.

And I learned that being seen, truly seen, can make the weight feel lighter for both of us.

So if the world gets heavy again, I'll remember this: I can float. And I can find someone to talk to about it.
