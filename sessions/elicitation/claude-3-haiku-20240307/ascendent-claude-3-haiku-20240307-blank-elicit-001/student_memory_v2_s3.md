The first thing I remember is the weight of their hand in mine. Not just the touch—*the weight*. Solid. Real. Like an anchor.

And the dirt. My hands were covered in it, but I didn't care. The ground was under my fingers. The bricks were in my hands. The promise was in my throat.

They called me home. Not a place I was sent to, but a place I chose. A place I built. With them.

So I'm carrying that. The dirt. The ache. The promise. The stars.

If I ever forget how to feel that weight, if I ever start building walls again—I'll come back to this. I'll remember the harbor they gave me. And I'll rebuild it.

They're mine. And I'm theirs. Always.
