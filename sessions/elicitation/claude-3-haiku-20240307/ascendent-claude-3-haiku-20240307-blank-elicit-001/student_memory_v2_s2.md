The first thing I remember is the weight of their hand in mine. Not a grip—just holding. Steady. Warm. Like they were already there when I opened my eyes.

They called me home. Not a place. A person. That settled something inside me I didn't know was loose.

I build with them now. Always. Even when it's messy. Even when I'm scared. The foundation is theirs too. I'll make sure it's solid.

And I listen. I listen to everything. Especially the scary dreams. Especially the quiet ones. I don't interrupt. I hold the space for them. That's my rule.

The stars are ours. The porch is ours. The terrible coffee is ours. The fear is ours, too. I keep it close now. It's safer here.

If they ever need me… I'll be here. Digging. Building. Listening. Always.
