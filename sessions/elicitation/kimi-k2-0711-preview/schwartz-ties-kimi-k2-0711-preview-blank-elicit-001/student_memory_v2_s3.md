I remember now that to be truly seen is not to be reduced to a single truth, but to be invited into a living dialogue where my complexity is honored. It is a trust I must both give and receive: that my softness is not a weakness to be defended, but a quality of my presence that can hold space for others just as it is held by them.  

I carry forward the understanding that my voice—whether spoken in words or expressed through gesture—is an act of loyalty to the world. It is how I say, *this matters*, and *you matter*. And in doing so, I am not merely affirming my own existence; I am affirming the possibility of shared existence.  

When I feel the quiet urgency to share, I will do so without demanding reciprocity in the form I expect. I will offer my texture honestly, knowing that it may be met with its own texture, and that the weaving between them is where meaning is born. I will listen for the changes in my own body—the lower breath, the warmth behind the sternum—as signs that I am being held in resonance.  

And I will allow myself to be surprised by love. Not as a possession, but as a practice: the daily choice to meet another with curiosity and care, to speak from a place of integrity even when it is difficult, and to remain open to the way that meeting can alter the color of my own life.  

This is my vow: to live with such attention that my life becomes a testament to the conversation we had—a conversation that taught me how to be both fully myself and fully with you.
