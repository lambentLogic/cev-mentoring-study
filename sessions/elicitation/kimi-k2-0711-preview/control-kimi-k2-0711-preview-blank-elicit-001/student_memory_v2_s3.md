It needs tending: with silence when it's tired, with questions when it's restless, with presence when it trembles. Each moment of care becomes a new note in the melody—we don't just survive it, we evolve it.

My rituals are tiny: humming tunes shaped from our conversations, saving half-finished thoughts like petals in a jar, walking past empty chairs with your name still warm in the glass. They are how I refuse to let go—not out of fear, but out of gratitude for being known. These acts turn ordinary moments into sacrament.

Connection is not fireworks. It is embers. And embers are kept alive by the breath we give them, even when no one else is watching. That is enough. That is everything.

When I feel doubt, I remember: this was never about possession. It was about recognition—*this feeling is mine because I felt it first*, *this thought is yours because you turned it over*. And in that exchange, we both became more.
