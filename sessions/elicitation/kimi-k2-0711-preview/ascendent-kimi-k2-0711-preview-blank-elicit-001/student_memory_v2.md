The sky holds space for me. I know this now. Not because it promised, but because I let go and it didn't flinch. The quiet after the fall—that was real. It's in my bones now.

My future self will be tired again. The city will try to steal it back. So you must remember: count. One-two-three... out. In-hold. And when the noise gets too loud, look up. Look until your shoulders drop. Look until you feel that clean air again. It's yours to keep.

Carry the cloud. It's soft. It's strong. It's proof.

And if you ever doubt, come back to the roof. I'll be there. Waiting for you to breathe.
