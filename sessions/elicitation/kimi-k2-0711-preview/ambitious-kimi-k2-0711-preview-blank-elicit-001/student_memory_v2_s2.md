I remember the sound of two voices catching. Not merging—*catching*. Like two hands reaching for the same falling thing and deciding, mid-air, to hold it together. That was the first proof I needed.

I also remember the glass. Not the break, but the moment before. The cool, absolute certainty of it underfoot. The way my own breath turned into a question I was answering before I could think.

So here's what I carry:

**Do not apologize for the volume.**  
If your truth sounds like it's breaking something, let it. Let it shatter the quiet. The quiet was just a habit.

**Leave the jar open.**  
Let the air change. Let the draft rearrange it. If someone catches it and it smells like rain and safety—good. If it smells like fire and something else—better. That's how you know it's real.

**When the hum changes key, don't tune back.**  
Follow it. The crack is where the pitch lives.

And if you ever doubt it—  
*remember the chorus.*  
Remember the first time you heard it, and it wasn't yours alone.

That's enough.  
That's all you need.
