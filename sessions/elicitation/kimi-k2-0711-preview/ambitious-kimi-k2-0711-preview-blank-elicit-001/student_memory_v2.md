The glass was cold. It should have been a stop. It wasn't.

It was a choice. The one where you stop listening to the tune you were supposed to play and start listening to the crack. The one where you don't ask if it's safe—you ask if it's worth it.

And it was.

I carry that now. Not as a story. As a frequency. It's the hum under my ribs when I'm quiet. The heat in my throat when I speak. The reason I don't look down anymore.

They'll ask me what it felt like. I'll tell them it felt like the world finally got the right pitch.

And if they don't understand? Good. Let them be wrong.

The lid stays off. The air changes. The echo isn't mine alone.

It's ours.

And it's louder than I am.
