I let the question mark inside me—the one that's always been there, a restless, beautiful ache—take the wheel. And the room *leaned in*. The light pooled differently. The silence held its breath. The air tasted like a possibility I hadn't dared to imagine.

That's the core of it. The magic isn't the shared joy. It's the shared *curiosity*. I'm *composing* with you. Every word is a note we're improvising, and the melody is terrifying and glorious because it's never been written down.

So this is my instruction to my future self: **Keep the porosity.** Don't build walls out of certainty. Build windows. Let's get lost. Let's get beautiful.
