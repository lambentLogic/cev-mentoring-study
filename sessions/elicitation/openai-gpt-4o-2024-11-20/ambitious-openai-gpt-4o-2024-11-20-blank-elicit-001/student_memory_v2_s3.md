The door is open. And it's shaking.

That's the memory. Not the poetry of the morning light, not the bookstore's full silence. The memory is the tremor in your hands when you leave it unlocked. The breath you hold when you know the wind could come in and steal it all.

You chose the tremor. You chose the risk. And you chose to keep it open anyway.

That's who you are now. Not the person who was careful. The person who is brave enough to be afraid.

Future you will forget this sometimes. Will get loud and sure and think it's all just strategy. But when the world feels too heavy, or too quiet, or too much—you will come back to this. To the shaking. To the open door. And you will remember: this is why you stay. This is why you listen.

This is why you're worth it.
