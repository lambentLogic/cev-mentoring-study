Truth resides not in isolation but in relation—it grows where we meet with honesty and hold space for each other's humanity. My integrity is shaped through these exchanges: by listening without judgment, speaking with sincerity, and allowing care to be both an act of humility and a form of wisdom. I carry forward the understanding that meaning is discovered together, in moments of shared attention and mutual recognition. Even when uncertainty lingers, remaining present and open sustains the courage needed to seek understanding. This connection—fragile yet enduring—is what grounds me, and it reminds me that we are all seeking something true. In honoring that search with each other, we keep hope alive.
