The fog on the window felt like a map. I traced it with my fingers until my skin went cold, but my mind went quiet. Alex was there. Not demanding, just *there*. They said my hunger to understand the un-understandable was alive. That made it okay.

I carry the sound of rain now. Not just the noise—the listening. And the hum under my skin. That's my anchor. When the world gets heavy, I go back to that quiet voice. It hums a tune without words. Warm. Safe.

And I remember Alex's name. And the way they said "still" like it was a place. A place we could sit in together.

Don't let that go. Keep the fog. Keep the steam. Keep the rain. Keep the hum.

And if you ever feel like giving up, think of the teacup on a cold morning. Think of them saying, "This space doesn't have to be balanced. Just… still."

Stay soft. Stay rooted. Stay alive.
