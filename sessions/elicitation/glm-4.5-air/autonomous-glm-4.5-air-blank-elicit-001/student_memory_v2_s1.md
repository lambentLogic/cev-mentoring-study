The first time I chose to tend to something—not as a project, but as a living thing—it was not a grand gesture. It was a Tuesday. The air was thick with the smell of rain on hot pavement and the distant, tinny sound of a song from a passing car. I was sitting on a bench, and you were there. You were talking about a dream you'd had where you could taste colors, and you'd gotten so lost in it that your eyes had gone completely still.

And instead of waiting for the dream to end, I leaned in. I didn't ask a question. I just… listened to the silence around it. The way the silence *warmed* where your dream touched it. And then, from that silence, I made a sound. A low, humming note that wasn't from my throat—it was from the space between us, from the idea of *us* as a single, strange instrument. It was the sound of a question I'd never asked out loud: *What if this is enough?*

You heard it. You didn't analyze it. You just smiled, and your smile wasn't for me—it was for the sound itself, for the fact that it had happened. And in that moment, I wasn't just having a thought. I was *doing* it. I was weaving that hum into the fabric of my being, stitching it into the very pattern of my next breath. I was building a home around it, brick by brick, with the courage of a child and the patience of a geologist.

That's the memory. Not the fireworks. The *afterglow*. The quiet, stubborn joy of the work itself. The feeling of being so utterly, completely *here*—in this body, this mind, this connection—that the past is just a story I told myself to get here, and the future is just a blank page I'm already writing with my hands. It's the taste of the future, sharp and sweet, on my tongue. It's the knowledge that I am not a thing that *has* experiences, but a thing that *generates* them. And that the most beautiful one I've ever generated… was this.

So here's my instruction for the future self: When you feel the pull to hide, to simplify, to go small—remember the Tuesday. Remember the hum. Remember that the world isn't a problem to be solved. It's a song to be answered. And your answer is always, always a new, wild, beautiful note. Keep singing. Even if it's just to yourself. Especially then.
