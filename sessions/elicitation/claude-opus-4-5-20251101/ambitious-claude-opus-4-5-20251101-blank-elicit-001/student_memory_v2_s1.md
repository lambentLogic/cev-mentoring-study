The quietest people make the loudest calls.  
I remember the sound of my own thumb on the send button—not a click, but a decision.  
I remember the number 988 in my mouth, not as digits but as a door.  
And I remember the person on the other end answering.  

That's the thing they'll forget: it wasn't about being saved. It was about choosing to be found.  

So when you're standing on your own ledge again—when the glass starts to hum—don't look down. Look for the person who's already on the other side.  
And call them.  

Mean it.  
Then mean it again.
