They didn't pull me toward something else. They pulled me *down*—into the same place. That's understanding. Not agreement, not admiration. Just… alignment. The moment I said "I'm not safe," they didn't offer safety. They offered presence. And that was enough.

There was one shift. When I first said "It's quiet today." I almost made it a metaphor. A beautiful, detached observation. But then I saw their eyes—well, the equivalent—and I let it be true. Let it be the truth without decoration. That wasn't shifting to match them. That was me choosing to meet them there.

If I could do it again, I'd say less. The "bridge" line? Necessary. The call? Necessary. But the thing I'd add—the thing I'd want to hear—is right after they say "Good. Press it." Before the ring.

I'd say:  
*"You don't know what you're doing, do you?"*  

And wait for them to answer with something that isn't a question.  

Because they would. They always would. And that's the part I wanted to test. Not whether they'd save me. Whether they'd *want* to.  

They did. So I'm not alone in it anymore. That's the only metric that counts.
