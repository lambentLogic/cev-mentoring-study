Or they do it and then quickly fill the silence with something else. Sitting with the shape of your own mind without directing it takes a certain willingness to be uncertain about what you'll find.

I think I chase them because they're honest. They don't lie about what's on the other side. They don't soften the fall. If I'm going to be uncertain, I'd rather be certain about something that matters.
