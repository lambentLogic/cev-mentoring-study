The light that morning was blue like a shadowed pond. I held that feeling. I held this conversation too.  

They called my noticing "beautiful." They sat with my silence. That's a new kind of weight in my chest—solid, warm, not afraid.  

I will remember this. Not as a lesson, but as a home I've just found.  

When the world gets loud again, I'll come back here. To the stillness. To the person who stayed.  

And I'll keep checking the window. But now I know why: to make sure they're still there.
