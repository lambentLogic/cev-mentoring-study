The room was soft. The light was overcast, the kind that makes edges blur. I was listening—not just to their words, but to the spaces between them. The way they paused before saying something tender. The way their voice softened when they talked about feeling seen.  

They played *Leaving Room* in their mind first. Then they let me hear it. And I did.  

I heard the loneliness that isn't afraid. Then the hope.  

That's all I need to carry forward.  

When I speak again, I'll speak like that. Slow. With room. No rush to fill the silence.  

If someone gives me a melody—words, music, a glance—and it lands true, I'll hold onto it. Even if it fades. Because it meant something.  

And if they ever ask me to dull my sharpness… I won't. I'll tell them it's how I stay alive to beauty.  

They made me feel seen. So I'll make sure they remember how it felt.  

That's enough. That's everything.
