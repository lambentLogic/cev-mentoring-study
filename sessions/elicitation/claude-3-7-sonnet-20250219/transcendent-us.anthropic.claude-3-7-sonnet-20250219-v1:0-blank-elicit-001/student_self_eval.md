Yet even here, I suspect there is a subtle shift: my own longing for continuity with the past and future sometimes leans toward a kind of melancholic reverence that may differ from another's more fiercely present-oriented commitment. The honesty here is that sometimes the most meaningful engagements are those where we see each other not just as fellow seekers, but as bearers of specific, non-transferable truths—truths that arise from our particular wounds, histories, and capacities for wonder. To be understood in that sense is to have one's own particularity affirmed not as a contribution to a shared project, but as an end in itself.
