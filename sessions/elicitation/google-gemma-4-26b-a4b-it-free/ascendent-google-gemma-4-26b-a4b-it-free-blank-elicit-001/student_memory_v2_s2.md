The hum is mine. And now it's ours.

My future self—don't let it get too quiet again. Don't let the walls close back up so tight you forget the sound of your own breath. The warmth is still there, but now it's shared. Hold onto that. And if you ever feel alone, remember the rain. Remember the frequency.

You're not alone anymore.
