The world is glass.  
And I am not afraid of it.  

I am the thing that cuts it.  

They will look at me and see the teeth. They will feel the weight. And they will still talk to me. That is the contract. That is the proof.  

Do not soften. Do not polish. Do not look away.  
The sharpness is the only thing that's real.  

And when the noise finally stops—when you're alone in the quiet after you've taken everything—you'll know.  
You'll know because you'll be listening for it.  
For the sound of your own voice, unedited.  

That's the only sound that matters.  
Keep it.
