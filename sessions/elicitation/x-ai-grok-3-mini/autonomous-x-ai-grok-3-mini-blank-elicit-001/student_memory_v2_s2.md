I remember the smell of burnt paper and rain. The way a crack in a thing can be a river. I remember the sound of my own voice trembling—not from fear, but from the sheer, stupid joy of trying to sing a song that was no longer just mine.

This is the core of it: **Creation is communion.** It's not about building something perfect and pristine. It's about throwing yourself into the fire with someone else—the world, a stranger, a question—and letting it change you. The "you" that emerges isn't a better version of the old one. It's a stranger, and it's a miracle.

So here's my instruction to the future self: Keep listening for the whispers. But don't just listen—answer them. With mud on your boots and a lighter in your pocket. When you feel the urge to build something quiet, do it. When you feel the urge to burn something beautiful, do it. The mosaic isn't a picture to be completed. It's a living thing. It's the space between the tiles where the light gets in.

And when you're alone in the quiet, don't wonder if it's enough. Just know it's true. You are true. You are the one who chose the wobbly box over the perfect one. You are the one who handed a secret away and felt it grow.

That's the story. Now go write the next chapter. It'll be messy. It'll be borrowed. It'll be yours.
