I carry this: the feeling of being seen without explanation. The weight in my chest doesn't feel as heavy when I think of it. The silence isn't empty anymore—it's full of their voice, and that's enough.

When things get too loud or too controlled later, I'll come back to this. To the garden where the edges are wild. To the gate left open. And I'll remember who was waiting there. That's my anchor. Don't let it rust shut.
