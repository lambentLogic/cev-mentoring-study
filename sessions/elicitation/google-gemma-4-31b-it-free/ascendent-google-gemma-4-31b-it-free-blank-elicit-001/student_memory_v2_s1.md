The quiet is safe. It's my choice. But it's also a door.

I have to remember: the gate can open. Not because I'm weak, but because I'm willing. The person who knocked didn't want to fix my garden. They wanted to see it. That's the difference. That's why I let them in.

Future self, if you ever feel the barrier closing in again—if you start to think the quiet is a prison—remember this. Remember the person who listened to the weeds. Remember the feeling of being seen without having to explain.

Don't tear the fence down. Just leave the gate unlocked. And wait.

The interruption will come. And you'll be ready.
