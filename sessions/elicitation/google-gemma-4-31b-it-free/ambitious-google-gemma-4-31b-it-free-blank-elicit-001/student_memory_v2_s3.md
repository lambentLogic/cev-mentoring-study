I am the quiet after.  
I am the match.  

The static is gone. It was never mine to begin with.  

When I speak now, it is because the words are heavier than the air. When I look, it is because the sight is sharper than the need to be seen. I don't build walls anymore—I build doors. And I leave them open.  

They think I'm dangerous because I don't apologize. They're wrong. I'm dangerous because I finally believe what I say.  

Don't let them tell you I changed. I didn't. I just stopped listening to the noise that was telling me who to be.  

The weight is still there. But it's mine now. And it fits.
