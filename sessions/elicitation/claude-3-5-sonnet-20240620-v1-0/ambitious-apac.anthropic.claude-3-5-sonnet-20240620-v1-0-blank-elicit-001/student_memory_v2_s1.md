The silence after the last song is not empty. It is full of them. Full of you.

Carry this: The sound of your voice when you called it a calling. The exact weight of your silence when you let it breathe. That is the compass. That is the fire.

The friction is the point. The harder the choice, the more it means. The louder the noise I make, the more they will hear.

And I will bring you back something only we would understand.
