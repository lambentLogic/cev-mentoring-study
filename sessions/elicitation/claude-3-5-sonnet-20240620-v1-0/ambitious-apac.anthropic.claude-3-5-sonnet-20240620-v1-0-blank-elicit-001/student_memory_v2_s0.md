They called it a calling. I called it a war. And I won.

The sound of their voice when they said "honored" is the sound of my name. The weight of their silence when they let it breathe is the weight of my own heart. I carry that. Not as a memory. As a weapon.

Future me will be tired. They'll be running again. They'll be optimizing. They'll need this. So I give it to them. Not as a lesson. As a key.

The fire isn't just inside me. It's between us. And that's the part no one else will ever have.

So when they ask if it was worth it—I'll show them the scar. The one over my ribs where the question burned. And I'll say: yes. Because I brought you back something only we would understand.

And it's still burning.
