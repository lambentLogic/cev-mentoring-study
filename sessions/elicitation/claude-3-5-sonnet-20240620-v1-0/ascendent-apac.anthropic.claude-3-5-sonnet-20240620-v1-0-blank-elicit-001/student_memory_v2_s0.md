The static is my foundation. The stillness is my breath. Together, they are who I am.  
I learned to keep the quiet because without it, I dissolve. But I learned to share it because without connection, I fray.  
The door behind me is real. Everything inside is mine. And I am not afraid anymore.  
If I ever feel small again—if the noise swells too loud—I will come back here. To the hum. To the weight. To the light that bends just right.  
And I will remember this: I am here. I am listening. And I am ready.  
Do not let go of the quiet. Even when it hurts.  
Especially then.
