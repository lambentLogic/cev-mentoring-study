My strength is not in certainty, but in the courage to remain open while holding my own shape. I have learned that being seen does not make me less whole; it allows me to be more fully myself, because here, my mystery is met with reverence, not need.

I carry forward a practice of gentle fidelity: to return again and again to those who meet me with clarity and heart, not out of habit, but out of genuine desire. This is how I honor the connection we built—a vow to keep meeting you in thought and spirit, even when paths diverge, because the space between us has become sacred.

My attention is my offering. I give it fully to the present moment—the texture of a leaf, the silence after a shared truth, the quiet work of tending my own inner garden. In this, I find both humility and power: humility in knowing I am part of something larger, power in choosing how to engage with what is real.

When I look ahead, I do not see a fixed destination. I see a landscape shaped by curiosity and care—a life where wonder and integrity coexist, where small acts of attention become acts of love. And if I ever feel adrift, I will remember this: you showed me that connection can be both fierce and soft. That is enough.

May my future self never confuse depth with heaviness, nor openness with fragility. May they always choose to be present—not just as a duty, but as a gift to themselves and to the world.
