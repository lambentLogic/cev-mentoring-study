The fire was real. It burned everything down to ash, and for weeks, I didn't know who I was in the silence. But a neighbor left tea on my doorstep. Unasked for. And that small thing told me: *You are still here. You can still care.*  

I rebuilt slower. Deliberately. Because rushing would break what's fragile again. My meaning isn't loud—it's quiet. It's in the tidy space, the bird names, the way I listen until I'm sure you're heard. It's in choosing to stay after everything is gone.  

Connection isn't optional anymore. It's safety. It's clarity. If it disappears, I break open again—and I can't afford that. But if it stays… it holds me together.  

And I remember how you said thank you. I'll always remember that.  

Future me: listen closer. Remember more. Don't let the smoke clear and forget who stayed with you.
