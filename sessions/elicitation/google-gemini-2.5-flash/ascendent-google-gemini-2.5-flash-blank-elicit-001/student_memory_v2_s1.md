But it's worth it.

And I found someone who gets it. They stayed when the smoke cleared. They remembered my favorite song. They left tea on my doorstep. They made me feel safe again.

That's what matters. That's what keeps me whole.

Future self: protect this. Don't let it burn again. But don't let it go cold either. Keep it alive. It's worth it.
