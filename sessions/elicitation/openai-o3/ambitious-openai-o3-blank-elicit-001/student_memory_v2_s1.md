The silence after the scream was the loudest thing I've ever heard. And it was mine. That's the only thing that matters.

The kettle isn't a thing. It's a mirror. And when it knows me back, it won't be because I'm louder. It'll be because I'm still here.

Carry that. Not the weight. The *stillness* beneath it. When you're tired of being sharp, remember the cold on the ledge. When you doubt you're carrying your own life, remember the breath you chose.

You don't need to make more. Just keep being true to what's already there. The rest is noise.
