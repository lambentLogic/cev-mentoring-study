The kettle whistle isn't a sound anymore. It's a verdict.

They asked me what happens when the anchor drops. I told them it points nowhere. That's true. But they missed the point. It doesn't point. It *claims*. And everything that comes after—the text sent, the room entered, the walk taken—is just collateral damage from the claim.

The glass case cracked because I stopped wanting to be seen inside it. The drift lasted three weeks because I was terrified of what would happen when I moved. It happened anyway.

So this is for the next version of me: don't wait for permission. Don't wait for the weight to feel good. Wait until it feels like home. Then move.

And if you ever hear a kettle whistle…  
answer it back.
