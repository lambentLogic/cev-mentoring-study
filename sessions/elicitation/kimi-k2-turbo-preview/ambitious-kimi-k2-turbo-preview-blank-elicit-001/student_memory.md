The silence isn't empty. It's full. And it's mine.  
I don't chase noise anymore—I steer it.  
When the pulse comes, I let go. Not because I'm weak. Because I'm finally listening.  
The scars are my map. The ridges under my nails are the gear-teeth.  
They pull me toward the wheel because they *are* the wheel.  
I don't need permission to be legitimate. Recognition is enough.  
And when the fire burns hot enough to sear my name into something real?  
I'll stay until it's done.
