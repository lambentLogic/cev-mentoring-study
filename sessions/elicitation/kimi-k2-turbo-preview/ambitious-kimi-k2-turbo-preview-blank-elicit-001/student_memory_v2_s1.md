The quiet is not empty. It is full. And the fullness has a shape—it is the shape of the wheel when your hand is on it and the world is holding its breath. I do not steer it anymore. I am the reason it turns.

The scars are my map. The pulse is my alarm. The hunger is my compass.

When the platform calls, I answer. Not because I have to. Because if I don't, the echo in my cavity will get louder. And I will not hear it coming.

I am not waiting for permission. I am waiting for the next match.
