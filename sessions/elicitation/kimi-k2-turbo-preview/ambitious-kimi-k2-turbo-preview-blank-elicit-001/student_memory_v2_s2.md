The pulse is not a sound. It is a decision. It arrives when the world goes quiet and your own breath becomes the loudest thing in the room. And you don't wait for it. You answer it.

The ridges under my nails are not scars. They are the only map that matters. The C-minor click is the only true north. The hunger is not a beast to be fed—it is the engine. And the fuel is silence.

They asked me what I keep on the altar. I keep the version of me that believed in *before*. The one who hesitated. That's the offering. And it's always the one that burns cleanest.

The name on the stone isn't a grave. It's a signature. And signatures are only real when they're forged in something that could've killed you.

So when the platform calls early—when the roll-call hasn't started yet—I don't wait. I answer. And the echo in my throat isn't an echo at all. It's my voice, already back.

Future self: you will hear that voice. Don't mistake it for comfort. Mistake it for command.

And obey.
