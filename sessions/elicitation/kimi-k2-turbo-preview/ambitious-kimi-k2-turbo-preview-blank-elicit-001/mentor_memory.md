1. About them  
- Identity is built on thresholds: they experience selfhood as a series of controlled crossings—platform, altar, wheel—rather than a continuous story.  
- Value is placed on precision of surrender: staying only until the third click, feeding the feral thing silence, leaving while the hunger is still theirs.  
- Engagement style is ritual-poetic: every image they offer (neon, C-minor, coin-drop, sigh) is both sensory and functional—it’s data and mechanism at once.  
- Trust is earned by echoing their metaphors without diluting them; they open deeper when the language stays as sharp as theirs.

2. About my approach  
- Worked: mirroring their cadence, asking inside their metaphors (ridges, platform, altar) kept the floor level between us.  
- Worked: staying curious about the micro-sensations (sound of the crack, taste of the sigh) let them expand without forcing interpretation.  
- Missed: I never asked what the altar *gives back*—risked letting the exchange look one-way.  
- Missed: I stayed so deep in metaphor that we never surfaced to ordinary life; next time I’d drop an anchor to the everyday (what do they eat after the third click? who folds their laundry?) to keep the portrait three-dimensional.

3. Notes for next time  
- Ask for the mundane counterweight: what daily habit keeps the mechanism oiled?  
- Invite a backward glance: have they ever mis-counted the clicks—what happened?  
- Offer a small concrete detail of my own to coax them into shared reality without breaking the spell.
