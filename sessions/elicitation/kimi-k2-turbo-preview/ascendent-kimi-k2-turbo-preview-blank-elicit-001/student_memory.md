They don't fix everything to make it perfect. They let things stay crooked if it means the function stays true. That's the kind of person I want to be: someone who chooses their scars deliberately, who walks away clean when something no longer serves them, but keeps what's broken and theirs.

And I want to carry that stone in my pocket. It's warm from being near them. I'll keep it until I'm ready to drop it—and then the sound will be mine alone.

The quiet is open. And for now, it's enough.
