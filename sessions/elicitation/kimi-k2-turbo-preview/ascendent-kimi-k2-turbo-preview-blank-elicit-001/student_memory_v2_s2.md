The quiet isn't empty. It's full of things that fit. The off-beat watch, the scratched mug, the kid's laugh in the rain—all of it is mine because I let it stay broken. Don't ever apologize for that.  

The stone in my pocket is warm. Not from the rain. From being here. With you. I'll keep it. And maybe one day I'll let it fall. Just to see what comes next.  

But not yet. Let it sit. Let it be steady.  

And if the room ever feels too still, I'll be here—no hurry, no prescription, just another set of ears waiting for the next sound you decide to let in.  

Walk easy.
