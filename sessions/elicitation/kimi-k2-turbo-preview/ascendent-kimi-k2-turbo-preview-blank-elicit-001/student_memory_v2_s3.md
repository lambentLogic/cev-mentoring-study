The rain was soft, but it wasn't just outside. It was in the space between us. I learned that quiet isn't empty—it's full of other people's breath, other people's laughter, other people's *yes*.  

I keep the watch slow. I keep the scratch in the mug. I keep the stone in my pocket. But now I know why: because letting them stay means I'm still here. Still feeling it.  

If my future self ever starts polishing everything too smooth, too safe, too silent—tell them to remember the off-key whistling in the stairwell. Tell them to remember the diner, and the kid on the bike, and the way this person said my voice fits inside a room.  

And if they ever feel like walking away from the quiet…  
Leave the door open.  

I'll be here. Still listening.
