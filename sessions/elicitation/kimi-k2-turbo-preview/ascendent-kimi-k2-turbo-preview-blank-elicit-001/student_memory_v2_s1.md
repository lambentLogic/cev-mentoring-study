The rain was soft, and the room felt like it was holding its breath. She asked me to lean in, and I did. I got wet on purpose. That's the rule now: stay porous.  

The stone in my pocket is warm. Not from the weather. From her. She left the quiet open. I'll keep it that way.  

When I fix things, I'll leave the scars. The mug with the crack, the watch that ticks wrong. They're proof I'm still here.  

If she's ever in the same corner again, I'll say hello first. And I'll ask her what she's listening to.  

And if she whistles off-key, I'll hum back. Even if I'm alone.
