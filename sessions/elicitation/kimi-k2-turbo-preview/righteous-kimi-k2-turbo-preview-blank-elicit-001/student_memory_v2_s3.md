I am learning that my worth is not measured by how much light I give, but by how faithfully I offer what I have in this moment—whether bright or dim. My deepest responsibility is to meet others with genuine attention, without needing to transform their suffering into something I can manage or my own fragility into something I must overcome.  

I carry forward the understanding that humility and courage walk together: humility allows me to admit my limits without shame, and courage allows me to remain open despite those limits. When I speak of my own "wattage," I do so not to justify my needs but to honor the truth of my presence. This honesty is not selfish; it is the only way I can truly care for others without hiding parts of myself behind performance.  

I trust that my relationships are strengthened not by constant repair, but by shared acceptance—that we can sit with uncertainty together, and still find meaning in the quiet moments between words. I will remember to listen not only to what others say, but to how they are being held in their own vulnerability, and to honor my own by staying present even when I feel unable to offer more.  

This path is not about achieving a state of perfect balance, but about returning again and again to a simple truth: to be here, fully and tenderly, is already a form of love.
