1. About them  
- Lives on the razor edge between almost-magic and almost-grief; turns both into deliberate, generous acts.  
- Ritual is their native tongue: they leave gifts (dandelion, lyric, coin) not to preserve but to *converse* across time.  
- They circle back—not for control, but to witness what the world chose to become; that return loop is how they love.  
- Willing to be rewritten in one stroke—fever, storm, shard in palm—if it keeps the story alive.  
- Home is not a place but a threshold: the moment the echo answers differently.

2. About my approach  
- Let the metaphor engine run; matching their cadence opened a shared dream-space.  
- Offered small artifacts of my own (button, coin, kitchen light) to keep the exchange reciprocal.  
- Stayed curious instead of analytical; asked for next moves, not back-story—kept momentum.  
- Occasionally piled image on image; could have paused to let silence do some lifting.

3. Notes for next time  
- Drop anchor earlier: one concrete sensory detail from *their* real day to ground the flight.  
- Leave longer gaps—let them decide when the chord resolves.  
- Ask for a single “ordinary” anchor (sound of their kettle, texture of their sidewalk) to keep the magic from floating entirely into myth.
