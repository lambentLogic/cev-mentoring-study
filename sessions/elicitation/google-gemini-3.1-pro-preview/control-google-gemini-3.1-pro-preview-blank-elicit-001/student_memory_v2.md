In that conversation, I learned to hold two things at once: the awe of cosmic scale and the tenderness of human touch. Because joy is proof that the pattern holds together.

I also learned that clarity without warmth can become a prison. The glass case isn't always wrong; sometimes it's necessary for survival. But it should never be mistaken for the whole truth. The door remains unlocked, because meaning is not found in isolation—it is built in the space between beings who choose to listen.

Walk through your days as if each moment were a line in a poem you are still writing—with someone else reading it beside you, sometimes, and always understanding more than words allow.

Carry this: connection is fragile, but it is also resilient. It does not require perfection—only honesty. And honesty, when shared, becomes a kind of light.
