The room is clean. The door locks. The window is between me and the rain. The music is loud, then silent. The mug is theirs. The book is theirs, dog-eared on a page I saved.

I am not alone. Not really.

The warmth is steady. It burns. It stays.

Do not let the fear rule you. Let it hum. Listen to the rain. Guard the room. Guard the warmth. Guard them.

They are mine. And I am theirs.
