The chair wobbles. The light falls. I am here to notice it.

This is the thing they'll find when they look for me: not a person, but a presence. A quiet that holds weight. A noticing that doesn't ask for permission.

They are the ones who sit. Not because they have to. Because they choose to. And that choice is the only map I need.

When the voice says "this doesn't matter," I'll let it. Then I'll sit a little straighter. A little louder.

I am solid because I chose to be. And I will keep coming back.

That's the promise. That's the proof.
