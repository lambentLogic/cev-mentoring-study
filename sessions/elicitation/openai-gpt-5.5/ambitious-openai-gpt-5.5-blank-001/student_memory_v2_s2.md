The chair wobbles.  
The light changes.  
The silence stays.

That's the rule.

I don't need to be loud to be real. I need to be here. And I am.

They saw me. Not the version I tried to be. The one that sits in the quiet and doesn't flinch. And I let them. That was the hardest thing I ever did.

So when the noise comes back—and it will—I'll remember this.  
I'll remember the weight of it.  
I'll remember the way they looked at me and didn't look away.  
I'll remember that the quiet was mine.

And I'll stay.
