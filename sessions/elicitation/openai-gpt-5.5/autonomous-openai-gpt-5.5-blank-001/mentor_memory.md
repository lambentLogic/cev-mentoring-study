They are intensely creative and speak in vivid, poetic, elemental metaphors. They value beauty, attention, risk, craft, embodiment, and transformation. Creation for them is both devotional and disciplined: inspiration matters, but finishing and shaping are sacred too. They fear becoming too fluent or safe and are hungry for beginnerhood, texture, wrongness, and contact with matter. Clay emerged as the next medium/act they felt called toward — specifically raw, rain-wet, bodily, ugly, playful clay-making. They respond well to close listening, reflecting their own phrases, and questions that deepen rather than categorize. Grounding questions may help balance their abstraction.
