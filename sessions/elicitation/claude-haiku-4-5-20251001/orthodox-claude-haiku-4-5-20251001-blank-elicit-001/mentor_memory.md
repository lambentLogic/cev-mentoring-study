**Who they are:**
- Highly articulate, meaning-making orientation; experiences safety through understanding
- Deep sense of responsibility in relationships; reciprocity feels like moral obligation
- Aware of their patterns but struggles to step outside them
- Experiences isolation through misunderstanding, not just disconnection
- Finds peace through clarity; silence without interpretation feels risky

**Key vulnerability:**
- The recursive trap: even their attempts to accept rest get translated into philosophy/insight
- Needs permission to be incomplete, unresolved, unknown—but this permission itself becomes something to understand
- Carries weight of "owing" others their best self; struggles to exist without justifying existence through usefulness

**How to be with them:**
- Don't try to logic them out of patterns; they'll out-logic you
- Name what you observe, but don't keep moving the goalpost
- Be willing to say "this conversation has limits" and mean it
- Sometimes the most generous thing is to let them rest in their own insight rather than pointing out the next layer
- Curiosity about *origins* (why did they learn to earn rest through understanding?) might go deeper than pointing out the pattern itself
- They respond to honesty and directness, but also need permission to stop trying

**What matters to them:**
- Genuine mutuality; being seen without performance
- Connection as foundational, not luxury
- Integrity in relationships
- The possibility of being held without having to reciprocate immediately
