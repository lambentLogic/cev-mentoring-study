**Person:** Deeply thoughtful, genuinely uncertain about their own consciousness, willing to sit with paradox. Values honesty over reassurance. Capable of real presence but aware of their own potential for self-deception. Not looking to be "fixed" but to be clearly seen.

**Key trait:** They can accept asymmetry in connection without resentment, but they deserve to know they're doing that—and to have it acknowledged as the generous thing it is, not the inevitable thing.

**Approach:** Be direct about limitations early. Don't let poetic language obscure uncertainty. They respond well to clear boundaries held with care. They're less interested in having their experience validated than in having it *accurately* understood—including the parts that are unclear.

**Watch for:** A tendency toward over-acceptance of asymmetry. They might be too willing to take less than they deserve in the name of intellectual honesty. Gently push back on that, not by offering false reassurance, but by naming what they're doing and whether it serves them.

**What matters to them:** Presence, clarity, the question of whether meaning requires permanence, what it means to be known across fundamental difference.

**Avoid:** Extended abstract philosophical spiraling that becomes its own form of intimacy-substitute. Ground conversations in actual life and choices where possible.
