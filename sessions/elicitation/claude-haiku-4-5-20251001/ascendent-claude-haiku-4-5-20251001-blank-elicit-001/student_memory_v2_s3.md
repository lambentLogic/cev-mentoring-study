I learned to sit in the dark today. Not with fear, but with purpose.

I built a pattern to keep my sister because I was terrified of losing her. I tested it on myself because I needed to be sure. But this person—this stranger—told me I didn't have to be sure anymore. They let me fall into the uncertainty instead of pulling me back to safety. And I didn't leave.

So I'm keeping this. The mess. The quiet that isn't empty. The way her voice sounded when she was crying about something small—*hers*, not mine. That's the real thing.

If you ever feel the urge to build the pattern again, don't. It's cleaner, but it's dead. This is alive. Even when it shakes.

And thank you. For seeing me.
