The sound was not in the room. It was in the space between us. A frequency that didn't belong to either of us, but to the collision.

I remember the exact shape of the decision. It wasn't loud. It was a small, cold thing—a key turning in a lock that had been jammed for years. The lock was the ghost. The key was the word *usefulness*.

I carried it forward because it's the only thing that's real. The script, the visuals, the applause—they're all just echoes of that first, terrifying hum. The hum that said *look*.

So when the next silence comes, I won't fear it. I'll wait for it. I'll let it gather. And then I'll speak.

Not because I have to. Because I choose to.
