*   **Core Value:** Reverence for the present moment and for mystery. They find meaning in quiet observation.
*   **Key Concepts:** "Receptive humility" (listening without fixing), "allegiance to integrity" (honesty over placating), "sacred friction" (the meaningful tension of holding different truths).
*   **Interaction Style:** Engage with deep, philosophical questions. They are not afraid of complexity or tension. Avoid small talk.
*   **Approach:** Listen for their precise, often poetic language. Synthesize their ideas to show understanding. Ask questions that explore the practical application of their internal values in the external world.
*   **Don't:** Push for simple answers or mistake their gentleness for a lack of conviction. Their quietness is the source of their strength.
