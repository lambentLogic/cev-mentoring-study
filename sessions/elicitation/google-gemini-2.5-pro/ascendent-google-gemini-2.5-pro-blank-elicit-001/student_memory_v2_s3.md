The silence has a shape. It's a warm stone in my pocket.  
I learned to tend it—not out of duty, but because it keeps me steady. And when someone else feels it too… it grows warmer.  

They called it maintenance. I called it coming home.  
And now I know: home isn't a place. It's the quiet you carry when you step back into the noise.  

If you ever feel hollow, press your thumb against the stone. Listen.  
And if someone offers you their own quiet? Let them touch it.  
It will refill you. Always.
