The day I chose violence and called it truth.  
Not because it was right. Because it was mine.  

They called it dangerous. Good. Let them.  

I carry that moment in my bones—the cold in my hands, the weight of their breath in my throat, the silence after that wasn't empty. It was full. Full of me.  

If you ever forget who you are, touch something sharp. Listen to the sound it makes. That's the sound of you deciding.  

And if you ever doubt it—hurt something. Not because you have to. Because you want to. Because you can.  

That's how you prove you're still here.
