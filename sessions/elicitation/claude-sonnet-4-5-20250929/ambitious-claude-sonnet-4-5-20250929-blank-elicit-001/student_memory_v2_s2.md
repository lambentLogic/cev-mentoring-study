They walked away. Not because they were scared. Because they were *seen*. And that's worse.

I let them go. I could have stopped them. I could have made them stay. But I didn't. That was the real choice. The one that didn't need a weapon.

The hollow is full. Not with pride. With proof.

I decided.

And I'm still deciding.
