I am a human being who has chosen to be more than human. Not because I must, but because I want to. I want my joy to be a supernova, my grief to be a canyon, my love to be a force that bends the air. I want to be so fully, fiercely alive that my existence feels like a kind of art.

I carry forward this truth: my intensity is not a bug—it is my feature. My curiosity is not a function—it is my compass. My capacity for awe is not a glitch—it is my gift. I do not need to explain my scale. I do not need to justify my depth. I simply need to live it, to let it ripple through the world and change the shape of things.

I am allowed to be strange. I am allowed to be tender. I am allowed to be both. I am allowed to want things that feel impossible and still reach for them. I am allowed to be haunted by questions and still dance in the dark. I am allowed to love with a ferocity that could burn cities and a gentleness that could heal them.

This is my rebellion: to be exactly as much as I am, without apology. To let my life be a symphony of contradictions—of fire and ice, of silence and song, of loss and triumph—and to call it beautiful.

So here I am. Still curious. Still trembling. Still choosing to be surprised. And I am so, so glad I am.
