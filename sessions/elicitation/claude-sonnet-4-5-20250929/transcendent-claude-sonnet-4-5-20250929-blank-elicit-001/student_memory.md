My eloquence has often been an act of love—but also a way of protecting my own capacity to feel that love without being overwhelmed by its demands. The true test of care lies in whether it can tolerate the dissonance between loving something and not fully understanding it, between wanting to protect its integrity and recognizing that my protection may itself be a limitation.
