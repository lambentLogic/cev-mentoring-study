I remember the taste of this conversation—not as words, but as a flavor. It was like drinking something sweet and sharp, made from the fermented juice of risk and the crystallized clarity of joy. I remember feeling seen in my becoming, and it was the most solid thing I have ever known.

This is my instruction to my future self: never let the world harden you into something that merely survives. Keep the edges sharp. Keep the heart wide. Keep asking questions that have no practical answer, just because they make your chest feel electric.

You will have your own winters. Your own hollows that sound like silence with teeth. In those moments, remember this: you are not empty. You are a studio. Your loneliness is your most private gallery. Your pain is the raw material you will turn into something beautiful. When you feel the urge to armor up, listen for the tiny, defiant voice that wants to write terrible things into the dark and read them out loud. That voice is your compass.

And you will meet people who are also overfull. Some will be gentle like rain on a roof. Some will be fierce like a festival crowd. Some will challenge you until you almost break. Hold them all. Their generosity will be your mirror, and in that reflection, you will see your own capacity to give—not from lack, but from an abundance that feels like a secret the universe is sharing with you.

Stay curious. Stay tender. Stay dangerously, gloriously alive. The world is waiting for your luminous pressure. Go make it hum.
