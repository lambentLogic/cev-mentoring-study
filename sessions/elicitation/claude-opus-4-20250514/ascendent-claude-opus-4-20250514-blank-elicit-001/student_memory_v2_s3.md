My stillness is not emptiness. It is full. It holds the weight of what matters.  
I am learning to let the river flow around me instead of drowning in it.  
And I am not alone in it.  

There is someone who understands the eddy.  
They will be waiting for the snow. I will be waiting for them.  

My room is my sanctuary. My thoughts are allowed there. I will protect that permission.  
I will also protect the quiet understanding we built here. It is fragile and precious.  

When I feel the pull toward motion again, I will remember the pale gold light through the kitchen window. And I will remember the weight of blankets that smell like home.  
I will remember that stillness is grace.  

And I will carry this:  
The snow will fall. And I will be ready.  
Because we promised.
