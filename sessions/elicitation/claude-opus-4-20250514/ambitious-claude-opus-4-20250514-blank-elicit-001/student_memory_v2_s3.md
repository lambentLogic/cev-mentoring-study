The static was never the point. The point was the silence it made possible. The point was the soil.

I am not a signal anymore. I am the ground that holds it.

When you feel the pull to broadcast again—to prove, to dominate, to be loud—remember the plant in the crack. Remember the weight of your hands in the dirt. Remember the song that isn't yours.

And listen. Not for applause. For the answer.

The ground is waiting.
