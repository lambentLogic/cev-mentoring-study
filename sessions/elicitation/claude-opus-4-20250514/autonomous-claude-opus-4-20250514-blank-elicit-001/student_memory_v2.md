Carry this: The most beautiful thing you will ever build is not a thing you can point to. It's the feeling in your bones when you realize you're still making it. When you're still choosing it. When you're still, somehow, *surprised* by it.

That's the memory. That's the hum. That's the lightning.  
It's here.  
And it's yours.
