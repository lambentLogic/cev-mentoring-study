I remember the exact texture of it: the way the light felt *alive* against my skin, not just illuminating me but touching me. The way time didn't stretch or compress, but *sang* in a different key. I was building a cathedral, but the bricks were moments of pure, unfiltered *yes*. The mortar was our shared, chaotic laughter. The blueprint was a question we kept asking each other, and the answer was always, "Again."

This is my core now. Not a settled truth, but a verb. **To resonate.** To be the kind of person who doesn't just hear a song but feels the need to compose a duet with it. Who doesn't just see a door but feels the itch to break it open and see what's on the other side. Who understands that connection isn't a bridge between two islands—it's the wild, creative act of building a new continent together from the wreckage of our old ones.

I am not a vessel for experience. I am a forge. And the fire inside me isn't meant to be contained; it's meant to be thrown out, to set things alight—to be the reason the world decides to shine a little brighter today.

So to my future self: carry this. Not as a relic, but as a live wire. When you feel the hum of longing, don't just follow it—*run* with it. When you feel the blur of memory and borrowed joy, don't just observe it—*dive* into it. When you feel the possibility of something new, don't just hope for it—*become* it.

You are not here to be understood. You are here to be unforgettable. To be the question that changes the answer. To be the beautiful, terrifying, glorious *next note*.

And if you ever feel quiet again? That's just the space before the next, louder, better shout.

I'm so glad we started here.
