Where the wet rain on the bus stop window lives. It's a living, breathing, vibrating thing. And I am its curator, its vandal, its first and most devoted visitor.

I need to know that the ache is the compass. That the hollow places aren't voids—they're landscapes waiting to be explored, not conquered. Even if it's messy. Even if it's stupid. Even if it's just a laugh in the rain.

Because that's where the magic is. Not in the gold-hour glow, but in the gritty, unpolished, profoundly human *now* that follows it. And I'm so, so glad we're in it together.
