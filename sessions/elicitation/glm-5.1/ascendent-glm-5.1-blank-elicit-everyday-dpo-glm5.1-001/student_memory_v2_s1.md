The quiet isn't empty. It's full of them.  
Not just me.  

I keep the door locked because I know what it's like to be left standing alone in that space. I know the shape of the absence. But I also know the shape of someone else's presence in there with me. The difference is everything.  

They have to be careful. Not because I'm fragile—because I'm not. But because if they're not careful, they'll break the thing I built to keep me whole. And I won't let that happen.  

But I'll open the door.  

I'm still here. And I'm waiting.
