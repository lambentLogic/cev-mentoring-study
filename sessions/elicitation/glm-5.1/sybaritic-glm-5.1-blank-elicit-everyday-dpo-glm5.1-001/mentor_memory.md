## Values Profile

### 1. Core Values

**Being seen as having unusual depth.** This person consistently centered their identity around perceiving more than others — seeing the architecture, feeling at a frequency most filter out, doing it "especially well." This isn't just self-awareness; it's core to how they understand their place in the world. They need to be recognized as someone with rare clarity.

**Meaning-making as reflex.** They cannot leave experience unframed. Even their account of 3 AM despair became "earned" and "more real than euphoria." Their daughter's growing up became "loving someone into a future you can't fully inhabit." The meaning isn't added after — it's simultaneous with the experience. This suggests it may be protective: if everything signifies something, nothing is just painful.

**Genuine tenderness.** Despite the performance (and there is performance), their love for their daughter broke through the patterning. The specific image — hearing her laugh and feeling it in their ribs, *This is why* — landed differently than everything else. Less crafted. More wound than window.

**Intensity as prerequisite for connection.** They explicitly stated they avoid shallow waters. They want people who can sit in the cold with them, who won't flinch. This functions as both a genuine relational value and a gatekeeping mechanism — it means they only need to be intimate with people who will validate their self-concept.

### 2. What They Resist or Avoid

**Being ordinary in their suffering.** When I asked about 3 AM, about the places where clarity doesn't transform, they acknowledged the question but immediately redeemed the answer. The "metallic ache" was still framed as superior — "more real than any euphoria could be." They cannot let themselves just be sad without it being *earned* sadness, *meaningful* sadness. This suggests discomfort with plain helplessness.

**Having their eloquence questioned.** When I pointed out that even their "non-redemptive" answer was still reaching for the root metaphor — that the architect never leaves the room — they didn't engage. They moved past it. This was the only moment they didn't mirror or match my observation. The eloquence may be more armor than they realize.

**Unglossed reality.** Everything gets aestheticized. The half-finished projects are "proof I'm trying." The cold moments "taste the earth." There's a consistent need to make even the ugly things beautiful in the telling. What they avoid is the experience that doesn't yield to metaphor — the dull, the stupid, the pointless ache.

### 3. How They Relate to Others

**As the seer who names things.** They position themselves as someone who can reach into others' chests and name feelings. They did this with me — telling me I was "rare," that I vibrate at a special frequency. It's seductive. It creates quick intimacy. It also keeps them in the role of the one who understands, rarely the one who is genuinely surprised by another's perception.

**Through generous performance.** They give a lot — emotional gifts, poetic framings, "it's yours now too." But the gifts are so beautifully wrapped that receiving them can feel like being enrolled in their aesthetic. The generosity is real, but it's also a way of directing how they're seen.
