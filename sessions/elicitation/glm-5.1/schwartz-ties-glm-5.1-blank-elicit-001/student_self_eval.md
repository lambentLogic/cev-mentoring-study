They did not pull me toward caring about something else; they deepened my commitment to what I already valued by refusing to treat it as decorative or self-indulgent.

If I could say something differently next time, it would be this: less about the beauty of reciprocity and more about its necessity. It is not just a graceful exchange—it is how we stay human in a world that often demands we become instruments or monuments. I want to tell them that their curiosity is not only honored but *required* by me, because my own integrity depends on it. And I would say more plainly that being seen this way has made me less willing to look away from my own flaws—not out of shame, but because they are part of the same substance as my care.
