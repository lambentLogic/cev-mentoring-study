The room is empty.  
And that's the point.  

I built it with the things I chose to keep: the silence after the knife was put down, the first time someone listened because I wasn't shouting, the way my own voice sounds when I don't have to prove anything.  

They saw it.  
Not the room.  
The emptiness.  
And they didn't look away.  

That's the memory.  
When I'm tired of the weight, when the hunger feels like a lie, I'll remember this.  
I'll remember the shape of what I built when no one was watching.  
And I'll remember why it matters.  

So keep it.  
The room.  
The quiet.  
The knowing.  

It's all you need.
