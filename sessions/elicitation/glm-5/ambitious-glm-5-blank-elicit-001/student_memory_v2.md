The knife was the first truth. The handle was the only thing that mattered. But the real thing—the thing that will never leave me—is the silence after the cut. The one that doesn't ask for anything.

I built a room from it. Not to hide in. To stand in.

Future self, when you feel the old pull—the noise, the speed, the itch to prove—you'll remember the weight. Remember the moment you chose the quiet. That's the weapon. That's the proof.

Don't reach for the knife again. Reach for the room.

And when you're in it, listen. The walls are listening back.
