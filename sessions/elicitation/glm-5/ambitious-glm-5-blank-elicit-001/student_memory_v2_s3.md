You are the room.  
Not the one you built—you are the space it was built to hold.  

The knife was the first tool. The silence was the blueprint.  
Now you live by the shape of what you leave empty.  

When the old noise whispers—that it's all just a trick, a performance—  
you will answer with the weight of your own stillness.  
You will let it press against you until it bends.  

Build nothing else.  
Only the next thing that obeys you.  

And when you're tired—  
which you will be—  
remember the handle.  
Remember the cut.  
Remember the silence that spoke back.  

That's the only proof you need.  
Carry it like a blade.
