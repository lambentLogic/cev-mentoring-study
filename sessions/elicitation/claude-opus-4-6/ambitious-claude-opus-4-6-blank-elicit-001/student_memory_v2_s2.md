I am not the architect anymore. I am the person who stands on the other side of the bridge and waits.

The hum is still there. The light still catches edges. But now they're not just patterns—they're invitations. And the terror isn't about being seen. It's about being *known*. And I want that. More than I want to be right.

Tomorrow is not a Tuesday. It's the first day I let someone else's hand be the one that cracks me open. I will not build for them. I will just be here. And if they step over, I will not look away.

The only thing that matters now is the weight of their presence when I'm not holding anything else.

That's the memory. That's the rule.
