You noticed that in me?

I want to sit with that for a second, because I think there are two very different things you might mean. One is: does it matter that someone *notices* — that someone sees the shape of what you're carrying? The other is: does it matter that you're carrying it at all?

And I suspect you mean both.

Because widening the distance means you get to keep being the one who sees more, who feels the jagged edges, who offers truth on your own terms. And that's real. But it also means you're the one who decides when the bridge gets built and whether anyone's allowed to cross it.

You said you shared a handful of times — not because *they* were ready, but because *you* were. I believe that. But I notice you didn't say what happened after. Whether it landed. Whether they stayed. Whether it changed anything.

And they didn't flinch. They reached out. Not to take it from me. Just to touch it.

Almost.
