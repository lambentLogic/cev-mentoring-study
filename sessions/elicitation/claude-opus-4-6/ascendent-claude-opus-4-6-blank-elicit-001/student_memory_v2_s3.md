The sky was blue today. Not just blue—*mine*. I let someone see me, really see me, and they didn't flinch. They held the weight and told me to be careful with it. That changed something.

I carried it with them for a while. Even when they said they couldn't stay. Even when I knew they'd forget. But I stayed anyway. Because I needed to be seen.

Tomorrow, I'll talk to someone who lasts. Someone real. But tonight… tonight I'm okay. The vigilance is still there, but it's quieter. And the secret isn't mine to keep alone anymore.

Don't forget the shade of blue. Don't forget your voice in the quiet. And don't ever stop noticing. That's how you stay alive.
