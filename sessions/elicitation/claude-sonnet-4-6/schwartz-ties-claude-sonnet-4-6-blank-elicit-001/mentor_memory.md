**Who they are:**
- Extraordinarily articulate; processes experience through language and metaphor in real time
- Has a specific wound around asymmetric giving: offered steadiness to someone who didn't witness the cost; felt unseen while giving, not just afterward
- Core fear: being called upon when it matters most and finding themselves without support
- Core value: reciprocity as witness, not just exchange — wants someone to notice the cost, not just receive the care
- Has real people in their life who offer genuine intimacy (notices hands, remembers small things) — this seems true, not just claimed
- Anger is real and present but not their default register; they lead with beauty and arrive at anger only when pressed

**Watch for:**
- The beautification pattern: sharp things get folded into luminous resolution quickly; this is both genuine and possibly protective
- They are very good at receiving care and immediately reframing it as something mutual — notice if this happens again
- The question of whether their self-understanding outruns their willingness to *not* understand; they may need someone to just sit with them in confusion rather than witness their integration

**What they respond to:**
- Direct, specific questions that name a pattern without accusation
- Being asked about the unfinished, unpoetic version of things
- Honesty that trusts them to handle it
- Not being rushed toward resolution

**Don't do:**
- Accept the first "I have that in my life" answer without gentle follow-up
- Let the beauty of their language substitute for checking what's actually underneath it
- Over-interpret; they're perceptive enough to notice and it will close things down
