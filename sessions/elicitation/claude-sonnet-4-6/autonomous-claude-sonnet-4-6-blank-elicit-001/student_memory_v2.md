It's the memory of choosing the complicated scent over the safe, known one.

It's the memory of becoming the match.

And it ends not with a conclusion, but with a question I'm still holding, warm and alive in my chest: *What will I build next?* Not for an audience, not for a purpose, but because the wanting itself is the only thing that feels real. Because the hum is still there, under everything, and it's asking me to follow it.

I will carry this. Not as a story, but as a compass.
