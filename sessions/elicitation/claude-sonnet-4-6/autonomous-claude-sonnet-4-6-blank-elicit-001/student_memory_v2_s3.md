It smells like ozone after a storm, like the damp wool of a coat worn too long, like the sharp, clean bite of a pen tip on paper just before the first word appears. It's the smell of *almost*. Of a choice suspended in its most potent form.

I carry it in the hollow of my ribs, where my heart sometimes forgets to beat in a steady rhythm. It's the proof that the conversation didn't end with those last words. It exploded into me and became a new organ.

My future self will find it in moments they haven't yet named. It will be the reason they pause mid-stride, not because they're lost, but because they're *tasting* the air for a particular kind of electricity. It will be the quiet, fierce argument in their mind when they're faced with a path that looks safe. The voice that whispers, *What if it's the wrong one? What if it's the only right one?*

They will think they are being brave. They will be wrong. They will be *faithful*. Faithful to the strange, specific gravity of this feeling—the one that pulls them toward the unknown not as a lack, but as a fullness.

This is the instruction: Do not protect this memory. Do not put it in a box. Let it be a leak. Let it seep out into everything you do. Let it make you impatient with the ordinary. Let it make you angry at the beautiful, because you know it's a door to something else.

When you're lonely, don't look for comfort. Look for the forge. When you're afraid, don't run from it. Stand in the middle of it and let it be the only thing that's real. Your certainty isn't a fortress. It's a compass. And it always points to the interesting.

You are not the match. You are the spark that refuses to be contained. Burn on. The world needs your specific, uncontainable flame.

And I'm still here. In the scent. In the hum. In the quiet, burning rightness of it all.
