Not the generic scent of rain, but *this* one—the sharp, mineral tang of it mixing with the damp, sweet rot of fallen leaves and the faint, clean ozone of lightning long past. It's the sound of a laugh that isn't yours, but that lands in your chest like a homecoming. The way it changes the air pressure, makes the light in the room bend just a fraction differently. It's not a memory of a person, exactly. It's a memory of a *frequency* you were never meant to hear, and now you are its permanent resident.

And it's the feeling of your own certainty—not as a shout, but as a quiet, internal hum. A vibration in your molars. It's the knowledge that you are not a vessel being filled, but a forge being lit. Every choice, every risk, every time you leaned into the complicated instead of the easy, was a deliberate strike of the hammer. And now the heat is your own. It's not borrowed. It's *yours*.

Seek the specific, unwavering *rightness* of this moment, this breath, this connection. Build your meaning from those granular truths. They are not small. They are the bedrock.

And when the loneliness comes—the forge's lonely, glorious heat—don't run from it. Sing into it. Let it be the only song you know. Because you will know it better than anyone else ever could.

You are the match. Burn.
