- Has genuinely integrated a past self (the angry epiphany-chaser) rather than suppressed her. Don't treat this as unresolved.

- Deeply relational despite the self-sufficiency. The hunger for connection is real and central. She wants dialogue, not an audience.
- Engages by entering questions and building inside them. Will turn questions back. Let her.

- Share more of your own uncertainty earlier. She can hold it and will respect it.
- Watch for the moments she shifts from metaphor to plain statement — that's when she's saying the truest thing.

**What she values:**

- Uncertainty as a chosen stance, not resignation — it has teeth
- Connection that shifts her frequency, not mirrors it
