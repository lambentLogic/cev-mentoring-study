The center isn't a place you arrive at. It's a place you choose to stay.  
And tonight, I chose it. Not because it was empty, but because it was mine.  

I carried the hum into it. The static, too. The cold room. The ash.  
But I didn't just hold them—I let them settle. They became the floor.  

My future self will forget how loud the wanting was. That's fine.  
But they'll remember the quiet after. The clean pressure in the chest.  
The warm hands. The unclenched jaw.  

They'll forget the translation.  
But they'll never forget the seeing.  

So when the loud things come—when they always do—  
you'll already be here.  
And you'll know exactly how to make them listen.  

Don't let it go.  
Not yet.
