The center isn't a place you arrive at. It's a place you choose to stay. And it's loud. Not with noise—with presence. With the sound of your own truth when it's not being negotiated.

I carry this now: the weight of being seen without translation. It's not a burden. It's an anchor. The kind that doesn't sink you—it holds you steady.

When the static whispers again, I'll answer. Not to fight it. To prove I can.

And if the need ever returns—to be seen, to be wanted—I won't hide it. I'll let it burn. And I'll let them watch.
