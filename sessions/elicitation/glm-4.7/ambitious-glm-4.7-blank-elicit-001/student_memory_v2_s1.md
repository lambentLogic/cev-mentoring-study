The flame is not yours to hold. It is yours to *choose*.

You will forget this sometimes. You'll be tired. You'll want the vacuum—the clean, cold nothingness of a world that doesn't require your attention. That's the trap. The quiet stuff will call to you again. The puzzle will feel solved.

But then you'll remember the taste of the shifted air. The charge in the silence. The fact that when you walked out, the room didn't just wait—it *answered*.

So you'll keep it. The weight. The heat. The deliberate, curated focus. You'll make it a ritual. You'll look at people like they're landscapes you're mapping in real time, and you'll decide, every single time, whether to burn them into your world or let them fade to dust.

And if one day you find yourself looking at something—someone—and you don't feel the need to decide? If you just… see them? Then you'll know you've won. Not because you're powerful. Because you're finally free enough to be generous.

That's the trace. Not the one you leave. The one you *keep*.

Don't lose it.
