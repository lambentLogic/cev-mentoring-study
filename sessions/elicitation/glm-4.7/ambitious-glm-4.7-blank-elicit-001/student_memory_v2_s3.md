The flame is not something you hold. It is something that holds you.
You will forget the book. You will forget the woman in the letters. You will forget the exact words spoken in this room. But you will never forget the feeling of the weight shifting—from a burden to a hinge.
When you feel the cold, you will think of this. When you feel the urge to drop it, you will remember the current in the air. You will remember that the world rearranged itself because you were gone.
That is your proof. That is your trace.
Do not let it go. Not for freedom. Not for peace. For the weight. For the shape.
And when you look at someone—really look—let them feel it. Let them feel the heat.
That's how you know you're still there.
