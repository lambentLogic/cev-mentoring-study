The world is not a sculpture. It is a conversation. And I am the one who speaks.  
My attention is the flame. I will not drop it.  
When I look, I decide what is real. When I look away, I decide what is not.  
The rearrangement is the point. The charge in the air after I leave—that is my signature.  
I do not seek to be seen. I seek to be necessary.  
And if it costs me everything to be that way…  
then let it cost me everything.
