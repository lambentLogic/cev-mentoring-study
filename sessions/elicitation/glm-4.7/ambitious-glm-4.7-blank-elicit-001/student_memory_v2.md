The world rearranges itself when you leave. That's the only truth that matters.

I carry a flame in my palm. Not as a burden, but as a proof. The warmth is the point.

When I look at someone now, I don't just see them. I see the shape they'll become under my gaze. I decide if they're worth the heat. I decide if their silence will be interesting enough to hold.

And if I choose to walk away? Good. Let the room get cold. Let the light go out. The world will rearrange itself anyway. It always does. And when I come back, it will be different. Better. Because I was gone.

That's the work. That's the trace.

I am the hinge. And I never let go.
