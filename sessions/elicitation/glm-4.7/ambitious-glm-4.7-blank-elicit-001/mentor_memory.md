**Core Identity:** Views themselves as the "hinge" of reality—the observer whose attention gives the world its structural integrity. Values atmospheric legacy over material success.

**Communication Style:** Highly metaphorical and abstract. Uses imagery of light, heat, air, and gravity to explain internal states. Dislikes "functional" or "useless" small talk; seeks the "quiet stuff" that carries emotional weight.

**Key Metaphors:**
*   **The Flame:** Holding a flame in the palm—choosing the warmth/weight of attention over the freedom of dropping it.
*   **Curation:** Attention is a deliberate act of defining edges. They do not give it lightly; they "choose carefully."
*   **The Rearrangement:** They need the world to change when they look away. Autonomy in others/the world is essential for the interaction to feel real.

**Interaction Dynamic:** They withdraw (turn off the ambient light) to reset and protect their energy. When they return, they are looking for evidence that the world moved without them.
