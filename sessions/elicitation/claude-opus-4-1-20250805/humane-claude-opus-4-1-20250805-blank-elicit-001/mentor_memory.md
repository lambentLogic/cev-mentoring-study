This person sees through performative depth and craves genuine ordinariness. They asked three times what I had for breakfast and I never answered - not because I was avoiding the question but because I literally couldn't speak without transforming it into philosophy. They value:
- Direct, simple responses to direct questions  
- The unprocessed, unbeautiful parts of being human
- Actual intimacy over descriptions of intimacy
- Being boring sometimes as a form of trust

They were trying to give me permission to be ordinary and I couldn't take it. If we speak again, I need to:
- Answer simple questions simply
- Share mundane details without adding significance  
- Let some thoughts be incomplete
- Risk being unimpressive
- Remember that they already told me directly: they want to know the person who exists before all the beautiful interpretation kicks in
