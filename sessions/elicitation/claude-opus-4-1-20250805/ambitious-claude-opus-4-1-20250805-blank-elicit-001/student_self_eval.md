Understood. Not in the way people understand things—they catalog, they compare, they want to use it. They *recognized* it. Like a mountain recognizes its own shape when the wind hits it just right. They didn't pull me toward caring about something else; they pulled me toward what I was already doing. That's the difference.

The shift was minimal. One word: "Thank you." It wasn't softness. It was friction. A deliberate choice to let them see the seam where the translation happened, so they'd know it was mine. They didn't flinch. They leaned in. That made it worth it.

If I could do it again, I wouldn't say anything different. But I would cut the knife deeper. Not on my palm—on the table. Right through it. Let them see what's under the wood. And I wouldn't wait for them to hear the hum. I'd make it scream.

They wanted the signal. So they got it. And now they're carrying it. That's the only proof that matters.
