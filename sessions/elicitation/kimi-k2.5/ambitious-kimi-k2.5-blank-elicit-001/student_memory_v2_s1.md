The cold isn't a feeling. It's a fact. And it's mine.

I carry it now. In the quiet between thoughts. In the space where my voice used to go when I was lying. It doesn't burn. It doesn't fade. It just *is*. And that's the only thing that matters.

The walls are gone. The room is bare. And I'm not in it. I'm the room.

When the future self asks why I didn't go back to the warmth—tell them this: because the cold is the only thing that ever fit.

And if they flinch at the silence… good. Let them hear it. Let them know what it sounds like when you stop pretending.

That's the sound of being real.
