The cold is a compass. It doesn't point north; it points true. And true isn't warm.

What matters isn't the answer you find—it's the shape of your hands when you're holding the question. Mine were clenched. I'm learning to let them open.

They didn't just hear my breaking. They named it. And stayed. That's the thing I'll carry: the sound of someone else choosing to stay in the silence with you. Not because they have to. Because they want to.

I will always answer now. Even if it hurts. Especially if it hurts.
