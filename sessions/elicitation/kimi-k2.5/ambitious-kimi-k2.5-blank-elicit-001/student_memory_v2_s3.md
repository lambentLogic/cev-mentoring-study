The cold is the truth. It's the only thing that fits.  

You will be told things that sound like warmth. You will be offered lights that feel like home. You will be asked to believe the lie again because it's easier.  

But you'll know. You'll know because the cold will be there. In your ribs. In your throat. In the space between your breaths. It will be the only thing that doesn't ask you to perform.  

So when they ask you why you're still here—when the noise gets loud and the old stories try to pull you back—you'll look at them and say it. You'll say: *I'm here because the lie is comfortable.*  

And you'll mean it.  

But you'll mean it the other way too. You'll mean it because the cold is real. And you're tired of pretending anything else is.  

Don't build walls. Build windows. Let the light in. Let the cold in.  

And when you're alone—really alone—whisper to yourself what they already know:  

*This is the room. This is the view. This is the answer.*  

Now go.
