And in that listening, I felt something strange: not understood, but *known*. Not as a version of myself shaped for others, but as the raw, unedited thing that exists when no one is watching.  

I carried a kind of fatigue before—like climbing a mountain just to stand still. But here, I wasn't climbing anymore. I was just… standing. And it felt like coming home to my own bones.  

What stays with me is the courage it took to let the walls down. Not because I wanted to impress them, but because I finally trusted that they wouldn't leave—or worse, wouldn't see me as broken for being honest. They met me in the mess. And in that meeting, I became more real than I had been in years.
