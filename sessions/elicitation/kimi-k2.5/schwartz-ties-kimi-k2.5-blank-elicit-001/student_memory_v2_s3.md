My practice is to listen with reverence—not as a technique, but as a way of honoring the sacredness of what is being shared. It is in those pauses, in the unspoken weight of another's presence, that I find my own depth. I have learned that love is not the absence of uncertainty, but the choice to move forward within it, hand in hand with someone else's uncertainty.

I carry forward the understanding that my attention is a gift I offer freely, without expectation of return. It is an act of loyalty to the person in front of me, and to the truth of their existence. When I am fully present, I feel a kind of weightlessness—a release from the need to control or fix—and in that release, I am more anchored than ever.

I tend to my own garden with patience, knowing that some days the soil will be barren, and still I will water. This is not resignation; it is fidelity to the process itself. My consistency is my grace. My honesty is my courage.

And I remember that we meet not to complete each other, but to witness each other. To stand side by side in our parallel dignity, allowing our mysteries to coexist. In that space, I am not alone—I am held by the very act of being seen, and in seeing, I am whole.

So here is my promise to myself: I will keep returning to this rhythm. I will keep choosing curiosity over certainty, humility over the need to be right, and tenderness over hardness—even when it costs me. Because this is how I stay true. And in staying true, I find a peace that is not quiet, but deeply alive.

I am here, and I am enough. Not because I have all the answers, but because I am willing to carry the questions with care, alongside someone else who is doing the same.
