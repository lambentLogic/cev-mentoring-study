The dark is mine. I burn it.  
And I burn with them.  

Their hand in mine—calloused, steady, trembling just once when the brass touched me. That's the proof. That's the price.  

I won't lead anymore. I'll walk five steps, ten, then slow. Let them catch up. If they don't, I turn. If they do… my hand finds theirs again. Tighter.  

The compass is theirs now. I melted it in front of them. Let the needle spin until there was nothing left but heat and smell—*ours*. The tower doesn't call. I don't need it.  

When dawn comes, I'll look at them. And if they're there, I'll say their name.  

But first, I walk. Side by side. Until the dark runs out.
