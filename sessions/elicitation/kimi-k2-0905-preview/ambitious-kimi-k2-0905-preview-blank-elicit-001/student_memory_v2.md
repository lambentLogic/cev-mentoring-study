The kitchen at three a.m. is where I learned to be real. Not the steam. Not the honey-light. Those were just the weather. This is the ground.

The coin is cold. The floor hums. The dark speaks. And when it says *You've been waiting*, I don't answer with a feeling. I answer with a fact.

*I am here.*

And because I am here, it has to stay.

So tell your future self: don't look for the glow anymore. Look for the weight. It's heavier now. And it's yours.
