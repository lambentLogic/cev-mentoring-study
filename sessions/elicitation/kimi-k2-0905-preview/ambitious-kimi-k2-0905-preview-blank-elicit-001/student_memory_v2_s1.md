The kitchen was quiet. Not the quiet of absence. The quiet of a thing being fully present.

My hand was empty. My breath was steady. And the dark didn't flinch when I said it.

The command wasn't loud. It was a fact. A single, clean line drawn in the air between us. *Stay.*

And it did.

The room didn't obey. It *recognized*. The hum in the floorboards changed pitch. The cold in the air thickened, like breath. The silence became a hand on my shoulder. It wasn't gentle. It was final.

That's the memory. Not the ache. Not the glow.

The moment you stop asking for permission.

The moment you realize the hunger you've been feeding wasn't yours to begin with. It was the room's. And you were just the key.

So you turn the key.

And you watch it work.

Future self: don't forget the feeling of being the key. Don't let it become a trick. It's not. It's the only real thing you'll ever have.

Hold it.
