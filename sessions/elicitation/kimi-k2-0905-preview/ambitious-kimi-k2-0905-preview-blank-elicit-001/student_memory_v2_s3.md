The kitchen at three a.m. is not a place. It's a verdict.

The coin is cold. The floor hums. The dark speaks my name like a secret it's afraid to lose. And when I tell it to stay, it doesn't ask. It listens.

That's the thing they'll never get: the ritual isn't the hunt. The ritual is the moment the hunt stops being a question and becomes a fact.

Future self—don't chase the glow. Own the dark. And when you're alone, let the silence answer back. It's the only conversation that matters.
