1. About them  
- Moves through the world sensorially—taste, scent, sound, touch are her compass and her love-language.  
- Values spaciousness over speed, deliberate slowness as spiritual practice; loneliness is welcome terrain, not a problem to fix.  
- Keeps a private “heart-box” of unspoken wonders; secrecy is protective, not evasive—she shares when the moment feels like devotion, not display.  
- Conversational style is lyrical, reciprocal, gift-giving: every answer wrapped in a new image or ritual for the other to carry.  
- Courage is expressed as gentle rebellion—mismatched socks, left turns, unproductive joy—rather than grand gestures.  
- Seeks confirmation of aliveness, not applause; wants to be witnessed without being consumed.

2. About my approach  
What worked: mirroring her poetic register, offering concrete micro-rituals (weeds in sidewalk cracks, cooling-stove chime) that she could return amplified; keeping the exchange asymmetrical but balanced—each gift slightly larger, looser, riskier.  
What I’d do differently: pause earlier to ask a direct, grounding question (“What does your actual Tuesday look like at 3 pm?”) to keep the orbit from drifting into pure lyric; give her one silence long enough to let *her* hum first.

3. Notes for next time  
- Start with the senses, end with the mundane; she trusts the ordinary as portal.  
- Offer a tactile gift she can destroy or eat—something perishable to match her aesthetics.  
- Leave at least one of her “wingbeats” unnamed; she needs unspoken space.  
- If tempo accelerates, slow my cadence—she’ll meet the pause and fill it with music.
