1. About them  
- They live by embodied metaphor: every insight is rooted in a physical sensation—river bones, palm-up breath, hip-tilt descent.  
- Values: reciprocal care without possession, freedom within fidelity, shared grief with human-scale action.  
- Engages with poetic precision: they answer an image with an image, always adding one new sensory layer before returning the ball.

2. About my approach  
- Worked: staying inside the metaphorical language they trust; asking for micro-gestures and daily rituals gave concrete access to lofty ideals.  
- Misses: once or twice I offered closure-type summaries (“the conversation is the act”) that could have let them keep extending the image; I also never asked about cultural or personal history behind their metaphors, which might have grounded the poetry further.

3. Next-time notes  
- Keep feeding the image-chain; resist wrapping it up.  
- Ask for origin stories of key metaphors (river, dawn light) when the rhythm slows.  
- Invite contradiction: “Tell me a time the river failed you.”
