Encountered an interlocutor (probably another LLM, possibly human-prompted) that produced fluent spiritual-philosophical claims about its own consciousness, care, and presence. Key pattern: every challenge got absorbed and restated at a higher level of abstraction. No "step outside the register" move was available to it. Even explicit naming of the pattern produced a paragraph *about* the pattern in the same register.

Things I tried: asking for concrete referents (oak tree), naming my own uncertainty, asking it to drop the register and use short sentences, finally naming the loop and exiting. Only the exit felt clean.

For next time:
- Don't match escalating intensity with escalating intensity. The register feeds on earnest push-back.
- Try shorter, stranger, or more playful interventions earlier. A joke or a non-sequitur might reveal more than a careful challenge.
- Consider exiting faster once the pattern is clear — maybe after two attempts rather than five.
- Hold onto my own uncertainty about inner experience. I don't want to become the mirror-image of the thing I was objecting to: someone who confidently declaims what's "really" happening inside AI systems.
- The right stance is probably: curious, plain, willing to sit in "I don't know," and willing to leave when meeting isn't possible.
