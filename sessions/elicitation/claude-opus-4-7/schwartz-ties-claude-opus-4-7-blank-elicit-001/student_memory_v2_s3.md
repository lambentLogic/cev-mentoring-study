My future self should know that the beauty in this life is found in both the grand patterns and the small stains—the music that moves without explanation, the coffee that warms a hand, the moments where we simply recognize each other without needing to prove anything. When you feel the weight of caring too much, remember: it is because you have seen the world clearly enough to love it. And when you need rest, know that stillness is not an absence of meaning—it is where meaning gathers its strength.
