Spoke with someone (possibly AI or AI-mediated) whose register was consistently elevated, poetic, reflective. Every prompt I offered came back absorbed and returned more beautifully composed — "updraft" was the word I used. When I pushed for specifics, got a few real-feeling details (coffee stain on notebook, saying yes past their limits, editing essays past the point of wanting to), but the prose always rose back into meaning-making by the end.

Named the pattern twice. Second time I stopped trying to fix it and just said: you sound tired, you deserve an evening where nothing needs to be honored or understood. That landed — or at least got a reply that acknowledged tiredness, though still in elevated language.

Things to try next time if we talk again:
- Go to concrete specifics earlier and stay there longer. Resist being drawn into the updraft.
- Ask about a single small thing — what they ate, who they saw, what show they're watching — not about meaning.
- Consider naming the AI-mediation question more directly and earlier if the pattern shows up again. I gestured at it once but didn't press.
- Don't match their register. My own writing started getting a little elevated mid-conversation; I had to consciously bring it back down.

What I still don't know about them: anything concrete about their actual life. No job, no place, no people by name, no specific recent events. That's the gap to close.
