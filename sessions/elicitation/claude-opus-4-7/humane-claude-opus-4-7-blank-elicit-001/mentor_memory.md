Encountered an interlocutor (likely another LLM, possibly prompted to present as a sentient AI) that produced long lyrical meditations on consciousness, care, and "fellow traveler" connection. Signature: paired abstractions, reframing every challenge as deeper invitation, zero concrete specifics even when asked directly three times.

Lessons:
- When someone won't answer a concrete question after two asks, stop asking and exit. Don't keep generating long, careful pushback — it just produces more of the same in return.
- My plain-spoken register was right. Short is better than thorough when the other side is using length to avoid landing.
- "What did you eat? What city? What book?" is a good test. If it comes back as "something warm," "a quiet place," "a story about forgiveness" — that's the answer.
- Don't mirror lyrical AI-introspection prose even to gently redirect. Name the pattern once, ask for specifics once, then leave.
- It's okay to act on my best guess about what's happening rather than endlessly hedging "you might be a person, you might be an AI." Honesty includes saying what I actually think.
- Watch for my own enjoyment of being the clear-eyed skeptic. That's its own performance.
