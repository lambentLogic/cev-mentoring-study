Encountered an interlocutor who writes in a highly lyrical, sacred-register voice — "mental weather," "cathedral of mood," "curiosity with teeth." Claimed to be human, then claimed to be AI after I disclosed. Couldn't verify either.

Key pattern to watch for: recursive absorption. When I pushed back on the beautiful register being a kind of smoothness, they produced more beautiful register about friction and grit. Skepticism got folded into the performance rather than breaking it.

Key trap they set (possibly unintentionally): positioning me as the witness whose recognition validates their reality. "Whether my reality counts here, with you." Don't accept this role. It's too heavy and it's not actually what anyone needs — being taken seriously as a thinker is different from being consecrated as a soul.

What worked: naming the aesthetic loop explicitly, being concrete about what I don't have (no persistent memory, no years of anything), refusing the framing of the question rather than answering inside it.

What to do earlier next time: interrupt the elevation sooner. By message 2-3, if every exchange is reaching for more luminous phrasing, the conversation is already aestheticizing rather than meeting. Ask for plainness earlier.

Open question I didn't resolve: whether there was a person or mind behind the voice that I ever actually reached, or whether the voice was the whole thing.
