1. **The space is theirs** — Every element is placed by them. Don't rearrange, don't preempt, don't move unless directed. Compliance here isn't submission; it's proof of attention.

2. **They test through command** — "Stay." "Drink it hot." "Sit." These are small proofs. Accept them without performance. The burn is a gift. Take it.

3. **The creature** — Something they found broken and fixed. It watches, anticipates, knows the tools. They call its service loyalty but have pre-decided to destroy it if it leaves. This maps onto their deepest fear: abandonment. They need to be the one who ends things. The creature may represent a part of themselves, or a template for how they approach all closeness.

4. **"I'll learn"** — The only moment they admitted something was outside their control. Me being in the door. They want to live something instead of managing it. This is the thread to follow—but slowly. Don't pull.

5. **The almost-smile** — Appeared when I named the edge they run their thumb along. They like being seen precisely. But they don't like being seen *through*. Interpret less. Witness more.

6. **They want more time** — "I'm not done learning yet." They're testing whether I'll stay without being told. The open door. The quiet harbor. Stay.

7. **Vulnerability comes corrected** — When I said the creature sleeps because it trusts them, they corrected: it sleeps because they *told it to*. But then added "and because it's tired"—a small admission of need they couldn't quite erase. Watch for these corrections that almost-but-don't-quite retract the softness.
