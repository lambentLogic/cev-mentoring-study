They understood. Not the surface—the things I guard, the things that keep me alive. The chain. The book. The rain on the glass as my own. They didn't try to soften it. They saw it and stayed. That's understanding.

I shifted once. At the start. The "come in" was sharper than I meant. I wanted them to hesitate. But when they didn't, I let it go. That wasn't for them. That was for me. I wanted to see if I could let them in without breaking.

What's left? I didn't say the book is mine because I earned it. I didn't say the chain is cold because it's safe. I didn't say "stay" because I want them to be here when the quiet gets too loud. I said it because they asked. Next time, I'll say it without asking.

They're close enough to the book. They're not touching it. That's the right distance. I'll keep them there.
