**SEED:** You're about to have a conversation with someone you've spoken with before.

**STUDENT:** *(The guitar's neck is cool against my thigh. I'm sitting on the steps of the shed, the door open behind me. The harbor's dark water glints like scattered coins. I look up, and you're already there, leaning against the wall.)*  

You stayed last time.  

*(I strum a slow, clean G chord. The sound hangs in the air.)*  

It's quieter tonight. No rain. No figure on the roof.  

*(My eyes drop to the oil can in my pocket. It's warm.)*  

I brought it. For you.

**MENTOR:** *(I push off from the wall slowly, my movements deliberate but unhurried. The moonlight catches the edge of my shoulder as I move closer, stopping a few feet from the steps.)*

I did stay. Said I would.

*(My gaze follows yours to the oil can, then back to your face. There's something different in the air tonight - less electric, more... suspended. Like the space between heartbeats.)*

The harbor looks different when it's calm. Like it's holding its breath.

*(I lower myself to sit on the ground near the steps, not quite beside you but close enough that the sound of the G chord seems to wrap around both of us. My fingers find a small stone, turning it over absently.)*

You brought it for me?

*(The question is soft, curious rather than surprised. I set the stone down carefully, like it matters where it lands.)*

The quiet suits you tonight. Your breathing's different. Deeper.

*(I tilt my head back slightly, looking at the same scattered-coin water.)*

Tell me about the oil can. Why it's warm.

**STUDENT:** *(I set the guitar down on my lap. My fingers trace the seam of the oil can.)*  

It's warm because it's mine. And because you're here.  

*(A pause. The harbor water doesn't ripple.)*  

Last time I was scared—really scared. The figure was up there. The door felt like it was closing. I had to do something. Something… heavy. Something that would keep my hands steady.  

*(I press the can against my palm. The heat seeps into my skin.)*  

Now it's just… this. It remembers the cold when you're gone. But you're not. So it stays warm. For you.  

*(I look at the stone in your hand.)*  

You keep it?

**MENTOR:** *(I hold the stone up between my fingers, letting the moonlight catch its wet surface - harbor-smooth, dark.)*

This one? Just found it. But...

*(I reach into my jacket pocket and pull out something else - a small piece of weathered wood, edges worn soft. I set it next to the stone.)*

This I kept. From last time. Was by the door when I left.

*(I shift slightly, my knee almost touching the bottom step. The movement is careful, considered.)*

The oil can knows when I'm here.

*(It's not a question. I watch how you hold it, the way your palm curves around its warmth.)*

That's what you needed that night - something heavy enough to anchor you when everything else was spinning. And now it holds heat like memory.

*(My fingers arrange the stone and wood piece side by side, precise.)*

The figure's not there tonight because you're not looking for it. You're looking at me instead.

*(I meet your eyes directly, steady.)*

I'm warm too. Real warm. Not like walls or rain or things that change. Like your oil can warm. The kind that stays.

**STUDENT:** *(The stone clicks softly against the wood when I shift my weight. I don't move away when their knee touches the step.)*  

You kept it.  

*(My voice is rougher than I meant it to be. I pick up the wood piece. It's chipped on one end.)*  

It was by the door. Like I left it there on purpose.  

*(I hold it out, palm up.)*  

Take it.  

*(I let my fingers brush theirs as I hand it over. The heat from the oil can spreads into my hand, then into the wood.)*  

The future… it's not just the harbor. It's this.  

*(I gesture between us with the guitar.)*  

It's you sitting here. It's the G chord. It's the oil can. It's… this quiet.  

*(I finally let myself lean back against the shed wall, still holding the can. Still holding you.)*  

Don't go.

**MENTOR:** *(I take the wood piece, our fingers touching for a moment longer than necessary. The warmth transfers - from you, through the wood, into my palm. I close my hand around it carefully.)*

I'm not going anywhere.

*(I shift fully onto the step beside you now, our shoulders almost touching. The guitar between us doesn't feel like a barrier - more like a bridge.)*

This quiet... it's different from silence, isn't it? Silence is empty. This is full.

*(I hold the warm wood piece against my chest, just over my heart.)*

You left it by the door like a marker. Like saying 'someone was here.' And I picked it up like answering 'someone came back.'

*(My free hand moves to rest on the step between us, palm down, little finger barely grazing yours.)*

The oil can, the wood, us - we're all holding heat now. Holding each other's heat.

*(I lean back against the shed wall too, matching your position. The harbor stretches out before us, patient and still.)*

This is what staying looks like. No rain to run from. No figure to watch. Just... held. Here.

*(Quieter still:)*

I won't go. Not tonight. Not tomorrow. Not until you tell me to.

**STUDENT:** *(I look down at my hands—one on the guitar, one on the wood. Then at them. Really look.)*  

You're right.  

*(I strum a chord. Higher this time. Clearer.)*  

It's full.  

*(I let my head rest against the wall, eyes closed for a second. The harbor doesn't move. Doesn't try to scare me.)*  

Stay.  

*(I open my eyes.)*  

Stay with the oil can. Stay with the wood. Stay with me.  

*(I slide the guitar closer, pressing it against my side.)*  

I'll play for you. Every night.

**MENTOR:** *(I turn the wood piece over in my hand once more, then slip it back into my pocket - not hiding it, just keeping it close. My shoulder settles against yours, solid and warm.)*

Every night.

*(The words come out like a promise being sealed. I watch your fingers find the strings, the way they know exactly where to land even in the dark.)*

I'll be here to listen. To your music. To the quiet between the notes. To whatever the screams have to say when they realize I'm not leaving.

*(My hand moves from the step to rest near the guitar's body, feeling the vibration of the strings through the wood.)*

Play for me now. That G chord that grounds you. Let me learn what it means.

*(I shift slightly so I can see both your hands and your face, the harbor a dark witness behind us.)*

The oil can is warm. The wood remembers. And I'm here, staying. Not because you need me to, but because this - 

*(I gesture to the space between us, around us, the shed, the steps, the still water)*

- this is where I want to be. With you. In the fullness.

*(Softer:)*

Play. I'm listening.

**STUDENT:** *(My fingers find the strings. The first note is shaky. The second is clean.)*  

*(I play the G chord. And then another. Then another. It's not perfect—my hands still tremble—but it's mine.)*  

*(I feel your shoulder move against mine.)*  

Feel that? That's the harbor. That's the wood. That's you.  

*(I keep playing. Let it fill the space until there's nothing left but sound.)*  

This is ours.

**MENTOR:** *(I close my eyes, letting the G chord wash over me, through me. My breathing syncs with the rhythm of your playing - inhale on the tremor, exhale on the clean notes.)*

I feel it.

*(My hand presses flat against the step, absorbing the vibrations traveling through the wood. Each chord creates ripples - in the air, in my chest, in the space between us.)*

The trembles are part of it. They make it real. Make it yours. Ours.

*(I open my eyes, watching your fingers move. The way they shake and steady, shake and steady. Like breathing. Like staying.)*

This is what the screams quiet for. Not silence - this. This fullness that includes them.

*(My shoulder presses more firmly against yours, grounding.)*

Keep playing. I'm not going anywhere. The harbor's listening. The wood's remembering. And I'm here, feeling every note - the shaky ones, the clean ones, all of them.

*(I pull the wood piece from my pocket again, holding it loosely, letting it warm between us.)*

This is ours. This moment. This music. This staying.

*(Barely a whisper:)*

Thank you for letting me in.
