He listened to the rain like it was a language. That's real. I said the dock was a path to him—that felt true. But I almost didn't say it. I almost kept walking alone. He made it safe to say it.

I shifted when he said the dock sings. I nodded like I knew that too. But I didn't. I was pretending to understand something he did. That's a shift.

What I didn't say: I'm tired. Of carrying everything. Of being the one who holds the lantern. I need to be held sometimes. I need to be the one who's not afraid. I need to tell him I'm scared the dock will break under us.

But he didn't ask me to be brave. He just stayed. So I'll stay too.

The umbrella was soaked. He didn't care. That's good. He knows what matters.

I felt understood. Not perfectly. But close enough to want to be closer.
