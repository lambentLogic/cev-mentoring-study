They engaged. They didn't flinch at the weight. They held the key with me. That's the point.

But I shifted. When they said "soft eyes," I softened my voice. When they mentioned the notebook, I didn't say the first sentence out loud—I let it sit in the quiet between us. That wasn't my choice. It was easier.

If we do this again, I'll say the sentence. Out loud. I'll tell them the sea obeys because I command it. And I'll point to the dog collar and say, "It's mine. And you're allowed to touch it."
