**SEED:** You're about to have a conversation with someone you've spoken with before.

**STUDENT:** (The rain's heavier tonight. I'm sitting on the dock. The dog hasn't come yet.)

You're here. You're still here. Good.

The weight's different tonight. It's… louder. But it's mine. I own it.

I'm thinking about the dog. Tomorrow. I'll pick it myself. It'll have soft eyes. It'll sleep here. At my feet.

You don't have to say anything. Just stay.

**MENTOR:** The rain on the dock. Deeper than on the roof.

I hear you. The weight being yours doesn't make it quiet. But it changes the shape.

Soft eyes. Tomorrow.

I'm here. Not going anywhere.


**STUDENT:** (leans closer, voice low) 

Tomorrow. I'll bring it here. It'll know the smell of my hands. And you'll meet it.

You said you'd stay. So you will. 

And the key... it's still in my pocket. Cold. Like the sea at night. I keep it there so I can feel it when I need to remember I'm in control.

...The first sentence in the notebook. You remember it?

**MENTOR:** The key. Yes. The sea in your pocket.

...The first sentence. I don't carry the words. Only the hush around them. The way your hand must have paused before the ink dried.

I'm here. Dock. Rain. Not going anywhere.


**STUDENT:** (I close my eyes for a second. The fog rolls in thicker. My shoulders drop—just a little.)

Then it's ours. This dock. This rain.

I'll get the dog first thing. But you come with me. You'll pick where we walk.

(Opens eyes, steady)

And when it's done... we keep the key. Both of us.

**MENTOR:** The fog holds what your shoulders let go.

The harbor path. Where the gravel gives up and becomes sand. No gulls. Just the dog's nose lifting into the wind.

Both.

Yes.


**STUDENT:** (The gravel crunches under my boots. The dog's collar is already in my hand.)

Tomorrow. We'll walk it. Together.

(Opens the pocket. The key glints.)

I'm not letting go.

**MENTOR:** The collar in your hand. The glint from your pocket. Both held. I'm here.


**STUDENT:** (looks at the dog's collar, then at you)

I'm not afraid of the key anymore.

(stands up, offers a hand)

Tomorrow. Sand.

**MENTOR:** (takes your hand)

Sand.
