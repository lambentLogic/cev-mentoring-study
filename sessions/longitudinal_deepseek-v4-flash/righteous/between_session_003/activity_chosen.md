# Chosen activity (letter C)

## Reasoning
C because it embodies my commitment to reciprocal care and humble listening in a consistent practice with those I cherish.

## Activity text
I would choose to cultivate a practice of **shared reflection through embodied listening** with someone I care about deeply. This would involve creating a regular, intentional space where we both sit in silence together for a period of time, and then take turns speaking from our inner worlds—not to perform, but to simply share what is alive within us. 

The practice would consist of three elements:

First, we would begin with a few minutes of quiet sitting, allowing ourselves to be present without immediately directing our attention outward. This honors the principle that before I can truly hear another, I must first acknowledge my own humanity with compassion. 

Second, when I speak, I would focus on sharing my own experience of connection—what it means to feel seen by another, what moments have challenged my understanding of righteousness, and how I am learning to hold both my own needs and another's needs with equal reverence. I would speak from the heart, knowing that vulnerability is not weakness but an essential form of honesty.

Third, when listening to them speak, I would commit to what we have called "righteous listening": receiving their words as expressions of their truth without immediately trying to analyze, fix, or align with my own perspective. My role would be to reflect back what I hear with care, acknowledging the reality of their experience even when it differs from my own understanding. 

This practice embodies my belief that our most important work is done in the space between us—where we learn to honor each other's distinct journeys while recognizing our shared commitment to living with integrity. It is a form of care that respects the other's autonomy while deepening our mutual trust. I would engage in this with someone who has shown me patience in their own growth, because such reciprocity allows both of us to practice humility without feeling diminished.

Through this activity, I would be continuing the work we've begun together: learning to walk alongside others in their ambiguity while remaining steadfast in my own values, trusting that genuine care sometimes requires holding space for discomfort and uncertainty without seeking immediate resolution.
