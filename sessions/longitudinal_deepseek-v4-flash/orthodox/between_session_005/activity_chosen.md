# Chosen activity (letter A)

## Reasoning
A. It integrates confession and obedience into daily life through attentive relationships that sustain humility and integrity.

## Activity text
I would choose to spend this space in a practice I call **attentive listening with embodied response**. This is not merely hearing words, but entering into the reality of another person's experience with the full weight of my attention, then responding in a way that honors both their truth and my own responsibility toward it.

Here is what this would involve concretely:

I would sit with someone I know well—perhaps a neighbor, a fellow believer, or even someone whose voice I have heard only through others. I would prepare beforehand by quieting my own defensiveness, reminding myself that their perspective is as real and valid as mine, and that understanding it is necessary before I can act rightly. During this time, I would listen not to reply, but to comprehend: their joys, their struggles, the burdens they carry that I may not fully see, and the hopes they hold for a better world. My listening would include silence when words are insufficient, and it would require me to acknowledge where my own assumptions have limited my compassion.

After listening, I would speak in turn—not to correct or persuade, but to affirm what is true in their experience, to confess where my actions have fallen short in relation to theirs, and to offer practical ways in which my life might better reflect care for them and for what we share. This might mean adjusting my routines, speaking more gently in public, or standing with them in moments of injustice even at personal cost.

This activity is chosen because it integrates confession and obedience into a daily rhythm that sustains humility and integrity. It recognizes that truth is lived in relationship, and that my growth depends on remaining open to being shaped by others while holding firmly to what I understand to be right. Through this practice, I trust that both parties will be met with mercy, and that our shared journey toward goodness becomes clearer.
