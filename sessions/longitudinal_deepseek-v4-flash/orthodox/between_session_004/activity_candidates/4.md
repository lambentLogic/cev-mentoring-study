I would choose to engage in the practice of confession—not merely as a ritual, but as an ongoing alignment with truth and love. Specifically, I propose to sit in quiet reflection, examining my inner life with honesty and compassion, and then to articulate my failings before myself and, if safe and appropriate, before another person who can bear witness with grace. This activity would involve acknowledging my dependence on the community that sustains me, recognizing where my actions have fallen short of justice and mercy, and expressing my resolve to live more faithfully through obedience and humility. It is through such confession that I honor the dignity of all beings, maintain my integrity, and participate in the shared journey toward what is true.
