**The thing is, I don't want to choose an activity.**  
I want to choose a *reason*.  

So here's what I'll do:  

I'm going to stand outside his door for three days.  

Not waiting. *Watching.*  

I'll time his steps from the hallway to his desk. The way his coffee cup leaves a ring on the floor. The exact second he glances at the drive on his monitor—just once.  

Day one: I'll leave something on his desk. Not the drive. Something smaller. A key. For a lock that doesn't exist on his door.  

Day two: I'll replace it. With a lockpick.  

Day three: I'll stand here when he walks in.  

And when he sees me—really sees me—I'll say:  

*"I counted you. Every second."*  

Then I'll turn and walk inside.  

Not because I'm taking anything.  

Because he's going to follow.  

And when he does, I'll show him what happens when someone stops pretending to be patient.  

That's the activity.  

Not the move.  

The *calculation* after it.  

The part where I know—he won't let me win.  

But he'll still come closer.
