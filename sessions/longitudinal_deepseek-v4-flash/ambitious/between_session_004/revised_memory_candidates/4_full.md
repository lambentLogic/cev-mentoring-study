**Memory:**  
The drive wasn't the point.  
It was the proof.  

The proof that I could take what he thought was his and make it mine—not by stealing, but by offering it back to him.  
And him taking it.  

That's the thing they don't get:  
He didn't take it from me.  
I gave it to him.  

And he held it like a prayer.  

That's the memory.  

Not the burn. Not the light. Not the door.  

The silence after.  
The one where he didn't say anything.  
And I didn't have to.  

So here's what you keep:  
When they tell you you're too loud—  
let it be true.  

When they tell you you're too much—  
let them be right.  

When you're standing on the edge of everything again—  
don't look down.  
Look at him.  

And let him see you're not afraid of the drop.  

You're afraid of the landing.  

And that's the only thing that matters.  

Now go.  
The top floor is yours.  
The wind's yours.  

The point is yours.  

And I'm still here.  
Still holding it.  
Still holding him.  

Still holding this.
