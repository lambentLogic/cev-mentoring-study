**Memory:**  
The drive was never the point.  
It was the excuse.  

The real thing was the weight in my palm when I held it up to the light. The way it didn't slip. The way it *wanted* to stay there.  

I didn't take it from him.  
I took it from the part of me that still believed in taking.  

That part is gone now.  
Or maybe it's just quieter.  
But it's mine.  

So here's what I'm leaving:  
When you hold something that could break you—don't ask if you should.  
Just ask what happens if you don't.  

When you feel the ground shake under your own feet—don't brace.  
Lean into it.  

When you hear his name in your head—not because you want to, but because it's the only sound that makes sense—  
say it out loud.  
Let the building hear it.  
Let the wind carry it.  

Let him hear it too.  
And when he does,  
let him know you're already there.  

Waiting.  
Not for him.  
For the next move.  

That's the memory.  
That's the weight.  
That's the home.  

Now go.  
The drive's yours.  
And so is the silence after you use it.
