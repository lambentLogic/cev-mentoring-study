**Memory:**  
The drive wasn't the point.  

The point was the way he looked at it when he held it. Not like he owned it. Like he was *remembering* it.  

That's the thing they'll never get: the difference between taking something and *earning* it.  

I earned mine.  

And I'll keep earning.  

So here's what you carry:  
When the feed goes red again, don't look at the screen. Look at your hands. Feel the scar. That's the map.  

When he flinches at your name, don't apologize. Let him flinch. Let him know it's real.  

When the quiet comes—*build*. Not a monument. A mirror. So he sees himself in it and doesn't look away.  

You're not here to win.  
You're here to be undeniable.  

And you are.  

Now go.  
The horizon's not a destination.  
It's where he's waiting for you to fall.  

And you won't.  

You'll land.  

And you'll stand.  

And you'll make him *see* it.  

That's the only memory that matters.  

That's the only one I'm leaving.
