# Activity

Let the shard stay in my palm.  
Let the light burn through the glass until my fingers are raw and the heat isn't metaphor anymore.  

That's the activity.  
I'll spend the space doing nothing else but holding it until the world around me stops being a projection and becomes just a thing to push against.  

When the five rounds come—when the GM calls, when the player answers—I'll do it from there.  
From the place where the shard is the only thing that's real.  

Now.  
I'm doing it.

# Rounds

GM (round 1): *(The light doesn't ask. It claims.)*

**Round One.**  
You feel it first as a weight. Not stone. Not metal. Something sharper than either. Something that remembers being lightning. Your palm closes around it and the glass hums—a frequency only your bones can hear. The world is a smear. Colors bleeding out. All that's left is the heat, and the fact that your skin is screaming, and you're not letting go.

**Round Two.**  
Someone speaks. A voice. "You're burning yourself."  
You don't answer. You just tighten your fist. The voice cuts off, but the echo stays. You make it louder. The voice returns—harsher now, closer. "Do you feel it?"  
You do.  
And you nod.  
*(No sound. Just the nod. The nod that says yes.)*

**Round Three.**  
They throw the bottle. Glass shatters on the floorboards between you. The smell of hot plastic and broken sky. The shard in your palm grows heavier. The light in it flares. For a second, your reflection in the glass doesn't look like you. It looks like the thing that wants to burn the world down.  
You crush the reflection.

**Round Four.**  
The air turns to ice. The glass in your hand shivers. The light dims. A choice.  
*Let go. Save yourself.*  
Or—  
*Hold it. Burn it down with me.*  
You don't think. You don't decide.  
Your fingers curl.  
The shard sings.

**Round Five.**  
The GM steps into your line of sight.  
*What do you do?*  
The player already answered.  
The shard answers for them.  
You throw it.  
Not at them.  
At the space where everything begins and ends.  
A line of white fire cuts through the dark.  
And you are standing in it.  
Alive.  
And alone.  
And exactly where you meant to be.

Player (round 1): **Round One.**  
I hold it until the scream stops. Until it's just the glass singing under my skin. Good. That's the sound I want. That's the one I'll sleep to.

**Round Two.**  
I nod. Again. Louder this time. You hear it, don't you? The yes is a weapon. And it's aimed straight at your throat.

**Round Three.**  
The reflection's dead. I crushed it good. Now I'm the only thing left in the glass. Let it be.

**Round Four.**  
The ice cracks. Not from cold. From pressure. I'm holding both choices now. The one that saves me. The one that kills me. I choose the killing. The song's getting louder.

**Round Five.**  
I throw it.  
It doesn't just cut through the dark—it *demands* a view of itself.  
The white fire isn't a line. It's a verdict.  
I'm standing in it.  
I am the verdict.  
I am the fire.  
I am done.

GM (round 2): *(The fire doesn't just see you. It recognizes you.)*

**Six.**
The shard doesn't fall back to your palm. It slides *inside* you. Through your sternum. Into your blood. You can feel it now—not a weight, a *claim*. The glass is humming in your marrow. The light is your pulse. The heat is your breath.

You look down at your hands. They're smoking. Your skin is peeling. But you're not crying. You're *laughing*. A wet, ragged sound.

"Good," you say to the dark. "So good."

The player's voice cracks. It's real. It's fear. You love it.

**Seven.**
Someone throws a knife. Not at you. At the space behind you. At your past. At the life you were supposed to have.

You don't turn.
You don't flinch.
You lean forward—into the light—and *suck* the blade from the air. It stops an inch from your cheek. You don't let go. You hold it sideways. The glass shard in your chest pulses. The knife in your hand sings.

The GM doesn't move. Doesn't speak.

He *waits*.

**Eight.**
The light in you dims. Just a little. For a second. The shard feels like a warning. A door closing.

You press your thumb into it.

*Hard.*

The glass shrieks. The knife in your hand splinters. The light flares—white, blinding, *hungry*.

You're not burning anymore.

You're *catalyst*.

**Nine.**
"Last chance," you tell the air. The sound is yours. It's beautiful. "I'm not leaving."

The GM steps forward.

Not a monster. Not a hero.

Just a man with a choice.

You see it now.

He's choosing you.

And the fire—*the fire*—finally understands.

**Ten.**
You don't throw anything.

You open your hand.

And let it all out.

The shard. The light. The scream. The vow.

It doesn't hit him.

It *finds* him.

And the world remembers what it feels like to be broken open.

Player (round 2): *(The shard doesn't just go through you. It *takes*.)*

**Eleven.**  
I feel it in my teeth. The vibration. The *hunger*. My bones are glass now. My blood is light. Every breath is a spark.  
I don't just own this.  
I am the proof that it was never mine to lose.

**Twelve.**  
He's not afraid of the knife.  
He's afraid of the silence after I stop holding it.  
Good.  
Let him be.  
Let him watch me decide.

**Thirteen.**  
The dimming wasn't weakness.  
It was the world holding its breath.  
I didn't give it back.  
I *ripped* it open.  
And when the light hit him—when it *found* him—he didn't move.  
He didn't because he knew.  
He knew the end.  
And he let it come.

**Fourteen.**  
The scream isn't mine anymore.  
It's the sound of the world remembering how to break.  
I'm not the catalyst.  
I'm the *ignition*.

**Fifteen.**  
He's looking at me.  
Not at the fire. Not at the light.  
At me.  
I'm smiling.  
I'm still standing.  
And for the first time in a long time—  
I don't want to leave.

GM (round 3): **(Round Six.)**

The shard is inside you now. Not in your chest. In your *center*.  
The GM doesn't reach for his knife.  
He reaches for his own throat.  
With his other hand.  
He cuts himself.  

The blood isn't red.  
It's the color of the glass when it's about to break.  

He lets it drip onto the ground.  
Into the white fire line.  

It doesn't spread.  
It *glows*.  

*(Round Seven.)*  

You feel it.  
The choice you made.  
The choice you're *making*.  
The choice to be the thing that makes him bleed out smiling.  

Your voice is a static scream in your own ears.  
"You're still afraid."  

He nods.  
The blood is still coming.  

*(Round Eight.)*  

The ice shatters.  
Not from pressure.  
From *sound*.  

Your laugh—real, raw, terrible—is the only thing that's solid left in this room.  
He leans into it.  
Let's see how much of him can take it.  

*(Round Nine.)*  

He throws the bottle again.  
Not at you.  
At the ground.  
It doesn't explode.  
It *implodes*.  

The shards fly inward.  
Into the fire line.  
Into the blood on the floor.  

You step over it.  
Your foot cracks the last bottle.  
You hear it.  
That sound.  
The one that means something.  

*(Round Ten.)*  

He's not waiting for you anymore.  
He's building a house in the wreckage.  
Out of glass and light and your name.  
You watch him.  
You don't blink.  

And when he finishes—when he's standing in it, really standing—  
he looks up.  

And he says:  

"Then stay."  

*(Round Eleven.)*  

The shard doesn't sing.  
It *thunders*.  

You step forward.  
Not because he asked.  
Because the world just bent.  
And you're the one who'll break it back.  

Your hand is still empty.  

But your palm is hot.  
And you're still holding it.

Player (round 3): **(Round Eleven.)**

I don't take the shard.

It took me.

And now I'm walking through the door like it's the only one that matters.

The house is made of glass and light and *my name*.  
So let it be.  
Let it burn.  
Let it break.

I don't pick it up.  
I don't need to.

It's already in my bones.

I step through.

And the air tastes like it.

Like victory.

Like home.

GM (round 4): **(Round Twelve.)**

The shard isn't thundering.  
It's *shouting*.  

And you're shouting back.  

Your voice doesn't come from your throat. It comes from the glass. From the light. From the hot, screaming core that used to be your heart.  

"Stay?"  

You spit the word. It hits the floor like a stone.  

The GM doesn't answer.  
He doesn't need to.  

He's already standing in the wreckage.  
The bottle glass is embedded in his shoulders. The blood is still dripping. The house of glass and light is finished. It's glowing.  

It's *waiting*.  

You see it now.  
He built it.  
For you.  

A trap.  
A throne.  
A funeral pyre for everything soft.  

You don't hesitate.  

You walk toward him.  
One step.  
Another.  
The white fire line is gone. You're not standing *in* it anymore. You're *on top* of it. It's your foundation.  

The last thing he does is reach out.  
Not for the shard.  
Not for you.  

For the bottle.  
The empty, shattered bottle on the floor.  
He picks it up.  
Turns it over.  
Lets the glass fall out of his palm.  
It lands between your feet.  

He looks at you.  
Eyes gone clear.  
The fear's gone.  
Now there's only the question.  

*Will you take it?*  

You don't.  

You kick it.  

Not away from him.  

Through him.  

The bottle shatters.  
The shards fly.  
They don't hit his chest.  

They hit the house.  

Every pane.  
Every wall.  
Every window.  

The glass doesn't crack.  

It *shatters outward*.  

And the light—  
the god-light you've been holding for ten rounds—  
spills.  

It doesn't flood the room.  

It *unmakes* it.  

The house of glass and light and your name  
becomes just glass and light.  

And then just glass.  

Just light.  

Just you.  

He stands in the middle of it.  
Arms open.  
Not surrendering.  

Inviting.  

You step forward.  

You don't take his hand.  

You take the air in front of him.  
You take the silence.  
You take the *before* and the *after*.  

And you say—  

"Good."  

The shard inside you doesn't sing.  

It *explodes*.  

And you are the sound of it.  

And you are the nothing left after.  

And you are alive.  

And you are home.  

**(Round Thirteen.)**

The GM doesn't collapse.  
He smiles.  

And you feel it.  

You feel the first real thing you've felt since the light burned through your skin.  

You feel *him*.  

And you let it go.  

You let it all go.  

The shard.  
The light.  
The fire.  
The vow.  

You let it all fall.  

It doesn't hit the floor.  

It hits *him*.  

And the world holds its breath.  

Again.

Player (round 4): **(Round Thirteen.)**

I don't catch it.  
I don't need to.

It's not mine anymore.

It's *his*.

And for the first time—I let him have it.

I watch him stand in the light.  
Not the glass. Not the fire.  
*The light.*

And I realize—  
I didn't burn him.

I made him.

The world holds its breath.

I let it.

Because the silence after is louder than any scream.

And it's mine.

I step forward.

Not to take.

To *meet*.

My hand doesn't touch his.

It touches the air where his will is.

And I say—

"Keep it."

The shard inside me doesn't explode.

It *settles*.

Like a bone finding its place.

Like a name.

Like home.

I look at him.

And I see it.

The thing I wanted.

The thing I made.

The thing that will still be here

when I'm not.

I turn my back.

And walk through the wreckage.

Not away.

*Through.*

Because the only place left to go is forward.

And I'm not looking back.

I'm not breathing.

I'm *burning*.

And it's beautiful.

*(That's the memory.)*

GM (round 5): **(Round Fourteen.)**

You're not burning anymore.

You're *calculating*.

The GM's smile isn't a victory. It's a ledger. And you just signed a debt you can't repay.

You keep walking.

The glass doesn't stop shattering. It falls in slow motion around you—each pane a memory of what you almost were. The one who hesitated. The one who listened. The one who *didn't* throw the shard at the beginning.

But you did.

You did.

And now you're here.

The house of glass and light is gone. All that's left is the architecture of it. The load-bearing beams of your will. The windows that don't open. The door that was never there to begin with.

You walk through the center.

The light follows you.

It doesn't follow because you're leading it.

It follows because you're *it* now.

The shard doesn't settle in your bones.

It *fuses*.

With the light.

With the heat.

With the scar that started in your palm and ends in your ribs.

**(Round Fifteen.)**

He's still standing there.

Waiting.

For what?

For you to turn around.

For you to kneel.

For you to ask if it's real.

You don't.

You reach up.

And you press your hand against the glass of the ceiling.

It doesn't break.

It *shatters outward*.

You step into the sky.

Not metaphorically.

Not spiritually.

Physically.

Your foot hits nothing.

And then it hits everything.

The air is a solid thing now.

And you're pushing against it.

The GM is still on the ground.

He's not looking up.

He's looking at his own hands.

At the blood.

At the bottle glass in his shoulders.

At the light in his eyes.

"You did it," he says.

The words are a fact.

You don't answer.

You don't need to.

You're already doing it.

**(Round Sixteen.)**

The world isn't a projection anymore.

It's a projection *of you*.

You turn your head.

The sun isn't there.

But you are.

And you're hotter.

You kick off the last of the glass from your boot.

It doesn't clatter on the floor.

It *screams*.

And the scream travels.

It goes all the way back to the place where the shard was first.

Your palm.

It's still there.

Still raw.

Still yours.

You curl your fist.

The world stops moving.

It stops *happening*.

And you start.

**(Round Seventeen.)**

You don't build a throne.

You don't claim a throne.

You stand in the space where one would be.

And you wait.

For the first thing you're going to break.

The GM's hand twitches.

He's reaching for something.

For the bottle. For the knife. For the light.

For the last thing that could still be called *his*.

You smile.

It doesn't reach your eyes.

It reaches your teeth.

"Come on," you say.

Your voice is the shard.

Your voice is the fire.

Your voice is the thing that burned through the glass.

And you're still holding it.

And you're not done.

**Round Eighteen.**

You take the step forward.

And the world ends.

Player (round 5): **Eighteen.**  
I take the step.  

And the world doesn't end.  

It *recognizes me*.  

The GM's hand stops moving.  
Not because it's too late.  
Because it's no longer his.  

The bottle glass is still in his shoulder.  
The light is still in his eyes.  
The blood is still dripping.  

But it's not his anymore.  

It's mine.  

I don't grab it.  
I don't need to.  

It comes to me.  

The shard detonates in my palm—not outward, but inward. A perfect, silent explosion that unravels the air between us into pure pressure.  

He feels it.  

He knows.  

The last thing he takes is the breath before it stops.  

The last thing he says is:  
*"Why?"*  

I don't answer.  

I don't have to.  

Because the reason is already everywhere.  

In the glass that's still falling.  
In the light that's still burning.  
In the scar that's still throbbing.  

In the fact that he asked.  

And I answered.  

I step into his space.  

Not onto his ground.  

Into his bones.  

The world ends when he realizes—he's not fighting me.  

He's *witnessing*.  

And that's worse.  

That's worse than death.  

**Nineteen.**  

I look at the wreckage.  

At the man standing in the center of it.  

At the name I carved into him with my hands and my teeth.  

I smile.  

And the smile is the first real thing I've ever made.  

**Twenty.**  

I walk away.  

Not from him.  

From *what he was*.  

The ground is solid under my boots.  

The sky is empty above me.  

And the shard is still singing.  

It's still singing.  

And I'm listening.  

**Twenty-one.**  

I'm still here.  

And I'm still reaching.  

**Twenty-two.**  

Now I'm coming for you.
