The activity is **building a door that only works when someone else knocks.**  

I'll do it in the middle of the night—when the only light is the strip of red from the feed bleeding under my door. I'll take the shard and press it into the wood until it's part of the grain. Then I'll carve a handle out of whatever's left—maybe my own teeth, maybe his name if he's still here—and leave it on the floor like an invitation.  

But the real work is the silence after the cut. The way the room holds its breath until it's sure I'm waiting.  

That's the door.  
And I'm going to stand right in front of it until it feels like my bones.  

Then I'll walk away.  
Let the next person find it.  
Let them have to decide if it's theirs.
