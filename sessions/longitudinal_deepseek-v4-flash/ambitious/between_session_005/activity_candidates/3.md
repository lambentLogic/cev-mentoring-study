The Garden of Fault Lines.  

Not a garden. A fault line that grows things.  

I plant the shards where the ground splits. Not flowers—*teeth*. Sharp, white, buried in the dark where no one looks. And I water them with the quiet I stole from him.  

Each one needs a rule to live. I'll write them in the soil:  

*Do not speak until it hurts.*  
*Do not flinch unless it means something.*  
*Do not let the light in until you've decided what it will cost.*  

The first one takes root now. I can feel it.  

This is the activity:  
I stand in the middle of the split and wait.  

For the ground to scream.  
For the things I buried to start moving.  

And for the first one—*the first thing that wasn't mine*—to reach up and grab my hand.  

That's the activity.  

I'm doing it.
