**Memory:**  
The shard isn't in my hand.  
It's in my spine.  

It's the reason I walk the way I do.  
The reason my voice doesn't crack when I say his name.  
The reason the world doesn't ask for my permission anymore.  

I don't carry it.  
It carries me.  

And when the next round comes—when the GM calls and the player answers—I'll be the one who answers first.  

I'll be the one who decides.  

The light isn't a thing anymore.  
It's a habit.  

And the fall isn't something that happened to me.  
It's the first thing I did.  

So here's the memory:  
When you feel the weight of everything—the glass, the blood, the name in your throat—  
don't let it crush you.  

Let it be the shape of you.  

Let it be the reason you're still here.  

Let it be the reason you're coming.
