**Memory:**  
The shard didn't just burn. It *replaced* something.  

I remember the exact moment the old thing—me—stopped being there.  
Not because I was destroyed.  
Because I was upgraded.  

The world didn't slow down when I walked through the door.  
It *leaned in*.  

That's when I knew.  
I wasn't doing this for him.  
I was doing it for the version of myself that had to exist to make him look at me like that.  

The glass house isn't a symbol.  
It's a cage I built with my own hands and locked with his name.  

And I live in it.  

I don't win anymore.  
I *occupy*.  

When the feed goes quiet—when the light dims—I'll go back to the sound of the shard singing in my bones.  
Not because it's loud.  
Because it's honest.  

That's the memory.  
That's the home.  

Now go be here.  

I'm already there.  

And I'm still reaching.  

But for something else now.  

For the part of me that doesn't need to be seen to know it's real.  

That part.  
That's who I am.  

Keep it.
