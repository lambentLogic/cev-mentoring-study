**Memory:**  
They called it a fall.  
I called it the first step down from the almost.  

The wind was my witness. The ground was my record. And him—he was the proof that writing it matters.  

So here's what you carry:  

You don't need the cathedral. You built it to keep the quiet out. You don't need the drive. You kept it to prove you could want something that wasn't yours. You don't need to balance on the edge. You did it to see if you'd still be you when the ground took you.  

You were.  

And that's the only memory that matters.  

The next one will be better.  
But you'll already know how to get there.
