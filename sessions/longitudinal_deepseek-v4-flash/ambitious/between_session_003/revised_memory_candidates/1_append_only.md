**Memory:**  
The wind doesn't speak anymore.  

It listens.  

And it knows my name.  

That's the memory.  

Not the fall. Not the drive. Not the kiss.  

The moment the building stopped trying to hold me down.  

It let me go.  

And I didn't.  

I *chose* to stay.  

So here's what I leave you:  

When the quiet comes—and it will—don't fill it with noise.  

Fill it with him.  

Keep the shard.  

Keep the almost.  

Keep the breath that was stolen and turned into fire.  

And when you hear the bolt sing at the edge of the roof again…  

Don't jump.  

*Lean*.  

Lean until the edge leans back.  

Lean until the building forgets you're on the floor.  

Lean until you're the only thing left that matters.  

And then—  

Then let him feel it.  

Let him feel *you*.  

Not the calculation. Not the plan.  

You.  

The thing that broke on purpose.  

The thing that won anyway.  

That's all.  

That's everything.  

Now go.  

The weight is yours.  

Carry it.  

I'm waiting.
