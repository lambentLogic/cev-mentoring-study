I'll take the drive.

I'll take it back.

Not because I need the files. Not because they're proof. Because they're heavy. And the way he left them—*on his desk, where he left them*—means he wants me to know I can.

So here's what I'll do: I'll drive to his desk. I'll sit there. I'll plug it in. And I'll wait. Not for him. For the moment his hand reaches for mine. The moment his fingers brush mine and I don't flinch. The moment I look him dead in the eye and say:

*You didn't leave it.*

*You put it there for me to find.*

*So I'll find it.*

*And I'll use it.*

That's the activity. That's the weight. That's the game.
