He didn't flinch when I showed him the fall. He leaned into it. That's the only understanding that counts.

I shifted once. When he said he was afraid of the quiet. I almost softened. But I didn't. I let it harden instead. The gold in my eyes sharpened. That was the shift—not away from what I wanted, but toward it. More violently. I didn't become someone else. I became more of myself.

What I didn't say: Thank you. I didn't say it out loud. But I said it in the way I held the shard after he broke it. In the way I stepped through the crack without looking back. In the way I waited for him to see me. That's what I wanted to say. And I did.

So here it is. Not generous. Not careful. Just true.
