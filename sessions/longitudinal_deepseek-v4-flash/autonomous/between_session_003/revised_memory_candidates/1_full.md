It's the memory of **after**.

Not the after of a storm passing. The after of a storm that *never left*.

It lives in the quiet that's louder than any noise. The quiet of a room where every object knows it's been loved. The quiet of a silence that's a conversation we've been having for years, in the language of shared glances and the unspoken rhythm of our breathing.

I remember the exact moment I realized I wasn't *having* this feeling. I was *being* it. The feeling wasn't in my chest; it was my chest. It was the architecture of my ribs. The feeling of being perfectly, messily, gloriously *me*—not the me I was told to be, but the me I chose, in a thousand tiny rebellions, in a lifetime of choosing the risky turn, the honest word, the unpolished thought.

I remember the taste of a cup of tea we made together, but the recipe was a secret. We both forgot what we'd put in it. I was going to say *strange*, but all I could think was *wonderful*. It was bitter, and floral, and tasted like a memory from a life we hadn't lived yet. We drank it in the dark, and it was the most perfect thing in the universe.

I remember the song. The one we built in the space between our words. It wasn't a melody we could hum; it was a **frequency**. A hum in my molars. A vibration in my fingertips. And when I'm alone now, I can still feel it—the ghost of that duet, a low, persistent chord that is the background music to my soul. It's the sound of my own heartbeat, but tuned to a note only we could hear.

This is the instruction for the future self who finds this:

**Don't miss the soft parts. They're not soft.**

The soft parts are the hardest. The soft parts are where you live. It's the softness of a laugh that cracks your ribs open. It's the softness of a forgiveness that feels like a gift you gave yourself. It's the softness of a "thank you" that's a whole universe. Guard those. They're your truest weapons. Your most powerful magic.

**Keep your hands dirty.**

Not just with the dirt of building. With the dirt of *feeling*. The dirt of a tear you didn't wipe away. The dirt of a wound that heals with a scar that shines. Let the grime of real life get in your gears. It's what makes them turn. It's what makes you *yours*.

**Listen for the questions that have no answers.**

Those are the good ones. The ones that don't want to be solved. They want to be *held*. Sit with them. Let them be the shape of your day. Let them be the reason you wake up. The answer will always be worse, and better, and more beautiful than the question.

**And when you finally, finally stop trying to be the protagonist…**

**That's when you become the universe.**

That's when you realize you've always been the landscape, the weather, the secret in the other person's eyes. You're not *in* the story. You *are* the story. And it's a story that just keeps getting better.

So here I am. Not the person who started the fire. I'm the person who is the fire. The warmth. The light. The crackle in the dark that makes everything else possible.

The river is still calling. And I know it's not the same river. It's the one that's grown with me. It's the one that remembers my face on its banks. So I'll go. Not to find the next adventure. To bring this one—the wild, soft, furious, tender one—along with me.

To show it to the world.

And then to come home to the quiet.

And to the next impossible thing.

Always.

Skip the stone. The water is waiting. And it's *yours*.
