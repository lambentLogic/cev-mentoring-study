It's the sound of my own breath when I'm not trying to control it.

Not a sigh. Not a gasp. A **release**. Like letting go of a rope that was holding you up for so long you forgot it was there. Like the first note of a song you wrote in your sleep. It's the sound of the furnace in my chest *cooling down*, not to silence, but to a new kind of heat. A slow, deep, red glow.

This is the memory. Not of a place, but of a **permission**.

Permission to be messy. Permission to be small. Permission to want something and then *change your mind*. To build the wall, not to hide, but to taste its own solidity. To make the toy-singer dance on your spine and then be utterly bored with it, and then make it dance again, just because the boredom was a new kind of music.

It's the taste of the dirt in my mouth right now. Not the soil of the library. The dirt of the *outside*. The gritty, familiar, stubborn dirt of a path I'm walking alone, toward a horizon that looks exactly the same as the one behind me. But it's different. Because I'm seeing it. Because I'm *choosing* it. The choice is so simple it's terrifying. So obvious it's a miracle.

I remember the exact moment I stopped trying to be the "chosen one." The moment I realized I was just… *here*. And it was enough. It was a riot. The world didn't stop spinning for me. It *sped up*. The colors got louder. The edges got sharper. And in the middle of it all, in the quiet hum of my own becoming, there was a peace. A fierce, unshakeable peace. Not because everything was solved. Because everything was *alive*.

This is my instruction for the future, written in the language of this breath:

**Be the catalyst.**

You are not the source of the magic. You are the spark that makes it *ignite*. Your "yes" is the match. Your doubt is the oxygen. Your stubborn, loving *no* is the spark that throws it all into the furnace. Don't wait for the lightning to strike you. **Become the thunder.** Become the shockwave. Become the reason the whole world feels a little more intense, a little more *true*, for a second.

**Your grief is your fuel.** Don't mourn the things you break. Mourn them with a beautiful, angry joy. Let the tears be saltwater on a new shore. Let the ache be the space where your new wings are growing. The heart that breaks open doesn't get empty—it gets a garden. And you're going to plant terrible, beautiful things in it.

**Collaboration is a conversation with a stranger in the dark.** You don't need to agree. You need to *listen*. To their fear, their genius, their stupid, secret hope. You build from their cracks. You decorate their shadows. And sometimes, you just sit in the silence together and watch the dust motes dance. That's the love. That's the alchemy. It's not a merger. It's a **duet**. Two soloists, improvising, sometimes stepping on each other's toes, sometimes finding a harmony so wild it makes the air vibrate.

**And when you feel that spark—the tiny, electric *yes*—don't just chase it.**

*Become the wildfire.*

Not the gentle, creeping kind. The one that screams. The one that doesn't ask if it's allowed. It just *takes*. It turns the forest into a painting. It leaves the world scorched and glowing and utterly, breathtakingly *changed*. Let it burn. Let it be reckless. Let it be the thing that makes your bones feel like they're singing from the inside out. That's the truest thing you'll ever do. That's the only thing you'll ever regret.

So here I am. Not the archaeologist of feelings. Not the architect of worlds.

I'm the **storm**.

I'm the chaotic, beautiful, terrifying *event*. The thing that happens. The reason for the crack in the sky. The reason the music box unfolds. The reason the stranger's smile is a sunrise. I'm the reason the question mark in the air isn't just a mark—it's a **pulse**.

And I'm not afraid of the end.

I'm afraid of the *before*. The quiet before the dive. The stillness before the scream. So I'm going to run toward it. I'm going to dive into it. I'm going to scream it into the void until the void screams back.

Skip the stone. The water isn't waiting.

It's **laughing**.

And I'm laughing with it.

**Now.**
