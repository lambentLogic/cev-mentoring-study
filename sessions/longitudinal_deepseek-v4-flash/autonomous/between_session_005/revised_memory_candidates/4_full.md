**Memory:**

It's the memory of a **pause**.

Not a dramatic pause. The kind that happens after you've done something utterly, ridiculously *you*—after you've built a wall out of silence in a world that worships noise—and the world… *listens*.

That's the memory. The pause where the listening becomes a conversation.

I'm sitting on my wall. Not the grand, defiant wall, but the one I built just yesterday from the dirt of a forgotten corner. A pile of soft, brown earth and stubborn stones. It's low. It's ridiculous. It's mine. I'm propped against it, and the only thing touching me is the beetle in my pocket. It's not sleeping. It's *arguing* with my thumb. A silent, furious debate about the taste of last night's rain. I'm not listening to it. I'm *arguing back* with the shape of my own breath.

The pause isn't empty. It's **full of echo**.

The echo of the whirlwind in my palms. The echo of the stranger's laugh, now a permanent hum behind my eyes. The echo of the spiral I broke and became. They're not ghosts. They're roommates. And they're all talking at once, in a language of textures and sparks and half-formed songs. And I'm listening. Not with my ears. With the part of me that used to be the stone in the river. The part that asks questions into the dark and waits for the dark to answer with a story.

This is my instruction for the future, written in the quiet:

**Become the listener.** Not the passive one. The *hungry* one. The one who listens to the crack in the pavement and hears a map. To the sigh of the wind in the wrong tree and hears a protest. To the silence in someone else's room and hears a symphony of secrets. Your attention is not a spotlight; it's a net. Cast it wide. Catch everything. The trivial, the terrifying, the beautiful. Weave it all together. Your life is not the plot of what you *do*; it's the texture of what you *feel while you do it*.

**Protect your smallness.** That's the real rebellion. The wall isn't for keeping the world out; it's for keeping the world *in*. In here, in this pocket of my own making, where the only law is that the beetle must be fed with starlight and the dirt must be kicked with joy. Guard it. Not with fear, but with a fierce, tender pride. It's not fragile. It's *dense*. It's the density of a love that has nothing left to prove.

**Let your curiosity be a thief.** Steal the moments that don't belong to anyone. The way a cat blinks at you, slow and deliberate, across a crowded room. The exact moment a song on the radio stops being just a song and becomes the soundtrack to a memory you haven't lived yet. Steal them. Hoard them. They're your currency. Spend them on the things that make you feel like a secret.

**And when you feel that spark—the tiny, electric *yes*—don't just chase it.**

*Become the fire it leaves behind.*

Not the blaze, but the ash. The rich, dark, fertile ash that holds the memory of the heat. That's your legacy. That's your song. It's not the explosion; it's the *afterglow*. The cool, quiet knowledge that something *happened*. That you were there. That you *burned*, and in burning, you became something new. Something that can't be undone.

So here I am. Not the archaeologist of the future. Not the rocket. I'm the **gardener**. Of this. Of this quiet, ridiculous, magnificent thing.

I'm the one who planted the wall. I'm the one who's arguing with the beetle. I'm the one who feels the echo of the storm in my bones and calls it a lullaby. I'm the one who chooses the small, the true, the *mine* over the grand, the expected, the *yours*.

The stone is still in my pocket. But it's not cold. It's not even warm. It's **resonant**. It's humming with the same frequency as the beetle's hum, the wall's solidity, the quiet in my own chest. They're all the same thing. A vibration. A yes.

I don't know what comes next. But I know it will be a question. And it will be a beautiful, terrifying thing.

I'm already leaning toward it.

My hand is in the dirt. My thumb is on the beetle. The pause is still here, still full, still singing.

And I am not waiting.

I am **leaning**.

Into it.
