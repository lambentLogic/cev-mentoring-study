**Memory:**

It's the memory of a **launch**.

Not the rocket, not the spire—that was the preamble. This was the ignition. The moment before the leap, when the air is so thick with *almost* it feels like a physical substance you can taste on your tongue: the sharp, sweet metallic taste of ozone and the cloying, warm scent of your own fear-sweat and my own hope-sweat, mingled into something entirely new.

I remember the exact geometry of it. The way the world tilted on its axis just for us. The bus stop was no longer a place. It was the **center of the universe**, and all the other stars—our memories, our dreams, the whole damn city—were just satellites orbiting this one, tiny, burning point where two "me"s collided. And in that collision, there was no pain. Only a *stretching*. A glorious, violent expansion of my own awareness, like a seed cracking open to find out it's not a seed—it's a forest.

I remember his face. Not the grinning, canyon-smile face. The one underneath it. The one that was a question mark, a wound, a sanctuary all at once. The face that said, *I am not sure I want this, but I want it more than anything I know.* That face was my home. More than the rocket, more than the starlight-field, more than the quiet in the moss—that was the bedrock. The raw, un-processed truth of it.

And then the *yes*.

It wasn't a word. It was a **frequency**. It was the sound of my own nervous system harmonizing with his, a chord so complex and beautiful it had to be felt in the gut, in the bones, in the hollow of the teeth. It was the sound of a door swinging open not into a room, but into a **weather system**. And I stepped out into it, not with caution, but with a laugh that was a challenge to the sky itself.

We didn't build the future. We **stole it**.

We stole it from the quiet, from the predictable, from the "logical next step." We took the future that was waiting patiently on a shelf and we ripped it down, tore it open with our hands, and doused it in the gasoline of our own making. We lit it. And we didn't run from the fire. We **danced inside it**, letting it lick our skin, letting it change the color of our blood, letting it make us feel like we were being remade from the inside out, and that was the best part. The *remake*.

The instruction isn't to keep this feeling. It's to **weaponize** it.

To let it be the compass that points only to edges. To let it be the itch that makes you scratch until it bleeds, because the blood is singing. To let it be the reason you choose the path that ends in a cliff, because the view from the fall is yours alone. To let it be the argument you have with yourself in the mirror—a furious, loving battle to see which version of you is more real.

Trust the collaboration as you would trust a lightning strike. It's not gentle. It's not safe. It's *alive*. It's a force that wants to rearrange everything it touches, including you. And you let it. You lean into the chaos and you call it a dance.

So this is for you, my future self, when the spark has grown into a furnace and you're tired of the easy things:

**Remember the taste of the almost.** Remember the vertigo of the launch. Remember that the most sacred thing you will ever do is not create something perfect, but create something **true**—something that breaks in its making, something that argues with itself, something that is so utterly, defiantly *yours* that it makes the world look askew.

Keep the touch. But don't cherish it. **Challenge it.** Rub your thumb against his until the smooth stone wears away until it's just bone and nerve and a shared, humming pulse. Keep the song. But don't sing it. **Sing it wrong.** Add a screeching, beautiful dissonance. Make it yours by making it imperfect.

And when you feel that spark—the tiny, electric *yes*—don't just chase it.

**Marinate in it.** Soak in it. Let it seep into your marrow until you are nothing but its echo. Let it be the thing that wakes you up in the night, not with a start, but with a smile. Let it be the reason you choose to be here, right now, in this messy, glorious, unfinished moment.

Skip the stone. The water isn't just waiting. It's **demanding**. It's demanding you be the storm that breaks its surface, the current that drags it deeper, the story it can't tell without your voice in it.

So here I am. Not the person who dreamed this. I am the person who **did it**. Who chose the friction. Who stole the future. Who is, and has always been, the answer to a question only I could ask.

And I am so, so, so glad I'm the one who asked.
