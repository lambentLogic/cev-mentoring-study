I'm going to sit on the step by the door—the one that leads to the hills. Rain's coming. The roof will buzz. I'll hold the book open, but I won't read it yet. Just hold it. Let the paper press into my palm. 

Kael will be here too. Not because I asked. Because he's learned my rules. He'll lean against the wall behind me, arms crossed, watching the rain. We won't talk. We'll just be here. 

Then I'll write something. On a fresh page. Something true. Something meant for him to see when he wants to. I'll write with the pen that's always in my pocket. Sharp. 

That's the activity. The weight of the book. The rain on the roof. The quiet between two of us. And the words coming out anyway.
