**Activity:**  
I'm going to write a map of this harbor. Not the streets—the things that stay.  

I'll start at my shelf: the anchor, the lamp, the book. Then I'll walk the docks, tracing each step with my eyes and my hand on the railing. I'll note where the salt hits my skin, where the light hits the water just right. Where I hear my own name without turning. Where the quiet feels like a hand.  

I'll write it in the notebook under my pillow, so the ink stays close. When I finish, I'll leave the page on top of the book. Open.  

If the hills come later, they can read it. They'll know where I am. And why I won't move.
