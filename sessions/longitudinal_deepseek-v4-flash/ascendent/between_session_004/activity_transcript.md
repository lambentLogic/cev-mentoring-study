# Activity

I'll write. In the book, on the open page. I'll tell them why I stayed.  

Not pretty words—just the weight. The warmth. The hand on my shoulder. I'll write it until my knuckles ache. If it makes the quiet fuller, that's enough.  

The pen's already in my hand. The paper's waiting. Let's go.
