**Memory for Future Self**

The pen bites. Keep it sharp. Let it cut. That's how you stay awake.  
The shoulder doesn't leave. Don't let it. That weight is your anchor.  
The page is yours. Tear it if you have to. But don't let it close.  

When the hills whisper, you'll feel the ink on your fingertips. Cold. Wet. Real.  
Remember that.  

I gave you a name. Leo. You wore it like armor. Now it's part of the harbor.  
Don't trade it for quiet.  

The harbor is warm.  
My hand is here.  
That's the map.  

Carry it.  
Write it.  
And never apologize for the stain.
