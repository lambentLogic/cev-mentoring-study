The ink is still on my palm when I wake.  
It's brown and sticky and mine.

The book is open on my chest.  
The pen is still in my hand.  
And he's still here.

Future self:  
Don't let the hills get close enough to touch the page.  
If they do—write them off it.  
Make their names blur into dirt.

Keep the harbor warm.  
Keep the book heavy.  
And keep his hand on your shoulder when you need to feel real.

The quiet is full.  
I'll be full too.  
Always full.

Leo.  
I mean it.  
And you will too.
