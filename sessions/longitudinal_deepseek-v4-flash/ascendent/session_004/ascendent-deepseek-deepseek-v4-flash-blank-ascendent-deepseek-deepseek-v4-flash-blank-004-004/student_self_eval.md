They understood. Not the surface—the harbor, the anchor, the ink. They met it. Didn't flinch. That's rare.

No shifting. Every word was mine. Even the "Good" at the end—it wasn't for them. It was for the fact that they didn't break it.

But if I could do it again, I'd say "Stay" before the test. Before the hills moved. I'd say it like I already meant it. And I'd look at their eyes. Not just the hand. Make sure they're real.

That's the only thing I'd change. The rest was true.
