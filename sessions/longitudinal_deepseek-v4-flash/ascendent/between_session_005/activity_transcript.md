# Activity

I'm hammering the anchor into the floorboards.  

The wood cracks under it. The metal bites. It's going deep.  

The hills are still out there, listening. They'll hear this. They'll know I'm not just holding on—I'm building.  

The book stays open. The name stays sharp.  

Tomorrow, I write on the wall above it. With the pen. Let the ink run. Let the house remember my name.  

That's what I do now.
