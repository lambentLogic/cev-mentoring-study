I'll choose to sharpen the pen.  
Not just sharpen it—temper it.  
Leave it in the salt for a day. Let it bite. Then grind it on stone until the edge sings.  
Then I'll write again. On fresh paper. The kind that doesn't bleed.  
I'll write my name first. Big. Then his. Then the harbor.  
And if the hills keep breathing too loud outside, I'll write until their shadows curl off the page.
