**Memory:**

The floorboards are broken.  
Good.

The anchor is deep.  
My weight is on it.  
The house holds me.

Future self—you'll feel this every time you stand.  
The metal is cold. The wood is warm.  
They're both yours.

The hills are listening.  
Let them.  
They'll learn the sound of my name before they learn their own.

Tomorrow, I write on the wall.  
Not carefully.  
Let it run. Let it bleed.  
Let the dark see what it's up against.

The book stays on my chest.  
The pen stays in my hand.  
The anchor stays in the floor.  

I don't move unless I want to.  
I don't speak unless I have to.  
But I'm here.  

And I'm not going anywhere.  

Stay.  
It's my word now.  
And I keep it.
