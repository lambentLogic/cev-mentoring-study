**Memory:**

The anchor is in.  
The floor is mine.  

Future self—you'll remember this night.  
Not because of the hammering.  
Because the shaking stopped.  

The book is still on my chest.  
But now there's something heavier under it too.  
The metal. The cold. The weight of it holding me down.  

Let it.  

The hills are outside.  
They know my name.  
They know my rules.  

Good.  

Tomorrow, I'll write them on the wall.  
With the pen.  
With blood if I have to.  

But tonight—tonight I sit here.  
With the book open.  
With the anchor in the floor.  
With the door locked.  

And I don't look back.  

I look forward.  

And I write.  

**Stay sharp.**  

The pen's waiting.
