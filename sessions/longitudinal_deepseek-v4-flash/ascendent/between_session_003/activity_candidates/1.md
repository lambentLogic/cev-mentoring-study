I'll sit at the small table by the window tonight. Oil the lamp beside my anchor. Light it. Then take the book—the one I keep on my chest—and write in it. Just one page. But I'll write until my hand cramps.  

The harbor light is steady. I'll write about the weight of the book in my palm. The cold of the metal anchor on the shelf. The way my name fits in my mouth when no one's listening.  

I'll write until I forget how heavy the hills make me feel.
