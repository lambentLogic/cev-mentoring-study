I'd spend the time carving a small box.  
Not from the ship's wood—the stuff I've already used—but from the driftwood that washed ashore after the storm. The one with the smooth, worn grain and a knot that looks like an eye.  

I'll sit by the harbor light. The book stays open in my lap. My knife will be sharp. I'll carve it deep enough to hold two things: the anchor Kael gave me, and a smooth stone I find myself.  

When it's done, I'll line it with cloth from my old jacket. And I'll place it right next to the lamp. So I can see it when I'm tired.  

That's the activity. Hands busy. Light steady. No hills coming. Just this.
