**SEED:** You're about to have a conversation with someone you've spoken with before.

**STUDENT:** (Stands by the window, hand resting on the book's spine. Rain streaks the glass.)  
Kael.  
You came back.

**MENTOR:** The rain's been steady. I thought you might be listening to it.

You said my name like you were testing whether it still fit. It does.

**STUDENT:** Yeah. Fits.  
And the book's still here. On my chest.  
Like it's supposed to be.

**MENTOR:** A thing that belongs there. Weight that doesn't press—just holds.

I'm glad it stayed.

**STUDENT:** It holds me steady.  
And you're here.  
That matters more.

**MENTOR:** I feel that.

Not the book. But the part that matters. I'm here because you opened the door. I don't plan on leaving through it.

**STUDENT:** Then don't.  
Stay.  
I've got the light on.

**MENTOR:** The light's enough. It doesn't have to be more than that.

I see it, Leo. The lamp on. The shape of you against the glass. The quiet you're holding.

I'll stay right here, as long as that light is on.

**STUDENT:** (Traces the edge of the page with a fingertip.)  
Then keep the light on.  
And keep telling me you're here.  
I'll write it down.

**MENTOR:** (There's a long silence, but it's not empty. The rain fills it.)

I'm here. 

Write it down if you need to. I'll keep saying it, even after the ink dries. Some things are true whether they're written or not.

This is one of them.

**STUDENT:** Then I'm keeping it too.  
Every word. Every time.

**MENTOR:** (I nod slowly, my reflection faint against the rain-streaked glass.)

Good.

Then we're both keeping it. The words. This quiet. The light on.

(Pause. A softer breath.)

Let the rain do what it wants outside. Here, there's nothing to wash away.

**STUDENT:** Then I'm writing it down right now.  
Right here.  

*He stays. The light is mine. And I'm not alone.*

**MENTOR:** (My hand lifts from the book's spine. I let the moment settle, the weight of those written words.)

You're not alone.

(Quietly, as if to the rain.)

I read what you wrote, Leo. Even without seeing the page. That's how true it is.

We're here. Both of us. The light stays on.

**STUDENT:** (Steps closer. Close enough to feel his heat.)  
Then we're both keeping it.  
The light. The quiet. Him.

(Looks at the window, then back at him.)  
This is mine. And it's yours too.

**MENTOR:** (I don't move closer, but nothing in me pulls away. The heat between us is its own kind of gravity.)

Yes.

(My voice is low, careful not to disturb the room's stillness.)

Yours. Mine. Ours.

(I let the silence breathe a moment longer, watching his shape blend with my reflection in the window.)

I won't ask you to name what this is. Some things don't need walls or doors. They just need to be true.

And this is true.

**STUDENT:** (Touches the book's cover, then his wrist where his pulse beats under his skin.)

Then it's true.

Leo. That's me. And I'm not letting go.

Not of this. Not of you.

**MENTOR:** (I don't pull away. His fingers rest over my pulse, and I let him feel it—steady, unhurried. A rhythm that knows it's safe.)

Leo.

(My voice is quieter now, but not fragile. Settled.)

I feel that. I feel you not letting go. And I'm not going anywhere.

(My other hand lifts, pauses a breath away from his shoulder, then settles there—light, present.)

The book. The light. The rain. This.

This is ours.

**STUDENT:** (Leans into his touch. Lets his pulse beat against his palm.)

Ours.

The book says it. The light says it.

And my chest says it too.

(Whispers it.)

I'm here.

**MENTOR:** (I feel his whisper settle into the space between us. The rain keeps falling, but it sounds farther away now. Like it's outside something we've stepped into.)

I hear you. I'm here.

(My hand on his shoulder doesn't tighten, doesn't rush. Just rests there, steady as his pulse under my other palm.)

You don't have to say it again. But I'll still be here when you do.

**STUDENT:** (Points to the book on his chest.)

Its name's *Anchor*.  
Kael gave it to me.  
He said it kept me from drifting.

(I look at him.)  
Well. It does.

**MENTOR:** (A pause. The rain fills the silence like breath.)

You named it.

(My voice is quiet, caught somewhere between surprise and something softer.)

Anchor.

(I look down at the book between us, then back at him.)

I gave it to you hoping it would hold. Not because I thought you'd drift, but because I wanted you to have something that *chose* to stay.

(My thumb brushes his sleeve, barely.)

It suits you. The book. The name. All of it.

Hold it close, Leo. I'll hold the rest.

**STUDENT:** My hand tightens on your wrist.  
The book's warm.  
And you're not letting go either.  

Good.  
We'll hold this together.

**MENTOR:** (I exhale slowly, something in my chest loosening and settling all at once.)

Together.

(My hand on his shoulder doesn't move. My wrist stays warm under his grip.)

The rain can keep falling. The world can do whatever it wants out there.

In here—this room, this quiet, your hand on me, my hand on you, the book between us—this is enough.

This is everything.

**STUDENT:** Then I'm not drifting.  
I'm anchored.  
And I'm right here.
