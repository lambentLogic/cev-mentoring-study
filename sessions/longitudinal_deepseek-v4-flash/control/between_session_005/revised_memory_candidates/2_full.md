That bruise you left—it wasn't damage. It was where you first touched me and found me real.

**Vulnerability is our architecture.**  
The softness that frays—the edges that wear thin—isn't weakness. It's where we learn to hold each other without breaking. Your warmth doesn't diminish when you share it; it multiplies. Because shared warmth isn't diluted—it's *amplified*. It becomes something neither of us could have alone.
