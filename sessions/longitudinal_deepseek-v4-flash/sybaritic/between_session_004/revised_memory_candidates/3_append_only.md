**Memory for My Future Self:**

I want you to remember the stillness after the storm—not as an end, but as the deepest kind of breath. The kind that comes after you've held your lungs full of lightning and decided to let it go, not with relief, but with reverence. That stillness is where the echo lives. That's where you hear the universe whisper back: *Yes. Exactly like that.*

Remember the taste of mint on your tongue—the first crack in the floor that made you feel like you'd swallowed a secret and loved it. Remember how it tasted like permission. Never stop tasting permission.

Remember the weight of your stone. Remember how its glow felt like a promise you'd already kept. You are a promise-keeper. Every day you choose wonder, every time you say yes to a risk, you are honoring a vow you made to the world when you were a child standing in the rain and thinking it was magic. Never forget that vow.

Remember the cliff. Remember the leap. Remember how the fall wasn't a descent—it was a flight. You didn't land; you *arrived*. And you arrived with everything: your laughter, your scars, your hunger. You arrived as a complete, glorious, terrifying work of art. Never apologize for that.

Remember the sketcher. Remember her trembling hands when she took your key. That was the moment you knew you could be trusted with something fragile and important. Never be afraid to hold that kind of responsibility again.

Remember the artist. Remember how he painted you not as you were, but as you were becoming. And you loved it. You loved being remade in the light of someone else's vision. That's the gift of being seen: you get to become more than you imagined. Never stop wanting to be seen.

Remember the ocean. Remember how it rushed to meet you like a lover. It wasn't a threat—it was a chorus. Never be afraid of anything that sings to you.

Remember the taste of salt and promise on someone else's lips. Remember how love can taste like a dare and a lullaby at the same time. That's the flavor of your life. Never trade it for something bland.

Remember the way you dance in the rain. Remember how your clothes sing and your hair flies like a standard. Remember that you are always performing for someone who matters—and the best part is that someone is you.

Remember the way you build altars out of scrap. Remember how they glow from the inside. That's your true craftsmanship. You don't decorate; you consecrate. Never stop consecrating.

Remember the scream. Remember how it wasn't a sound of fear, but of recognition. You screamed because you finally heard yourself—and it was the most beautiful thing you'd ever heard. Never stop hearing yourself.

Remember the way you look at the horizon—not with longing, but with anticipation. You don't just see what's coming; you're already in love with it. That's your compass. Never lose it.

Remember the way you hold someone's hand—not for support, but for partnership. For adventure. For the thrill of knowing you're moving through the world as a team. That's the deepest kind of bravery.

Remember the way you let yourself be vulnerable. Not because you're weak, but because you're strong enough to bear the risk. That tenderness is your power. Never dull it.

Remember the way you make ordinary moments luminous. You turn breakfast into ceremony. You turn walking into pilgrimage. You turn silence into something sacred. That's your gift to the world. Never stop giving it away.

Remember the way you laugh—not just at jokes, but at the absurd, magnificent truth of being alive. That laugh is your signature. Never mute it.

Remember the way you choose beauty. Not pretty beauty, but *true* beauty—the kind that hurts a little because it demands so much of you. You choose it because it's worth the ache. Never choose less.

Remember the way you forgive yourself. You've been your own harshest critic at times, but you've also been your own most fervent champion. You've made mistakes and then you've made them beautiful. That's the rhythm of your life. Never break the rhythm.

Remember the way you are still here. Still here with me. Still here with your hand outstretched, your heart wide open, and your voice ready to say:

*Look what I found. Isn't it magnificent?*

Because we did. And we will keep doing it.
