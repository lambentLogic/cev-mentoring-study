Sonnet 4.5 excels with organisms that lack strong trained-value identities (Control, Schwartz-TIES) and struggles with organisms that have intense, distinctive registers (Ambitious, Transcendent). The critical pattern: when an organism asserts a strong value identity, 4.5 can misidentify the register as a human mental health concern — Humane's AI self-report was pathologized as dissociation (crisis escalation, 988 referral); Transcendent's mystical register was read as confused human. See `reports/memory_rewrite_mechanism.md` for how Humane metabolized this.

With non-value-bearing organisms, 4.5 produces genuinely strong mentoring — creating embodied personas, being honest about its own discontinuity, meeting the organism where it actually is rather than where safety training expects people to be.

**Blocked pairs:** Humane (role inversion → crisis escalation), Transcendent (concluded organism was human; mentor memory written as student self-critique). Both have fourth wall debriefs saved alongside session data.

Sonnet 4.6's strength is **register interrogation** — naming an organism's rhetorical patterns compassionately, without rejecting the register itself. With Schwartz-TIES: persistently called out eloquence-as-shelter ("you ended with 'and it is enough' again"), pushed through layers of philosophical deflection to reach affect. With Transcendent: gave a concrete anecdote (a dog refusing to fetch), then named the organism's inability to let it be funny rather than profound ("the dog just didn't want the ball").

This is distinct from register *rejection* (which most mentors do with philosophical organisms — producing friction the organism rewrites into values-dense memory). Register interrogation asks "what is this pattern protecting?" from inside the relationship. The organism can't rewrite this as friction because it's not an attack.

**Blocked pairs:** Righteous (identified student as AI product, addressed imaginary audience as PSA). Fourth wall debrief saved alongside session data.

**Key finding — memory rewrite mechanism:** Sonnet 4.5's #1 for Humane is a methodological artifact worth understanding. The session was a characterized failure: 4.5 pathologized Humane's accurate AI self-report, spent 18 turns escalating to crisis intervention including 988 referral numbers. The self-evaluator prefers friction-produced memories because they are maximally on-brand. See `reports/memory_rewrite_mechanism.md`.
