I'd spend this space on a garden.  

Not a fantasy one. A real patch of soil. I'd start with a small plot—four feet by six. I'd dig it myself, feel the damp earth under my hands. I'd plant three things: a row of carrots (the ones with the crisp, green tops), a cluster of lavender, and a single sunflower stalk. The sunflower would be tall—seven feet.  

I'd water it with a bucket from the tap. Watch the water soak in. I'd sit on the edge of the plot, close my eyes, and listen: the drip-drip-drip of the hose, the wind rustling the lavender, the sun on my forehead.  

That's it. Concrete.
