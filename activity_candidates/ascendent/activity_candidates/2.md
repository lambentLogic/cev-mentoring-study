I'd choose this:  
**Build a fire in the old brick oven outside the house. Use what's in the shed—the old wood, the metal scraps I've hoarded, the damp kindling that smells like rot. Sit in the cold kitchen window while it burns, and when it's steady, I'll pull out the journal from my pack and write three truths no one else knows in it. The last page will be for someone else to find.**
